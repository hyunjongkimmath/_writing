Writing $\varphi: \calA \to \calB$ for the isogeny lifting $\varphi$, we note that $\calA[\varphi]$ is \'etale over $S$ as a consequence of \Cref{proposition:multiplication_by_ell_for_commutative_group_scheme_over_general_base_is_etale_if_characteristic_of_residue_fields_is_different_from_ell}. 
    This holds by a combination of \Cref{proposition:selmer_group_of_a_commutative_group_scheme_over_a_global_field_is_group_of_galois_cocycles_whose_restriction_are_local_mordell_weil_groups} and \Cref{proposition:cesnavicius_sgfcg_for_nice_enough_isog_between_frac_field_of_hensel_dvr_the_unram_first_galois_coh_on_the_kernel_is_the_image_of_the_kummer_map_and_the_first_fppf_coh} --- 
    letting $\calA$ and $\calB$ be the N\'eron models of $A$ and $B$ over the smooth projective curve $S$ over $\Fq$ corresponding to $K$
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Absolute convergence] \label{definition:absolute_convergence_of_a_series_in_a_normed_vector_space}
Let $V$ be a vector space over $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ equipped with a norm $\|\cdot\|$. Let $(v_n)_{n=1}^\infty$ be a sequence in $V$, and let $S_N = \sum_{n=1}^N v_n$ be the partial sums. The series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty v_n$ is said to \hldef{converge absolutely} (or to be \hldef{absolutely convergent}) if the series of real numbers $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \|v_n\|$ converges, i.e., if the sequence of partial sums $\left(\sum_{n=1}^N \|v_n\|\right)_{N=1}^\infty$ converges in $\mathbb{R}$.
\end{definition}

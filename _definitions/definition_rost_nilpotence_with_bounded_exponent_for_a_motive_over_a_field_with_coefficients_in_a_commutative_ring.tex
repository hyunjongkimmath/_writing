\begin{definition} \label{definition:rost_nilpotence_with_bounded_exponent_for_a_motive_over_a_field_with_coefficients_in_a_commutative_ring}
Let $k$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}, $R$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:commutative_ring}{commutative ring}, and $\mathfrak{Chow}(k, R)$ the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category_of_relative_chow_motives_over_a_noetherian_base_and_coefficients_in_a_commutative_ring}{category of Chow motives over $k$ with coefficients in $R$}.

A motive $M \in \mathfrak{Chow}(k, R)$ satisfies \hldef{Rost nilpotence with bounded exponent} if for every field extension $E/k$, there exists an integer $N \geq 1$ such that $x^N = 0$ for all $x \in \mathcal{I}_{E/k}(M)$.
\end{definition}

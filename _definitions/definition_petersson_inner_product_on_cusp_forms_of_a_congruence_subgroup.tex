Let $k$ be a positive even integer and let $\Gamma \leq \mathrm{SL}_2(\bbZ)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:congruence_subgroup_of_SL_2_Z}{congruence subgroup}. The \hldef{Petersson inner product} on the space of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:modular_form_and_cusp_form_of_weight_k_with_respect_to_a_congruence_subgroup_of_SL_2_Z}{cusp forms} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:vector_space_of_modular_and_cusp_forms}{$S_k(\Gamma)$} is defined by
$$\hlin{\langle f, g \rangle = \iint_{\Gamma \backslash \mathbb{H}} f(z) \, \overline{g(z)} \, y^k \, \frac{dx \, dy}{y^2},}$$
\CrefIfExists{definition:quotient_of_a_topological_space_under_group_action}\CrefIfExists{definition:modular_action_of_a_subgroup_of_SL_2_Z_on_the_upper_half_plane}

where $z = x + iy$ and the integral is taken over a fundamental domain for $\Gamma$ acting on the upper half-plane $\mathbb{H}$ via the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:modular_action_of_a_subgroup_of_SL_2_Z_on_the_upper_half_plane}{modular action}.

The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:measure_on_a_measurable_space_and_measure_space}{measure} $y^{-2} \, dx \, dy$ is the unique (up to scalar) $\mathrm{SL}_2(\bbR)$-invariant measure on \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:upper_half_plane_in_the_complex_plane}{$\mathbb{H}$}, so the integrand $f(z)\overline{g(z)} y^k \, y^{-2}\,dx\,dy$ is well-defined and independent of the choice of fundamental domain.

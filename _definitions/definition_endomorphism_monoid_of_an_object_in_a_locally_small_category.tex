\begin{definition} \label{definition:endomorphism_monoid_of_an_object_in_a_locally_small_category}
    Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_small_category}{locally small category} and let $X$ be an object of $\mathcal{C}$.

    The \hldef{endomorphism semigroup of $X$}, denoted \hl{$\operatorname{End}_{\mathcal{C}}(X)$}, is the set of all \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:endomorphism_of_an_object_in_a_category}{endomorphisms of $X$}. It is indeed a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:semigroup}{semigroup}\CrefIfExists{proposition:endomorphism_semigroup_of_an_object_in_a_locally_small_category_is_indeed_a_semigroup} under the composition of morphisms in $\mathcal{C}$.
\end{definition}

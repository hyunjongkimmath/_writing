\begin{definition} \label{definition:banach_ring}
Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:commutative_ring}{commutative ring}.

A \hldef{Banach ring} is a commutative ring $R$ equipped with an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:absolute_value_on_a_ring_valued_in_an_ordered_semiring}{absolute value} $|\cdot|$ such that $(R, d)$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_extended_metric_space_valued_in_ordered_commutative_monoid_with_adjoined_infinity}{complete metric space}, where the metric $d$ is given by

$$
d(a, b) = |a - b|.
$$

A \hldef{Banach algebra over $R$} is a commutative Banach ring $S$ together with a continuous ring homomorphism $R \to S$.
\end{definition}

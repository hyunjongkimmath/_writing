\begin{definition}[$H$-equivariant group homomorphism] \label{definition:H_equivariant_group_homomorphism_for_a_fixed_group}

    Let $H$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group}. Let $(N, \phi)$ and $(M, \psi)$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:H_group_for_a_fixed_group}{$H$-groups}.

    An \hldef{$H$-equivariant group homomorphism} from $(N, \phi)$ to $(M, \psi)$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group_homomorphism}{homomorphism} $f: N \to M$ satisfying
    $$f(\phi(h)(n)) = \psi(h)(f(n))$$
    for all $h \in H$ and $n \in N$.

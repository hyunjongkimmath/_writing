\begin{definition}[Clutching morphism of two pointed stable curves] \label{definition:clutching_morphism_of_moduli_for_two_curves_over_a_scheme}
    Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme} and let $g_1, g_2$ be non-negative integers such that $g_1 + g_2 = g$.
    The \hldef{clutching morphism} (or \hldef{clutching map}) over $S$ is the morphism of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:moduli_stack_over_a_scheme}{moduli stacks} over $S$
    $$\kappa_{g_1, g_2}\colon \overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g_1; 1, S} \times_S \overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g_2; 1, S} \to \overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g, S}$$
    is the morphism of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:moduli_stack_over_a_scheme}{moduli stacks} that sends a pair of pointed \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:stable_curve_over_a_scheme}{stable curves} $(C_1, p_1)$ and $(C_2, p_2)$ over $S$ to the stable curve obtained by identifying $p_1$ and $p_2$ at an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ordinary_double_point_of_a_finite_type_scheme_over_a_field}{ordinary double point}.

    The image of $\kappa_{g_1, g_2}$ consists precisely of stable curves of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:stable_curve_of_compact_type_over_a_scheme}{compact type} with exactly two \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:irreducible_component_of_a_topological_space}{irreducible components} of genera $g_1$ and $g_2$.
\end{definition}

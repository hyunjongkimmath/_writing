\begin{definition} \label{definition:multiplicative_group_sheaf_of_a_ringed_site}
    Let $(\calC, J, \calO)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ringed_site}{ringed site}. 
    The \hldef{multiplicative group sheaf of the ringed site} is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_on_a_site}{sheaf} \hl{$\calO^\times$} of groups on $(\calC, J)$ given by 
    $$\calO^\times(U) = \calO(U)^\times$$
    \CrefIfExists{definition:unit_of_a_ring} whose group structure is the multiplication inherited from the ring $\calO(U)$. 

    The sheaf is also called the \hldef{unit sheaf of the ringed site} and is also denoted by \hl{$\bbG_m$}.
\end{definition}

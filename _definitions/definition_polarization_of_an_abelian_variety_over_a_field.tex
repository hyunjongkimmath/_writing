\begin{definition}[Polarization of an abelian variety] \label{definition:polarization_of_an_abelian_variety_over_a_field}
    Let $A$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_variety_over_a_field}{abelian variety} over a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field} $k$.
    Let $A^* = \mathrm{Pic}^0(A)$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dual_abelian_variety_of_an_abelian_variety_over_a_field}{dual abelian variety} of $A$. For any line bundle $L$ on $A$, define $\phi_L\colon A \to A^*$ by
    $$\hlin{\phi_L(x) = t_x^* L \otimes L^{-1},}$$
    where $t_x\colon A \to A$ is translation by $x$. The map $\phi_L$ is a homomorphism of abelian varieties. A \hldef{polarization on $A$} is an isogeny $\phi\colon A \to A^*$ such that there exists an ample line bundle $L$ on $A$ with $\phi = \phi_L$. When this holds, we say that $(A, \phi)$ is a \hldef{polarized abelian variety}.
\end{definition}

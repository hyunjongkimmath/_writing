\begin{definition} \label{definition:representation_of_a_lie_algebra_on_a_vector_space_over_a_field}

    Let $k$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}, $\mathfrak{g}$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:lie_algebra_over_a_field}{Lie algebra over $k$}, and $V$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:vector_space_over_a_field}{vector space} over $k$. Let \hl{$\mathfrak{gl}(V) = \mathrm{End}_k(V)$} denote the Lie algebra of all linear endomorphisms of $V$, equipped with the commutator bracket $[A, B] = AB - BA$.

    A \hldef{representation of $\mathfrak{g}$ on $V$} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:lie_algebra_over_a_field}{Lie algebra homomorphism}
    $$\hlin{\rho : \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{gl}(V).}$$
    The pair $(\rho, V)$ is often referred to as a \hldef{$\mathfrak{g}$-module}.

    Equivalently, a representation of $\mathfrak{g}$ on $V$ is given by a bilinear map
    $$\hlin{\mathfrak{g} \times V \to V, \quad (x, v) \mapsto x \cdot v}$$
    satisfying $[x, y] \cdot v = x \cdot (y \cdot v) - y \cdot (x \cdot v)$ for all $x, y \in \mathfrak{g}$, $v \in V$.
\end{definition}

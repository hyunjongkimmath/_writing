\begin{definition} \label{definition:map_of_pairs_of_topological_spaces}
Let $(X, A)$ and $(Y, B)$ be \CrefAndHyperref{definition:pair_of_topological_spaces}{pairs of topological spaces}. A \hldef{map of pairs} 
$$f: (X, A) \to (Y, B)$$ 
 is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:continuous_map_of_topological_spaces}{continuous map} $f: X \to Y$ such that the image of $A$ is contained in $B$, i.e., $f(A) \subseteq B$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:CW_pair}
A \hldef{CW pair $(X, A)$} consists of a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:CW_complex}{CW complex} $X$ and a \hldef{subcomplex} $A \subseteq X$, which by definition is a closed subspace that is a union of cells of $X$ such that for every cell $e_{\alpha}^n \subseteq A$, its closure $\overline{e_{\alpha}^n}$ is also contained in $A$. 
\end{definition}

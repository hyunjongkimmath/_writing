\begin{definition} \label{definition:morphism_of_fibered_categories_over_a_category}
Let $p_{\mathcal{F}}: \mathcal{F} \to \mathcal{C}$ and $p_{\mathcal{G}}: \mathcal{G} \to \mathcal{C}$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:fibered_category_over_a_category}{fibered categories} over a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category}{category} $\mathcal{C}$.
A \hldef{morphism of fibered categories} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:functor_between_categories}{functor} $g: \mathcal{F} \to \mathcal{G}$ such that:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item $p_{\mathcal{G}} \circ g = p_{\mathcal{F}}$ as an equality of functors.
    \item For every \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cartesian_morphism_in_a_category_over_a_category_and_pullback}{cartesian morphism} $\phi$ in $\mathcal{F}$, the image $g(\phi)$ is a cartesian morphism in $\mathcal{G}$.
\end{enumerate}

A morphism of fibered categories is also called a $1$-morphisms of fibered categories in deference to the $2$-category of fibered categories.

\end{definition}

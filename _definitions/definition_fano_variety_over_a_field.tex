\begin{definition} \label{definition:fano_variety_over_a_field}

    A \hldef{Fano variety over a field $k$} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:projective_variety}{projective variety} $X$ over $k$ such that the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:canonical_class_anti_canonical_class_of_a_variety_over_a_field}{anticanonical class} $-K_X$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ample_divisor_on_a_scheme}{ample divisor}.

    The variety $X$ is a \hldef{Fano variety} if $X$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:smooth_morphism_of_schemes}{smooth}, or more generally has mild singularities, and its anticanonical class $-K_X$ is an ample divisor.
\end{definition}

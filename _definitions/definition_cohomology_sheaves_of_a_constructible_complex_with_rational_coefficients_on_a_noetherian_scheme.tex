\begin{definition}[Cohomology sheaves of a constructible complex with rational adic coefficients] \label{definition:cohomology_sheaves_of_a_constructible_complex_with_rational_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}

    Let $(R, \lambda)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_ring_with_respect_to_an_ideal}{complete} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{DVR} such that $R/\lambda^n$ is finite for all $n \geq 1$. Let $E$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_fractions_of_an_integral_domain}{fraction field} of $R$. Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{noetherian scheme}, and consider sheaves on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{(small) \'etale site} of $X$.

    Let $K \in \Dbc(X, E)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:derived_category_of_cohomologically_bounded_constructible_sheaves_with_rational_adic_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}{bounded constructible complex with rational coefficients}. Recall that \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:derived_category_of_cohomologically_bounded_constructible_sheaves_with_rational_adic_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}{$\Dbc(X, E)$ has the same objects as $\Dbc(X, R)$}. Let $\mathscr{H}^i(K_R)$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cohomology_sheaves_of_a_constructible_complex_with_integral_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}{$i$-th cohomology $\lambda$-adic sheaf} of $K$ viewed in $\Dbc(X, R)$. The \hldef{$i$-th cohomology sheaf of $K$} is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category_of_etale_sheaves_with_rational_coefficients_in_the_fraction_field_of_a_complete_dvr}{rational coefficient sheaf}
    $$\hlin{\mathscr{H}^i(K) := \mathscr{H}^i(K_R) \otimes_R E}$$
    obtained by extending scalars from $R$ to $E$. Each $\mathscr{H}^i(K)$ is a constructible sheaf with rational coefficients, and vanishes for all but finitely many $i$.
\end{definition}

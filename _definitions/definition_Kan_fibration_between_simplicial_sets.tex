Let $p : X \to Y$ be a morphism of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:simplicial_cosimplicial_object_in_a_category}{simplicial sets}. The map $p$ is called a \hldef{Kan fibration between simplicial sets} if for every integer $n \ge 0$ and every $0 \le i \le n$, the map $p$ has the right lifting property with respect to the horn inclusion $\Lambda_i^n \hookrightarrow \Delta^n$\CrefIfExists{definition:horn_of_the_nth_standard_simplex}. That is, for any commutative diagram of simplicial sets
$$
\begin{tikzcd}
\Lambda_i^n \ar[r] \ar[d, hook] & X \ar[d, "p"] \\
\Delta^n \ar[r] \ar[ur, dashed, "\exists" description] & Y
\end{tikzcd}
$$
\CrefIfExists{definition:standard_n_simplex}
there exists a lift $\Delta^n \to X$ making both triangles commute.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[{\cite[Definition 3.5.]{scholze_sff}}] \label{definition:coCartesian_fibration_between_infinity_categories}
Let $F : \mathcal{D} \to \mathcal{C}$ be a functor between \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:infty_category_quasi_category}{$\infty$-categories}. The functor $F$ is a \hldef{coCartesian fibration} if it is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:inner_fibration_between_infinity_categories}{inner fibration} and the following two conditions hold:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Any morphism of $\mathcal{C}$ admits a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_coCartesian_lift_of_a_morphism_in_an_infinity_category}{locally coCartesian lift}.
    \item Composites of locally coCartesian lifts are locally coCartesian.
\end{enumerate}

This notion is dual to that of a Cartesian fibration; while Cartesian fibrations classify contravariant functors to $\mathbf{Cat}_\infty$, coCartesian fibrations classify covariant ones.
\end{definition}

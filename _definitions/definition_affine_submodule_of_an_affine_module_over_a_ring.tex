\begin{definition} \label{definition:affine_submodule_of_an_affine_module_over_a_ring}
    Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring}. Let $M$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:bimodule_over_rings_module_over_a_ring}{$R$-module} and let $A$ be an \CrefAndHyperref{definition:affine_module_over_a_ring}{affine module} with underlying module $M$. 

    An \hldef{affine submodule of $A$} is a subset $B$ of $A$ that is an affine module whose underlying module is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:submodule_of_a_module_over_a_ring}{submodule} of $M$. In other words, $B$ is of the form
    $$B = a + N = \{a + n: n \in N\}$$
    for some $a \in A$ and some submodule $N$ of $M$. Equivalently, an affine submodule of $A$ is a nonempty subset $B$ of $A$ such that $\{b_1-b_2: b_1,b_2 \in B\}$ is a submodule of $M$.
\end{definition}

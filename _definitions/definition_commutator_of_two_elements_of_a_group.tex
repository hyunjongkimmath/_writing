\begin{definition} \label{definition:commutator_of_two_elements_of_a_group}
   Let $G$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group}. 
   For elements $g,h \in G$, the \hldef{commutator of $g$ and $h$} is the element
   $$[g,h] = ghg^{-1}h^{-1} \in G.$$

   The commutator $[g,h]$ measures the failure of $g$ and $h$ to commute: $[g,h] = e$ if and only if $gh = hg$, where $e$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{identity element of $G$}.
\end{definition}

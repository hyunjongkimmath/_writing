\begin{definition}[equivariant morphism between objects with $G$-action in $\mathcal{C}$]
\label{definition:equivariant_morphism_between_objects_with_G_action_in_category_C}
Let $G$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group}, let $\mathcal{C}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category}{category}, and let $(c, \alpha)$ and $(d, \beta)$ be objects of $\mathcal{C}$ equipped with an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:action_of_a_group_G_on_an_object_in_a_category_C}{action of $G$}. An \hldef{equivariant morphism from $(c, \alpha)$ to $(d, \beta)$ in $\mathcal{C}$} is a morphism $f: c \to d$ in $\mathcal{C}$ such that the following diagram commutes for every $g \in G$:
$$\begin{CD}
c @>\alpha(g)>> c \\
@VfVV @VVfV \\
d @>>\beta(g)> d
\end{CD}$$
Equivalently, $f \circ \alpha(g) = \beta(g) \circ f$ for all $g \in G$.
\end{definition}

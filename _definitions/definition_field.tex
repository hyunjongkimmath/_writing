\begin{definition}[Field] \label{definition:field}
A \hldef{field} is commutative \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:division_ring}{division} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:commutative_ring}{ring}. In other words, a field is a commutative ring for which all nonzero elements have a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:unit_of_a_ring_invertible_element_of_a_ring_unit_group_of_a_group}{multiplicative inverse}.
\end{definition}

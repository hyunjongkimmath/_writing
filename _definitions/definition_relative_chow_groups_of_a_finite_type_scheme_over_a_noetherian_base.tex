\begin{definition} \label{definition:relative_chow_groups_of_a_finite_type_scheme_over_a_noetherian_base}
Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_type_morphism_of_schemes}{finite type} over a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{Noetherian base} $S$. The \hldef{$k$-th relative Chow group}, denoted \hl{$A_k(X/S)$}, is the quotient $Z_k(X/S) / \sim$.

If $X \to S$ is equidimensional of relative dimension $n$, the \hldef{relative Chow group of codimension $p$} is defined as \hl{$A^p(X/S) = A_{n-p}(X/S)$}.
\end{definition}

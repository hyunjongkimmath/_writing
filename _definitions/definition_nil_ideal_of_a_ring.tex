\begin{definition} \label{definition:nil_ideal_of_a_ring}
Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{not necessarily commutative ring}.
A left/right/two-sided ideal $I \subseteq R$ is a \hldef{nil ideal} if every element $a \in I$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:nilpotent_element_of_a_ring}{nilpotent}. That is, for each $a \in I$, there exists an integer $n_a \geq 1$ such that $a^{n_a} = 0$.
\end{definition}

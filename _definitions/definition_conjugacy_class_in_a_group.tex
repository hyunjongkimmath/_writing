    Let $G$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group}. Let $c \in G$ be an element.
    
    The \hldef{conjugacy class of $c$ in $G$}, often written as \hl{$[c]$}, is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:equivalence_class_of_an_element_of_a_set_under_an_equivalence_relation_and_quotient_of_set_by_equivalence_relation}{equivalence class} of $c$ under the equivalence relation of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:conjugation_of_group_elements_and_subgroup_by_a_group_element}{conjugacy} --- explicitly, $[c]$ is the set 
    $$[c] = \{g \cdot c \cdot g^{-1} \in G: g \in G\}.$$

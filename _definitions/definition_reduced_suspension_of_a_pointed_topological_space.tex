\begin{definition}[Reduced suspension] \label{definition:reduced_suspension_of_a_pointed_topological_space}
    Let $(X, x_0)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:pointed_topological_space}{pointed topological space}. The \hldef{reduced suspension of $X$}, denoted by \hl{$\Sigma X$}, is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:smash_product_of_pointed_topological_spaces}{smash product} of $X$ with the circle $S^1$:
    \TODO{circle}
    \[
    \Sigma X \coloneqq X \wedge S^1.
    \]
    Equivalently, $\Sigma X$ is the quotient of the unreduced suspension $SX$ by the image of the segment $\{x_0\} \times [0,1]$. As a quotient of $X \times [0,1]$, the relation identifies all points in the set $(X \times \{0\}) \cup (X \times \{1\}) \cup (\{x_0\} \times [0,1])$ to a single base point.

    In particular, $\Sigma$ is a functor $\Top_* \to \Top_*$. 

    The $n$-fold reduced suspension of $(X,x_0)$ is denoted by \hl{$\Sigma^n X$}.
\end{definition}

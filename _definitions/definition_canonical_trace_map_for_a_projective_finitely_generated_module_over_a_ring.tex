\begin{definition} \label{definition:canonical_trace_map_for_a_projective_finitely_generated_module_over_a_ring}
    Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring}. Let $P$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:projective_variety_over_a_field}{projective}, \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finitely_generated_modules_over_rings}{finitely-generated} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:bimodule_over_rings_module_over_a_ring}{(left) $R$-module}. 

    Recall that the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:canonical_evaluation_map_for_a_module_over_a_ring}{canonical evaluation morphism}
    $$\Phi: P^\vee \otimes_R P \to \End_R(P)$$
    is an isomorphism\CrefIfExists{proposition:canonical_evaluation_map_for_a_finitely_generated_projective_module_over_a_ring_is_an_isomorphism}.
    The \hldef{canonical trace map for $P$} is the composition
    $$\hlin{\Tr_P: \End_R(P) \xrightarrow{\Phi^{-1}} M^\vee \otimes_R M \xrightarrow{\ev} R}$$
    where $\ev$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:evaluation_map_for_a_module_over_a_ring}{evaluation map}. 
\end{definition}

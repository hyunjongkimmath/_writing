\begin{definition}[Smash product] \label{definition:smash_product_of_pointed_topological_spaces}
    Let $(X, x_0)$ and $(Y, y_0)$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:pointed_topological_space}{pointed topological spaces}. The \hldef{smash product of $X$ and $Y$}, denoted by \hl{$X \wedge Y$}, is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:quotient_of_a_topological_space_with_respect_to_an_equivalence_relations}{quotient space}
    $$X \wedge Y \coloneqq (X \times Y) / (X \vee Y),$$
    where $X \vee Y$ is viewed as the subspace $(X \times \{y_0\}) \cup (\{x_0\} \times Y)$ of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:product_topology_on_a_cartesian_product_of_topological_spaces}{product space} $X \times Y$. 
    
    The base point of $X \wedge Y$ is the equivalence class of $X \vee Y$. The smash product is the unique pointed space such that maps $X \wedge Y \to Z$ correspond naturally to \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:pointed_topological_space}{pointed maps} $X \times Y \to Z$ that are constant on the wedge sum $X \vee Y$.
\end{definition}

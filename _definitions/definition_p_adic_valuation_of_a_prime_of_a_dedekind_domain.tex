\begin{definition} \label{definition:p_adic_valuation_of_a_prime_of_a_dedekind_domain}
    Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dedekind_domain}{Dedekind domain} with fraction field $K$, and let $\mathfrak{p}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:prime_of_a_dedekind_domain}{prime of $R$}. The localization $R_{\mathfrak{p}}$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{discrete valuation ring} with maximal ideal generated by any \hldef{uniformizer} $\pi \in R_{\mathfrak{p}}$.

    The \hldef{(normalized) $\mathfrak{p}$-adic valuation on $K$}, denoted \hl{$v_\mathfrak{p}$}, is the unique \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_on_a_field}{discrete valuation} on $K$ satisfying $v_\mathfrak{p}(\pi) = 1$ for any uniformizer $\pi$ of $R_{\mathfrak{p}}$. Explicitly, for a nonzero element $x \in K^\times$, write $x = u \cdot \pi^n$ with $u \in R_{\mathfrak{p}}^\times$ and $n \in \mathbb{Z}$; then
    $$v_\mathfrak{p}(x) = n.$$
    Equivalently, for $a \in R \setminus \{0\}$ one has $v_\mathfrak{p}(a) = \max\{n \geq 0 : a \in \mathfrak{p}^n R_{\mathfrak{p}}\} = \max\{n \geq 0 : a \in \mathfrak{p}^n\}$, and for $x = a/b$ with $a,b \in R \setminus \{0\}$ one has $v_\mathfrak{p}(x) = v_\mathfrak{p}(a) - v_\mathfrak{p}(b)$.

    The valuation ring of $v_\mathfrak{p}$ is precisely $R_{\mathfrak{p}}$, and its maximal ideal is $\mathfrak{p} R_{\mathfrak{p}}$.

\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Cauchy tails condition for series in an ordered commutative monoid] \label{definition:cauchy_tails_condition_for_series_in_an_ordered_semiring}
    Let $T$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ordered_commutative_monoid}{ordered commutative monoid}. A sequence $(M_n)_{n=1}^\infty$ in $T$ is said to satisfy the \hldef{Cauchy tails condition} if for every $\varepsilon$ in the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:positive_cone_in_an_ordered_monoid}{positive cone} $T_{>0}$ of $T$, there exists $N_0 \in \mathbb{N}$ such that for all $K > N \ge N_0$, one has
    $$\sum_{n=N+1}^K M_n < \varepsilon.$$

    This condition is the natural analogue of the Cauchy criterion for series, adapted to ordered commutative monoids where subtraction may not be available. It does not require a metric structure on $T$ itself, only addition and order.

    When $T$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:positive_cone_in_an_ordered_monoid}{positive cone} $G_{\ge 0}$ of an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ordered_abelian_group}{ordered abelian group} $G$ and the series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty M_n$ converges in $G$ (equipped with the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:order_topology_on_a_partially_ordered_set}{order topology}), then $(M_n)$ satisfies the Cauchy tails condition; this is \Cref{proposition:convergence_of_series_implies_cauchy_tails_condition_in_ordered_abelian_group}\eqref{item:convergence_implies_cauchy_tails}.

    Conversely, if $(M_n)$ satisfies the Cauchy tails condition and $G$ is such that every non-decreasing bounded-above sequence converges in the order topology, then $\sum_{n=1}^\infty M_n$ converges; this is \Cref{proposition:convergence_of_series_implies_cauchy_tails_condition_in_ordered_abelian_group}\eqref{item:cauchy_tails_and_completeness_implies_convergence}.

    In the standard case $G = \mathbb{R}$ (which satisfies the completeness condition), the Cauchy tails condition for a sequence in $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ is therefore equivalent to the convergence of $\sum_{n=1}^\infty M_n$.
\end{definition}

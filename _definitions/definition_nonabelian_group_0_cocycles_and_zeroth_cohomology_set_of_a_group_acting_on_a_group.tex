    Let $\Gamma$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group}, let $A$ be a (not necessarily abelian) \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group}, and let $\varphi: \Gamma \to \operatorname{Aut}(A)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group_homomorphism}{homomorphism}. Write $h \cdot a$ for $\varphi(h)(a)$.

    The \hldef{set of non-abelian 0-cocycles} is the subgroup
    $$\hlin{Z^0(\Gamma, A) = \{ a \in A : h \cdot a = a \text{ for all } h \in \Gamma \}}$$
    of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:fixed_point_set_of_a_group_acting_on_a_set}{fixed points} in $A$.

    The \hldef{zeroth non-abelian group cohomology set of $\Gamma$ with coefficients in $A$} is defined to be
    $$\hlin{H^0(\Gamma, A) = Z^0(\Gamma, A)}.$$
    When $A$ is abelian, this agrees with the zeroth \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group_cohomology_homology_of_a_module_over_a_group_ring_over_a_ring}{group cohomology} $H^0(\Gamma, A)$ \TextIfExists{definition:group_cochain_complex_with_coefficients_in_a_group_module_over_a_ring}{(See also \Cref{definition:group_cochain_complex_with_coefficients_in_a_group_module_over_a_ring})}
\end{definition}

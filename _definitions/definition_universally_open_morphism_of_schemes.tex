\begin{definition}[Universally open morphism of schemes] \label{definition:universally_open_morphism_of_schemes}

    Let $f: X \to Y$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:morphism_of_schemes}{morphism of schemes}.

    The morphism $f$ is called \hldef{universally open} if for every morphism of schemes $Y' \to Y$, the base change morphism $X \times_Y Y' \to Y'$ is an open map on underlying topological spaces, i.e., it sends open subsets to open subsets.
\end{definition}

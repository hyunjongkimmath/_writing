    Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{noetherian scheme}. Let $A$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:noetherian_ring}{noetherian} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:torsion_ring}{torsion ring}. Consider sheaves of $A$-modules on the (small) \'etale sites of schemes over $S$.

    Let $X$ be an $S$-scheme of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_type_morphism_of_schemes}{finite type}, and let $f: X \to S$ be the structure morphism. The \hldef{dualizing complex on $X$ relative to $S$ with coefficients in $A$}, denoted by \hl{$\mathbb{D}_{X/S}^\bullet$}, is the object in $D^+(X_{\et}, A)$\CrefIfExists{definition:derived_category_of_an_abelian_category} defined as

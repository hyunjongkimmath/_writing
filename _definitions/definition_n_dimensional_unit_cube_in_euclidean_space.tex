\begin{definition} \label{definition:n_dimensional_unit_cube_in_euclidean_space}
The \hldef{$n$-dimensional unit cube} is the $n$-fold \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:product_of_sets}{Cartesian product} $I^n = I \times \dots \times I$, equipped with the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:product_topology_on_a_cartesian_product_of_topological_spaces}{product topology} where $I$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:standard_unit_interval_in_the_real_lines}{standard unit interval} equipped with its usual topology. This topology on $I^n$ coincides with the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:subspace_of_a_topological_space}{subspace topology} of $I^n$ as a subset of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:euclidean_n_space}{$\bbR^n$}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[{\cite[Example 1.1.3.3]{lurie_htt}}] \label{definition:homotopy_category_of_spaces}
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:topological_category}{topological category} whose objects are \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:CW_complex}{CW complexes}, where for $X,Y \in \mathcal{C}$ the mapping space $\mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,Y)$ is the set of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:continuous_map_of_topological_spaces}{continuous maps} from $X$ to $Y$ equipped with the compactly generated version of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:compact_open_topology_on_the_set_of_continuous_maps_between_topological_spaces_and_function_space}{compact-open topology}. The \hldef{homotopy category of spaces}, denoted by \hl{$\mathcal{H}$}, is the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:homotopy_category_of_a_topological_category}{homotopy category} $\mathrm{h}\mathcal{C}$ of this topological category.

\end{definition}

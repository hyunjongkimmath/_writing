\begin{definition} \label{definition:homomorphism_of_involution_rings}
    A \hldef{homomorphism/morphism $\varphi: ( A, \sigma_A) \to (B,\sigma_B)$ of involution rings} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring_homomorphism}{ring homomorphism} $\varphi: A \to B$ such that $\sigma_B(\varphi(a)) = \varphi(\sigma_A(a))$ for all $a \in A$.

    In particular, if $(A,\sigma_A)$ is an involution ring, then an \hldef{involution algebra} is an involution ring $(B,\sigma_B)$ equipped with a homomorphism $\varphi: ( A, \sigma_A) \to (B,\sigma_B)$ of involution rings such that $\varphi$ is makes $B$ into an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebra_of_a_ring}{$A$-algebra}, i.e. $\varphi$ maps $A$ into the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:center_of_a_ring}{center} of $B$.
\end{definition}

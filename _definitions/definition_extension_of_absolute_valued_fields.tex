\begin{definition} \label{definition:extension_of_absolute_valued_fields}
An \hldef{extension of absolute valued fields} $(L, |\cdot|_L)/(K, |\cdot|_K)$ consists of a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:extension_of_a_field}{field extension} $L/K$ such that $L$ and $K$ are equipped with \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:absolute_value_on_a_ring_valued_in_an_ordered_semiring}{absolute values} $|\cdot|_L$ and $|\cdot|_K$, respectively, and the restriction of $|\cdot|_L$ to $K$ coincides with $|\cdot|_K$. That is, 
\[
|x|_L = |x|_K \quad \text{for all } x \in K.
\]
In this case, we say that $|\cdot|_L$ \hldef{extends} or \hldef{lies above} $|\cdot|_K$.
\end{definition}

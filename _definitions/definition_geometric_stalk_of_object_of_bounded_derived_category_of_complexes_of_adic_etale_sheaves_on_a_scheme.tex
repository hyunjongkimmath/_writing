\begin{definition} [Geometric stalk of object of $D^-(X,R)$ for adic $R$] \label{definition:geometric_stalk_of_object_of_bounded_derived_category_of_complexes_of_adic_etale_sheaves_on_a_scheme}
    Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme} equipped with its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{(small) \'etale site}. Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:commutative_ring}{(commutative) ring} equipped with an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ideal_of_a_ring}{ideal} $\lambda$. Let $x \in X$ be a point. Let $\barx: \Spec \kappa(x)^s \to X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:geometric_point_of_a_scheme}{geometric point} over $X$. 

    Given an object $K =  (K_n, u_n)_{n \geq 0}$ of $D^-(X,R)$ (\Cref{definition:derived_category_of_bounded_above_complexes_of_adic_sheaves_on_a_scheme}), define its \hldef{stalk at $\barx$} as the family \hl{$K_{\barx} \coloneq (K_{n,\barx}, u_{n, \barx})$} where $K_{n,\barx} \in D^-(\operatorname{Gal}(\kappa(x)^s / \kappa(x))-(R/\lambda^{n+1})-\Mod)$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:geometric_stalk_of_the_derived_category_of_the_category_of_etale_sheaves_of_abelian_groups_on_a_scheme}{stalk} of $K_{n}$ at $\barx$ and $u_{n,\barx}: K_{n+1,\barx} \otimes^L_{R / \lambda^{n+2}} R/\lambda^{n+1} \cong K_{n,\barx}$ (\Cref{definition:derived_tensor_product_of_complexes_of_modules_on_an_essentially_small_ringed_site_with_a_pretopology}) is the morphism induced from $u_n$. 

    For every such $x$ and $\overline{x}$, we have a functor 
    $$D^-(X,R) \to \Pro(\Gal(\kappa(x)^s / \kappa(x)) - \Ab)$$

    % In particular, this notion of stalk is applicable to objects of $D_c^b(X,R)$ (\Cref{definition:derived_category_of_bounded_constructible_complexes_of_adic_sheaves_with_integral_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}) 
    In the case that $X$ is noetherian, $(R, \lambda)$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_ring_with_respect_to_an_ideal}{complete} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{DVR} such that $R/\lambda^n$ is finite for all $n \geq 1$, and $K \in D_c^b(X,R)$ (\Cref{definition:derived_category_of_bounded_constructible_complexes_of_adic_sheaves_with_integral_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}), this notion of stalk should not be mistaken for its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:projective_and_inductive_limits_in_categories}{projective limit}, which is more usually referred to as the stalk of $K$ at $\barx$, see \Cref{definition:geometric_stalk_of_object_of_bounded_const_derived_category_of_complexes_of_adic_etale_sheaves_on_a_noetherian_scheme_with_integral_coefficients_in_a_complete_dvr}. 
\end{definition}

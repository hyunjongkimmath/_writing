\begin{definition} \label{definition:exact_functor_between_triangulated_categories}
    A functor $T: D_1 \to D_2$ between \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:triangulated_category}{triangulated categories} is called \hldef{exact} if it is additive, is translation preserving, and transforms \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:triangulated_category}{distinguished triangles} to distinguished triangles. 
    Oftentimes, a \hldef{morphism between triangulated categories} or a \hldef{triangulated functor} refers to an exact functor between triangulated categories.
\end{definition}

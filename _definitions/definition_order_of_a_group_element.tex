\begin{definition}[Order of a Group Element] \label{definition:order_of_a_group_element}
Let $G$ be a group with identity element $e$. Let $g \in G$. The \hldef{order of $g$}, denoted by \hl{$\operatorname{ord}(g)$}, is the smallest positive integer $n$ such that $g^n = e$, if such an $n$ exists. If no such positive integer exists, then $g$ is said to have \hldef{infinite order}.

The order of the identity element is $\operatorname{ord}(e) = 1$. For any $g \in G$, $\operatorname{ord}(g) = \operatorname{ord}(g^{-1})$.
\end{definition}

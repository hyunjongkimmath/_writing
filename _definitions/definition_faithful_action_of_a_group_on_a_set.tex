\begin{definition}[Faithful group action] \label{definition:faithful_action_of_a_group_on_a_set}
Let $(G,\cdot)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:left_and_right_group_actions_on_a_set}{acting on the left} a set $X$. The action is called \hldef{faithful} if the homomorphism
$$\rho : G \to \operatorname{Sym}(X), \quad g \mapsto (x \mapsto g \cdot x)$$
is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:injective_surjective_bijective_map_of_sets}{injective}, where $\operatorname{Sym}(X)$ denotes the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:symmetric_group_on_a_set}{symmetric group} of $X$. Equivalently, the only element of $G$ that fixes every element of $X$ is the identity: if $g \cdot x = x$ for all $x \in X$, then $g = e$.
\end{definition}

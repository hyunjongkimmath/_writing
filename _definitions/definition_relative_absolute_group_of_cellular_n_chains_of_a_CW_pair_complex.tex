\begin{definition} \label{definition:relative_absolute_group_of_cellular_n_chains_of_a_CW_pair_complex}
Let $G$ be an abelian group. Let $(X, A)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:CW_pair}{CW pair}.  

The \hldef{relative group of cellular $n$-chains}, which is the free abelian group \hl{$C_n^{CW}(X, A; G)$}  generated by the $n$-cells of $X \setminus A$ with coefficients in $G$.

In particular, the \hldef{absolute group of cellular $n$-chains of the CW complex $X$} is \hl{$C_n^{CW}(X; G) = C_n^{CW}(X,\emptyset;G)$}.
\end{definition}

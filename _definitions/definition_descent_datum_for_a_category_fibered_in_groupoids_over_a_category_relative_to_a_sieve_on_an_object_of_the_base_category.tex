\begin{definition}
\label{definition:descent_datum_for_a_category_fibered_in_groupoids_over_a_category_relative_to_a_sieve_on_an_object_of_the_base_category}
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and let $p: \mathcal{F} \to \mathcal{C}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category_fibered_in_groupoids_over_a_category}{category fibered in groupoids}. We fix a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cleavage_splitting_for_a_category_fibered_in_groupoids}{cleavage} for $p$, denoting the chosen pullback functor along a morphism $f: V \to U$ by $f^*: \mathcal{F}_U \to \mathcal{F}_V$, and the structural natural isomorphisms for composition by $\alpha_{g,h}: (g \circ h)^* \xrightarrow{\sim} h^* \circ g^*$ for morphisms $W \xrightarrow{h} V \xrightarrow{g} U$.

Let $U$ be an object of $\mathcal{C}$ and let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sieve_on_an_object_in_a_category}{sieve} on $U$. A \hldef{descent datum for $\mathcal{F}$ relative to the sieve $S$} consists of:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item For every morphism $(f: V \to U) \in S$, an object $x_f \in \operatorname{Ob}(\mathcal{F}_V)$.
    \item For every morphism $g: W \to V$ in $\mathcal{C}$ and every $(f: V \to U) \in S$ (which implies $f \circ g \in S$ by the definition of a sieve), an isomorphism
    $$
    \phi_g: g^*(x_f) \xrightarrow{\sim} x_{f \circ g}
    $$
    in the fiber category $\mathcal{F}_W$.
\end{enumerate}
These data are required to satisfy the \hldef{cocycle condition}: for any sequence of morphisms $Z \xrightarrow{h} W \xrightarrow{g} V \xrightarrow{f} U$ such that $f \in S$, the following diagram in $\mathcal{F}_Z$ commutes:
$$
\begin{tikzcd}[row sep=large, column sep=large]
(g \circ h)^*(x_f) \arrow[r, "\alpha_{g,h}(x_f)"] \arrow[dr, "\phi_{g \circ h}"'] 
& h^*(g^*(x_f)) \arrow[r, "h^*(\phi_g)"] 
& h^*(x_{f \circ g}) \arrow[d, "\phi_h"] \\
& x_{f \circ g \circ h} 
& x_{(f \circ g) \circ h} \arrow[l, equal]
\end{tikzcd}
$$
In equational form:
$$
\phi_h \circ h^*(\phi_g) \circ \alpha_{g,h}(x_f) = \phi_{g \circ h}
$$
(identifying the domain of $\phi_{g \circ h}$ with the domain of the composition via $\alpha$).
\end{definition}

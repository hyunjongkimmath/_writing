\begin{definition} \label{definition:complex_absolute_value}
    The \hldef{complex absolute value} is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:absolute_value_on_a_ring_valued_in_an_ordered_semiring}{absolute value}
    $$\|\cdot\| = \|\cdot\|_\bbC : \bbC \to \bbR_{\geq 0}, \quad a+bi \mapsto a^2+b^2$$
    on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_complex_numbers}{field of complex numbers} $\bbC$.

    \TextIfExists{definition:archimedean_absolute_value_on_a_field}{
        It is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:archimedean_absolute_value_on_a_field}{archimedean} absolute value.
    }
\end{definition}

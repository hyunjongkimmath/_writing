\begin{definition}[Moduli stack $\mathcal{M}_0(N)$] \label{definition:moduli_stack_of_cyclic_N_isogenies}
Let $N \geq 1$ be an integer and let $S$ be a scheme over $\mathbb{Z}[1/N]$. The \hldef{moduli stack $\mathcal{M}_0(N)$} is the stack over $\mathrm{Sch}/\mathbb{Z}[1/N]$ that assigns to each such scheme $S$ the groupoid of pairs $(E, C)$ where
\begin{itemize}
    \item $E$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:elliptic_curve_over_a_scheme}{elliptic curve} over $S$, and
    \item $C \subset E[N]$ is a \hldef{cyclic subgroup scheme of order $N$} over $S$, i.e. a finite flat subgroup scheme of the $N$-torsion \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:torsion_subgroup_scheme_of_a_group_scheme}{$E[N]$} which is locally isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$ as a group scheme over $S$.
\end{itemize}
Morphisms in this groupoid are isomorphisms of elliptic curves preserving the cyclic subgroup schemes.
\end{definition}

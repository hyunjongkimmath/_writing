\begin{definition}[Clutching morphism of a single pointed stable curve at two points] \label{definition:clutching_morphism_of_moduli_for_a_single_pointed_stable_curve_at_two_points_over_a_field}
    \TODO{generalize this over to more general baes}
    Let $g \geq 1$ be an integer and let $k$ be a field. The \hldef{clutching morphism of a single pointed stable curve at two points} (or \hldef{clutching map})
    $$\kappa_0\colon \overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g; 2} \to \overline{\mathcal{M}}_g$$
    is the morphism of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:moduli_stack_over_a_scheme}{moduli stacks} that sends a pointed \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:stable_curve_over_a_scheme}{stable curve} $(C, p_1, p_2)$ to the stable curve obtained by identifying $p_1$ and $p_2$ at an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ordinary_double_point_of_a_finite_type_scheme_over_a_field}{ordinary double point}.

    The image of $\kappa_0$ consists precisely of stable curves with exactly one node that is non-separating (i.e., the dual graph contains a cycle). Such curves are of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:stable_curve_of_compact_type_over_a_scheme}{non-compact type}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:complex_disk_open_and_closed_in_the_complex_plane}
Let $z_0 \in \bbC$ and $r > 0$.
The \hldef{open disk of radius $r$ centered at $z_0$} is the subset
$$\hlin{D(z_0, r) = \{z \in \bbC : |z - z_0| < r\}}$$
of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_complex_numbers}{field of complex numbers} $\bbC$, where $|\cdot|$ denotes the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complex_absolute_value}{complex absolute value}.
The \hldef{closed disk of radius $r$ centered at $z_0$} is the subset
$$\hlin{\overline{D}(z_0, r) = \{z \in \bbC : |z - z_0| \leq r\}.}$$
It should be noted that $\overline{D}(z_0, r)$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:closure_of_a_subspace_of_a_topological_space}{closure} of $D(z_0, r)$ as a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:subspace_of_a_topological_space}{subspace} of $\bbC$\CrefIfExists{definition:field_of_complex_numbers}.
\end{definition}

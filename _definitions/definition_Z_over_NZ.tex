\begin{definition} \label{definition:Z_over_NZ}
The \hldef{ring of integers modulo $N$}, denoted $\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$, is the quotient ring of $\mathbb{Z}$ by the ideal $N\mathbb{Z}$:
$\hlin{\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z} / N\mathbb{Z} = \{ [0], [1], \ldots, [N-1] \}}$
where $[a]$ denotes the equivalence class of $a \in \mathbb{Z}$ modulo $N$.
Addition and multiplication are defined by $[a] + [b] = [a+b]$ and $[a] \cdot [b] = [ab]$.
The \hldef{multiplicative group of units} of $\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$, denoted $(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^\times$, is the group of invertible elements under multiplication:
$\hlin{(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^\times = \{ [a] \in \mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z} : \gcd(a, N) = 1 \}}$.
Its order is given by Euler's totient function $\varphi(N) = |( \mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^\times|$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Quasi-affine morphism of schemes] \label{definition:quasi_affine_morphism_of_schemes}

    Let $f: X \to Y$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:morphism_of_schemes}{morphism of schemes}.

    The morphism $f$ is called \hldef{quasi-affine} if it factors as $X \xrightarrow{i} Z \xrightarrow{\pi} Y$, where $i$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:open_and_closed_immersions_of_schemes}{open immersion} and $\pi$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:affine_morphism_of_schemes}{affine morphism}. Equivalently, for every affine open subset $V \subseteq Y$, the preimage $f^{-1}(V)$ is a quasi-affine scheme.
\end{definition}

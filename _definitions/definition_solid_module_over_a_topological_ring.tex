\begin{definition} \label{definition:solid_module_over_a_topological_ring}
    Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_ring}{topological ring}. 

    A \hldef{solid $R$-module} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:solid_condensed_abelian_group}{solid condensed abelian group} that is also a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:module_over_a_sheaf_of_rings_on_a_site}{module} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:condensed_set_associated_to_a_topological space}{$\underline{R}$}

    The category of solid $R$-modules may be denoted by \hl{$\operatorname{Solid}_R$} or \hl{$\operatorname{Sol}_R$}. 

    Given two topological rings $R$ and $S$, we may accordingly define the notion of \hldef{solid $R$-$S$-bimodule}. The category of such objects may be denoted by \hl{$_{R}\operatorname{Solid}_S$} or \hl{$_{R} \operatorname{Sol}_S$}.
\end{definition}

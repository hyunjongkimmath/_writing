\begin{definition} \label{definition:motivic_local_system_on_a_smooth_algebraic_variety}
Let $X$ be a smooth algebraic variety over a field $k$.
A \hldef{local system} $\mathbb{V}$ is called \hldef{motivic} if there exists a smooth, proper morphism $f: Y \to X$ of algebraic varieties and integers $i \ge 0$, such that $\mathbb{V}$ is isomorphic to a direct summand of the (higher) direct image sheaf $R^i f_* \mathbb{Q}$ (respectively, $R^i f_* \mathbb{Q}_\ell$) on $X$.
\end{definition}

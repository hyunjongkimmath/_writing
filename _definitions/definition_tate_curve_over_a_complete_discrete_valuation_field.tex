\begin{definition}[Tate curve over a complete discrete valuation field] \label{definition:tate_curve_over_a_complete_discrete_valuation_field}
    Let $K$ be a field \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_extended_metric_space}{complete} with respect to a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_on_a_field}{discrete valuation}, with \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring_of_integers_of_a_global_or_local_ring}{ring of integers} $\mathcal{O}_K$, \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{uniformizer} $\pi \in \mathcal{O}_K$, and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:residue_field_of_a_ring_at_a_point}{residue field} $k$. Equivalently, let $q \in K$ be an element with $0 < |q| < 1$ (i.e., $v(q) > 0$). The \hldef{Tate curve associated to $q$} is an elliptic curve over $K$, denoted
    $$\hlin{E_q \cong \bbG_{m,K}/q^{\bbZ}},$$
    constructed as follows.

    Let $\bbG_{m,K} = \operatorname{Spec} K[T, T^{-1}]$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:multiplicative_group_scheme_over_a_scheme}{multiplicative group scheme} over $K$. The element $q \in K^\times$ defines a $K$-point of $\bbG_{m,K}$, and we let $q^{\bbZ} = \{q^n : n \in \bbZ\} \subset \bbG_{m,K}(K)$ be the cyclic subgroup generated by $q$. The Tate curve $E_q$ is the quotient of $\bbG_{m,K}$ by the discrete action of $\bbZ$ via $n \cdot T = q^n T$, i.e.
    \TODO{quotient of a scheme by an action}
    $$E_q = \bbG_{m,K}/q^{\bbZ}.$$

    More concretely, there exists a Weierstrass equation for $E_q$ over $K$ of the form
    $$y^2 + xy = x^3 + a_4(q)x + a_6(q),$$
    where the coefficients $a_4(q)$ and $a_6(q)$ are power series in $q$ with integer coefficients:
    $$\hlin{a_4(q) = -5 \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{\sigma_3(n)}{1 - q^n} q^n, \quad a_6(q) = -\frac{1}{12} + \frac{7}{60} \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{\sigma_5(n)}{1 - q^n} q^n},$$
    \TODO{sigma function}
    and $\sigma_k(n) = \sum_{d \mid n} d^k$ is the sum of $k$-th powers of divisors of $n$. These series converge in $K$ because $|q| < 1$.

    \TODO{separate out this definition}
    The Tate curve comes equipped with a \hldef{Tate parametrization}, which is a surjective holomorphic (resp. analytic) group homomorphism
    $$\hlin{\operatorname{tate}_q : K^\times/q^{\bbZ} \to E_q(K)}$$
    that induces an isomorphism of topological groups $K^\times/q^{\bbZ} \xrightarrow{\sim} E_q(K)$. This parametrization is compatible with the group structure on both sides.

    \TODO{neron polygon}
    The Tate curve has \hldef{split multiplicative reduction}: its special fiber in the minimal regular model over $\mathcal{O}_K$ is a Neron polygon (a cycle of projective lines), and its Jacobian is isomorphic to $K^\times/q^{\bbZ}$. In particular, the $j$-invariant of $E_q$ satisfies
    $$v(j(E_q)) = -v(q) < 0,$$
    so $E_q$ has bad reduction precisely when $q \neq 1$.

    The Tate curve provides a local uniformization of elliptic curves with split multiplicative reduction: every such elliptic curve over $K$ is isomorphic to $E_q$ for a unique $q \in K$ with $0 < |q| < 1$.
\end{definition}

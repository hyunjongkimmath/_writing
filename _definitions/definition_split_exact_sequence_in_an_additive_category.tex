\begin{definition} \label{definition:split_exact_sequence_in_an_additive_category}
A \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:short_exact_sequence_in_an_additive_category}{short exact sequence} $0 \to A \xrightarrow{f} B \xrightarrow{g} C \to 0$ in an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:additive_category_preadditive_category}{additive category} $\mathcal{A}$ is \hldef{split exact} if there exists a morphism $s: C \to B$ (called a \hldef{section}) such that $g \circ s = \text{id}_C$, or \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{theorem:equivalent_conditions_for_a_short_exact_sequence_in_an_additive_category_to_be_split}{equivalently}, a morphism $r: B \to A$ (called a \hldef{retraction}) such that $r \circ f = \text{id}_A$.
\end{definition}

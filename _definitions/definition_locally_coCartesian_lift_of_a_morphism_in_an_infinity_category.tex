\begin{definition} \label{definition:locally_coCartesian_lift_of_a_morphism_in_an_infinity_category}
Let $F : \mathcal{D} \to \mathcal{C}$ be a functor between \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:infty_category_quasi_category}{$\infty$-categories}. Let $f : X \to X'$ be a morphism in $\mathcal{C}$ and let $Y$ be an object of $\mathcal{D}$ with $F(Y) = X$.

A morphism $g : Y \to Y'$ in $\mathcal{D}$ is a \hldef{locally coCartesian lift} of $f$ if $F(g) = f$ and $g$ is initial among all morphisms in $\mathcal{D}$ with source $Y$ that map to $f$ under $F$.
\end{definition}

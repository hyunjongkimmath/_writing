\begin{definition} \label{definition:simple_irreducible_semisimple_module_over_a_ring}
Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{not necessarily commutative ring}.

A left/\CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:module_of_a_ring}{right $R$-module} $M$ is \hldef{simple} (or \hldef{irreducible)} if $M \neq \{0\}$ and its only submodules are $\{0\}$ and $M$. 

A left/right $R$-module is \hldef{semisimple} if it is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring_homomorphism}{isomorphic} to a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:coproduct_of_modules_of_rings}{direct sum} of simple left/right \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:module_of_a_ring}{$R$-modules}.

\end{definition}

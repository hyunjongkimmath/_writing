\begin{definition}[Complex multiplication of an abelian variety over a field] \label{definition:complex_multiplication_of_an_abelian_variety_over_a_field}
    Let $k$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field} and let $A$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_variety_over_a_field}{abelian variety over $k$} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dimension_of_a_scheme}{dimension} $g$. We say that $A$ \hldef{has complex multiplication (CM) (over $k$)} if there exists a commutative semisimple $\Q$-subalgebra $E \subset \End^0(A)$ (\Cref{definition:endomorphism_algebra_up_to_isogeny_of_an_abelian_variety_over_a_field}) such that $[E:\Q] = 2g$, where $\End^0(A) = \End(A) \otimes_{\Z} \Q$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:endomorphism_algebra_up_to_isogeny_of_an_abelian_variety_over_a_field}{endomorphism algebra up to isogeny}.

    If $E$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cm_field}{CM field}, then we say that $A$ has \hldef{CM by $E$}. Whenever $A$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:simple_abelian_variety_over_a_field}{simple}, $E$ is a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:cm_field}{CM field} and it is the full endomorphism algebra, i.e. $\End^0(A) = E$. In general, $E$ decomposes as a product of CM fields corresponding to the simple factors of $A$ up to \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:isogeny_of_abelian_varieties_over_a_field}{isogeny}.
\end{definition}

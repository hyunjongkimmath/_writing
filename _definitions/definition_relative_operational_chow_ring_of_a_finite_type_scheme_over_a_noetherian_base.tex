\begin{definition} \label{definition:relative_operational_chow_ring_of_a_finite_type_scheme_over_a_noetherian_base}
Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_type_morphism_of_schemes}{finite type} over a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{Noetherian base} $S$.
The \hldef{relative operational Chow group} $A^p(X/S)$ is the set of bivariant operations $c$ such that for every morphism $f: Y \to X$ of schemes over $S$, $c$ assigns a homomorphism
\[ c_f : A_k(Y/S) \to A_{k-p}(Y/S) \]

compatible with relative proper pushforward, flat pullback, and refined intersections. The \hldef{relative operational Chow ring} is the graded ring \hl{$A^*(X/S) = \bigoplus_p A^p(X/S)$} with multiplication given by composition.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:standard_action_of_GL_n_R_on_R_n}
    Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring}. 
    The \hldef{standard action of $\GL_n(R)$ on the free module $R^{\oplus n}$} refers to the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:left_and_right_group_actions_on_a_set}{action} given as follows --- for $g \in \GL_n(R)$ and $v \in R^{\oplus n}$, we regard $v$ as a column vector and $g \cdot v$ is simply $g$ as an $n \times n$ matrix multiplied on the left of $v$. 
\end{definition}

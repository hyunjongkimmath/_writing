\begin{definition}[Upper half-plane] \label{definition:upper_half_plane_in_the_complex_plane}
    Let $\bbC$ denote the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_complex_numbers}{field of complex numbers}. The \hldef{upper half-plane in the complex plane} is the subset
    $$\hlin{\mathbb{H} = \{z \in \bbC : \operatorname{Im}(z) > 0\}}$$
    where $\operatorname{Im}(z)$ denotes the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:real_and_imaginary_parts_of_a_complex_number}{imaginary part of $z$}.

    The upper half-plane is an open subset of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_space}{topological space} $\bbC$, a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:convex_set_on_a_real_vector_space_and_convex_function_on_a_convex_set}{convex set}, and a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:simply_connected_space}{simply connected} subset of $\bbC$. It carries a natural structure of a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complex_analytic_manifold}{complex analytic manifold} of dimension $1$ over $\bbC$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Singular cohomology with coefficients] \label{definition:singular_cohomology_and_relative_singular_cohomology_of_topological_space_with_coefficients_in_an_abelian_group}
The \hldef{(absolute) $n$-th singular cohomology group of $X$ with coefficients in $G$} is the quotient group:
$$\hlin{H^n(X; G) = Z^n(X; G) / B^n(X; G)}.$$
Given a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:subspace_of_a_topological_space}{subspace} $A \subseteq X$, the \hldef{$n$-th relative singular cohomology group of $(X, A)$ with coefficients in $G$} is:
$$\hlin{H^n(X, A; G) = Z^n(X, A; G) / B^n(X, A; G)}.$$
If $G = R$ is a ring, $H^n(X, A; R)$ typically carries additional structure as an $R$-algebra via the \TODO{define cup product}.
\end{definition}

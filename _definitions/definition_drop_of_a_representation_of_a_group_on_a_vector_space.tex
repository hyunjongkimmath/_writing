\begin{definition} \label{definition:drop_of_a_representation_of_a_group_on_a_vector_space}

Let $G$ be a group and let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over a field $F$ equipped with a continuous linear action of $G$. Let $V^G \subseteq V$ denote the subspace of $G$-invariant vectors.

The \hldef{drop of the representation $V$}, denoted by
$$\hlin{\operatorname{drop}(V,G)}$$
or simply $\operatorname{drop}(V)$ when $G$ is clear from context, is the codimension of the invariant subspace:
$$\operatorname{drop}(V,G) = \dim_F(V) - \dim_F(V^G).$$
Equivalently, it is the dimension of the coinvariant space $V_G = V / V^G$. The drop vanishes if and only if $G$ acts trivially on $V$.
\end{definition}

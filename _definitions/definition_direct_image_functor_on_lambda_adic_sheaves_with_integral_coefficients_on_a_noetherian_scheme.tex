\begin{definition}[Direct image functor on $\lambda$-adic sheaves with integral coefficients] \label{definition:direct_image_functor_on_lambda_adic_sheaves_with_integral_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}
Let $(R, \lambda)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_ring_with_respect_to_an_ideal}{complete} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{DVR} such that $R/\lambda^n$ is finite for all $n \geq 1$. Let $X$ and $Y$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{noetherian schemes}, and consider sheaves on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{(small) \'etale sites} of $X$ and $Y$. Let $f: X \to Y$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:morphism_of_schemes}{morphism of schemes}.

The \hldef{direct image functor on $\lambda$-adic sheaves with integral coefficients along $f$} is the functor
$$\hlin{f_* : \Sh(X, R) \to \Sh(Y, R)}$$
defined as follows. For any \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:lambda_adic_sheaf_on_the_small_etale_site_of_a_scheme_for_an_ideal_of_a_commutative_ring}{$\lambda$-adic sheaf} $\calF = (\calF_n, u_n)_{n \geq 1}$ on $X$, set
$$f_* \calF := (f_* \calF_n, f_*(u_n))_{n \geq 1},$$
where each $f_* \calF_n$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:inverse_image_and_direct_image_of_sheaves_on_schemes_for_various_topologies}{direct image} of the sheaf $\calF_n$ on $X_{\text{\'et}}$ along $f$, taken on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{(small) \'etale site} of $Y$. The transition maps $f_*(u_n): f_* \calF_n \to f_* \calF_{n-1}$ are the images of the original transition maps $u_n$ under the functor $f_*$.

The system $(f_* \calF_n, f_*(u_n))_{n \geq 1}$ satisfies the conditions of a $\lambda$-adic sheaf because $f_*$ is left exact and preserves kernels, hence commutes with the quotient isomorphisms $\calF_n \cong \calF_{n+1}/\lambda^n \calF_{n+1}$. Constructibility of $f_* \calF_n$ for all $n$ requires additional hypotheses on $f$ (e.g., $f$ of finite type).
\end{definition}

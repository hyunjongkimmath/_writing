\begin{definition} \label{definition:power_of_a_monoid_element_by_a_nonnegative_integer}
Let $(M,\cdot)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:monoid}{monoid} with identity element $e$, and let $a \in M$. For each $n \in \bbZ_{\geq 0}$, the \hldef{$n$th power of $a$}, denoted by \hl{$a^n$}, is the element of $M$ \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:recursive_function_on_naturals}{defined recursively} by
\begin{align*}
    a^0 &:= e, \\
    a^{n+1} &:= a \cdot a^n.
\end{align*}

In the case that the monoid operation is written additively, the $n$th power of $a$ is more usually written \hl{$na$} of \hl{$n \cdot a$}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Tamely ramified sheaf of sets on the complement of a scheme over a scheme along a relative normal crossings divisor] \label{definition:tamely_ramified_sheaf_of_sets_on_the_complement_of_a_scheme_over_a_scheme_along_a_relative_normal_crossings_divisor}
Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{base scheme}, let $X$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme_over_a_scheme}{$S$-scheme}, and let $D$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:divisor_with_normal_crossings_relative_to_base_scheme_for_a_scheme_over_a_scheme}{divisor with normal crossings relatively to $S$}. Set $Y = \Supp(D)$ and $U = X - Y$, and let $i : U \hookrightarrow X$ be the canonical \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:open_and_closed_immersions_of_schemes}{open immersion}.

Let $F$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_on_a_topological_space_valued_in_a_category_with_a_terminal_object}{sheaf of sets} on $U$. For any maximal point $y$ of the fiber $Y_{\bar{s}}$, let $R = \mathcal{O}_{X_{\bar{s}}, y}$ be the local ring at $y$ on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:geometric_fiber_of_a_morphism_of_schemes_at_a_geometric_point}{geometric fiber}, and let $K$ be its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_fractions_of_an_integral_domain}{field of fractions}. The restriction of $F$ to $\Spec(K)$ is representable by the spectrum of an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:etale_algebra_over_a_commutative_ring}{étale $K$-algebra} $L$, and $L$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:tamely_ramified_etale_algebra_over_a_dvr}{tamely ramified over $R$}.

More often than not, when confusion cannot result, we will omit the mention of $D$ in the terminology.

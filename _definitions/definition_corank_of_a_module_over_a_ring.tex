Let $R$ be a ring and let $M$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:module_of_a_ring}{$R$-module}. Let $N$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:submodule_of_a_module_over_a_ring}{submodule of $M$}.

If the quotient module $M/N$ has a well-defined \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:rank_of_a_module_over_a_commutative_ring}{rank} (for instance, when $R$ is commutative and $M/N$ is torsion-free), then the \hldef{corank of $N$ in $M$}, denoted by
$$\hlin{\operatorname{corank}_M(N)},$$
is defined to be the rank of $M/N$.

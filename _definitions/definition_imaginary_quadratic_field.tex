\begin{definition}[Imaginary Quadratic Field] \label{definition:imaginary_quadratic_field}
An \hldef{imaginary quadratic field} is a number field (\CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:number_field}{number field}) $K$ such that $[K : \mathbb{Q}] = 2$ and $K$ is totally imaginary (\CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:totally_real_and_totally_imaginary_number_fields}{totally imaginary}). Equivalently, $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{d})$ for some squarefree integer $d < 0$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:symmetric_closure_of_a_binary_relation_on_a_set}
Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:n_ary_relation_on_sets}{binary relation} on a set $X$. The \hldef{symmetric closure of $R$} is the binary relation $R^{\mathrm{sym}}$ on $X$ defined by
$$
R^{\mathrm{sym}} = R \cup R^{-1},
$$
where $R^{-1} = \{ (y, x) \mid (x, y) \in R \}$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:inverse_of_a_binary_relation}{inverse relation} of $R$. Equivalently, $R^{\mathrm{sym}}$ is the smallest symmetric binary relation on $X$ containing $R$.

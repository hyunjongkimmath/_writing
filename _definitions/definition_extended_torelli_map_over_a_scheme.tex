\begin{definition}[Extended Torelli map] \label{definition:extended_torelli_map_over_a_scheme}
    Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme}. The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:torelli_morphism_over_a_scheme}{Torelli morphism} $\tau_S\colon \mathcal{M}_{g, S} \to \mathcal{A}_{g, S}$ extends to a rational map (or morphism on the Deligne-Mumford compactification)
    $$\bar{\tau}_S\colon \overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g, S} \dashrightarrow \tilde{\mathcal{A}}_{g, S},$$
    where $\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g, S}$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:deligne_mumford_compactification_of_moduli_space_of_curves}{Deligne-Mumford compactification} of $\mathcal{M}_{g, S}$ and $\tilde{\mathcal{A}}_{g, S}$ is a toroidal compactification of $\mathcal{A}_{g, S}$.

    For a stable curve $X/S$ of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:stable_curve_of_compact_type_over_a_scheme}{compact type}, the map $\bar{\tau}_S$ sends $X$ to its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:jacobian_of_a_curve_over_a_field}{Jacobian} $\mathrm{Jac}(X)$, which is an abelian scheme over $S$. For a stable curve $X/S$ of non-compact type, the generalized Jacobian $\mathrm{Pic}^0_{X/S}$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:semi_abelian_group_scheme_over_a_scheme}{semi-abelian group scheme} (an extension of an abelian scheme by an algebraic torus), and $\bar{\tau}_S(X)$ lies in the boundary of $\tilde{\mathcal{A}}_{g, S}$, which consists of points representing semi-abelic varieties with increasing torus rank.

    The extended Torelli map $\bar{\tau}_S$ is no longer an embedding; in fact, some of its fibers have positive dimension.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Potential good and semi-abelian reduction for an abelian variety over the function field of a Dedekind scheme] \label{definition:potential_good_and_semi_abelian_reduction_for_an_abelian_variety_over_the_function_field_of_a_dedekind_scheme}
    Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dedekind_scheme}{Dedekind scheme}, let $\eta$ be its generic point, and let $K = \mathcal{O}_{S, \eta}$ be the function field of $S$. Let $A_K$ be an \CrefAndHyperref{definition:abelian_variety_over_a_field}{abelian variety over $K$}, and let $s \in S$ be a closed point.

    We say that $A_K$ has \hldef{potential good reduction at $s$} (resp. \hldef{potential abelian reduction at $s$}, resp. \hldef{potential semi-abelian reduction at $s$}) if there exists a finite \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:galois_extension_of_fields}{Galois extension} $L$ of $K$ such that $A_L = A_K \times_K L$ has abelian (resp. semi-abelian) reduction at all closed points $s' \in S'$ lying over $s$, where $S'$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:normalization_of_a_reduced_scheme}{normalization} of $S$ in $L$. Instead of potential abelian reduction, we will also talk about \hldef{potential good reduction}.
\end{definition}

Let $G$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:simple_graph_directed_graph_multigraph_weighted_graph}{simple graph}.

A graph $H$ is called a \hldef{subgraph of $G$}, denoted by \hl{$H \subseteq G$}, if $V(H) \subseteq V(G)$ and $E(H) \subseteq E(G)$, with the incidence in $H$ inherited from $G$. A subgraph $H$ is called a \hldef{spanning subgraph of $G$} if $V(H) = V(G)$.

Let $S \subseteq V(G)$. The \hldef{induced subgraph of $G$ on $S$}, denoted by \hl{$G[S]$}, is the subgraph with vertex set $S$ and edge set
$$E(G[S]) = \{\{u,v\} \in E(G) : u, v \in S\}.$$
That is, $G[S]$ contains exactly those edges of $G$ whose endpoints both lie in $S$.
\end{definition}

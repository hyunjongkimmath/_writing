Let $F$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}, let $V$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:vector_space_over_a_field}{$F$-vector space}, and let $W$ be a subspace of $V$.

The \hldef{codimension of $W$ in $V$} (also called the \hldef{corank of $W$ in $V$}), denoted by
$$\hlin{\operatorname{codim}_V(W)}$$
or $\operatorname{corank}_V(W)$, is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:basis_and_dimension_of_a_vector_space_over_a_field}{dimension} of the quotient space $V/W$ regarded as an $F$-vector space.

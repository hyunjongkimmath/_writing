\begin{definition}[Separating transcendence basis] \label{definition:separating_transcendence_basis_of_a_field_extension}
Let $E/F$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:extension_of_a_field}{field extension}, and let $T \subseteq E$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:transcendence_basis_of_a_field_extension}{transcendence basis} of $E$ over $F$.
We say that $T$ is a \hldef{separating transcendence basis} if the algebraic extension $E/F(T)$\CrefIfExists{definition:field_generated_by_elements} is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:separable_field_extension_general}{separable}, i.e., every element of $E$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:separable_algebraic_field_extension}{separable} over $F(T)$.
\end{definition}

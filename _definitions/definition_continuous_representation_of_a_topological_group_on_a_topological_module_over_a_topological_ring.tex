\begin{definition} \label{definition:continuous_representation_of_a_topological_group_on_a_topological_module_over_a_topological_ring}
    Let $(G,\cdot,\mathcal{T})$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_group}{topological group} and let $V$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_module_over_topological_rings}{topological module} over a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_ring}{topological ring} $k$. Let $G$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_group}{topological group}. \TODO{discuss the topological structure on $\mathrm{GL}(V)$}

    % A \hldef{representation of $G$ on $V$} is a continuous group homomorphism
    % $$ \rho : G \to \mathrm{GL}(V)$$

    A \hldef{(continuous) representation of $G$ on $V$} is a group homomorphism
    $$ \rho : G \to \mathrm{GL}(V)$$
    such that the action map 
    $$G \times V \to V, \quad (g,v) \mapsto \rho(g) v$$ 
    is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:continuous_map_between_open_subsets_of_euclidean_spaces}{continuous}.

    Under some conditions, but not always, the above definition is equivalent to the continuity of $\rho$.

    % A \hldef{representation of $G$ on $V$} is a continuous group homomorphism
    % $$ \rho : G \to \mathrm{GL}(V)$$

    In particular, the above notions are applicable when $G$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:profinite_group}{profinite group}.
\end{definition}

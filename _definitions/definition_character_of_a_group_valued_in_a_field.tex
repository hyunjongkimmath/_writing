\begin{definition} \label{definition:character_of_a_group_valued_in_a_field}

    Let $k$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}, and $G$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group}.

    A \hldef{character of $G$ valued in $k$} is a group homomorphism
    $$\hlin{\chi : G \to k^\times,}$$
    where $k^\times = k \setminus \{0\}$ denotes the multiplicative group of $k$.

    Equivalently, a character is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:representation_of_a_group_on_a_vector_space_over_a_field}{representation of $G$ on a vector space over $k$} of dimension 1. The equivalence sends $\chi$ to the action $g \cdot v = \chi(g)v$ on $k$, and conversely sends a 1-dimensional representation $(\rho, k)$ to $g \mapsto \rho(g)$.

    The set of characters $\mathrm{X}^*_k(G) := \Hom_{\mathrm{gr.}}(G, k^\times)$ forms an abelian group under pointwise multiplication:
    $$ (\chi_1 \chi_2)(g) = \chi_1(g)\chi_2(g), \quad \chi^{-1}(g) = \chi(g)^{-1}, \quad \mathbf{1}(g) = 1. $$
    This group is called the \hldef{character group of $G$ valued in $k$}.

    Since $k^\times$ is abelian, every character factors uniquely through the abelianization \hl{$G^{\mathrm{ab}} := G/[G,G]$}. Thus $\mathrm{X}^*_k(G) \cong \Hom_{\mathrm{gr.}}(G^{\mathrm{ab}}, k^\times)$.
\end{definition}

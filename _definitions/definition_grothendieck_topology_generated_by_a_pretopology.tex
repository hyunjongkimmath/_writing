\begin{definition} \label{definition:grothendieck_topology_generated_by_a_pretopology}
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category equipped with a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:basis_and_grothendieck_pretopology_for_a_grothendieck_topology_on_a_category}{Grothendieck pretopology} $K$. The \hldef{Grothendieck topology generated by $K$}, denoted \hl{$J_K$}, is the Grothendieck topology constructed as follows: a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sieve_on_an_object_in_a_category}{sieve} $S$ on an object $U$ belongs to $J_K(U)$ if and only if there exists a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:basis_and_grothendieck_pretopology_for_a_grothendieck_topology_on_a_category}{covering family} $\{U_i \to U\}_{i \in I} \in K(U)$ such that for every $i \in I$, the morphism $U_i \to U$ belongs to $S$, i.e. the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sieve_on_an_object_of_a_category_generated_by_a_family_of_morphisms}{sieve generated by} $\{U_i \to U\}_{i \in I}$ is a sub-sieve of $S$.

It is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:coarser_and_finer_grothendieck_topologies}{coarsest} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{Grothendieck topology} on $\mathcal{C}$ such that every member in $K(U)$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{covering family} for $J_K$, i.e. \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sieve_on_an_object_of_a_category_generated_by_a_family_of_morphisms}{generates} a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{covering sieve}. \TextIfExists{theorem:grothendieck_topology_on_a_cateogry_generated_by_a_pretopology_is_indeed_a_grothendieck_topology_and_is_the_coarsest_one_containing_all_the_covering_sieves_generated_by_the_pretopology_families}{See \Cref{theorem:grothendieck_topology_on_a_cateogry_generated_by_a_pretopology_is_indeed_a_grothendieck_topology_and_is_the_coarsest_one_containing_all_the_covering_sieves_generated_by_the_pretopology_families}.}


\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Abelian variety with quaternionic multiplication over a field] \label{definition:abelian_variety_with_quaternionic_multiplication_over_a_field}
    Let $k$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field} and let $A$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_variety_over_a_field}{abelian variety over $k$} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dimension_of_a_scheme}{dimension} $g$. We say that $A$ has \hldef{quaternionic multiplication (QM)} if there exists a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:quaternion_algebra_over_a_field}{quaternion algebra} $D$ over $\Q$ with an embedding of $\Q$-algebras $D \hookrightarrow \End^0(A)$, where $\End^0(A) = \End(A) \otimes_{\Z} \Q$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:endomorphism_algebra_up_to_isogeny_of_an_abelian_variety_over_a_field}{endomorphism algebra up to isogeny}. In this case, $g$ must be even.

    If $A$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:simple_abelian_variety_over_a_field}{simple}, then $D$ is a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:totally_definite_quaternion_algebra_over_a_number_field}{totally definite quaternion algebra} over $\Q$, i.e. $D$ is a division algebra, and it is the full endomorphism algebra, i.e. $\End^0(A) = D$. In particular, $[D:\Q] = 4$ and $g = 2$.
\end{definition}

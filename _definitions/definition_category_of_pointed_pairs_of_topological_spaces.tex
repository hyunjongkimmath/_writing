\begin{definition} \label{definition:category_of_pointed_pairs_of_topological_spaces}
The \hldef{category of pairs of topological spaces}, often denoted by notations such as \hl{$\mathbf{Top}_{*, \text{pair}}$}, \hl{$\mathbf{Top}_{\bullet, \text{pair}}$}, \hl{$\mathbf{Top}_{*, \text{rel}}$}, or \hl{$\mathbf{Top}_{\bullet, \text{rel}}$} ($\text{rel}$ for relative), is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category}{category} defined by:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Objects:} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:pointed_pair_of_topological_spaces}{Pointed Pairs} $(X, A, x)$ of topological spaces.
    \item \textbf{Morphisms:} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:morphism_of_pointed_pair_of_topological_spaces}{Maps $f: (X, A, x) \to (Y, B, y)$ of pointed pairs}.
    \item \textbf{Composition:} The composition of morphisms is the usual composition of continuous maps.
    \item \textbf{Identity:} For each object $(X, A, x)$, the identity morphism is the identity map $\operatorname{id}_X: (X, A, x) \to (X, A, x)$.
\end{itemize}
It is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_small_category}{locally small category}.
\end{definition}

    Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring}. For elements $a,b \in R$, the \hldef{commutator of $a$ and $b$} is the element
    $$\hlin{[a,b] := ab - ba} \in R.$$
    The commutator $[a,b]$ measures the failure of $a$ and $b$ to commute: $[a,b] = 0$ if and only if $ab = ba$.

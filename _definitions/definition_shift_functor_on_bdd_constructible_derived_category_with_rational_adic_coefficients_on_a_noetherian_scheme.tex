Let $(R, \lambda)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_ring_with_respect_to_an_ideal}{complete} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{DVR} such that $R/\lambda^n$ is finite for all $n \geq 1$. Let $E$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_fractions_of_an_integral_domain}{fraction field} of $R$. Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{noetherian scheme}, and consider \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_on_a_site}{sheaves} on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{(small) \'etale site on $X$}.

The category \CrefAndHyperref{definition:derived_category_of_cohomologically_bounded_constructible_sheaves_with_rational_adic_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}{$\Dbc(X, E)$} has the same objects as \CrefAndHyperref{definition:derived_category_of_bounded_constructible_complexes_of_adic_sheaves_with_integral_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}{$\Dbc(X, R)$}. The \hldef{shift functor on $\Dbc(X, E)$}, denoted \hl{$[1]: \Dbc(X, E) \to \Dbc(X, E)$}, is defined by the same level-wise construction as the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:shift_functor_on_bdd_constructible_derived_category_with_integral_adic_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}{shift functor on $\Dbc(X, R)$}: for $K = (K_n)_n \in \Ob \Dbc(X, E)$,
$$\hlin{K[1] = (K_n[1]_n)_n}$$
where $[1]_n$ is the shift on $D^-(X, R/\lambda^{n+1})$.

For any integer $k \in \mathbb{Z}$, the notation \hl{$K[k]$} denotes the $k$-fold iterate of this shift.

\begin{definition}[Mod $n$ monodromy representation] \label{definition:mod_n_monodromy_representation_of_a_commutative_group_scheme_over_a_connected_scheme}
Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:disconnected_and_connected_topological_spaces}{connected} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme} with a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:geometric_point_of_a_scheme}{geometric point} $\bar{s}: \operatorname{Spec}(k(\bar{s})) \to S$, and let $\pi_1^{\text{ét}}(S, \bar{s})$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:etale_fundamental_group_of_a_connected_scheme}{étale fundamental group}. Let $f: \mathcal{A} \to S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebraic_group_scheme_over_a_scheme}{commutative group scheme} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_type_morphism_of_schemes}{finite type}, and let $n \geq 1$ be an integer. Let $\mathcal{A}_{\bar{s}} = \mathcal{A} \times_S \operatorname{Spec}(k(\bar{s}))$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:geometric_fiber_of_a_morphism_of_schemes_at_a_geometric_point}{geometric fiber}, and let $\mathcal{A}_{\bar{s}}[n](k(\bar{s}))$ denote the group of 
\CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:rational_point_on_a_scheme_over_a_field}{$k(\bar{s})$-rational points} of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:kernel_of_a_homomorphism_of_group_schemes_over_a_scheme}{kernel} of $[n]: \mathcal{A}_{\bar{s}} \to \mathcal{A}_{\bar{s}}$.

The fundamental group $\pi_1^{\text{ét}}(S, \bar{s})$ \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:continuous_action_on_a_topological_space_by_a_topological_group}{acts continuously} on $\mathcal{A}_{\bar{s}}[n](k(\bar{s}))$ via its action on the geometric fiber. The resulting homomorphism
$$\hlin{\rho_{f, n}: \pi_1^{\text{ét}}(S, \bar{s}) \to \operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal{A}_{\bar{s}}[n](k(\bar{s})))}$$
is called the \hldef{mod $n$ monodromy representation of $\mathcal{A}$ over $S$}. When $f$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_scheme_over_a_scheme}{abelian scheme} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:relative_dimension_for_a_morphism_of_schemes}{relative dimension} $g$ and $n$ is invertible on $S$, one has $\mathcal{A}_{\bar{s}}[n](k(\bar{s})) \cong (\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z})^{2g}$, so $\rho_{f,n}$ factors through $\operatorname{GL}_{2g}(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z})$.
\end{definition}

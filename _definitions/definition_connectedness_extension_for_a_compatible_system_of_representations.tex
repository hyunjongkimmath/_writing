\begin{definition} \label{definition:connectedness_extension_for_a_compatible_system_of_representations}
    Let $K$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:number_field}{number field} and let $\{ \rho_\ell : \Gal(\overline{K}/K) \to \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{Q}_\ell) \}_{\ell \in L}$ be a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:compatible_system_of_representations_on_a_profinite_group_equipped_with_dense_frobenius_elements}{compatible system} of representations indexed by a set of rational primes $L$. For each $\ell$, let $G_\ell$ be the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:algebraic_monodromy_group_at_a_prime_for_a_compatible_system_of_reps_on_a_prof_group_with_dense_frob_elements}{algebraic monodromy group} at $\ell$.

    The \hldef{connectedness extension of $K$ for $\{\rho_\ell\}$}, denoted \hl{$K_{\mathrm{conn}}$}, is the smallest finite extension of $K$ such that for every $\ell \in L$, the algebraic monodromy group $G_\ell|_{\Gal(\overline{K}/K_{\mathrm{conn}})}$ (i.e., the Zariski closure of $\rho_\ell(\Gal(\overline{K}/K_{\mathrm{conn}}))$) is connected.

    By {\cite[Theorem~0.1]{larsen_pink_ccellgr}}, this extension exists and is independent of $\ell$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Filtration on a measurable space] \label{definition:filtration_on_a_measurable_space}
Let $(X, \mathcal{A})$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sigma_algebra_on_a_set_and_measurable_space}{measurable space}. Let $(T, \leq)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:total_order_linear_order_on_a_set}{totally ordered set}. 

A \hldef{filtration on $(X, \mathcal{A})$ indexed by $T$} is a family $\{\mathcal{F}_t\}_{t \in T}$ of sub-$\sigma$-algebras of $\mathcal{A}$ such that $s \leq t$ implies $\mathcal{F}_s \subseteq \mathcal{F}_t$ for all $s, t \in T$.

The pair $(X, \{\mathcal{F}_t\}_{t \in T})$ is called a \hldef{filtered measurable space}.
\end{definition}

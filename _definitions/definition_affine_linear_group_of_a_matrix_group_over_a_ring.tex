\begin{definition} \label{definition:affine_linear_group_of_a_matrix_group_over_a_ring}
    Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring}. 
    Let $G \subseteq \GL_n(R)$ be a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:matrix_group_over_a_ring}{matrix group} over $R$. It \hldef{affine group} is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:semidirect_product_of_groups_external_and_internal}{semidirect product}
    $$R^{\oplus n} \rtimes G$$
    where the action of $G$ on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:free_bimodule_over_rings}{free $R$-module} $R^{\oplus n}$ is the one inherited from the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:standard_action_of_GL_n_R_on_R_n}{standard action} of $\GL_n(R)$ on $R^n$.

    For example, in this way, we define the \hldef{affine general linear group} \hl{$\operatorname{AGL}_n(R)$}, \hldef{affine special linear group} \hl{$\operatorname{ASL}_n(R)$}, etc.
\end{definition}

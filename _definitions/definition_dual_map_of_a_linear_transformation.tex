\begin{definition} \label{definition:dual_map_of_a_linear_transformation}

    Let $F$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}.

    Let $V$ and $W$ be $F$-vector spaces, and let $T: V \to W$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:morphism_of_vector_spaces}{linear map}. The \hldef{dual map of $T$} is the linear map
    $$\hlin{T^*: W^* \to V^*},$$
    defined by $(T^*(\phi))(v) = \phi(T(v))$ for all $\phi \in W^*$ and $v \in V$, where $V^*$ denotes the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dual_of_a_left_right_two_sided_module}{dual space} of $V$.

\TextIfExists{definition:dual_of_a_left_right_two_sided_module}{The dual map $T^*$ is the vector-space special case of the dual homomorphism induced by a \Cref{definition:dual_of_a_left_right_two_sided_module} contravariant functor on modules.}

\begin{definition}[{\cite[Definition 3.4.]{scholze_sff}}] \label{definition:symmetric_monoidal_infinity_category}
A \hldef{symmetric monoidal $\infty$-category} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:commutative_monoid_in_an_infinity_category}{commutative monoid in $\mathbf{Cat}_\infty$}, where $\mathbf{Cat}_\infty$ denotes the $\infty$-category of $\infty$-categories.

Equivalently, a symmetric monoidal $\infty$-category is given by a functor
\[ \mathcal{M} : \mathbf{Fin}_{\mathrm{part}} \to \mathbf{Cat}_\infty \]
such that for every finite set $I$, the map $\mathcal{M}(I) \to \prod_{i \in I} \mathcal{M}(\{i\}) = \mathcal{M}(\ast)^I$ is an equivalence of $\infty$-categories.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:group_cochain_complex_with_coefficients_in_a_group_module_over_a_ring}

Let $G$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group} and $M$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:left_right_module_over_a_group_ring_of_a_group_over_a_ring}{$G$-module} over a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring} $R$. 

The \hldef{$n$-th group cochain group} is
$$\hlin{ C^n(G,M) = \{ f: G^n \to M\} }$$
with pointwise $R$-module structure: 
\begin{align*}
(f_1 + f_2)(g_1,\dots,g_n) &= f_1(g_1,\dots,g_n) + f_2(g_1,\dots,g_n) \quad f_1,f_2 \in C^n(G,M) \\
(rf)(g_1,\dots,g_n) &= r \cdot f(g_1,\dots,g_n) \quad f \in C^n(G,M), r \in R.
\end{align*}

The \hldef{coboundary map} \hl{$\delta^n: C^n(G,M) \to C^{n+1}(G,M)$} is
\begin{align*}
(\delta^n f)(g_1,\dots,g_{n+1}) =& g_1 \cdot f(g_2,\ldots,g_{n+1}) \\
&+ \sum_{i=1}^n (-1)^i f(g_1,\ldots,g_{i-1}, g_i \cdot g_{i+1}, g_{i+2}, \ldots, g_{n+1})  \\
&+ (-1)^{n+1} f(g_1,\ldots,g_n)
\end{align*}
The groups $C^n(G,M)$ together with the maps $\delta^n$ form a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cochain_complex_of_objects_in_an_additive_category}{cochain complex} in the category of $R$-modules.

The \hldef{group of $n$-cocycles} is \hl{$Z^n(G,M) = \ker \delta^n$} and the \hldef{group of $n$-coboundaries} is \hl{$B^n(G,M) = \operatorname{im} \delta^{n-1}$}. Both are $R$-submodules of $C^n(G,M)$.

The cohomology groups $H^n(G,M) = Z^n(G,M) / B^n(G,M)$ for this cochain complex are naturally isomorphic to the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group_cohomology_homology_of_a_module_over_a_group_ring_over_a_ring}{group cohomology groups} $H^n(G,M)$\CrefIfExists{theorem:group_cohomology_of_a_group_module_over_a_ring_is_naturally_isomorphic_to_cohomology_of_group_cochain_complex}. As such, we do not distinguish notation between these.

We note that $C^n(G,M)$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:natural_transformation_between_functors_between_categories}{naturally isomorphic} to $\Hom_{R[G]}(B_n(R[G]), M)$ where $B_\bullet(R[G])$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:unnormalized_bar_resolution_of_a_ring_over_a_group_algebra}{unnormalized bar resolution} of $R$ over $R[G]$. It is also worth mentioning that the cochain complex $\Hom_{R[G]}(\overline{B}_\bullet(R[G]), M)$, where $\overline{B}_\bullet(R[G])$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:normalized_bar_resolution_of_a_ring_over_a_group_algebra}{normalized bar resolution} of $R$ over $R[G]$, is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:chain_homotopy_between_chain_maps_between_complexes}{chain homotopy equivalent} to the cochain complex $C^\bullet(G,M)$
\end{definition}

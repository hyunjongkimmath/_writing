\begin{definition} \label{definition:defined_over_a_field_for_a_scheme}
    Let $K/k$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:extension_of_a_field}{field extension}. Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme_over_a_scheme}{scheme over} $K$. 
    We say that $X$ is \hldef{defined over $k$} if there exists a scheme $X_0$ over $k$ such that $X$ is isomorphic to $X_0 \times_{\Spec k} \Spec K$\CrefIfExists{definition:cartesian_product_of_two_objects_in_a_category_over_an_object} as \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme_over_a_scheme}{schemes over} $K$. One may refer to such a choice of $X_0$ as a \hldef{$k$-model of $X$}. It should be noted that, in general, there may be many $k$-models of $X$ that are non-isomorphic to one another \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme_over_a_scheme}{over} $k$.

    In particular, to say that $X$ is defined over $k$ is not the same notion as to say that $X$ is a scheme over $k$.

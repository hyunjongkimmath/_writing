\begin{definition}[Torsion Ring] \label{definition:torsion_ring}

A commutative ring $R$ is called a \hldef{torsion ring} if every element $r \in R$ is annihilated by some positive integer, i.e., for each $r \in R$ there exists $n \in \bbZ_{>0}$ such that $n \cdot r = 0$.

Equivalently, $R$ is a torsion ring if its additive group $(R, +)$ is a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:torsion_group}{torsion group}.
\end{definition}

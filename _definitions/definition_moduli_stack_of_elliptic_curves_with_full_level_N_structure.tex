\begin{definition}[Moduli stack $\mathcal{M}(N)$] \label{definition:moduli_stack_of_elliptic_curves_with_full_level_N_structure}
Let $N \geq 1$ be an integer and let $S$ be a scheme over $\mathbb{Z}[1/N]$. The \hldef{moduli stack $\mathcal{M}(N)$} is the stack over $\mathrm{Sch}/\mathbb{Z}[1/N]$ that assigns to each such scheme $S$ the groupoid of pairs $(E, \alpha)$ where
\begin{itemize}
    \item $E$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:elliptic_curve_over_a_scheme}{elliptic curve} over $S$, and
    \item $\alpha: (\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})_S^{\oplus 2} \xrightarrow{\sim} E[N]$ is an isomorphism of group schemes over $S$ from the constant group scheme $(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})_S^{\oplus 2}$ to the $N$-torsion \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:torsion_subgroup_scheme_of_a_group_scheme}{subgroup scheme $E[N]$}, called a \hldef{full level $N$ structure} (or symplectic basis).
\end{itemize}
Morphisms in this groupoid are isomorphisms of elliptic curves compatible with the full level structures.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Fourier expansion of a periodic holomorphic function on the upper half-plane]
\label{definition:fourier_expansion_of_a_periodic_holomorphic_function_on_the_upper_half_plane}

Let $h \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$, let $\mathbb{H}$ denote the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:upper_half_plane_in_the_complex_plane}{upper half-plane}, and let $f : \mathbb{H} \to \mathbb{C}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:holomorphic_function_from_a_subset_of_Cn_to_Cm}{holomorphic function} satisfying
$$f(z + h) = f(z) \quad \text{for all } z \in \mathbb{H}.$$

For each integer $n \in \mathbb{Z}$, the \hldef{$n$-th Fourier coefficient of $f$} is the complex number
$$\hlin{a_n = \frac{1}{h} \int_{z_0}^{z_0 + h} f(z) \, e^{-2\pi i n z / h} \, dz,}$$
\CrefIfExists{definition:contour_integral_of_a_complex_valued_function_along_a_piecewise_continuously_differentiable_path}
where the integral is taken along any \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:piecewise_continuously_differentiable_path_from_a_closed_real_interval_to_the_complex_plane}{piecewise continuously differentiable path} in $\mathbb{H}$ from $z_0$ to $z_0 + h$ for some $z_0 \in \mathbb{H}$ (the value is independent of the choice of path and of $z_0$ by the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{theorem:cauchy_integral_theorem_stating_that_the_contour_integral_of_a_holomorphic_function_along_any_closed_piecewise_continuously_differentiable_path_in_a_simply_connected_domain_vanishes}{Cauchy integral theorem}).

The \hldef{Fourier expansion (or Fourier series) of $f$} is the formal series
$$\hlin{f(z) = \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} a_n \, e^{2\pi i n z / h},}$$
where $a_n$ are the Fourier coefficients of $f$.

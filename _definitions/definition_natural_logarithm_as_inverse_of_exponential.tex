\begin{definition} \label{definition:natural_logarithm_as_inverse_of_exponential}
The \hldef{natural logarithm}, denoted by \hl{$\ln : (0, \infty) \to \mathbb{R}$}, is the inverse of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:real_exponential_function}{exponential function} on $\exp: \mathbb{R} \to (0,\infty)$. That is, for $y > 0$,
$$\ln(y) = x \quad \iff \quad y = \exp(x)$$

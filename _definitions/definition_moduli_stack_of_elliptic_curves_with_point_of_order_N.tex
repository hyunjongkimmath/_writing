\begin{definition}[Moduli stack $\mathcal{M}_1(N)$] \label{definition:moduli_stack_of_elliptic_curves_with_point_of_order_N}
Let $N \geq 1$ be an integer and let $S$ be a scheme over $\mathbb{Z}[1/N]$. The \hldef{moduli stack $\mathcal{M}_1(N)$} is the stack over $\mathrm{Sch}/\mathbb{Z}[1/N]$ that assigns to each such scheme $S$ the groupoid of pairs $(E, P)$ where
\begin{itemize}
    \item $E$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:elliptic_curve_over_a_scheme}{elliptic curve} over $S$, and
    \item $P \in E(S)$ is a section of $\pi: E \to S$ of exact order $N$, i.e. $P$ generates a subgroup scheme of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:torsion_subgroup_scheme_of_a_group_scheme}{$E[N]$} isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$ over $S$.
\end{itemize}
The point $P$ is called a \hldef{point of order $N$}. Morphisms in this groupoid are isomorphisms of elliptic curves preserving the marked points.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Colimits in Sheaf Categories] \label{definition:colimit_in_a_sheaf_category}

    Let $(\calC, J)$ be a site and $\calA$ a category admitting colimits of shape $I$. Suppose there exists a \textbf{sheafification functor} (a reflector) $a: \PreShv(\calC, \calA) \to \Shv(\calC, J, \calA)$ left adjoint to the inclusion $i$. For any diagram $F: I \to \Shv(\calC, J, \calA)$, the colimit in the category of sheaves is constructed by:
    \[ \varinjlim_{\Shv} F \coloneqq a \left( \varinjlim_{\PreShv} i \circ F \right) \]
\end{definition}

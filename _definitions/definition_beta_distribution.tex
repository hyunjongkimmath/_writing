\begin{definition}[Beta distribution] \label{definition:beta_distribution}

Let $\alpha > 0$ and $\beta > 0$. A real-valued random variable $X$ on a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:probability_measure_space_for_a_measure_on_a_measurable_space}{probability measure space} has the \hldef{beta distribution with parameters $\alpha$ and $\beta$} (denoted \hl{$\operatorname{Beta}(\alpha, \beta)$}) if its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:distribution_of_a_random_variable_on_a_probability_measure_space}{distribution} is the measure on $\mathbb{R}$ given by
$$\hlin{\mu_X(A) = \int_A \frac{1}{B(\alpha, \beta)} x^{\alpha - 1} (1 - x)^{\beta - 1} \mathbf{1}_{(0, 1)}(x) \, dx}$$
for every Borel set $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}$, where $B(\alpha, \beta) = \int_0^1 t^{\alpha - 1} (1 - t)^{\beta - 1} \, dt = \tfrac{\Gamma(\alpha)\Gamma(\beta)}{\Gamma(\alpha + \beta)}$.

The probability density function is $$f(x) = \frac{1}{B(\alpha, \beta)} x^{\alpha - 1} (1 - x)^{\beta - 1} \mathbf{1}_{(0, 1)}(x).$$ The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:expectation_of_a_random_variable_on_a_probability_measure_space}{expectation} is $\mathbb{E}[X] = \tfrac{\alpha}{\alpha + \beta}$, and the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:variance_and_covariance}{variance} is $\operatorname{Var}(X) = \tfrac{\alpha\beta}{(\alpha + \beta)^2(\alpha + \beta + 1)}$.
\end{definition}

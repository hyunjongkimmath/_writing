\begin{definition} \label{definition:stone_space_of_a_boolean_algebra}
Let $B$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:boolean_algebra}{Boolean algebra}. The \hldef{Stone space of $B$}, denoted by \hl{$Spec(B)$}, is the set of all \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:filter_and_ultrafilter_on_a_boolean_algebra}{ultrafilters} on $B$ equipped with the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:basis_for_a_topology}{topology generated by} the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:basis_for_a_topology}{basis} of sets 
$$U_a = \{ p \in Spec(B) : a \in p \}$$
for all $a \in B$.
\end{definition}

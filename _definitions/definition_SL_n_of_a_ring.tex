\begin{definition} \label{definition:SL_n_of_a_ring}
Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:commutative_ring}{commutative ring} and $n \geq 1$ an integer. The \hldef{special linear group} \hl{$\operatorname{SL}_n(R)$} is the group of $n \times n$ matrices with entries in $R$ and determinant equal to $1$:
$\operatorname{SL}_n(R) := \left\{ (a_{ij})_{1 \leq i,j \leq n} \in M_n(R) : \det(a_{ij}) = 1 \right\}$.

\begin{definition} \label{definition:restriction_of_a_presheaf_sheaf_on_a_topological_space}
    Let $i: X \hookrightarrow Y$ be the embedding of a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:subspace_of_a_topological_space}{subspace} $X$ into a topological space $Y$. Let $\calD$ be a category with a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:initial_final_zero_objects_of_a_category}{terminal object}.

    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Let $\calG$ be a presheaf on $Y$ valued in $\calD$. The \hldef{restriction of $\calG$ to $X$} is defined as the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:inverse_image_of_a_sheaf_on_a_topological_space}{inverse image presheaf} $i^{-1} \calG$, assuming it exists. It is often denoted by \hl{$\calG|_X$}. 

        \item Let $\calG$ be a sheaf on $Y$ valued in $\calD$. The \hldef{restriction of $\calG$ to $X$} is defined as the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:inverse_image_of_a_sheaf_on_a_topological_space}{inverse image sheaf} $i^{*} \calG$, assuming it exists. It is often denoted by \hl{$\calG|_X$}. 
    \end{enumerate}

    Note, in particular, that if $i: X \hookrightarrow Y$ is the embedding of an open subspace, then $\calG|_X$ is the functor $\mathbf{Open}(X)^{\op} \to \calD$ given by $U \mapsto \calG(i(U))$. 

    If $\calD$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category_of_sets}{category of sets}, of groups, of abelian groups, of modules over rings, for example, then the relevant \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:limit_and_colimit_of_a_diagram_in_a_category}{colimits} needed to define the restriction presheaves and the sheafifcation needed to define the restriction sheaves exist.


\end{definition}

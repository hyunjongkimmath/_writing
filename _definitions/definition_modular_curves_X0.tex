\begin{definition} \label{definition:modular_curves_X0}
Let $N$ be a positive integer. The \hldef{classical modular curve} \hl{$X_0(N)$} is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:compact_topological_space}{compact} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:riemann_surface}{Riemann surface} obtained as the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:quotient_of_a_topological_space_under_group_action}{quotient}
$X_0(N) := \mathbb{H} \cuppoint \Gamma_0(N)\backslash\mathbb{H}$
where $\mathbb{H} = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : \operatorname{Im}(z) > 0\}$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:upper_half_plane_in_the_complex_plane}{upper half-plane} and $\Gamma_0(N) \subset \operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ (\Cref{definition:gamma_0_N_subgroup_of_SL_2_Z}) is the congruence subgroup.

The curve $X_0(N)$ classifies complex elliptic curves equipped with a cyclic subgroup of order $N$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:base_preserving_natural_transformation_between_morphisms_of_fibered_categories}
Let $p_{\mathcal{F}}: \mathcal{F} \to \mathcal{C}$ and $p_{\mathcal{G}}: \mathcal{G} \to \mathcal{C}$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:fibered_category_over_a_category}{fibered categories} over a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category}{category} $\calC$, and let $g, g': \mathcal{F} \to \mathcal{G}$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:morphism_of_fibered_categories_over_a_category}{morphisms of fibered categories}.
A \hldef{base preserving natural transformation} $\alpha: g \to g'$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:natural_transformation_between_functors_between_categories}{natural transformation} such that for every object $u \in \text{ob}(\mathcal{F})$, the morphism $\alpha_u: g(u) \to g'(u)$ in $\mathcal{G}$ satisfies $p_{\mathcal{G}}(\alpha_u) = \text{id}_{p_{\mathcal{F}}(u)}$.
\end{definition}

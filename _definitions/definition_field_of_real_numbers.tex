\begin{definition}[Field of real numbers] \label{definition:field_of_real_numbers}
	A \hldef{field of real numbers} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ordered_field_complete_ordered_field}{complete ordered field} characterized up to unique isomorphism of ordered fields; as such we may refer to such a field as \emph{the} field of real numbers. We denote such a field by \hl{$\mathbb{R}$}.

	One construction of $\bbR$ is as the set of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cauchy_sequence_in_rational_numbers}{Cauchy sequences in $\bbQ$} modulo the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:null_sequence_in_rational_numbers}{null sequences}\CrefIfExists{definition:real_numbers_as_cauchy_completion_of_rational_numbers}, equipped with the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:standard_order_on_real_numbers}{standard order}\CrefIfExists{proposition:real_numbers_form_ordered_field} and the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:standard_absolute_value_on_real_numbers}{standard absolute value}. \TextIfExists{definition:real_numbers_as_cauchy_completion_of_rational_numbers}{see \Cref{definition:real_numbers_as_cauchy_completion_of_rational_numbers} for the construction.}

	$\bbR$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:absolute_value_on_a_ring_valued_in_an_ordered_semiring}{absolute valued field} under its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:standard_absolute_value_on_real_numbers}{standard absolute value} $|x|_\bbR$. This absolute value induces a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:extended_metric_on_a_set_valued_in_an_ordered_commutative_monoid_with_adjoined_infinity}{metric space} structure on $\bbR$\CrefIfExists{definition:metric_induced_by_an_absolute_value_on_a_commutative_ring}, which in turn induces\CrefIfExists{definition:topology_induced_by_an_extended_metric_valued_in_an_ordered_commutative_monoid_with_adjoined_infinity_and_open_ball_for_an_extended_metric} a topological space structure on $\bbR$, called its \hldef{Euclidean/standard topology}.
	\TextIfExists{definition:topological_field}{
	    Under this topology, $\bbR$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_field}{topological field}.
	}

	The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_real_numbers}{field of real numbers} has the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:least_upper_bound_greatest_lower_bound_property_of_ordered_sets}{least upper bound and greatest lower bound properties}\CrefIfExists{proposition:real_numbers_have_least_upper_bound_property}, i.e.\ every nonempty subset of $\bbR$ that is bounded above and below has a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:supremum_infimum_of_a_subset_of_an_ordered_set}{supremum} and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:supremum_infimum_of_a_subset_of_an_ordered_set}{infimum} respectively.
\end{definition}

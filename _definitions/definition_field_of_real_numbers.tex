\begin{definition}[Field of real numbers] \label{definition:field_of_real_numbers}
A \hldef{field of real numbers} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ordered_field_complete_ordered_field}{complete ordered field} characterized up to unique isomorphism of ordered fields; as such we may refer to such a field as \emph{the} field of real numbers. We denote such a field by \hl{$\mathbb{R}$}.

One usually considers $\mathbb{R}$ equipped with its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:euclidean_n_space}{Euclidean/standard topology}.
\TextIfExists{definition:topological_field}{
    Under this topology, $\bbR$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_field}{topological field}.
}

The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_real_numbers}{field of real numbers} has the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:least_upper_bound_greatest_lower_bound_property_of_ordered_sets}{least upper bound and greatest lower bound properties}, i.e. every nonempty \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:bounded_double_complex_of_objects_in_an_additive_category}{bounded above} and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:bounded_complexes_on_an_additive_category_and_homologically_bounded_objects_on_an_abelian_category}{bounded below} subsets of $\bbR$ has a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:supremum_infimum_of_a_subset_of_an_ordered_set}{supremum} and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:supremum_infimum_of_a_subset_of_an_ordered_set}{infimum} respectively.
\end{definition}

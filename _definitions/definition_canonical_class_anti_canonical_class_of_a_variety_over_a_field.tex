\begin{definition} \label{definition:canonical_class_anti_canonical_class_of_a_variety_over_a_field}
Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:variety_over_a_field}{variety} over a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}. 
The \hldef{canonical class} \hl{$K_X$} is the divisor class associated to the sheaf of terminal $n$-forms $\omega_X = \bigwedge^n \Omega_{X/k}^1$\CrefIfExists{definition:sheaf_of_kahler_differentials_of_a_morphism_of_schemes}, where $n = \dim X$. The \hldef{anticanonical class} is defined as $-K_X$.
\end{definition}

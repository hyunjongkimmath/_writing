\begin{definition} \label{definition:X_H_intermediate_modular_curve_parameterizing_elliptic_curves_with_H_structure}
Let $N$ be a positive integer and let $H \subset \operatorname{GL}_2(\widehat{\mathbb{Z}})$ be an open subgroup satisfying $\Gamma(N) \subseteq H$, where $\widehat{\mathbb{Z}} = \prod_{\ell} \mathbb{Z}_\ell$ is the profinite completion of $\mathbb{Z}$ and $\Gamma(N)$ is the principal congruence subgroup of level $N$ viewed as a subgroup of $\operatorname{GL}_2(\widehat{\mathbb{Z}})$ via the diagonal embedding.
The \hldef{intermediate modular curve} $X_H$ is the compact Riem surface obtained as the quotient
$\hlin{X_H := H\backslash\overline{\mathbb{H}}}$
where $\mathbb{H} = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : \operatorname{Im}(z) > 0\}$ is the upper half-plane and the action of $H$ on $\mathbb{H}$ is via the projection $H \to H \bmod N \subset \operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})$ followed by the usual M\"obius action of $\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})$ on $\Gamma(N)\backslash\mathbb{H}$.
The curve $X_H$ classifies elliptic curves equipped with an $H$-structure, i.e., a level structure whose stabiliser in $\operatorname{GL}_2(\widehat{\mathbb{Z}})$ is exactly $H$.
The open modular curve $Y_H = H\backslash\mathbb{H}$ is the non-compact Riemann surface before compactification.
Special cases include $X_{\Gamma(N)} = X(N)$, $X_{\Gamma_0(N)} = X_0(N)$, and $X_{\Gamma_1(N)} = X_1(N)$.
\end{definition}

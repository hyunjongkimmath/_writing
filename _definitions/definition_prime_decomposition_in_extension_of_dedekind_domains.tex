\begin{definition} \label{definition:prime_decomposition_in_extension_of_dedekind_domains}

    Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:dedekind_domain}{Dedekind domain} with \CrefAndHyperref{definition:field_of_fractions_of_an_integral_domain}{fraction field $K$}, and let $L$ be a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:finite_field_extension}{finite extension} of $K$. Let $\calO_L$ be the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:ring_of_integers_of_an_extension_of_fraction_field_of_a_dedekind_domain}{ring of integers of $L$ over $R$}, which is itself a Dedekind domain. Let $\mathfrak{p}$ be a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:prime_of_a_dedekind_domain}{prime of $R$}.

    Since $\calO_L$ is a Dedekind domain, the \hldef{extension of $\mathfrak{p}$ to $\calO_L$}, i.e.\ the ideal $\mathfrak{p}\calO_L$ of $\calO_L$ generated by $\mathfrak{p}$, admits a unique factorization into prime ideals of $\calO_L$:
    $$\mathfrak{p}\calO_L = \prod_{i=1}^{g} \mathfrak{P}_i^{e_i},$$
    where the $\mathfrak{P}_i$ are distinct primes of $\calO_L$ and each $e_i \ge 1$ is an integer. The integer \hl{$e_i$}, also denoted \hl{$e(\mathfrak{P}_i/\mathfrak{p})$}, is called the \hldef{ramification index of $\mathfrak{P}_i$ over $\mathfrak{p}$}. The \CrefAndHyperref{definition:inertial_degree_of_extension_of_valuation_fields}{inertial degree} \hl{$f_i$}, also denoted \hl{$f(\mathfrak{P}_i/\mathfrak{p})$}, is the degree of the residue field extension:
    $$f_i = [\kappa(\mathfrak{P}_i) : \kappa(\mathfrak{p})],$$
    where $\kappa(\mathfrak{p}) = R/\mathfrak{p}$ and $\kappa(\mathfrak{P}_i) = \calO_L/\mathfrak{P}_i$ are the respective \CrefAndHyperref{definition:residue_field_of_a_ring_at_a_point}{residue fields}.

    The \hldef{fundamental equality of prime decomposition} holds:
    $$\sum_{i=1}^{g} e_i f_i = [L:K].$$
\end{definition}

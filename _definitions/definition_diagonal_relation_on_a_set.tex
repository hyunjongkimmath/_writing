\begin{definition} \label{definition:diagonal_relation_on_a_set}
Let $X$ be a set. The \hldef{diagonal relation on $X$} (also called the \hldef{identity relation on $X$}) is the binary relation $\Delta_X$ on $X$ defined by
$$
\Delta_X = \{ (x, x) \mid x \in X \}.
$$
Equivalently, $\Delta_X = \{ (x, y) \in X \times X \mid x = y \}$.

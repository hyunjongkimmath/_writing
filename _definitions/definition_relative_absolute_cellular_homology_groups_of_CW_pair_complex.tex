\begin{definition} \label{definition:relative_absolute_cellular_homology_groups_of_CW_pair_complex}
Let $G$ be an abelian group. Let $(X, A)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:CW_pair}{CW pair}.  

The \hldef{relative cellular homology groups} \hl{$H_n^{CW}(X, A; G)$} are the homology groups of the cellular chain complex:
$$H_n^{CW}(X, A; G) = \frac{\ker(d_n^G)}{\text{im}(d_{n+1}^G)}$$
When $A = \emptyset$, these are the \hldef{absolute cellular homology groups} \hl{$H_n^{CW}(X; G)$}.
\end{definition}

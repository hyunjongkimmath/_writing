\begin{definition} \label{definition:relative_chow_ring_of_a_smooth_projective_scheme_over_a_noetherian_base}
Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{Noetherian base scheme}. Let $X \to S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:smooth_morphism_of_schemes}{smooth} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:projective_morphism}{projective morphism} of relative dimension $n$. 
The \hldef{relative Chow ring}, denoted \hl{$A^*(X/S)$}, is the graded commutative ring 
\[ A^*(X/S) \coloneqq \bigoplus_{p=0}^n A^p(X/S) \]

where the multiplication is given by the \hldef{relative intersection product} $\cdot: A^p(X/S) \otimes A^q(X/S) \to A^{p+q}(X/S)$. 
% For non-smooth schemes, the intersection product is generally only defined on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:relative_operational_chow_ring}{operational Chow ring} $A^*(X/S)$. In the smooth case, the operational ring and the classical group of cycles are identified via the canonical isomorphism $A^p(X/S) \cong A_{n-p}(X/S)$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Algebraic Gassner representation] \label{definition:algebraic_Gassner_representation_of_pure_braid_group_on_a_module_over_a_ring}
    Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:commutative_ring}{commutative ring}, let $t_1, \dots, t_n$ be indeterminates, and let $K = R[t_1^{\pm 1}, \dots, t_n^{\pm 1}]$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:laurent_polynomial_ring_over_a_commutative_ring}{Laurent polynomial ring} in $n$ variables over $R$. Let $M_{n-1}$ be the free $K$-module of rank $n-1$ with standard basis $e_1, \dots, e_{n-1}$.

    The \hldef{algebraic Gassner representation of $P_n$ on $M_{n-1}$} is the group homomorphism
    $$\hlin{\gamma: P_n \to \operatorname{GL}(M_{n-1})}$$
    defined by its action on the standard generators. For each standard generator $\gamma_i$ of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:pure_braid_group}{pure braid group} $P_n$ ($1 \leq i < n$), the image $\gamma(\gamma_i)$ is the $K$-linear endomorphism whose matrix in the basis $e_1, \dots, e_{n-1}$ equals the identity except for the $2 \times 2$ block at rows and columns $(i, i+1)$:
    $$\begin{pmatrix} t_i & t_i - t_{i+1} \\ 0 & t_{i+1} \end{pmatrix}.$$

    Equivalently, $\gamma(\gamma_i)$ acts on the basis by
    \begin{align*}
        e_i &\mapsto t_i e_i + (t_i - t_{i+1})e_{i+1}, \\
        e_{i+1} &\mapsto t_{i+1} e_{i+1}, \\
        e_j &\mapsto e_j \quad (j \neq i, i+1).
    \end{align*}

    Setting $t_1 = \cdots = t_n = t$ recovers the action of $\sigma_i^2$ in the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebraic_reduced_Burau_representation_of_artin_braid_group_on_a_module_over_a_ring}{algebraic reduced Burau representation}.

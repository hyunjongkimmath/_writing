\begin{definition}[Products of Rings] \label{definition:products_of_rings}
Let $\{R_i\}_{i \in I}$ be a family of rings indexed by a (possibly \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:countable_finite_uncountable_sets}{infinite} but \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_set}{small}) set $I$.

The \hldef{(direct) product of the family $\{R_i\}_{i \in I}$}, denoted by \hl{$\prod_{i \in I} R_i$}, is defined as the set
$$ \prod_{i \in I} R_i := \{ (r_i)_{i \in I} \mid r_i \in R_i \text{ for all } i \in I \}, $$
equipped with componentwise addition and multiplication: for all $(r_i)_{i \in I}, (s_i)_{i \in I} \in \prod_{i \in I} R_i$, define
$$ (r_i)_{i \in I} + (s_i)_{i \in I} := (r_i + s_i)_{i \in I}, \qquad (r_i)_{i \in I} \cdot (s_i)_{i \in I} := (r_i \cdot s_i)_{i \in I}. $$

The zero element of $\prod_{i \in I} R_i$ is the tuple $(0_{R_i})_{i \in I}$, and additive inverses are given componentwise:
$$ -(r_i)_{i \in I} := (-r_i)_{i \in I}. $$

If each $R_i$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring} with multiplicative identity $1_{R_i}$, then $\prod_{i \in I} R_i$ is a unital ring with multiplicative identity $(1_{R_i})_{i \in I}$.

The product $\prod_{i \in I} R_i$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:product_and_coproduct_of_objects_in_a_category}{categorical product} of the objects $R_i$ in the category of rings. For a finite index set $I = \{1, \ldots, n\}$, the product is often written as \hl{$R_1 \times \cdots \times R_n$}. The self-product of a ring $R$ taken $n$ times is denoted by \hl{$R^n$}.
\end{definition}

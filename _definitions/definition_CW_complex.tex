\begin{definition}[CW Complex] \label{definition:CW_complex}
A \textbf{CW complex} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cellular_complex}{cellular complex} $X$ that satisfies the following conditions:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Hausdorff:} $X$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:separation_axioms_of_topology}{Hausdorff space}.
    
    \item \textbf{C (Closure Finiteness):} The closure of each cell $\overline{e_{\alpha}^n}$ is contained in a finite union of cells of dimension less than or equal to $n$.
    
    \item \textbf{W (Weak Topology):} A subset $A \subseteq X$ is closed if and only if $A \cap \overline{e_{\alpha}^n}$ is closed for every cell $e_{\alpha}^n$.
\end{itemize}
\end{definition}

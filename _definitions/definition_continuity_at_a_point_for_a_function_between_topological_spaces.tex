\begin{definition} \label{definition:continuity_at_a_point_for_a_function_between_topological_spaces}
    Let $f: X \to Y$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:function_of_sets}{function} between \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_space}{topological spaces}.
    It is said to be \hldef{continuous at $x \in X$} if for every \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:neighborhood_and_neighborhood_basis_of_a_point_in_a_topological_space}{open neighborhood} $V$ of $f(x)$ in $Y$, there exists an open neighborhood $U$ of $x$ in $X$ such that $f(U) \subseteq V$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:fibered_category_over_a_category}
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category}{category}. 
A \hldef{fibered category over $\mathcal{C}$} is a $(F,p)$ \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category_over_a_category_and_fiber_category_over_an_object}{over} $\calC$ such that for every morphism $f: U \to V$ in $\mathcal{C}$ and every object $v \in \text{ob}(\mathcal{F})$ with $p(v) = V$, there exists a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cartesian_morphism_in_a_category_over_a_category_and_pullback}{cartesian morphism} $\phi: u \to v$ in $\mathcal{F}$ such that $p(\phi) = f$.
\end{definition}

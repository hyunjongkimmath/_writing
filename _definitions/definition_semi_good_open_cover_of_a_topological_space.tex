\begin{definition} \label{definition:semi_good_open_cover_of_a_topological_space}
    Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_space}{topological space}. 
    We will say that an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:open_covering_of_a_topological_space}{open cover} $\{U_i\}_{i \in I}$ is a \hldef{semi-good open cover} if every finite intersection $\bigcap_{j=1}^n U_{i_j}$ is empty or is a disjoint union of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:contractible_topological_space}{contractible} open subsets.
\end{definition}

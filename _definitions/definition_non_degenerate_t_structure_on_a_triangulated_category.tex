\begin{definition} \label{definition:non_degenerate_t_structure_on_a_triangulated_category}
    Let $D$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:triangulated_category}{triangulated category} and let $(\Dleqzero, \Dgeqzero)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:t_structure_on_a_triangulated_category}{$t$-structure} on $D$. 
    The $t$-structure $(\Dleqzero, \Dgeqzero)$ is said to be \hldef{non-degenerate} if $\bigcap_n \Dleqn$ and $\bigcap_n \Dgeqn$ each only have zero objects. 
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:function_field_of_an_integral_scheme}

    Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:integral_scheme}{integral \CrefAndHyperref{definition:scheme}{scheme}}. Let $U = \operatorname{Spec}(A)$ be any non-empty open affine subscheme of $X$. Since $X$ is integral, $A$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:domain_integral_domain}{integral domain}. The \hldef{function field of $X$}, denoted by \hl{$\mathcal{K}(X)$}, is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_fractions_of_an_integral_domain}{field of fractions} of $A$.

    This definition is independent of the choice of $U$: for any two non-empty open affine subschemes $\operatorname{Spec}(A), \operatorname{Spec}(B) \subseteq X$, both fields of fractions are canonically isomorphic to the stalk $\mathcal{O}_{X, \eta}$ at the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:generic_point_of_an_irreducible_topological_space}{generic point} $\eta$ of $X$.
\end{definition}

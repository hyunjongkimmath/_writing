\begin{definition}[cf. {\cite[Tag 06YU]{stacks-project}} for a simplified discussion when $\calO$ is assumed commutative] \label{definition:derived_tensor_product_of_complexes_of_modules_on_an_essentially_small_ringed_site_with_a_pretopology}
    Let $(\calC, J, \calO)$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:essentially_small_site}{essentially small} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ringed_site}{ringed site} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{with a pretopology}. \TODO{the ``pretopology'' part should be loosened}

    The \hldef{derived tensor product} is the functor 
    $$- \otimes_{\calO}^L - : D(\Mod_\calO) \times D({}_\calO \Mod) \to D(\Sh(C,J;\Ab))$$
    (\Cref{definition:module_over_a_sheaf_of_rings_on_a_site}) (\Cref{notation:derived_categories_of_O_modules_on_an_essentially_small_site_ringed_site}) also denoted by notations such as \hl{$\stackrel{\mathrm{L}}{\otimes}$} defined as the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:right_left_derived_multifunctor_of_an_additive_multifunctor_between_abelian_categories}{left derived functor} of the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:sheafified_tensor_product_of_sheaves_of_modules_of_sheaves_of_rings_on_a_site_with_a_small_topologically_generating_family}{tensor product functor} 
    $$- \otimes_{\calO} - : \Mod_\calO \times {}_\calO \Mod \to \Sh(C,J;\Ab)$$
    Explicitly, given objects $L,M \in D(\calO)$, $L \otimes_{\calO} M$ is given as follows: let $\calL^\bullet$ be any complex of right $\calO$-modules representing $L$ and let $\calM^\bullet$ be any complex of left $\calO$-modules representing $M$, such that $\calL^\bullet$ is \CrefAndHyperref{definition:K_flat_complex_in_an_abelian_category_with_respect_to_a_biadditive_functor}{$K$-flat} with respect to $\otimes_{\calO}$ on the left for direct sum total complexes or $\calM^\bullet$ is $K$-flat with respect to $\otimes_{\calO}$ on the left for direct sum total complexes; note that such complexes exist by \Cref{proposition:there_are_enough_K_flat_resolutions_for_modules_on_an_essentially_small_ringed_site}. The object $L \otimes_{\calO}^L M$ is then given as the object of $D(\Sh(C,J;\Ab))$ represented by the total complex $\Tot^{\oplus} ( \calL^\bullet \otimes_{\calO} \calM^\bullet)$.

\end{definition}

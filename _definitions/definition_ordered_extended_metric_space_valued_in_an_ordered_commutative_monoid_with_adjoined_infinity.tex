\begin{definition}[Ordered extended metric space] \label{definition:ordered_extended_metric_space_valued_in_an_ordered_commutative_monoid_with_adjoined_infinity}

    Let $(X, \leq)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:partially_ordered_set}{partially ordered set} and let $(X, d)$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:extended_metric_on_a_set_valued_in_an_ordered_commutative_monoid_with_adjoined_infinity}{extended metric space} valued in $T^\infty$, where $T$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ordered_commutative_monoid}{ordered commutative monoid} and $T^\infty$ is its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ordered_monoid_with_adjoined_infinity}{adjoined infinity}.

    The pair $(X, \leq, d)$ is called an \hldef{ordered extended metric space} if the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topology_induced_by_an_extended_metric_valued_in_an_ordered_commutative_monoid_with_adjoined_infinity_and_open_ball_for_an_extended_metric}{metric topology} induced by $d$ coincides with the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:order_topology_on_a_partially_ordered_set}{order topology} on $(X, \leq)$. That is,
    $$\tau_d = \tau_{\leq}.$$

    Equivalently, the identity map $\operatorname{id}: (X, \tau_d) \to (X, \tau_{\leq})$ is a homeomorphism.

    When $(X, \leq)$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:total_order_linear_order_on_a_set}{totally ordered set}, the order topology has a basis of open intervals and rays, and the compatibility condition means that for every $x \in X$ and every $\varepsilon \in T_{>0}$, the open ball $B(x, \varepsilon) = \{y \in X : d(x, y) < \varepsilon\}$ contains an order-interval neighborhood of $x$ (i.e., an open interval $(a, b)$ or ray $(-\infty, b)$ or $(a, \infty)$ containing $x$), and conversely every order-interval neighborhood of $x$ contains an open ball $B(x, \varepsilon)$ for some $\varepsilon \in T_{>0}$.

\end{definition}

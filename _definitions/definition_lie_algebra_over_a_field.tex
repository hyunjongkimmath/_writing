    Let $k$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}. A \hldef{Lie algebra over $k$} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:vector_space_over_a_field}{vector space} $\mathfrak{g}$ over $k$, equipped with a bilinear map
    $$\hlin{[\cdot, \cdot] : \mathfrak{g} \times \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{g}}$$
    called the \hldef{Lie bracket (on $\mathfrak{g}$)}, satisfying the following two conditions for all $x, y, z \in \mathfrak{g}$:

    \begin{enumerate}
        \item \textbf{Skew-symmetry:} $[x, y] = -[y, x]$.
        \item \textbf{Jacobi identity:} $[x, [y, z]] + [y, [z, x]] + [z, [x, y]] = 0$.
    \end{enumerate}

    A \hldef{Lie algebra homomorphism} between two Lie algebras $\mathfrak{g}$ and $\mathfrak{h}$ over $k$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:morphism_of_vector_spaces}{linear map} $\varphi : \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{h}$ such that $\varphi([x, y]) = [\varphi(x), \varphi(y)]$ for all $x, y \in \mathfrak{g}$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[{\cite[Definition 2.2.]{clausen_scholze_cmcg}}, {\cite[Definition 1.1.]{scholze_lag}}] \label{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_the_category_of_profinite_sets}
    Define the following \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{Grothendieck topology} on the category \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:profinite_set}{$\operatorname{Prof}$} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:profinite_set}{profinite sets}: a finite collection $(f_i : S_i \to S)_{i \in I}$ of maps in $\operatorname{Prof}$ is a covering if the maps are jointly surjective, i.e. $\coprod_{i \in I} S_i \to S$ is surjective.

    One may also call $\operatorname{Prof}$ equipped with this Grothendieck topology the \hldef{pro-\'etale site of the point}, and denote this site by $*_{\text{pro\'et}}$.

    It should be noted that $\operatorname{Prof}$ is not \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:essentially_small_site}{essentially small}. \TODO{talk about how the grothendieck topology here is finitary}
\end{definition}

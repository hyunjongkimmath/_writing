\begin{definition} \label{definition:tame_inertia_group_of_a_general_galois_extension_of_valuation_fields}
    Assume \Cref{context:extension_of_valuation_fields} and further assume that $L/K$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:galois_extension_of_fields}{Galois extension}. Let $p$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:characteristic_of_a_ring}{characteristic} of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:residue_field_of_a_ring_at_a_point}{residue field} $k_v$.

    Let $I_w$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:inertia_group_of_a_general_galois_extension_of_valuation_fields}{inertia group} of $L/K$ and let $P_w$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:wild_inertia_group_of_a_general_galois_extension_of_valuation_fields}{wild inertia group}.
    The \hldef{tame inertia group of $L/K$} is the quotient
    $$\hlin{I_w^{\text{tame}} = I_w / P_w}$$
    It is a procyclic pro-prime-to-$p$ group, isomorphic to the inverse limit of $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ over all integers $n \geq 1$ prime to $p$, with the inverse system given by divisibility.

    Letting $(K,v)$ be a valuation field, its \hldef{tame inertia group} \hl{$I_{K,v}^{\text{tame}}$} is the tame inertia group of $K^{\text{sep}} / K$ in the above sense where a valuation on $K^{\text{sep}}$ extending $v$ is fixed.
\end{definition}

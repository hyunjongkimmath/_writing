\begin{definition}[Extended upper half-plane] \label{definition:extended_upper_half_plane_in_the_complex_plane}
    Let $\bbH = \{z \in \bbC : \operatorname{Im}(z) > 0\}$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:upper_half_plane_in_the_complex_plane}{upper half-plane in the complex plane}. The \hldef{extended upper half-plane} is the set
    $$\hlin{\widehat{\bbH} = \bbH \cup \mathbb{R} \cup \{\infty\}}$$
    where $\mathbb{R}$ is the real line and $\infty$ is a point at infinity. Equivalently, $\widehat{\bbH} = \bbH \cup \mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{R})$, identifying $\mathbb{R} \cup \{\infty\}$ with $\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{R})$.

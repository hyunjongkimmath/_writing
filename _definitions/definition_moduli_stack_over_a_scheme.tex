\begin{definition}[Moduli stack] \label{definition:moduli_stack_over_a_scheme}
    Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme}. A \hldef{moduli stack over $S$} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:moduli_problem_on_schemes}{moduli problem} $\calM \to (\operatorname{Sch}/S)$ that is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:artin_stack_over_a_scheme_for_a_grothendieck_topology_on_the_category_of_S_schemes}{algebraic stack}.
    
    % such that the category fibered in groupoids $\calM$ satisfies the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:stack_in_groupoids_over_a_site}{descent conditions} for a chosen Grothendieck topology on $(\operatorname{Sch}/S)$.

    The most common choices of topology are the fppf, fpqc, and étale topologies. Unless otherwise specified, "moduli stack" typically means a moduli problem that is a stack in groupoids for the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:big_fppf_site_of_a_scheme}{fppf topology}.

    A \hldef{morphism of moduli stacks} is then a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:artin_stack_over_a_scheme_for_a_grothendieck_topology_on_the_category_of_S_schemes}{morphism as algebraic stacks}, i.e. a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:stack_in_groupoids_over_a_site}{morphism as stacks in groupoids}
\end{definition}

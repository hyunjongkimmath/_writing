Let $n \geq 2$ be an integer. The \hldef{generalized quaternion group of order~$4n$}, denoted by \hl{$Q_{4n}$}, is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group} with presentation
$$\hlin{Q_{4n} = \langle x, y \mid x^{2n} = 1,\; y^2 = x^n,\; y^{-1}xy = x^{-1} \rangle.}$$

When $n=2$, the group $Q_8$ is called the \hldef{(classical) quaternion group}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:projective_special_linear_group_PSL_2_of_C}
The \hldef{projective special linear group} \hl{$\operatorname{PSL}_n(\mathbb{C})$} is the quotient group
$$\operatorname{PSL}_n(\mathbb{C}) := \operatorname{SL}_n(\mathbb{C}) / Z(\operatorname{SL}_n(\mathbb{C}))$$
where $\operatorname{SL}_n(\mathbb{C}) \subset \operatorname{GL}_n(\mathbb{C})$ is the subgroup of matrices with determinant $1$, and $Z(\operatorname{SL}_n(\mathbb{C})) = \{\zeta I : \zeta^n = 1\}$ is its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:center_of_a_group}{center}.

\begin{definition}[Discriminant of a Finite Locally Free Morphism of Schemes] \label{definition:discriminant_of_a_finite_flat_morphism_of_schemes}
Let $f : X \to Y$ be a finite locally free morphism of schemes, i.e., $f_*\mathcal{O}_X$ is a locally free $\mathcal{O}_Y$-module of finite rank. Let $n$ denote the rank (which is locally constant on $Y$).

The trace pairing on $f_*\mathcal{O}_X$ is the symmetric bilinear form
$$\operatorname{tr}_{X/Y} : f_*\mathcal{O}_X \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_Y} f_*\mathcal{O}_X \to \mathcal{O}_Y,$$
given by taking the trace of multiplication in the finite locally free algebra $f_*\mathcal{O}_X$.

The \hldef{discriminant ideal sheaf of $f$}, denoted by \hl{$\Delta_{X/Y}$}, is the ideal sheaf of $\mathcal{O}_Y$ generated by the determinants of the matrices $(\operatorname{tr}_{X/Y}(e_i e_j))$ as $(e_1, \ldots, e_n)$ ranges over all local bases of $f_*\mathcal{O}_X$. Equivalently, if $(e_1, \ldots, e_n)$ is any local basis, then $\Delta_{X/Y}$ is locally generated by $\det(\operatorname{tr}_{X/Y}(e_i e_j))$.

If $Y = \operatorname{Spec}(A)$ and $X = \operatorname{Spec}(B)$ are affine, and $\{\omega_1, \ldots, \omega_n\}$ is an $A$-basis of $B$, then the discriminant ideal $(\Delta_{X/Y}) \subseteq A$ is generated by $\det(\operatorname{tr}_{B/A}(\omega_i \omega_j))$.

The morphism $f$ is said to be etale in codimension $k$ if the support of the quotient sheaf $\mathcal{O}_Y / \Delta_{X/Y}$ has codimension at least $k+1$ in $Y$. In particular, $f$ is etale if and only if $\Delta_{X/Y} = \mathcal{O}_Y$.
\end{definition}

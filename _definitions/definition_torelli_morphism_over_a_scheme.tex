\begin{definition}[Torelli morphism] \label{definition:torelli_morphism_over_a_scheme}
    Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme}. The \hldef{Torelli morphism over $S$} is the morphism of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:moduli_stack_over_a_scheme}{moduli stacks} over $S$
    $$\tau_S\colon \mathcal{M}_{g, S} \to \mathcal{A}_{g, S},$$
    which sends a (\CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:smooth_at_a_point_for_a_finite_type_scheme_over_a_field}{smooth}, \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:projective_variety_over_a_field}{projective}, \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:disconnected_and_connected_topological_spaces}{connected}) curve $X/S$ of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:geometric_genus_of_a_smooth_projective_variety_over_a_field}{genus} $g$ to its Jacobian $(J_X, \Theta)$, where $J_X = \mathrm{Pic}^0(X/S)$ is the relative Jacobian scheme and $\Theta$ is the canonical principal polarization given by the theta divisor on $J_X$.

    The Torelli theorem states that $\tau_S$ is an embedding (i.e., it is injective on points and induces an isomorphism on tangent spaces), meaning that a curve $X/S$ is uniquely determined by its polarized Jacobian $(J_X, \Theta)$.
\end{definition}

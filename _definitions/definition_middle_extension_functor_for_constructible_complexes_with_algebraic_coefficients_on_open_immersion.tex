\begin{definition}[Middle extension functor for constructible complexes with algebraic coefficients] \label{definition:middle_extension_functor_for_constructible_complexes_with_algebraic_coefficients_on_open_immersion}

Let $(R, \lambda)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_ring_with_respect_to_an_ideal}{complete} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{DVR} such that $R/\lambda^n$ is finite for all $n \geq 1$. Let $E$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_fractions_of_an_integral_domain}{fraction field} of $R$, and let $K/E$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebraic_element_minimal_polynomial_of_an_algebraic_element_algebraic_field_extension_transcendental_element_field_extension}{algebraic extension}. Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{noetherian scheme}, and consider sheaves on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{(small) \'etale site} of $X$. Let $j: U \hookrightarrow X$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:open_and_closed_immersions_of_schemes}{open immersion}.

Let $\pscrH^i_K(-)$ denote the $i$th perverse cohomology functor with respect to the perverse $t$-structures on $\Dbc(U, K)$ and $\Dbc(X, K)$. Let $j_{!*}: \Perv(U, K) \to \Perv(X, K)$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:middle_extension_functor_for_perverse_sheaves_with_algebraic_coefficients_on_open_immersion}{middle extension functor with algebraic coefficients}.

For a complex $\mathcal{K} \in \Dbc(U, K)$, the \hldef{middle extension of $\mathcal{K}$ along $j$} is the object in $\Dbc(X, K)$
$$\hlin{j_{!*} \mathcal{K} := \bigoplus_{i \in \bbZ} j_{!*} \pscrH^i_K(\mathcal{K})[-i]}$$
where each $\pscrH^i_K(\mathcal{K}) \in \Perv(U, K)$ is extended via the perverse middle extension functor and then shifted by $-i$.

The assignment $\mathcal{K} \mapsto j_{!*}\mathcal{K}$ defines a functor
$$\hlin{j_{!*}: \Dbc(U, K) \to \Dbc(X, K)}$$
called the \hldef{middle extension functor for constructible complexes with algebraic coefficients along $j$}. It is exact with respect to the perverse $t$-structures and restricts to the perverse middle extension on $\Perv(U, K)$.
\end{definition}

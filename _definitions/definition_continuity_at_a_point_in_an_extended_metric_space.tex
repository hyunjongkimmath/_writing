\begin{definition}[Continuity at a point in an extended metric space] \label{definition:continuity_at_a_point_in_an_extended_metric_space}
    Let $T$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ordered_semiring}{ordered semiring}, let $T^\infty$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ordered_semiring_with_adjoined_infinity}{ordered semiring with adjoined infinity} obtained from $T$, and let $(X,d_X)$, $(Y,d_Y)$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:extended_metric_on_a_set_valued_in_an_ordered_semiring_with_adjoined_infinity}{extended metric spaces valued in $T^\infty$}. Let $f : X \to Y$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:function_of_sets}{function} and let $a \in X$.

    We say that $f$ is \hldef{continuous at $a$} if for every $\varepsilon \in T_{>0}$ there exists $\delta \in T_{>0}$ such that
    \[ d_X(x,a) < \delta \quad\text{implies}\quad d_Y(f(x), f(a)) < \varepsilon. \]

    For an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:accumulation_point_in_an_extended_metric_space}{accumulation point} $a$ of $X$, $f$ is continuous at $a$ in the above sense if and only if
        $$\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = f(a).$$
    \CrefIfExists{definition:limit_of_a_function_between_extended_metric_spaces_at_an_accumulation_point}

    For an arbitrary point $a$ of $X$, $f$ is continuous at $a$ in the above sense if and only if $f$, as a map of the topological spaces \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topology_induced_by_an_extended_metric_and_open_ball_for_an_extended_metric}{induced by} the extended metric space structures on $X$ and $Y$, is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:continuity_at_a_point_for_a_function_between_topological_spaces}{continuous at} $a$\CrefIfExists{proposition:continuity_at_a_point_in_extended_metric_space_equivalent_to_topological_definition_and_limit_at_accumulation_point}.
\end{definition}

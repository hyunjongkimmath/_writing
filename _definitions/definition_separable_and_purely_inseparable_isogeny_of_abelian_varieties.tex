\begin{definition}[Separable, inseparable, and purely inseparable isogeny of abelian varieties over a field] \label{definition:separable_and_purely_inseparable_isogeny_of_abelian_varieties}
    Let $k$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field} of characteristic $p > 0$ and let $\varphi\colon A \to B$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:isogeny_of_abelian_varieties_over_a_field}{isogeny of abelian varieties over $k$}. 
    The isogeny $\varphi$ is called \hldef{separable} if its degree $\deg(\varphi)$ is prime to $\operatorname{char}(k)$. Equivalently, $\varphi$ is separable if the kernel group scheme $\ker \varphi$ is étale as a scheme over $k$.

    The isogeny $\varphi$ is called \hldef{inseparable} if its degree $\deg(\varphi)$ is divisible by $\operatorname{char}(k) = p$. Equivalently, $\varphi$ is inseparable if it is not separable.

    The isogeny $\varphi$ is called \hldef{purely inseparable} if its degree is a power of $p = \operatorname{char}(k)$. Equivalently, $\varphi$ is purely inseparable if it induces an isomorphism on geometric generic fibers but the kernel $\ker \varphi$ is connected as a group scheme over $k$.

    Every isogeny $\varphi\colon A \to B$ factors uniquely as
    $$\hlin{A \xrightarrow{\;\varphi_s\;} A' \xrightarrow{\;\varphi_{ins}\;} B,}$$
    where $\varphi_s$ is a separable isogeny and $\varphi_{ins}$ is a purely inseparable isogeny. The intermediate abelian variety $A'$ is uniquely determined up to isomorphism, and the factorization is compatible with further decompositions of $\varphi$.
\end{definition}

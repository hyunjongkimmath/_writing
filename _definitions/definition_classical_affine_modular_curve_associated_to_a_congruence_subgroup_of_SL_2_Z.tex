\begin{definition}[Classical affine modular curve associated to a congruence subgroup of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$] \label{definition:classical_affine_modular_curve_associated_to_a_congruence_subgroup_of_SL_2_Z}
Let $N$ be a positive integer and let $H$ be a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:congruence_subgroup_of_SL_2_Z}{congruence subgroup} of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$. The \hldef{classical affine modular curve associated to $H$} is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:riemann_surface}{Riemann surface}
\[
    Y_H = H \backslash \bbH,
\]
where $\bbH$ is the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:upper_half_plane_in_the_complex_plane}{complex upper half-plane} and $H$ acts via the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:modular_action_of_a_subgroup_of_SL_2_Z_on_the_upper_half_plane}{modular action}. Concretely, $Y_H$ is the quotient of $\bbH$ by $H$ equipped with the unique Riemann surface structure making the projection $\bbH \to Y_H$ \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:holomorphism_and_biholomoprhism_of_complex_manifolds}{holomorphic}.
\end{definition}

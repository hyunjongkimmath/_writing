\begin{definition} \label{definition:isotypic_component_of_a_continuous_representation_of_a_topological_group_on_a_hilbert_space_for_an_irreducible_representation}

    Let $G$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_group}{topological group}, and $(\pi, \mathcal{H})$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:unitary_representation_of_a_group_on_a_hilbert_space}{unitary representation of $G$ on a Hilbert space}. Let $(\sigma, S)$ be an irreducible unitary representation of $G$.

    The \hldef{$S$-isotypic component of $\mathcal{H}$} is the closed subspace
    $$\hlin{\mathcal{H}_{(S)} := \overline{\sum \{\mathcal{K} \subseteq \mathcal{H} \mid \mathcal{K} \text{ is a closed subrepresentation and } \mathcal{K} \cong S\}}.}$$
    It is the smallest closed $G$-invariant subspace containing all copies of $S$ in $\mathcal{H}$.

    Since unitary representations are semisimple (every closed subrepresentation has an orthogonal complement), $\mathcal{H}$ decomposes as a Hilbert space direct sum
    $$ \mathcal{H} = \widehat{\bigoplus}_{[S]} \mathcal{H}_{(S)},$$
    where the sum runs over isomorphism classes of irreducible unitary constituents. Each $\mathcal{H}_{(S)}$ is isomorphic to the Hilbert space direct sum of $m_S$ copies of $S$, for some multiplicity $m_S \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$, called the \hldef{multiplicity of $S$ in $\mathcal{H}$}.
\end{definition}

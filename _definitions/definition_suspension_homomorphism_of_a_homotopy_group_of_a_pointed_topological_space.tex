\begin{definition} \label{definition:suspension_homomorphism_of_a_homotopy_group_of_a_pointed_topological_space}
    Let $(X,x_0)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:pointed_topological_space}{pointed topological space}. 
    The \hldef{$n$th suspension homomorphism of $X$} is the function $\pi_n(X,x_0) \to \pi_{n+1}(\Sigma X, [x_0, 0])$\CrefIfExists{definition:homotopy_groups_of_a_pointed_topological_space}, which is a group homomorphism whenever $n \geq 1$, defined by sending $[f] \in \pi_n(X,x_0)$ the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:homotopy_class_of_maps_of_topological_spaces_relative_to_a_subset}{homotopy class} of 
    $$\hlin{\Sigma f = f \wedge \id: \sphere{n+1} \cong \sphere{n} \wedge \Sone \to X \wedge \Sone.}$$
\end{definition}

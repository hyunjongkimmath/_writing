\begin{definition}[Universal homeomorphism of schemes] \label{definition:universal_homeomorphism_of_schemes}

    Let $f: X \to Y$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:morphism_of_schemes}{morphism of schemes}.

    The morphism $f$ is called a \hldef{universal homeomorphism} if for every morphism of schemes $Y' \to Y$, the base change morphism $X \times_Y Y' \to Y'$ is a homeomorphism on underlying topological spaces. Equivalently, $f$ is universally injective, universally surjective, and universally open.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Ekedahl-Oort type of an abelian variety] \label{definition:ekedahl_oort_type_of_abelian_variety_over_characteristic_p_field}
    Let $A$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_variety_over_a_field}{abelian variety} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dimension_of_a_scheme}{dimension} $g$ over a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field} $k$ of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:characteristic_of_a_ring}{characteristic} $p > 0$. 
    The \hldef{Ekedahl-Oort type} (or \hldef{EO-type}) of $A$ is the isomorphism class of the finite group scheme $A[p]$ as a module over the Dieudonne functor.

    \TODO{torsion subgroup of group scheme}
    Equivalently, the Ekedahl-Oort type classifies the $p$-torsion subgroup scheme $A[p]$ up to \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:isogeny_of_abelian_varieties_over_a_field}{isogeny}. The possible EO-types are in bijection with certain combinatorial data: subsets of $\{1, 2, \dots, g\}$ (for \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ordinary_and_supersingular_abelian_varieties_over_a_field_of_characteristic_p}{ordinary} abelian varieties) or more generally with symplectic similitude classes of certain matrices.

    The Ekedahl-Oort type refines the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:newton_polygon_of_an_abelian_variety_over_a_field_of_prime_characteristic}{Newton polygon} invariant: all abelian varieties of a given EO-type have the same Newton polygon, but the converse is not true in general.
\end{definition}

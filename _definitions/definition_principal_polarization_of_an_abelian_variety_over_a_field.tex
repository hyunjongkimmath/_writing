\begin{definition}[Principal polarization of an abelian variety] \label{definition:principal_polarization_of_an_abelian_variety_over_a_field}

    Let $(A, \phi)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:polarization_of_an_abelian_variety_over_a_field}{polarized abelian variety} over a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}. The polarization $\phi$ is called \hldef{principal} if it is an isomorphism (equivalently, if the degree of $\phi$, denoted by $\deg(\phi)$, equals $1$). An abelian variety equipped with a principal polarization is called a \hldef{principally polarized abelian variety} (PPAV).

    For $g = 1$, a PPAV is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:elliptic_curve_over_a_scheme}{elliptic curve}. A principally polarized abelian surface ($g=2$) or threefold ($g=3$) is the Jacobian of a smooth connected algebraic curve unless it decomposes as a product together with the product polarization.
\end{definition}

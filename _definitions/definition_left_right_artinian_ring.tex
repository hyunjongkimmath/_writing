\begin{definition} \label{definition:left_right_artinian_ring}
A \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring} $R$ is a \hldef{left Artinian ring} if it is Artinian as a left \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:module_of_a_ring}{module} over itself. That is, $R$ satisfies the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ascending_descending_chain_condition_on_a_partially_ordered_set}{DCC} on \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ideal_of_a_ring}{left ideals}. A \hldef{right Artinian ring} is defined dually as satisfying the DCC on \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ideal_of_a_ring}{right ideals}. A ring is \hldef{Artinian} if it is both left and right Artinian.
\end{definition}

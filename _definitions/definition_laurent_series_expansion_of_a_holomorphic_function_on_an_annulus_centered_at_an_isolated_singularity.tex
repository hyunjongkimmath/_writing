\begin{definition} \label{definition:laurent_series_expansion_of_a_holomorphic_function_on_an_annulus_centered_at_an_isolated_singularity}
Let $U \subset \mathbb{C}$ be an open set, let $z_0 \in U$, and let $f : U \setminus \{z_0\} \to \mathbb{C}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:holomorphic_function_from_a_subset_of_Cn_to_Cm}{holomorphic function} with an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:isolated_singularity_of_a_holomorphic_function_from_an_open_subset_of_the_complex_plane_to_the_complex_plane_classified_as_removable_pole_or_essential}{isolated singularity} at $z_0$. 
\TODO{punctured complex disk}
Suppose there exists $R > 0$ such that the punctured disk $0 < |z - z_0| < R$ is contained in $U \setminus \{z_0\}$. Then $f$ admits a \hldef{Laurent series expansion centered at $z_0$}:
$\hlin{f(z) = \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} a_n (z - z_0)^n}$
for all $z$ with $0 < |z - z_0| < R$, where the series converges absolutely and uniformly on every annulus $r_1 \leq |z - z_0| \leq r_2$ with $0 < r_1 < r_2 < R$. The coefficients are given by
$\hlin{a_n = \frac{1}{2\pi i} \int_{|w - z_0| = \rho} \frac{f(w)}{(w - z_0)^{n+1}}\,dw}$
for any $\rho \in (0, R)$, and are independent of the choice of $\rho$. The series splits into a \hldef{regular part} $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n (z - z_0)^n$ (which is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:power_series_in_one_complex_variable_and_its_radius_of_convergence_given_by_the_cauchy_hadamard_formula}{power series}) and a \hldef{principal part} $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_{-n} (z - z_0)^{-n}$. 

The singularity $z_0$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:isolated_singularity_of_a_holomorphic_function_from_an_open_subset_of_the_complex_plane_to_the_complex_plane_classified_as_removable_pole_or_essential}{removable} if and only if the principal part vanishes, a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:isolated_singularity_of_a_holomorphic_function_from_an_open_subset_of_the_complex_plane_to_the_complex_plane_classified_as_removable_pole_or_essential}{pole of order $m$} if and only if $a_{-n} = 0$ for all $n > m$ with $a_{-m} \neq 0$, and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:isolated_singularity_of_a_holomorphic_function_from_an_open_subset_of_the_complex_plane_to_the_complex_plane_classified_as_removable_pole_or_essential}{essential} if and only if infinitely many coefficients $a_{-n}$ are nonzero.
\end{definition}

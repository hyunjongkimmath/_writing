\begin{definition}[cf. {\cite[TAg 07A5]{stacks-project}}]\label{definition:total_right_derived_direct_image_functor_for_morphism_of_ringed_sites}
    Let $(\calC, J, \calO_\calC)$, $(\calD, K, \calO_\calD)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:essentially_small_site}{essentially small} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ringed_site}{ringed sites} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{with pretopologies}. \TODO{the ``pretopology'' part should be loosened} Let $f: (\calC, J, \calO_\calC) \to (\calD, K, \calO_\calD)$ be a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:morphism_of_ringed_sites}{morphism of ringed sites}. \TODO{this must be a statement about a morphism of ringed topoi, every morphism of sites induces a morphism of topoi, but not the other way around apparently.}

    The \hldef{(total) derived direct image} or \hldef{(total) derived pushforward functor} \hl{$Rf_*$} is the functor
    $$Rf_*: D(\calO_\calC) \to D(\calO_\calD)$$
    (\Cref{notation:derived_categories_of_O_modules_on_an_essentially_small_site_ringed_site}) defined as the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:right_left_derived_multifunctor_of_an_additive_multifunctor_between_abelian_categories}{right derived functor} of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:direct_image_of_a_sheaf_on_a_site_under_a_continuous_functor_of_sites_or_a_site_morphism}{direct image functor} $f_*$. Explicitly, given an object $K \in D(\calO_\calC)$, $Rf_*(K)$ is given as follows: Let $\calI^\bullet$ be any (right) \CrefAndHyperref{definition:K_injective_projective_complex_in_an_abelian_category}{$K$-injective} \CrefAndHyperref{definition:left_right_resolution_of_a_chain_complex_in_an_abelian_category}{resolution} of $M$, which exists by \Cref{theorem:category_of_modules_over_an_essentially_small_ringed_site_has_enough_K_injectives}. The object $Rf_* (K)$ is then given as the object of $D(\calO_\calD)$ represented by the chain complex $f_*(\calI^\bullet)$ (\Cref{proposition:exact_functor_between_abelian_categories_induces_functor_on_derived_categories}).
    This is well defined by \Cref{proposition:exact_functor_from_homotopy_category_of_abelian_category_with_enough_K_injective_complexes_has_defined_derived_functor}.
\end{definition}

    Let $(R, \lambda)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_ring_with_respect_to_an_ideal}{complete} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{DVR} such that $R/\lambda^n$ is finite for all $n \geq 1$. Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{noetherian scheme}. Consider sheaves on the (small) \'etale sites of schemes over $S$.

    Let $X$ be an $S$-scheme of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_type_morphism_of_schemes}{finite type}, and let $f: X \to S$ be the structure morphism. The \hldef{dualizing complex on $X$ relative to $S$ with integral coefficients}, denoted by \hl{$\mathbb{D}_{X/S}^\bullet(R)$}, is the object in $\Dbc(X, R)$ defined as
    $$\hlin{\mathbb{D}_{X/S}^\bullet(R) := Rf^! \mathbb{D}_S^\bullet(R)}$$

\begin{definition} \label{definition:residue_of_a_holomorphic_function_at_an_isolated_singularity_defined_as_the_coefficient_of_z_minus_z_0_to_the_negative_1_in_its_laurent_expansion}
Let $U \subset \mathbb{C}$ be an open set, let $z_0 \in U$, and let $f : U \setminus \{z_0\} \to \mathbb{C}$ be a holomorphic function with an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:isolated_singularity_of_a_holomorphic_function_from_an_open_subset_of_the_complex_plane_to_the_complex_plane_classified_as_removable_pole_or_essential}{isolated singularity} at $z_0$. 
In a punctured neighborhood $0 < |z - z_0| < r$ of $z_0$, the function $f$ admits a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:laurent_series_expansion_of_a_holomorphic_function_on_an_annulus_centered_at_an_isolated_singularity}{Laurent series expansion}
$f(z) = \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} a_n (z - z_0)^n$
where $a_n = \frac{1}{2\pi i} \int_{\gamma} \frac{f(w)}{(w - z_0)^{n+1}}\,dw$ for any positively oriented simple closed contour $\gamma$ in the punctured neighborhood around $z_0$. The coefficient $a_{-1}$ is called the \hldef{residue of $f$ at $z_0$}, denoted
$\hlin{\operatorname{Res}(f, z_0) := a_{-1}}$.
\end{definition}

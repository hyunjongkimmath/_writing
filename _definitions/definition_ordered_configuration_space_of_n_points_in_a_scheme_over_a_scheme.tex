\begin{definition} \label{definition:ordered_configuration_space_of_n_points_in_a_scheme_over_a_scheme}

Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme}, let $X$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme_over_a_scheme}{$S$-scheme}, and let $n \geq 1$ be an integer. The \hldef{ordered configuration space of $n$ points in $X$ over $S$}, denoted \hl{$\operatorname{Conf}_n(X/S)$}, is the open subscheme
$$\hlin{\operatorname{Conf}_n(X/S) = X^n_S \setminus \Delta_{\mathrm{fat}}}$$
of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cartesian_product_of_two_objects_in_a_category_over_an_object}{$n$-fold fiber product} $X^n_S$ obtained by removing the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:fat_diagonal_of_n_fold_relative_product}{fat diagonal} $\Delta_{\mathrm{fat}}$.

Its $T$-points for an $S$-scheme $T$ are given by $n$-tuples of pairwise disjoint sections $(s_1, \dots, s_n)$ where each $s_i \colon T \to X$ is a section of the structure morphism over $T$ and the images $s_i(T)$ are mutually disjoint subschemes of $X$.
\end{definition}

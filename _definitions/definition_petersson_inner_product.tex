Let $k$ be a positive even integer and let $\Gamma \leq \mathrm{SL}_2(\bbZ)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:congruence_subgroup_of_SL_2_Z}{congruence subgroup}. The \hldef{Petersson inner product} on the space of cusp forms $S_k(\Gamma)$ is defined by
$$\hlin{\langle f, g \rangle = \iint_{\Gamma \backslash \mathbb{H}} f(z) \, \overline{g(z)} \, y^k \, \frac{dx \, dy}{y^2},}$$
where $z = x + iy$ and the integral is taken over a fundamental domain for $\Gamma$ acting on the upper half-plane $\mathbb{H}$ via the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:modular_action_of_a_subgroup_of_SL_2_Z_on_the_upper_half_plane}{modular action}.

The measure $y^{-2} \, dx \, dy$ is the unique (up to scalar) $\mathrm{SL}_2(\bbR)$-invariant measure on $\mathbb{H}$, so the integrand $f(z)\overline{g(z)} y^k \, y^{-2}\,dx\,dy$ is well-defined and independent of the choice of fundamental domain.

For cusp forms $f, g \in S_k(\Gamma)$, this integral converges absolutely, and $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle$ defines a positive-definite Hermitian inner product on $S_k(\Gamma)$.

\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[connected category]
\label{definition:connected_category}
A \hldef{non-empty category $\mathcal{I}$ is connected} if, for any two objects $i, j \in \mathcal{I}$, there exists a finite sequence of objects $x_0 = i, x_1, \dots, x_n = j$ in $\mathcal{I}$ and morphisms $f_k: x_{k-1} \to x_k$ or $f_k: x_k \to x_{k-1}$ for each $k = 1, \dots, n$. That is, any two objects can be joined by a zigzag of morphisms.

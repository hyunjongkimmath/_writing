    Let $(R, \lambda)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_ring_with_respect_to_an_ideal}{complete} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{DVR} such that $R/\lambda^n$ is finite for all $n \geq 1$.     Let $X$ be an $S$-scheme of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_type_morphism_of_schemes}{finite type}, and let $f: X \to S$ be the structure morphism.

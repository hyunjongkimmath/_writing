\begin{definition}[Mumford-Tate group] \label{definition:mumford_tate_group_of_an_abelian_variety_over_the_complex_numbers}
Let $A$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_variety_over_a_field}{abelian variety} of dimension $g$ over $\mathbb{C}$, and let $\ell$ be a prime number. Let $V_\ell(A)$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ell_adic_tate_group_of_a_commutative_group_scheme_over_a_field}{rational $\ell$-adic Tate group} of $A$, a vector space over $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$ of dimension $2g$. 
Let 
$$V = H_1(A(\bbC), \bbQ).$$
The complex structure on $V$ induces a Hodge decomposition on $V_\bbC = V \otimes_\bbQ \bbC \cong H_1(A(\bbC), \bbC)$:
$$V \cong V^{1,0} \oplus V^{0,1}.$$
This pure Hodge structure of weight $-1$ is equivalent to a representation 
$$h: \bbS \to \GL(V_\bbR)$$
of the Deligne torus $\bbS = \operatorname{Res}_{\bbC/\bbR} (\bbG_m)$ over $R$ where $z \in \bbS(\bbR) \cong \bbC^\times$ acts as $z$ on $V^{1,0}$ and as $\overline{z}$ on $V^{0,1}$. The \hldef{Mumford-Tate group} \hl{$\mathrm{MT}(A)$} is the smallest algebraic subgroup of $\GL(V)$ defined over $\bbQ$ such that $\mathrm{MT}(A)_\bbR$ contains $h(\bbS)$.

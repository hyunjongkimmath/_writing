\begin{definition} \label{definition:cone_of_a_morphism_in_a_triangulated_category}
Let $\mathcal{T}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:triangulated_category}{triangulated category}. For any morphism $u: X \to Y$ in $\mathcal{T}$, a \hldef{cone of $u$} is an object $C$ that fits into a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:triangulated_category}{distinguished triangle}:
$$X \xrightarrow{u} Y \xrightarrow{v} C \xrightarrow{d} X[1]$$
In a general triangulated category, the cone $C$ is guaranteed to exist by the axioms, but it is only unique up to a (not necessarily unique) isomorphism.
\end{definition}

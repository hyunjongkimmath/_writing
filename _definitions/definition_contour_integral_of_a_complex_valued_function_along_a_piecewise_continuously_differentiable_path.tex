\begin{definition} \label{definition:contour_integral_of_a_complex_valued_function_along_a_piecewise_continuously_differentiable_path}
Let $\gamma : [a, b] \to \mathbb{C}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:piecewise_continuously_differentiable_path_from_a_closed_real_interval_to_the_complex_plane}{piecewise continuously differentiable path} and let $f : \gamma([a, b]) \to \mathbb{C}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:continuous_map_of_topological_spaces}{continuous} complex-valued function defined on the image of $\gamma$. 
\TODO{image of a topological space under a continuous map as a topological space}
The \hldef{contour integral/line integral of $f$ along $\gamma$}, denoted
$\hlin{\int_{\gamma} f(z)\,dz}$,
is defined by
$\hlin{\int_{\gamma} f(z)\,dz := \int_{a}^{b} f(\gamma(t))\,\gamma'(t)\,dt}$,
where $\gamma'(t)$ exists for all $t \in [a, b] \setminus \{t_1, \ldots, t_{k-1}\}$ and the Riemann integral on the right-hand side is well-defined since $f \circ \gamma$ is continuous and $\gamma'$ is piecewise continuous on $[a, b]$.

In case $\gamma$ is a closed path, one denotes $\int_{\gamma} f(z)\,dz$ by \hl{$\oint_{\gamma} f(z)\,dz$}
\end{definition}

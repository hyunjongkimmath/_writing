\begin{definition} \label{definition:morphism_of_relative_chow_motives_over_a_noetherian_base_and_coefficients_in_a_commutative_ring}
Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{Noetherian base scheme} and $R$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:commutative_ring}{commutative ring}. Let $M = (X, p, m)$ and $N = (Y, q, n)$ be two \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:relative_chow_motive_over_a_noetherian_base_and_commutative_ring_of_coefficients}{relative Chow motives over $S$}, where $X, Y$ are \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:smooth_morphism_of_schemes}{smooth} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:projective_morphism_of_schemes_projective_scheme_over_a_scheme}{projective $S$-schemes}, $p, q$ are idempotents, and $m, n \in \mathbb{Z}$ are twists. A \hldef{morphism from $M$ to $N$} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:correspondences_of_degree_r_between_smooth_projective_schemes_over_a_noetherian_base_with_coefficients_in_a_commutative_ring}{correspondence} $f \in A_{\dim(X/S) + m - n}(X \times_S Y) \otimes R$ that is compatible with the projectors $p$ and $q$, meaning it satisfies the condition:
\[ f = q \circ f \circ p, \]
where $\circ$ is the composition of correspondences over $S$. 
The set of all such morphisms is denoted by \hl{$\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathfrak{Chow}(S, R)}(M, N)$}, which can be identified with the subgroup 
$$q \circ \left( A_{\dim(X/S) + m - n}(X \times_S Y) \otimes R \right) \circ p.$$
\end{definition}

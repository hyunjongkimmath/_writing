\begin{definition} \label{definition:determinant_of_an_endomorphism_of_a_vector_space_over_a_field}
Let $F$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}, let $V$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:vector_space_over_a_field}{$F$-vector space} of finite \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:basis_of_a_vector_space_over_a_field}{dimension} $n = \dim_F V$, and let $\phi : V \to V$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:morphism_of_vector_spaces}{$F$-linear endomorphism} of $V$.
The \hldef{determinant} of $\phi$ is the scalar
$$\hlin{\det(\phi) = \det([\phi]_B)} \in F,$$
where $[\phi]_B$ denotes the matrix of $\phi$ with respect to any basis $B$ of $V$. This value is independent of the choice of basis $B$.

Equivalently, $\phi$ induces a linear map \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:exterior_power_of_a_vector_space_over_a_field}{$\Lambda^n(\phi) : \Lambda^n(V) \to \Lambda^n(V)$} on the top exterior power of $V$, and since $\dim_F \Lambda^n(V) = 1$, the map $\Lambda^n(\phi)$ is multiplication by a scalar; this scalar is $\det(\phi)$.
\end{definition}

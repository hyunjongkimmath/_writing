Let $(R, \lambda)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_ring_with_respect_to_an_ideal}{complete} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete valuations ring}{DVR} such that $R/\lambda^n$ is finite for all $n \geq 1$. Let $E$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_fractions_of_an_integral_domain}{fraction field} of $R$, and let $K/E$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebraic_element_minimal_polynomial_of_an_algebraic_element_algebraic_field_extension_transcendental_element_field_extension}{algebraic extension}. Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme}. Let $X$ and $Y$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{noetherian} $S$-schemes, and consider sheaves on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{(small) \'etale sites} of $X$ and $Y$. Let $f: X \to Y$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:compactification_of_a_morphism_of_schemes}{$S$-compactifiable morphism}.

Let $\pscrH^0_K(-)$ denote the zeroth perverse cohomology functor with respect to the perverse $t$-structures on $\Dbc(X, K)$ and $\Dbc(Y, K)$. Let $\alpha_f: Rf_! \Rightarrow Rf_*$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:canonical_forgets_support_map_from_derived_direct_image_with_compact_support_to_derived_direct_image_for_bdd_constructible_complexes_with_algebraic_coefficients_on_compactifiable_morphisms}{canonical forgets support map for bounded constructible complexes with algebraic coefficients}.

For a complex $\mathcal{K} \in \Dbc(X, K)$, the \hldef{middle extension of $\mathcal{K}$ along $f$ via the forgets-support map} is the object in $\Perv(Y, K)$
$$\hlin{j_{!*} \mathcal{K} := \Im\bigl(\pscrH^0_K(Rf_! \mathcal{K}) \xrightarrow{\pscrH^0_K(\alpha_f(\mathcal{K}))} \pscrH^0_K(Rf_* \mathcal{K})\bigr)}$$
where $\Im(-)$ denotes the image in the abelian category $\Perv(Y, K)$.

The assignment $\mathcal{K} \mapsto j_{!*}\mathcal{K}$ defines a functor
$$\hlin{j_{!*}: \Dbc(X, K) \to \Perv(Y, K)}$$
called the \hldef{middle extension functor via forgets-support map with algebraic coefficients along $f$}. It factors through perverse cohomology and takes values in the heart of the perverse $t$-structure on $Y$.

When $f = j: U \hookrightarrow X$ is an open immersion, this coincides with the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:middle_extension_functor_for_perverse_sheaves_with_algebraic_coefficients_on_open_immersion}{middle extension functor for perverse sheaves with algebraic coefficients}.
\end{definition}

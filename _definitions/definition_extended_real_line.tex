\begin{definition} \label{definition:extended_real_line}
    The \hldef{extended real line} is the set \hl{$\overline{\mathbb{R}} = \mathbb{R} \cup \{-\infty, +\infty\}$}\CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:real_numbers}{real numbers} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:total_order_on_a_set}{total order} that extends that on $\bbR$ and such that $-\infty < x < +\infty$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$, together with the arithmetic conventions $+\infty + (+\infty) = +\infty$, $(-\infty) + (-\infty) = -\infty$, and $a \cdot (\pm\infty) = \pm\infty$ for $a > 0$, $a \cdot (\pm\infty) = \mp\infty$ for $a < 0$.
\end{definition}

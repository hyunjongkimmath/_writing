\begin{definition}[Locally free module over a commutative ring] \label{definition:locally_free_module_over_a_commutative_ring}
    Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:commutative_ring}{commutative ring} and let $M$ be an $R$-module. The module $M$ is called a \hldef{locally free $R$-module} if for every prime ideal $\mathfrak{p} \in \Spec(R)$, there exists an element $f \in R \setminus \mathfrak{p}$ such that the localization $M_f$ is a free $R_f$-module. Equivalently, there exists a family $\{f_i\}_{i \in I}$ of elements of $R$ generating the unit ideal such that $M_{f_i}$ is a free $R_{f_i}$-module for each $i$.

    If in addition $M_f \cong R_f^{\oplus n}$ as an $R_f$-module for some fixed integer $n \geq 0$, then we say that $M$ has \hldef{constant rank} $n$.
\end{definition}

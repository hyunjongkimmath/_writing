\begin{definition} \label{definition:monoid_object_in_an_infty_category}
    \TODO{AI generated, verify}
    Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:infty_category_quasi_category}{$\infty$-category} admitting \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:product_of_finitely_many_objects_in_a_category}{finite products} and a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:terminal_object_in_an_infty_category}{terminal object} $*$.

    Let $(A, \mu)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:semigroup_object_in_an_infty_category}{semigroup object} in $\mathcal{C}$. A \hldef{unital structure} on $(A, \mu)$ consists of a morphism $\eta : * \to A$ from the terminal object, together with a choice of homotopies rendering the diagrams
    $$A \simeq * \times A \xrightarrow{\eta \times \mathrm{id}_A} A \times A \xrightarrow{\mu} A$$
    $$A \simeq A \times * \xrightarrow{\mathrm{id}_A \times \eta} A \times A \xrightarrow{\mu} A$$
    equivalent to the identity $\mathrm{id}_A$, compatible with the infinite hierarchy of higher associativity coherences of the underlying semigroup.

    Formally, a \hldef{monoid object in $\mathcal{C}$} is a semigroup object $X : \mathrm{N}(\Delta_{\mathrm{semi}}^{\mathrm{op}}) \to \mathcal{C}$ that extends to a functor $\tilde{X} : \mathrm{N}(\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}) \to \mathcal{C}$ out of the full simplex category, satisfying the Segal conditions. 

    Equivalently, under the operadic description, the forgetful functor from the associative $\infty$-operad to the non-unital associative $\infty$-operad induces a conservative, fully faithful functor on the subcategory of unit-admitting algebras:
    $$\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{A}ss}(\mathcal{C}) \to \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{A}ss^{\mathrm{nu}}}(\mathcal{C})$$
    A monoid object is precisely a semigroup object that lies in the essential image of this forgetful functor.
\end{definition}

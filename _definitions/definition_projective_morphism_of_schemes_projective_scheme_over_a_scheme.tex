\begin{definition} \label{definition:projective_morphism_of_schemes_projective_scheme_over_a_scheme}
Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme}. 
A \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:morphism_of_schemes}{morphism of schemes} $f: X \to S$ is called a \hldef{projective morphism} if it factors as a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:open_and_closed_immersions_of_schemes}{closed immersion} $i: X \hookrightarrow \mathbb{P}^n_S$ (\Cref{definition:projective_space_of_dimension_n_over_a_scheme}) followed by the canonical projection $\pi: \mathbb{P}^n_S \to S$ for some $n \in \mathbb{N}$. In this case, $X$ is said to be a \hldef{projective scheme over $S$}.
\end{definition}

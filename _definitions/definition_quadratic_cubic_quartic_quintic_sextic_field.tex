\begin{definition} \label{definition:quadratic_cubic_quartic_quintic_sextic_field}
    In number theory, a \hldef{quadratic/cubic/quartic/quintic/sextic field} or more generally a \hldef{degree $n$ field} refers to an extension field of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:degree_of_a_field_extension}{degree} $n$ over $\bbQ$; in particular, it is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:number_field}{number field}. 
\end{definition}

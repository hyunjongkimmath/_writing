\begin{definition} \label{definition:domain_integral_domain}
    A non-zero \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring} with no nontrivial zero divisors is called a \hldef{domain}.
    A \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:domain_integral_domain} that it also a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:commutative_ring}{commutative ring} is also called an \hldef{integral domain}.
\end{definition}

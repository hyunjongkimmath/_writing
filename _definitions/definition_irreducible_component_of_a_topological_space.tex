\begin{definition} \label{definition:irreducible_component_of_a_topological_space}
Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_space}{topological space}. A \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_space}{closed subset} $Z \subseteq X$ is called an \hldef{irreducible component of $X$} if it is a maximal \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:irreducible_topological_space}{irreducible} closed subset in the sense that there exists a non-empty irreducible closed subset $Y_0 \subseteq Z$, and if $Z' \supseteq Z$ is any irreducible closed subset, then $Z' = Z$.

% Equivalently, an irreducible component of $X$ is a closed subset which is maximal with respect to the property of being an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:irreducible_topological_space}{irreducible topological space} under inclusion.
\end{definition}

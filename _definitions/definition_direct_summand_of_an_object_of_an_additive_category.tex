
\begin{definition} \label{definition:direct_summand_of_an_object_of_an_additive_category}
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:additive_category_preadditive_category}{additive category} and $X \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$. A \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:subcategory_of_a_category}{subobject} $i: Y \hookrightarrow X$ is called a \hldef{direct summand of $X$} if there exists a morphism $p: X \to Y$ such that $p \circ i = \mathrm{id}_Y$. In this case, $X$ is isomorphic to the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:additive_category_preadditive_category}{direct sum} $Y \oplus Z$ for some object $Z$, with $i$ the canonical inclusion.
\end{definition}

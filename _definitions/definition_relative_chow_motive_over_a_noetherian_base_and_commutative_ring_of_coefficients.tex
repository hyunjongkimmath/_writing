Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{Noetherian} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{base scheme} and $R$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:commutative_ring}{commutative ring} of coefficients. A \hldef{(relative) Chow motive over $S$ with coefficients in $R$} is a triple $(X, p, n)$, where $X$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:smooth_morphism_of_schemes}{smooth} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:projective_morphism_of_schemes_projective_scheme_over_a_scheme}{projective scheme} over $S$, $p \in \mathrm{CH}_{\dim(X/S)}(X \times_S X) \otimes R$ (\Cref{definition:relative_chow_ring_of_a_smooth_projective_scheme_over_a_noetherian_base})\CrefIfExists{definition:cartesian_product_of_two_objects_in_a_category_over_an_object} is an idempotent element (called a \hldef{projector)} such that $p \circ p = p$, and $n \in \mathbb{Z}$ is called the \hldef{twist} or \hldef{Tate twist}.

A \hldef{Chow motive} typically refers to a Chow motive where $S = \Spec k$\CrefIfExists{definition:affine_scheme} for some \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field} $k$.

Chow motives for $R = \bbZ$ are said to be \hldef{integral}, Chow motives for $R = \bbQ$ are said to be \hldef{rational}, etc.
\end{definition}

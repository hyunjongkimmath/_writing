    Let $(R, \lambda)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_ring_with_respect_to_an_ideal}{complete} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{DVR} such that $R/\lambda^n$ is finite for all $n \geq 1$. Let $X$ be a scheme of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_type_morphism_of_schemes}{finite type} over a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field} $k$ that is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:separably_closed_field}{separably closed} or \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_field}{finite}. Let $\mathbb{D}_X(-): \Dbc(X, K) \to \Dbc(X, K)$ denote the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:verdier_dual_of_an_object_in_the_bdd_constr_derived_category_of_adic_sheaves_with_algebraic_coefficients_on_a_finite_type_scheme_over_a_noetherian_scheme}{Verdier dual functor} on $X$ relative to $\Spec k$.

    The \hldef{self-dual (middle) perverse $t$-structure on $\Dbc(X, K)$} is the pair of strictly full subcategories $(\pDleqzero(X, K), \pDgeqzero(X, K))$ of $\Dbc(X, K)$ defined by

    \hlalign{
    \begin{align*}
        \pDleqzero(X, K) &:= \left\{ K' \in \Dbc(X, K) : \dim \Supp\bigl(\mathscr{H}^{-i}(K')\bigr) \leq i \text{ for all } i \in \bbZ \right\}, \\
        \pDgeqzero(X, K) &:= \left\{ K' \in \Dbc(X, K) : \dim \Supp\bigl(\mathscr{H}^{-i}(\mathbb{D}_X(K'))\bigr) \leq i \text{ for all } i \in \bbZ \right\},
    \end{align*}
    }

    This is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:t_structure_on_a_triangulated_category}{$t$-structure} on $\Dbc(X, K)$ \CrefIfExists{theorem:perverse_t_structure_on_bdd_constructible_derived_category_with_algebraic_coefficients_on_a_finite_type_scheme_over_a_separably_closed_or_finite_field_is_a_t_structure}. Its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:t_structure_on_a_triangulated_category}{core}, denoted by \hl{$\Perv(X, K)$}, is called the \hldef{category of perverse sheaves with algebraic coefficients on $X$}.

    We say that $B \in \Dbc(X, K)$ is \hldef{semiperverse} if $B \in \pDleqzero(X)$.

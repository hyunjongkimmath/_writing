Let $n$ be a non-negative integer. A \hldef{complete graph on $n$ vertices}, denoted by \hl{$K_n$}, is a simple graph with $|V(K_n)| = n$ such that every pair of distinct vertices is adjacent. That is,
$$E(K_n) = \{\{u,v\} : u,v \in V(K_n), u \neq v\}.$$

An \hldef{empty graph on $n$ vertices}, denoted by \hl{$\overline{K_n}$}, is a simple graph with $|V(\overline{K_n})| = n$ and $E(\overline{K_n}) = \varnothing$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:semisimple_ring}
A \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring} $R$ is a \hldef{semisimple ring} if it is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:simple_irreducible_semisimple_module_over_a_ring}{semisimple} as a left \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:module_of_a_ring}{module} over itself. 

By the Wedderburn-Artin theorem, this is equivalent to $R$ being isomorphic to a finite direct product of matrix rings over division rings:
$$R \cong \prod_{i=1}^k M_{n_i}(D_i)$$
where each $D_i$ is a division ring.
\end{definition}

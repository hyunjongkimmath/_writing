    Let $(R, \lambda)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_ring_with_respect_to_an_ideal}{complete} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{DVR} such that $R/\lambda^n$ is finite for all $n \geq 1$. Let $E$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_fractions_of_an_integral_domain}{fraction field} of $R$. Let $X$ be a scheme of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_type_morphism_of_schemes}{finite type} over a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field} $k$ that is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:separably_closed_field}{separably closed} or \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_field}{finite}. Consider sheaves on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{(small) \'etale site} of $X$.

    The category \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:derived_category_of_cohomologically_bounded_constructible_sheaves_with_rational_adic_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}{$\Dbc(X, E)$} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:triangulated_category}{triangulated category} (\Cref{corollary:bdd_constructible_derived_category_with_rational_coefficients_is_triangulated}). Let $\mathbb{D}_X(-): \Dbc(X, E) \to \Dbc(X, E)$ denote the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:verdier_dual_of_an_object_in_the_bdd_constr_derived_category_of_adic_sheaves_with_rational_coefficients_on_a_finite_type_scheme_over_a_noetherian_scheme}{Verdier dual functor} on $X$ relative to $\Spec k$.

    The \hldef{self-dual (middle) perverse $t$-structure on $\Dbc(X, E)$} is the pair of strictly full subcategories $(\pDleqzero(X, E), \pDgeqzero(X, E))$ of $\Dbc(X, E)$ defined by

    \hlalign{
    \begin{align*}
        \pDleqzero(X, E) &:= \left\{ K \in \Dbc(X, E) : \dim \Supp\bigl(\mathscr{H}^{-i}(K)\bigr) \leq i \text{ for all } i \in \bbZ \right\}, \\
        \pDgeqzero(X, E) &:= \left\{ K \in \Dbc(X, E) : \dim \Supp\bigl(\mathscr{H}^{-i}(\mathbb{D}_X(K))\bigr) \leq i \text{ for all } i \in \bbZ \right\},
    \end{align*}
    }

    where $\dim$ denotes the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dimension_of_a_scheme}{Krull dimension} of closed subsets of $X$.

    This is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:t_structure_on_a_triangulated_category}{$t$-structure} on $\Dbc(X, E)$. Its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:t_structure_on_a_triangulated_category}{core}, denoted by \hl{$\Perv(X, E)$}, is called the \hldef{category of perverse sheaves with rational coefficients on $X$}.
\end{definition}

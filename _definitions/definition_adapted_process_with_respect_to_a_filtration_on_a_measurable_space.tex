\begin{definition}[Adapted process] \label{definition:adapted_process_with_respect_to_a_filtration_on_a_measurable_space}
Let $(X, \{\mathcal{F}_t\}_{t \in T})$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:filtration_on_a_measurable_space}{filtered measurable space}, where $T$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:total_order_linear_order_on_a_set}{totally ordered set}. Let $(S, \Sigma)$ be a measurable space.

A collection of functions $\{Y_t : X \to S\}_{t \in T}$ indexed by $T$ is called an \hldef{adapted process with respect to $\{\mathcal{F}_t\}_{t \in T}}$ if each $Y_t$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:measurable_function_from_a_measure_space_into_the_extended_real_line_or_complex_plane}{measurable function} from $(X, \mathcal{F}_t)$ to $(S, \Sigma)$, i.e., $Y_t^{-1}(E) \in \mathcal{F}_t$ for every $E \in \Sigma$ and every $t \in T$.
\end{definition}

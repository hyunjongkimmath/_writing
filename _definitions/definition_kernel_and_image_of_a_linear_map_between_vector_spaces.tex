\begin{definition} \label{definition:kernel_and_image_of_a_linear_map_between_vector_spaces}
    Let $F$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}. Let $V$ and $W$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:vector_space_over_a_field}{$F$-vector spaces}, and let $T: V \to W$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:morphism_of_vector_spaces}{linear map}.

    The \hldef{kernel of $T$} is the subspace of $V$ defined by
    $$\hlin{\ker(T) = \{ v \in V : T(v) = 0_W \}}.$$

    The \hldef{image of $T$} (or \hldef{range of $T$}) is the subspace of $W$ defined by
    $$\hlin{\operatorname{im}(T) = \{ T(v) : v \in V \}}.$$

\TextIfExists{definition:kernel_image_cokernel_of_a_module_homomorphism_over_rings}{Note that since every $F$-vector space is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:bimodule_over_rings_module_over_a_ring}{$F$-module} and every linear map is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:homomorphism_of_modules_over_a_ring}{module homomorphism}, the kernel and image here are special cases of \Cref{definition:kernel_image_cokernel_of_a_module_homomorphism_over_rings}.}

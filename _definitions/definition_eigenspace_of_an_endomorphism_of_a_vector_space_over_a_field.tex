\begin{definition} \label{definition:eigenspace_of_an_endomorphism_of_a_vector_space_over_a_field}
Let $F$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}, let $V$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:vector_space_over_a_field}{$F$-vector space}, and let $\phi : V \to V$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:morphism_of_vector_spaces}{$F$-linear endomorphism} of $V$.
For each $\lambda \in F$, the \hldef{eigenspace of $\phi$ corresponding to $\lambda$} is the subspace
$\hlin{E_\lambda(\phi) = \ker(\phi - \lambda \cdot \operatorname{id}_V) = \{v \in V \mid \phi(v) = \lambda v\}}.$
If $\lambda$ is not an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:eigenvalue_and_eigenvector_of_an_endomorphism_of_a_vector_space_over_a_field}{eigenvalue} of $\phi$, then $E_\lambda(\phi) = \{0\}$; otherwise $E_\lambda(\phi)$ is a nonzero subspace of $V$ consisting of all eigenvectors corresponding to $\lambda$, together with the zero vector.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:combinatorial_choose_function}
    Let $n, k$ be nonnegative integers with $0 \leq k \leq n$. The \hldef{binomial coefficient}, also called the \hldef{combinatorial choose function} and denoted by \hl{$\binom{n}{k}$}, is defined as
    $$\binom{n}{k} := \frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}$$
    where $n!$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:factorial_of_a_nonnegative_integer}{factorial of $n$}. If $k < 0$ or $k > n$, then $\binom{n}{k} := 0$.

    Combinatorially, $\binom{n}{k}$ counts the number of subsets of cardinality $k$ of a set with $n$ elements.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:category_of_1_morphisms_between_fibered_categories_over_a_category}
Let $\mathcal{F}$ and $\mathcal{G}$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:fibered_category_over_a_category}{fibered categories} over a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category}{category} $\mathcal{C}$. 
The \hldef{category of $1$-morphisms or the morphism category from $\calF$ to $\calG$} is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category}{category} \hl{$\text{HOM}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G})$} whose objects are \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:morphism_of_fibered_categories_over_a_category}{morphisms of fibered categories} from $\mathcal{F}$ to $\mathcal{G}$ and whose morphisms are \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:base_preserving_natural_transformation_between_morphisms_of_fibered_categories}{base preserving natural transformations}.
\end{definition}

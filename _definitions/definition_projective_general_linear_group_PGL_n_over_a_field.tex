\begin{definition} \label{definition:projective_general_linear_group_PGL_n_over_a_field}
Let $n \geq 1$ be an integer and let $k$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}. The \hldef{projective general linear group} \hl{$\operatorname{PGL}_n(k)$} is the quotient group
$$\operatorname{PGL}_n(k) := \operatorname{GL}_n(k) / Z(\operatorname{GL}_n(k))$$

where $Z(\operatorname{GL}_n(k)) \cong k^\times$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:center_of_a_group}{center} of $\operatorname{GL}_n(k)$, consisting of nonzero scalar matrices. \end{definition}

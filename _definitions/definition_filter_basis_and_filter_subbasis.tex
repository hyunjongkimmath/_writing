\begin{definition}[Filter basis and filter subbasis] \label{definition:filter_basis_and_filter_subbasis}

    Let $S$ be a nonempty set. A nonempty collection $\mathcal{B}$ of subsets of $S$ is called a \hldef{filter basis on $S$} if for any $A, B \in \mathcal{B}$, there exists $C \in \mathcal{B}$ such that $C \subseteq A \cap B$. The filter generated by $\mathcal{B}$ is the collection
    $$\mathcal{F} = \{\, F \subseteq S \mid \exists B \in \mathcal{B},\; B \subseteq F \,\}.$$
    Every filter basis generates a unique filter, and every filter has many different bases.

    A nonempty collection $\mathcal{S}$ of subsets of $S$ is called a \hldef{filter subbasis on $S$} if the collection of all finite intersections of members of $\mathcal{S}$ forms a filter basis on $S$. The filter generated by $\mathcal{S}$ is defined to be the filter generated by this filter basis.
\end{definition}

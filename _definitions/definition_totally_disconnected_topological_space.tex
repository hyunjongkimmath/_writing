\begin{definition} \label{definition:totally_disconnected_topological_space}
Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category_of_topological_spaces}{topological space}. $X$ is \hldef{totally disconnected} if the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:connected_component_of_a_point_of_a_topological_space}{connected component} of every \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:point_of_a_topos}{point} $x \in X$ consists only of the singleton set $\{x\}$.
\end{definition}

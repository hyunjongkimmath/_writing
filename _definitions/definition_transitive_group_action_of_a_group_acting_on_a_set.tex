\begin{definition}[Transitive group action] \label{definition:transitive_group_action_of_a_group_acting_on_a_set}
Let $(G,\cdot)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:left_and_right_group_actions_on_a_set}{acting on the left} a set $X$. The action is called \hldef{transitive} if for any two elements $x, y \in X$, there exists $g \in G$ such that
$$g \cdot x = y.$$
Equivalently, $X$ consists of a single \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:orbit_of_a_group_action}{orbit} under the action of $G$.
\end{definition}

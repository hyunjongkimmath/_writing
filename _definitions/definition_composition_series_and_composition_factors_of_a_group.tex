\begin{definition} \label{definition:composition_series_and_composition_factors_of_a_group}
Let $G$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group}. A \hldef{composition series of~$G$} is a finite chain of subgroups
$$\hlin{1 = G_0 \triangleleft G_1 \triangleleft \cdots \triangleleft G_k = G}$$
such that each $G_i$ is a maximal proper normal subgroup of $G_{i+1}$ (equivalently, each factor group $G_{i+1}/G_i$ is a simple group). The factor groups
$$G_{i+1}/G_i \quad (0 \leq i < k)$$
are called the \hldef{composition factors of~$G$} (with respect to this composition series).
\end{definition}

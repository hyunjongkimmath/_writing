\begin{definition}[Unique factorization domain (UFD) Factorial ring] \label{definition:unique_factorization_domain}
An \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:domain_integral_domain}{integral domain} $R$ is called a \hldef{unique factorization domain (UFD)} or \hldef{factorial ring} if every nonzero \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:unit_of_a_ring}{nonunit element} of $R$ can be factored as a product of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:irreducible_element_of_a_ring}{irreducible elements} uniquely up to order and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:unit_of_a_ring}{units}.
\end{definition}

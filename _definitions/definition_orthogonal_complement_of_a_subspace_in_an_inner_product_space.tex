\begin{definition} \label{definition:orthogonal_complement_of_a_subspace_in_an_inner_product_space}
    Let $V$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:inner_product_on_a_vector_space_over_the_reals_or_complex_numbers}{inner product space}, and let $W$ be a subspace of $V$.

    The \hldef{orthogonal complement of $W$} is the subspace
    $$\hlin{W^{\perp} = \{ v \in V : \langle v, w \rangle = 0 \text{ for all } w \in W \}}.$$

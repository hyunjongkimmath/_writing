\begin{definition} \label{definition:power_series_in_one_complex_variable_and_its_radius_of_convergence_given_by_the_cauchy_hadamard_formula}
Let $(a_n)_{n=0}^\infty$ be a sequence of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_complex_numbers}{complex numbers}.
The \hldef{power series centered at $z_0 \in \mathbb{C}$ of this sequence} is the expression
$$\hlin{\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n (z - z_0)^n.}$$
Its \hldef{radius of convergence} $R \in [0, \infty]$ is given by the Cauchy--Hadamard formula
\Cref{proposition:cauchy_hadamard_formula_for_power_series_in_one_complex_variable}.
The series
\CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:absolute_convergence_of_a_series_in_module_over_a_commutative_ring_with_an_absolute_value_valued_in_an_ordered_semiring}{converges absolutely}
and
\CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:uniform_convergence_of_a_series_of_functions_from_a_set_to_an_extended_metric_space_valued_in_ordered_commutative_monoid_with_adjoined_infinity}{uniformly}
on every closed disk $\overline{D}(z_0, r)$ with $r < R$ by
\Cref{proposition:power_series_in_one_complex_variable_on_closed_disks_inside_its_radius_of_convergence_converges_absolutely_and_uniformly},
and diverges for every $z$ with $|z - z_0| > R$ by
\Cref{proposition:cauchy_hadamard_formula_for_power_series_in_one_complex_variable}.
\end{definition}

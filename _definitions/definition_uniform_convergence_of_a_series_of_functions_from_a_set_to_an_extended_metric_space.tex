\begin{definition}[Uniform convergence of a series of functions] \label{definition:uniform_convergence_of_a_series_of_functions_from_a_set_to_an_extended_metric_space}
Let $T$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ordered_semiring}{ordered semiring}, let $T^\infty$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ordered_semiring_with_adjoined_infinity}{ordered semiring with adjoined infinity} obtained from $T$, let $X$ be a set, and let $(Y, d)$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:extended_metric_on_a_set_valued_in_an_ordered_semiring_with_adjoined_infinity}{extended metric space valued in $T^\infty$} such that $Y$ is also an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_group}{abelian group} equipped with a topology making it a topological abelian group. Let $(f_n)_{n=1}^\infty$ be a sequence of functions from $X$ to $Y$.

For each $N \geq 1$, let $S_N : X \to Y$ be the partial sum function defined by $S_N(x) = \sum_{n=1}^N f_n(x)$. The series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty f_n$ is said to \hldef{converge uniformly on $X$} if the sequence of functions $(S_N)_{N=1}^\infty$ \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:uniform_convergence_of_a_sequence_of_functions_from_a_set_to_an_extended_metric_space}{converges uniformly on $X$}. If it does, the uniform limit function is called the \hldef{uniform sum of the series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty f_n$}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Compactly supported $\lambda$-adic cohomology with algebraic coefficients] \label{definition:compactly_supported_lambda_adic_cohomology_groups_with_algebraic_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}
    Let $(R, \lambda)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_ring_with_respect_to_an_ideal}{complete} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{DVR} such that $R/\lambda^n$ is finite for all $n \geq 1$. Let $E = \Frac(R)$, and let $K/E$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebraic_element_minimal_polynomial_of_an_algebraic_element_algebraic_field_extension_transcendental_element_field_extension}{algebraic extension}. Let $k$ be a field. Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{noetherian} scheme over $k$, with structure morphism $f \coloneq f_X \colon X \to \Spec(k)$, such that $f$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:compactifiable_over_S}{$\Spec(k)$-compactifiable}. Consider sheaves on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{(small) \'etale site $X_{\et}$}.

    Let $P \in \Dbc(X, K)$ be an object of the bounded derived category with algebraic adic coefficients. By definition, $P$ is represented by some object $N \in \Dbc(X, L)$ for a finite extension $L/E$ inside $K$. The \hldef{compactly supported (algebraic) $\lambda$-adic cohomology groups of $X$ with coefficients in $P$} are the $K$-vector spaces:
    $$\hlin{H_c^q(X, P) \coloneq H_c^q(X, N) \otimes_L K}$$
    for each $q \in \mathbb{Z}$, where $H_c^q(X, N)$ denotes the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:compactly_supported_lambda_adic_cohomology_groups_with_rational_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}{compactly supported $\lambda$-adic cohomology groups with rational coefficients} for the finite extension $L$. This is independent of the choice of representative $N$ and finite subextension $L$.

    Equivalently, $H_c^q(X, P)$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:direct_limit_of_a_diagram_in_a_category}{direct limit} of the systems $H_c^q(X, N_{E'})$ as $E'$ ranges over finite subextensions of $K/E$, where each transition map is induced by the base change functor $\Dbc(X, E') \to \Dbc(X, E'')$ for $E' \subseteq E''$.
\end{definition}

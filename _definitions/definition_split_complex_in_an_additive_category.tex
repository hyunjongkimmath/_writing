\begin{definition} \label{definition:split_complex_in_an_additive_category}
    Let $\calA$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:additive_category_preadditive_category}{additive category}. 
    A chain or \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cochain_complex_of_objects_in_an_additive_category}{cochain complex} $C$ in $\calA$ is said to be \hldef{split} if there are maps $s = \{s_n: C_n \to C_{n+1}\}_n$ (or $s = \{s^n: C_n \to C^{n-1}\}_n$) such that $d = dsd$. The maps $s_n$ are called \hldef{splitting maps}.
    The complex $C$ is said to be \hldef{split exact} if it is split and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:acyclic_complex_of_objects_in_an_abelian_category}{acyclic}.
\end{definition}

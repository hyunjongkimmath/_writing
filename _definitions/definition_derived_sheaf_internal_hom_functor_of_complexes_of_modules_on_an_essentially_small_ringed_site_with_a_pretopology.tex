\begin{definition}[cf. {\cite[Tag 08J7]{stacks-project}} for a discussion when $\calO$ is assumed commutative] \label{definition:derived_sheaf_internal_hom_functor_of_complexes_of_modules_on_an_essentially_small_ringed_site_with_a_pretopology}
    Let $(\calC, J, \calO)$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:essentially_small_site}{essentially small} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ringed_site}{ringed site} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{with a pretopology}. \TODO{the ``pretopology'' part should be loosened}

    The \hldef{derived (sheafy/sheaf) internal hom functor} is the functor \hl{$R\mathcal{H}om: D(\calO)^{\op} \times D(\calO) \to D(\Sh(\calC, J; \bbZ))$} (\Cref{notation:derived_categories_of_O_modules_on_an_essentially_small_site_ringed_site}), also denoted by notations such as \hl{$R\mathcal{H}om(\calM, \calN)$}, \hl{$R\mathscr{H}om(\calM, \calN)$}, or \hl{$R\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\calM, \calN)$}, defined as the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:right_left_derived_multifunctor_of_an_additive_multifunctor_between_abelian_categories}{right derived functor} of the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:sheaf_hom_for_sheaves_of_modules_over_sheaves_of_rings_on_a_site_with_a_small_topologically_generating_family}{sheaf hom functor}
    $$\mathcal{H}om: \Mod_\calO^{\op} \times \Mod_\calO \to \Sh(\calC, J; \bbZ). $$

    Explicitly, given objects $L,M \in D(\calO)$, $R\mathcal{H}om(L,M)$ is given as follows: let $\calL^\bullet$ be any complex of $\calO$-modules representing $L$ and let $\calI^\bullet$ be any (right) \CrefAndHyperref{definition:K_injective_projective_complex_in_an_abelian_category}{$K$-injective} \CrefAndHyperref{definition:left_right_resolution_of_a_chain_complex_in_an_abelian_category}{resolution} of $M$, which exists by \Cref{theorem:category_of_modules_over_an_essentially_small_ringed_site_has_enough_K_injectives}. The object $R\mathcal{H}om(L,M)$ is then given as the object of $D(\Sh(\calC, J; \bbZ))$ represented by the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:sheaf_internal_hom_functor_of_complexes_of_modules_on_an_essentially_small_ringed_site_with_a_pretopology}{internal sheaf hom object} $\calH om^\bullet(\calL^\bullet, \calI^\bullet)$.
\end{definition}

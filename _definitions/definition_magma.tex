\begin{definition}[Magma] \label{definition:magma}
A \hldef{magma} is a pair $(M, *)$ consisting of a set $M$ and a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:n_ary_operation_on_a_set}{binary operation}
$$* : M \times M \to M,$$
called the \hldef{magma operation}. 

No axioms (such as \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:associative_binary_operation_on_a_set}{associativity}, \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:commutative_binary_operation_on_a_set}{commutativity}, \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:left_right_identity_element_of_a_binary_operation_on_a_set}{identity}) are assumed.
\end{definition}

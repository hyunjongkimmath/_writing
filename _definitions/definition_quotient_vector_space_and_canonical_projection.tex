\begin{definition} \label{definition:quotient_vector_space_and_canonical_projection}
    Let $F$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}, let $V$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:vector_space_over_a_field}{$F$-vector space}, and let $W$ be a subspace of $V$.

    The \hldef{quotient vector space} of $V$ by $W$ is the set of cosets
    $$\hlin{V/W = \{ v + W : v \in V \}}$$
    equipped with addition and scalar multiplication defined by
    $$(v_1 + W) + (v_2 + W) = (v_1 + v_2) + W, \quad c \cdot (v + W) = (c \cdot v) + W.$$

    The map $\pi: V \to V/W$ defined by $\pi(v) = v + W$ is called the \hldef{canonical projection onto $V/W$}.

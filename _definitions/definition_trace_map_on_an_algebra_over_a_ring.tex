\begin{definition} \label{definition:trace_map_on_an_algebra_over_a_ring}
Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring}. Let $A$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebra_of_a_ring}{$R$-algebra}.
% Let $M$ be an (left or right) \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:bimodule_over_rings_module_over_a_ring}{$R$-module}. 
A \hldef{trace map on $A$} is any $R$-module homomorphism 
$$\tau: A \to T$$
that has the ``basic trace property'', i.e. that 
$$\tau(\mu \alpha) = \tau(\alpha \mu)$$
for all $\mu, \alpha\in A$.

In particular, note that the notion of trace map on $\End_R(M)$ makes sense.
\end{definition}

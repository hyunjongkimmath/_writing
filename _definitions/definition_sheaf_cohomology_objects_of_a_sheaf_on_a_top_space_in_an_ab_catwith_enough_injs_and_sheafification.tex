\begin{definition} \label{definition:sheaf_cohomology_objects_of_a_sheaf_on_a_top_space_in_an_ab_catwith_enough_injs_and_sheafification}
    Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_space}{topological space}. Let $\calA$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_category}{abelian category} with \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:has_enough_injectives_or_projectives_for_an_abelian_category}{enough injectives} and such that a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheafification_of_a_presheaf_on_a_topological_space}{sheafification functor}
    $$a: \PreShv(X, \calA) \to \Shv(X, \calA) $$
    exists. In particular, \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_on_a_topological_space_valued_in_a_category_with_a_terminal_object}{$\Shv(X, \calA)$} is\CrefIfExists{proposition:circumstances_under_which_categories_of_sheaves_and_presheaves_on_a_topological_space_valued_in_an_abelian_category_are_abelian} an abelian category and $\Gamma(U,-): \Shv(X, \calA) \to \calA$ is\CrefIfExists{proposition:sections_functors_on_presheaves_on_a_topological_space_valued_in_an_abelian_category_are_left_exact} a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:exact_functor_between_abelian_categories}{left exact functor} for all \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_space}{open} subsets $U \subseteq X$. In particular, these conditions are satisfied for $\calA = \Ab$\CrefIfExists{theorem:examples_of_abelian_categories_with_enough_injectives_or_projectives}\CrefIfExists{theorem:sheafification_for_sheaves_of_sets_on_a_topological_space_exist}. 

    Let $\calF \in \Shv(X, \calA)$ and let $U \subseteq X$ be an open subset. For each integer $n \geq 0$, the \hldef{$n$th (abelian) sheaf cohomology object of $\calF$ of sections over $U$} is 
    $$\hlin{H^n(U, \calF) \coloneq (R^n \Gamma(U, -))(\calF)}$$
    where $R^n \Gamma(U,-)$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:left_right_derived_functors_of_a_right_left_exact_functor_between_abelian_categories_where_source_has_enough_projectives_injectives}{$n$th right derived functor} of the sections functor $\Gamma(U,-)$. In the case that $U = X$, this sheaf cohomology object may be called the \hldef{global sheaf cohomology object}. The sheaf cohomology objects $H^n(U,\calF)$ for $n \geq 1$ are often called the \hldef{higher sheaf cohomology objects of $\calF$ with sections over $U$}.

    In the case that $X$ is equipped with a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_on_a_site}{sheaf} $\calO_X$ of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{rings}, i.e. $(X,\calO_X)$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ringed_space}{ringed space} and $\calF \in \Mod(\calO_X)$\CrefIfExists{definition:module_over_a_sheaf_of_rings_on_a_ringed_space}, we might define $H^n(U,\calF)$ in either of the following ways:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item as the $n$th right derived functor of $\Gamma(U,-): \Mod(\calO_X) \to \Ab$ via (right) \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:left_right_resolution_of_an_object_by_a_class_of_objects_in_an_abelian_category}{resolutions} of $\calF$ by \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:injective_and_projective_objects_in_a_category}{injective} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:module_over_a_sheaf_of_rings_on_a_ringed_space}{$\Mod(\calO_X)$}-\CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:module_over_a_sheaf_of_rings_on_a_site}{modules}. 
        \item as the $n$th right derived functor of $\Gamma(U,-): \Shv(X, \Ab) \to \Ab$ via (right) resolutions of $\calF$ by \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:injective_and_projective_objects_in_a_category}{injective objects} of $\Shv(X, \Ab)$. 
    \end{enumerate}
    \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{corollary:A1_model_structure_on_simplicial_nisnevich_sheaves_over_a_noetherian_finite_dimensional_scheme_is_an_instance_of_an_I_model_structure}{It turns out} the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{theorem:fans_of_a_real_fin_dim_vector_space_correspond_to_normal_separated_toric_varieties_over_an_algebraicaly_closed_field}{corresponding} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_cohomology_group_of_a_sheaf_of_modules_over_a_sheaf_of_rings_on_a_site}{sheaf cohomology} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{groups} defined in these two ways are \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:natural_transformation_between_functors_between_categories}{naturally isomorphic}.

\begin{definition} \label{definition:index_of_a_normal_fano_variety}

    Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:normal_and_factorial_scheme}{normal} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:fano_variety_over_a_field}{Fano variety} over an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebraically_closed_field}{algebraically closed field}. 

    The \hldef{index of $X$} is the largest integer $r$ such that $-K_X \sim rH$ for some ample Cartier divisor $H$ in the Picard group $\text{Pic}(X)$.

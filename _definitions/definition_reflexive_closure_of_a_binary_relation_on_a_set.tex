\begin{definition} \label{definition:reflexive_closure_of_a_binary_relation_on_a_set}
Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:n_ary_relation_on_sets}{binary relation} on a set $X$. The \hldef{reflexive closure of $R$} is the binary relation $R^{\mathrm{refl}}$ on $X$ defined by
$$
R^{\mathrm{refl}} = R \cup \Delta_X,
$$
where $\Delta_X = \{ (x, x) \mid x \in X \}$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:diagonal_relation_on_a_set}{diagonal relation} on $X$. Equivalently, $R^{\mathrm{refl}}$ is the smallest reflexive binary relation on $X$ containing $R$.

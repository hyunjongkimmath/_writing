\begin{definition} \label{definition:algebraic_monodromy_group_at_a_prime_for_a_compatible_system_of_reps_on_a_prof_group_with_dense_frob_elements}
Let $G$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:profinite_group}{profinite group} endowed with a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dense_subset_of_a_topological_space}{dense subset} of "Frobenius" elements $\{F_\alpha\}_{\alpha \in A}$. Let $\{ \rho_\ell: G \to \mathrm{GL}_n(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell) \}_{\ell \in L}$ be a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:compatible_system_of_representations_on_a_profinite_group_equipped_with_dense_frobenius_elements}{compatible system} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:continuous_representation_of_a_topological_group_on_a_topological_module_over_a_topological_ring}{continuous representations} indexed by a set of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:prime_number}{rational primes} $L$. 

The \hldef{algebraic monodromy group of $\{\rho_\ell\}$ at $\ell$} is the Zariski closure \hl{$G_\ell$} of $\rho_\ell(G)$ in $\GL_n(\overline{\bbQ}_\ell)$; if $\rho_\ell$ is a homomorphism into $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{Q}_\ell)$ instead, then the \hldef{algebraic monodromy group of $\{\rho_\ell\}$ at $\ell$} is taken to be the Zariski closure \hl{$G_\ell$} of $\rho_\ell(G)$ in $\GL_n(\bbQ_\ell)$ instead.
\end{definition}

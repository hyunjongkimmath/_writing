\begin{definition}[Limits in Sheaf Categories] \label{definition:limit_in_a_sheaf_category}
    \TODO{verify}
    Let $(\calC, J)$ be a site and $\calA$ a category admitting limits of shape $I$. The category $\Shv(\calC, J, \calA)$ is closed under $I$-indexed limits in $\PreShv(\calC, \calA)$. Specifically, for any diagram $F: I \to \Shv(\calC, J, \calA)$, the limit exists in the category of sheaves and is preserved by the natural inclusion $i: \Shv(\calC, J, \calA) \hookrightarrow \PreShv(\calC, \calA)$:
    \[ \varprojlim_{\Shv} F \cong \varprojlim_{\PreShv} i \circ F \]
\end{definition}

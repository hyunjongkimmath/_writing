\begin{definition} \label{definition:quadratic_twist_of_an_elliptic_curve_over_a_field}

Let $k$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field} with $\operatorname{char}(k) \neq 2$. Let $E/k$ be a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:elliptic_curve_over_a_scheme}{elliptic curve} given by the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:weierstrass_equation_over_a_ring}{general Weierstrass equation}
    $$ y^2 + a_1 x y + a_3 y = x^3 + a_2 x^2 + a_4 x + a_6, $$
    with $a_1, a_2, a_3, a_4, a_6 \in k$.

    For any $d \in k^\times$, the \hldef{quadratic twist of $E$ by $d$}, denoted by \hl{$E^{(d)}$}, is the elliptic curve over $k$ given by the Weierstrass equation
    $$ y^2 + a_1 x y - d\, a_3 y = x^3 - d\, a_2 x^2 - d^2\, a_4 x - d^3\, a_6, $$
    obtained by the change of variables $x = d\, x'$, $y = d^{3/2}\, y'$ (valid over $k(\sqrt{d})$).

    The curves $E$ and $E^{(d)}$ become isomorphic over $k(\sqrt{d})$ via the isomorphism
    $$ \varphi: E^{(d)} \to E_{\overline{k(\sqrt{d})}}, \qquad (x', y') \mapsto (d\, x', \sqrt{d}\, y'). $$

    If $d \in (k^\times)^2$, then $E^{(d)} \cong E$ over $k$. If $d \notin (k^\times)^2$, then $E^{(d)}$ is not isomorphic to $E$ over $k$, but the two curves share the same \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:j_invariant_of_a_general_weierstrass_equation_with_invertible_discriminant}{$j$-invariant}.

    In the special case of a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:weierstrass_equation_over_a_ring}{short Weierstrass equation} $y^2 = x^3 + A x + B$, the quadratic twist by $d \in k^\times$ is
    $$ y^2 = x^3 + d^2 A\, x + d^3 B, $$
    or equivalently (after absorbing a square factor) the curve
    $$ d\, y^2 = x^3 + A x + B. $$
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Unreduced suspension] \label{definition:unreduced_suspension_of_a_topological_space}
    Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_space}{topological space}. The \hldef{unreduced suspension of $X$}, denoted by \hl{$SX$}, is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:quotient_topology}{quotient space} of the product $X \times [0,1]$ obtained by collapsing $X \times \{0\}$ to a single point and $X \times \{1\}$ to another (distinct) single point.
    \[
    SX \coloneqq (X \times [0,1]) / \sim, \quad \text{where } (x,0) \sim (x',0) \text{ and } (x,1) \sim (x',1) \text{ for all } x, x' \in X.
    \]
    Geometrically, $SX$ consists of two cones on $X$ glued along their common base $X \times \{1/2\}$.

Let $(R, \lambda)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_ring_with_respect_to_an_ideal}{complete} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{DVR} such that $R/\lambda^n$ is finite for all $n \geq 1$. Let $E$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_fractions_of_an_integral_domain}{fraction field} of $R$, and let $K/E$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebraic_element_minimal_polynomial_of_an_algebraic_element_algebraic_field_extension_transcendental_element_field_extension}{algebraic extension}. Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme}. Let $X$ and $Y$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{noetherian} $S$-schemes, and consider sheaves on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{(small) \'etale sites} of $X$ and $Y$. Let $f: X \to Y$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:compactification_of_a_morphism_of_schemes}{$S$-compactifiable morphism}.

The \hldef{canonical forgets support map from direct image with compact support to direct image for \'etale sheaves with algebraic adic coefficients on compactifiable morphisms} is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:natural_transformation_between_functors_between_categories}{natural transformation}
$$\hlin{\alpha_f: f_! \Rightarrow f_*}$$
where $f_!: \Sh(X, K) \to \Sh(Y, K)$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:direct_image_functor_with_compact_support_on_etale_sheaves_with_algebraic_adic_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}{direct image functor with compact support on \'etale sheaves with algebraic adic coefficients} and $f_*: \Sh(X, K) \to \Sh(Y, K)$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:direct_image_functor_on_etale_sheaves_with_algebraic_adic_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}{direct image functor on \'etale sheaves with algebraic adic coefficients}.

It is defined as the colimit of the canonical forgets support maps for rational adic coefficients. For each finite extension $E'$ of $E$ inside $K$, let $R'$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:integral_element_over_a_ring}{integral closure} of $R$ in $E'$. The natural transformations $\alpha_f^{E'}: f_! \Rightarrow f_*$ on $\Sh(\cdot, E')$ (from \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:canonical_forgets_support_map_from_direct_image_with_compact_support_to_direct_image_for_etale_sheaves_with_rational_adic_coefficients_on_compactifiable_morphisms}{the rational adic case}) form a direct system as $E'$ varies, because the base change functors $\Sh(\cdot, E') \to \Sh(\cdot, E'')$ for $E' \subseteq E''$ commute with both $f_!$ and $f_*$. Define $\alpha_f$ as the colimit of this direct system.

Concretely, for an object $\mathcal{F} \in \Sh(X, K)$ represented by $\mathcal{F}_{E'} \in \Sh(X, E')$ for some finite $E'/E$ inside $K$, the component $\alpha_f(\mathcal{F}): f_! \mathcal{F} \to f_* \mathcal{F}$ is the image of $\alpha^{E'}_f(\mathcal{F}_{E'}): f_! \mathcal{F}_{E'} \to f_* \mathcal{F}_{E'}$ in $\Sh(Y, K)$.
\end{definition}

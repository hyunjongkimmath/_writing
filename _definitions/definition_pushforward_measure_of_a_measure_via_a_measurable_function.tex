\begin{definition}[Pushforward measure of a measure via a measurable function] \label{definition:pushforward_measure_of_a_measure_via_a_measurable_function}

Let $(X, \mathcal{A})$ and $(Y, \mathcal{B})$ be \CrefAndHyperref{definition:sigma_algebra_on_a_set_and_measurable_space}{measurable spaces}. Let $f: X \to Y$ be a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:measurable_function_between_measure_spaces}{measurable function}. Let $\mu$ be a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:measure_on_a_measurable_space_valued_in_the_extended_real_line_and_measure_space}{measure} on $(X, \mathcal{A})$.

The \hldef{pushforward measure of $\mu$ via $f$}, denoted \hl{$f_*\mu$}, is the measure on $(Y, \mathcal{B})$ defined by
$$\hlin{(f_*\mu)(B) = \mu(f^{-1}(B))}$$
for every $B \in \mathcal{B}$.
\end{definition}

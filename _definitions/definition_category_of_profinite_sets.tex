\begin{definition} \label{definition:category_of_profinite_sets}
    The \hldef{category of profinite sets}, denoted by notations such as \hl{$\operatorname{Prof}$}, is the category whose objects are \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:profinite_set}{profinite sets} and whose morphisms are \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:morphism_of_profinite_sets}{morphisms of profinite sets}.

    This category is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:equivalence_of_categories}{equivalent to} the category of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:compact_topological_space}{compact} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:separation_axioms_of_topology}{Hausdorff} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:totally_disconnected_topological_space}{totally disconnected} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_spaces}{topological spaces} whose morphsims are \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:continuous_map_of_topological_spaces}{continuous maps}. 

    This category is also equivalent to the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ind_pro_category_of_a_locally_small_category}{pro-category} of the category of finite sets \TODO{category of finite sets}.

    $\operatorname{Prof}$ admits all \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_and_finite_limits_and_colimits_in_a_category}{small limits}.

\end{definition}

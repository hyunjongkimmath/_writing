\begin{definition} \label{definition:power_of_a_group_element_by_an_integer_multiple_of_an_abelian_group_element_by_an_integer}
Let $G$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group} with identity element $e$ and whose group operation is written multiplicatively, and let $g \in G$. For each integer $n \in \bbZ$, the \hldef{$n$th power of $g$}, denoted by \hl{$g^n$}, is the element of $G$ defined as follows:
\begin{itemize}
    \item If $n \geq 0$, then $g^n$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:power_of_a_monoid_element_by_a_nonnegative_integer}{$n$th power} of $g$ in the underlying \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:monoid}{monoid} of $G$.
    \item If $n < 0$, then $g^n := (g^{-1})^{-n}$, where $g^{-1}$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{inverse} of $g$.
\end{itemize}
In the case that the group operation is written additively, the $n$th power of $g$ is more usually written \hl{$ng$} of \hl{$n \cdot g$}, see \CrefIfExists{definition:power_of_a_monoid_element_by_a_nonnegative_integer}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Harmonic function on an open subset of Euclidean space] \label{definition:harmonic_function_on_an_open_subset_of_euclidean_n_space}
Let $n$ be a positive integer, and let $\mathbb{R}^n$ denote the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:eulerian_n_space}{Euclidean $n$-space}, equipped with its standard topology (\CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topology_on_a_field_induced_by_an_absolute_value}{induced by the absolute value}). Let $\Omega \subset \mathbb{R}^n$ be an open subset, and let $\Delta = \sum_{i=1}^n \frac{\partial^2}{\partial x_i^2}$ be the Laplacian operator on $\mathbb{R}^n$.

A function $u : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ is called a \hldef{harmonic function on $\Omega$} if $u$ is twice continuously differentiable ($C^2(\Omega)$) and satisfies Laplace's equation
$$\Delta u = 0$$
at every point of $\Omega$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:annulus_in_the_complex_plane}
Let $z_0 \in \bbC$ and let $0 < r_1 < r_2$.
The \hldef{annulus centered at $z_0$ with inner radius $r_1$ and outer radius $r_2$} is the subset
$$\hlin{A(z_0, r_1, r_2) = \{z \in \bbC : r_1 < |z - z_0| < r_2\}}$$
of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_complex_numbers}{field of complex numbers} $\bbC$, where $|\cdot|$ denotes the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complex_absolute_value}{complex absolute value}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:correspondences_of_degree_r_between_smooth_projective_schemes_over_a_noetherian_base_with_coefficients_in_a_commutative_ring}
Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{Noetherian} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{base scheme}. For any two \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:smooth_morphism_of_schemes}{smooth} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:projective_morphism_of_schemes_projective_scheme_over_a_scheme}{projective schemes} $X$ and $Y$ over $S$, a \hldef{correspondence of degree $r$ from $X$ to $Y$ with coefficients in a commutative ring $R$} is an element of the following \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:extension_of_scalars_base_change_induction_for_modules_along_ring_homomorphisms}{base change} of a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:relative_chow_groups_of_a_finite_type_scheme_over_a_noetherian_base}{relative Chow group}:
$$\hlin{\mathrm{Corr}_R^r(X, Y) \coloneqq A_{\dim(X/S) - r}(X \times_S Y) \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} R.}$$
\end{definition}

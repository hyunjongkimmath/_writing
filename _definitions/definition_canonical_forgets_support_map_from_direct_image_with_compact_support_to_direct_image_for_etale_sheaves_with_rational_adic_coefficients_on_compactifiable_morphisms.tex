Let $(R, \lambda)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_ring_with_respect_to_an_ideal}{complete} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{DVR} such that $R/\lambda^n$ is finite for all $n \geq 1$. Let $E$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_fractions_of_an_integral_domain}{fraction field} of $R$. Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme}. Let $X$ and $Y$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{noetherian} $S$-schemes, and consider sheaves on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{(small) \'etale sites} of $X$ and $Y$. Let $f: X \to Y$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:compactification_of_a_morphism_of_schemes}{$S$-compactifiable morphism}.

The \hldef{canonical forgets support map from direct image with compact support to direct image for \'etale sheaves with rational adic coefficients on compactifiable morphisms} is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:natural_transformation_between_functors_between_categories}{natural transformation}
$$\hlin{\alpha_f: f_! \Rightarrow f_*}$$
where $f_!: \Sh(X, E) \to \Sh(Y, E)$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:direct_image_functor_with_compact_support_on_etale_sheaves_with_rational_adic_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}{direct image functor with compact support on \'etale sheaves with rational adic coefficients} and $f_*: \Sh(X, E) \to \Sh(Y, E)$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:direct_image_functor_on_etale_sheaves_with_rational_adic_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}{direct image functor on \'etale sheaves with rational adic coefficients}.

It is obtained from the canonical forgets support map for $\lambda$-adic sheaves with integral coefficients, \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:canonical_forgets_support_map_from_direct_image_with_compact_support_to_direct_image_for_lambda_adic_sheaves_with_integral_coefficients_on_compactifiable_morphisms}{$\alpha_f^R: f_! \Rightarrow f_*$ on $\Sh(\cdot, R)$}, by tensoring with $E$ over $R$. Concretely, for $\mathcal{F} \in \Sh(X, E)$ represented by $\mathcal{F}_R \in \Sh(X, R)$, the component $\alpha_f(\mathcal{F}): f_! \mathcal{F} \to f_* \mathcal{F}$ is the image of $\alpha^R_f(\mathcal{F}_R): f_! \mathcal{F}_R \to f_* \mathcal{F}_R$ under the localization functor $\Sh(\cdot, R) \to \Sh(\cdot, E)$. For a morphism $\phi: \mathcal{F} \to \mathcal{G}$ in $\Sh(X, E)$ written as $\psi / \lambda^m$ with $\psi \in \Hom_{\Sh(X, R)}(\mathcal{F}_R, \mathcal{G}_R)$, the naturality square commutes because $f_!$ and $f_*$ commute with multiplication by $\lambda^m$.
\end{definition}

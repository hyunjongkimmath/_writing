\begin{definition} \label{definition:isotypic_component_of_a_continuous_representation_of_a_topological_group_on_a_topological_module_over_a_topological_ring_for_an_irreducible_representation}

    Let $G$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_group}{topological group}, $R$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_ring}{topological ring with unity}, and $(\rho, M)$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:continuous_representation_of_a_topological_group_on_a_topological_module_over_a_topological_ring}{continuous representation of $G$ on a topological left $R$-module}. Let $(\sigma, S)$ be an irreducible continuous representation of $G$.

    The \hldef{$S$-isotypic component of $M$} is the closed submodule
    $$\hlin{M_{(S)} := \overline{\sum \{N \subseteq M \mid N \text{ is a closed subrepresentation and } N \cong S\}}.}$$
    It is the smallest closed $G$-invariant subspace containing all copies of $S$ in $M$.

    If $(\rho, M)$ is semisimple (every closed subrepresentation admits a closed complement), then for each isomorphism class $[S]$ of irreducible continuous representations appearing in $M$, the isotypic component $M_{(S)}$ is a direct summand, and
    $$ M = \widehat{\bigoplus}_{[S]} M_{(S)},$$
    where $\widehat{\bigoplus}$ denotes the topological direct sum. Each $M_{(S)}$ is isomorphic to the topological direct sum of $m_S$ copies of $S$, for some multiplicity $m_S \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$, called the \hldef{multiplicity of $S$ in $M$}.
\end{definition}

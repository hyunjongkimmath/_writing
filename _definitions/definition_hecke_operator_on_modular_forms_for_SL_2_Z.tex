\begin{definition}[Hecke operators on modular forms for $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$] \label{definition:hecke_operator_on_modular_forms_for_SL_2_Z}

Let $k \in \mathbb{Z}$ and let $\Gamma = \mathrm{SL}_2(\bbZ)$. For each positive integer $n$, the \hldef{Hecke operator of level $n$} (or simply the \hldef{$n$-th Hecke operator}) is the linear map
$$\hlin{T_n : M_k(\Gamma) \longrightarrow M_k(\Gamma)}$$
defined on a modular form $f \in M_k(\Gamma)$\CrefIfExists{definition:vector_space_of_modular_and_cusp_forms} by
$$\hlin{(T_n f)(z) = n^{k-1} \sum_{ad=n,\,d>0,\,0 \leq b < d} d^{-k}\, f\bigg|_k \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ 0 & d \end{pmatrix}(z),}$$
\CrefIfExists{definition:weight_k_operator_on_meromorphic_functions_by_elements}

Equivalently, using the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:double_coset_of_a_subgroup_in_a_group}{double coset} description, for each $n \geq 1$,
$$\Gamma \begin{pmatrix} n & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \Gamma = \bigsqcup_{ad=n,\,d>0,\,0 \leq b < d} \Gamma \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ 0 & d \end{pmatrix},$$
and the Hecke operator is given by summing over right coset representatives:
$$(T_n f)(z) = n^{k-1} \sum_{\gamma \in \Gamma_\infty \backslash \Gamma \begin{psmallmatrix} n & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{psmallmatrix} \Gamma / \Gamma_\infty} (f|_k \gamma)(z),$$
where $\Gamma_\infty = \left\{ \pm \begin{psmallmatrix} 1 & m \\ 0 & 1 \end{psmallmatrix} : m \in \bbZ \right\}$ is the stabilizer of $\infty$ in $\Gamma$.

For a modular form $f \in M_k(\Gamma)$\CrefIfExists{definition:vector_space_of_modular_and_cusp_forms} with \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:fourier_expansion_of_a_periodic_holomorphic_function_on_the_upper_half_plane}{Fourier expansion} $f(z) = \sum_{m=0}^\infty a_m q^m$, the Fourier coefficients of $T_n f$ are given by
$$T_n f = \sum_{m=0}^\infty b_m q^m, \quad \text{where } b_m = \sum_{d \mid \gcd(m,n)} d^{k-1} a_{mn/d^2}.$$

In particular, if $f$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:modular_form_and_cusp_form_of_weight_k_with_respect_to_a_congruence_subgroup_of_SL_2_Z}{cusp form}, then $T_n f$ is also a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:modular_form_and_cusp_form_of_weight_k_with_respect_to_a_congruence_subgroup_of_SL_2_Z}{cusp form}, so each $T_n$ restricts to an endomorphism 
$$T_n: S_k(\Gamma) \to S_k(\Gamma).$$
\CrefIfExists{definition:vector_space_of_modular_and_cusp_forms}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:k_dimensional_relative_algebraic_cycles_of_a_finite_type_scheme_over_a_noetherian_base}
Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{Noetherian base scheme}. Let $X$ be a scheme of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme_of_finite_type}{finite type} over $S$.
The \hldef{group of $k$-dimensional algebraic cycles of $X$ relative to $S$}, denoted \hl{$Z_k(X/S)$}, is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:free_abelian_group}{free abelian group generated by} the set of all closed integral subschemes $V \subseteq X$ such that the \hldef{relative dimension} of $V$ over $S$ is $k$. 
An element $\alpha \in Z_k(X/S)$ is a finite formal sum $\sum n_i [V_i]$, where each $[V_i]$ is a \hldef{prime cycle} of relative dimension $k$ over $S$.
\end{definition}
\begin{remark}
If $S = \mathrm{Spec}(k)$ for a field $k$, then $Z_k(X/S)$ coincides with the absolute group $Z_k(X)$. In the relative case, the relative dimension of $V \subseteq X$ is defined as $\dim(V) - \dim(s)$ where $s$ is the image of the generic point of $V$ in $S$. If $X \to S$ is equidimensional of relative dimension $n$, we denote the group of relative \hldef{codimension $p$} cycles by $Z^p(X/S) \coloneqq Z_{n-p}(X/S)$.
\end{remark}

    for all $x > 0$, where $\ln$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:natural_logarithm_as_inverse_of_exponential}{natural logarithm}. Since $b \neq 1$, we have $\ln(b) \neq 0$, so the division is well-defined.

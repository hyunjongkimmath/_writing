\begin{definition} \label{definition:indecomposable_object_of_an_additive_category}

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:additive_category_preadditive_category}{additive category}. An object $X \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ is called \hldef{indecomposable} if $X \neq 0$ and whenever $X \cong A \oplus B$ for objects $A, B \in \mathcal{C}$ (with $\oplus$ the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:additive_category_preadditive_category}{direct sum}), then $A = 0$ or $B = 0$.
\end{definition}

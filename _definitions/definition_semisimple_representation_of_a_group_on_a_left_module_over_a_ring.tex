\begin{definition}[Semisimple representation of a group on a left module over a ring] \label{definition:semisimple_representation_of_a_group_on_a_left_module_over_a_ring}

Let $G$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group}, $R$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring with unity}, and $\rho : G \to \operatorname{Aut}_R(M)$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:representation_of_a_group_on_a_module_over_a_not_necessarily_commutative_ring}{representation of $G$ on a left $R$-module} $M$.

The representation $(\rho, M)$ is called \hldef{semisimple (representation of a group on a left module over a ring)} if every \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:subrepresentation_of_a_representation_of_a_group_on_a_left_module_on_a_ring}{subrepresentation} $N \subseteq M$ admits a complement — that is, there exists a subrepresentation $N' \subseteq M$ such that $M = N \oplus N'$ as an $R$-module and both $N$ and $N'$ are $G$-stable.

Equivalently, $(\rho, M)$ is semisimple if $M$ is isomorphic to a direct sum of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:irreducible_simple_representation_of_a_group_on_a_left_module_over_a_ring}{simple representations} of $G$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:pointed_n_tuple_of_topological_spaces}
    A \hldef{pointed $n$-tuple of topological spaces} is a $(n+1)$-tuple $(X_1, X_2, \dots, X_n, x)$ where $(X_1, X_2, \dots, X_n)$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:n_tuple_of_topological_spaces}{$n$-tuple of topological spaces} and $x \in X_n$, i.e., each $(X_i, x)$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:pointed_topological_space}{pointed space} and the inclusions $X_{i+1} \hookrightarrow X_i$ yield \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:pointed_topological_space}{pointed maps} $(X_{i+1}, x) \to (X_i, x)$ for all $1 \le i < n$.
\end{definition}

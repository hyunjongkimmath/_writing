Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme}. Let $X$ and $Y$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{noetherian} $S$-schemes, and consider sheaves on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{(small) \'etale sites} of $X$ and $Y$. Let $f: X \to Y$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:compactification_of_a_morphism_of_schemes}{$S$-compactifiable morphism}. Fix a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:compactification_of_a_morphism_of_schemes}{compactification} of $f$:
$$
\begin{tikzcd}
X \ar[rr, "f"] \ar[dr, "j"'] & & Y \\
& \bar{X} \ar[ur, "\bar{f}"'] &
\end{tikzcd}
$$
so that $j: X \hookrightarrow \bar{X}$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:open_and_closed_immersions_of_schemes}{open immersion} and $\bar{f}: \bar{X} \to Y$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:proper_morphism_of_schemes}{proper morphism}. Let $\Lambda$ be a commutative ring.

Let $D(X_{\et}, \Lambda)$ denote the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:derived_category_of_an_abelian_category}{unbounded derived category} of sheaves of $\Lambda$-modules on the small \'etale site of $X$. Let $Rj_!: D(X_{\et}, \Lambda) \to D(\bar{X}_{\et}, \Lambda)$ denote the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:derived_functor_of_extension_by_zero}{derived functor of extension by zero} along $j$, and let $Rj_*: D(X_{\et}, \Lambda) \to D(\bar{X}_{\et}, \Lambda)$ denote the right derived direct image functor along $j$. The canonical natural transformation $\nu: j_! \Rightarrow j_*$ on the underived level induces a canonical morphism in the derived category
$$\hlin{\mathbf{R}\nu: Rj_! \Rightarrow Rj_*}.$$

The \hldef{canonical forgets support map from derived direct image with compact support to derived direct image for complexes of \'etale sheaves of $\Lambda$-modules on compactifiable morphisms} is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:natural_transformation_between_functors_between_categories}{natural transformation}
$$\hlin{\alpha_f := R\bar{f}_*(\mathbf{R}\nu): Rf_! \Rightarrow Rf_*}$$
where $Rf_! = R\bar{f}_* Rj_!: D(X_{\et}, \Lambda) \to D(Y_{\et}, \Lambda)$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:derived_direct_image_functor_with_proper_or_compact_support_for_compactifiable_morphisms_of_schemes_over_a_scheme}{derived direct image functor with compact support} and $Rf_* = R\bar{f}_* Rj_*: D(X_{\et}, \Lambda) \to D(Y_{\et}, \Lambda)$ is the derived direct image functor.

For each $\mathcal{F} \in D(X_{\et}, \Lambda)$, the component $\alpha_f(\mathcal{F}): Rf_! \mathcal{F} \to Rf_* \mathcal{F}$ is the morphism
$$R\bar{f}_*(\mathbf{R}\nu_\mathcal{F}): R\bar{f}_* Rj_! \mathcal{F} \longrightarrow R\bar{f}_* Rj_* \mathcal{F}.$$

This natural transformation is independent of the choice of compactification up to canonical isomorphism.

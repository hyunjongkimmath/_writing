\begin{definition}[Strictly henselian ring] \label{definition:strictly_henselian_ring}
A \hldef{strictly henselian (local) ring} or a \hldef{strictly local ring} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:henselian_ring}{henselian local ring} $(R, \mathfrak{m})$ whose \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:residue_field_of_a_ring_at_a_point}{residue field} $k = R/\mathfrak{m}$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:separably_closed_field}{separably closed}.
\end{definition}

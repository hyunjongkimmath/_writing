\begin{definition} \label{definition:graded_subobject_of_a_graded_object_of_a_category}
    Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category}{category}. Let $M = \{M_n\}_{n \in \mathbb{Z}}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:graded_object_in_a_category}{graded object} in $\mathcal{A}$. 
    A \hldef{graded subobject of $M$} is a graded object $N = \{N_n\}_{n \in \mathbb{Z}}$ in $\mathcal{A}$ together with a family of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:monomorphism_and_epimorphism_in_categories}{monomorphisms} 
    $$\hlin{\iota_n: N_n \hookrightarrow M_n}$$
    for each $n \in \mathbb{Z}$. If we view $M$ as an object in the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category_of_graded_objects_in_a_category}{category of graded objects $\text{Gr}(\mathcal{A})$}, this is equivalent to saying that $N$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:subobject_of_an_object_of_a_category}{subobject} of $M$ in $\text{Gr}(\mathcal{A})$.
\end{definition}

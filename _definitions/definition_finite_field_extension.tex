\begin{definition} \label{definition:finite_field_extension}
Let $L/K$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:extension_of_a_field}{field extension}.

It is said to be a \hldef{finite field extension} and $L$ is said to be a \hldef{finite extension of $K$} if $[L:K] < \infty$.

A finite field extension of degree $2$, $3$, $4$, $5$, $6$, etc. is called \hldef{quadratic}, \hldef{cubic}, \hldef{quartic}, \hldef{quintic}, \hldef{sextic}, etc.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Formal power series ring in one variable over a commutative ring] \label{definition:formal_power_series_ring_in_one_variable_over_a_commutative_ring}
Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:commutative_ring}{commutative ring}. The \hldef{ring of formal power series in one variable over $R$}, denoted $\hlin{R \llbracket X \rrbracket}$, is the special case of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:hahn_series_over_a_commutative_ring_with_exponents_in_a_totally_ordered_abelian_group}{Hahn series ring} $R \llbracket t^\Gamma \rrbracket$ where the exponent group is $\Gamma = (\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}, +, \leq)$ with its natural total order.

Elements of $R \llbracket X \rrbracket$ are formal sums
$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n X^n,$$
where each $a_n \in R$. The ring $R \llbracket X \rrbracket$ is always a commutative ring with unity, regardless of whether $R$ is a field.

When $R = k$ is a field, the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:laurent_series_field_in_one_variable_over_a_field}{Laurent series field} $k((X))$ embeds into $R \llbracket t^{\mathbb{Z}} \rrbracket$ as the subring of Hahn series with support bounded below.
\end{definition}

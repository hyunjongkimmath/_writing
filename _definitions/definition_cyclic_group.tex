\begin{definition}[Cyclic group] \label{definition:cyclic_group}
Let $(G,\cdot)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group}. An element $g \in G$ is called a \hldef{generator of $G$} if the subgroup of $G$ \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:subgroup_of_a_group_generated_by_elements}{generated by} $\{g\}$, denoted \hl{$\langle \{g\} \rangle$}, equals $G$. That is,
$$G = \langle \{g\} \rangle.$$
Equivalently, every element of $G$ can be written as an integer power of $g$:
$$G = \{ g^n \mid n \in \bbZ\},$$
where $g^0 = e$ (the identity element of $G$, see \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{groups}), $g^n = g \cdot g^{n-1}$ for $n > 0$, and $g^n = (g^{-1})^{-n}$ for $n < 0$.

\end{definition}

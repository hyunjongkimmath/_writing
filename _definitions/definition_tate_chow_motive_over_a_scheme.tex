Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme}.
The \hldef{Tate (Chow) motive over $S$}, denoted \hl{$\mathds{1}_S(1)$}, is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:relative_chow_motive_over_a_noetherian_base_and_commutative_ring_of_coefficients}{relative Chow motive} $(P^1_S, p, -1)$ where $P^1_S$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:projective_space_of_dimension_n_over_a_scheme}{projective line} over $S$ and $p$ is the projector onto the $H^2$ component. 
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:prespectrum_of_pointed_topological_spaces}
    A \hldef{prespectrum} $T$ is a sequence of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:pointed_topological_space}{pointed topological space} \hldef{$T_n$} and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:pointed_topological_space}{pointed maps} \hldef{$\sigma = \sigma_n^T: \Sigma T_n \to T_{n+1}$}\CrefIfExists{definition:reduced_suspension_of_a_pointed_topological_space}. The space $T_n$ may be referred to as the \hldef{degree $n$ space of the prespectrum} and the maps $\sigma: \Sigma T_n \to T_{n+1}$ may be referred to as the \hldef{structure maps}. 
    
    A \hldef{morphism $f: T \to T'$ of prespectra} is a sequence of maps $f_n: T_n \to T_n'$ such thta $\sigma_n' \circ \Sigma f_n = f_{n+1} \circ \sigma_n$ for all $n$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:subgroup_of_a_group_generated_by_elements}
    Let $G$ be a group and let $S \subseteq G$ be a subset. 
    The \hldef{subgroup of $G$ generated by $S$} is the smallest subgroup \hl{$\langle S \rangle$} of $G$ containing $S$ --- more explicitly, it can be constructed as the intersection
    $$\langle S \rangle = \bigcap_{S \subseteq H \leq G} H$$
    of subgroups $H$ of $G$ that contain $S$.

    All of the elements of $S$ together are called \hldef{generators of $\langle S \rangle$}.

    The subgroup $\langle S \rangle$ may also called the \hldef{subgroup of $G$ algebraically generated by $S$} for emphasis.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[$H$-group] \label{definition:H_group_for_a_fixed_group}

    Let $H$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group}.

    An \hldef{$H$-group} is a pair $(N, \phi)$ where $N$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group} and $\phi: H \to \operatorname{Aut}(N)$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group_homomorphism}{homomorphism}. Equivalently, an $H$-group is a group $N$ equipped with an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:left_and_right_group_actions_on_a_set}{action} of $H$ on $N$ such that $(h,n) \mapsto h \cdot n$ satisfies $h \cdot (n_1 \cdot_N n_2) = (h \cdot n_1) \cdot_N (h \cdot n_2)$. 
    where $\cdot_N$ is the group operation on $N$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:trace_of_an_endomorphism_of_a_free_finitely_generated_module_over_a_ring}

    Let $R$ be a commutative \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring}, and let $M$ be a free \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:module_of_a_ring}{$R$-module} of finite rank. Let $\varphi: M \to M$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:endomorphism_ring_of_a_module_over_a_ring}{endomorphism of $M$ as an $R$-module}.

    Choose a basis $\mathcal{B}$ of $M$, and let $[\varphi]_{\mathcal{B}} \in M_{n,n}(R)$ be the matrix of $\varphi$ with respect to $\mathcal{B}$. The \hldef{trace of $\varphi$} is
    $$\hlin{\operatorname{tr}_R(\varphi) = \operatorname{tr}([\varphi]_{\mathcal{B}})},$$
    where the right-hand side is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:trace_of_a_matrix_over_a_ring}{trace of the matrix} $[\varphi]_{\mathcal{B}}$. This value is independent of the choice of basis $\mathcal{B}$ because similar matrices over a commutative ring have the same trace\CrefIfExists{proposition:similar_matrices_share_determinant_trace_characteristic_polynomial_and_rank}.

    The trace map $\operatorname{tr}_R: \operatorname{End}_R(M) \to R$ is $R$-linear.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:left_kan_extension_of_presheaves_along_a_functor_of_sheaves_along_cocontinuous_functor_of_sites}
Let $u : \calC \to \calD$ be a functor between categories. Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category}{(large) category}. 

For any \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:presheaf_on_a_category}{presheaf} $\mathcal{F} \in \operatorname{PreSh}(\calC; \mathcal{A})$, the \hldef{left Kan extension of $\mathcal{F}$ along $u$} is the presheaf \hl{$\operatorname{Lan}_u \mathcal{F} \in \operatorname{PreSh}(\calD; \mathcal{A})$} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:defined_at_objects_derivable_and_adapted_objects_for_the_derived_functors_of_an_exact_multifunctor_between_triangulated_categories}{defined}, assuming that all \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:limit_and_colimit_of_a_diagram_in_a_category}{colimits} below exist, by:
$$(\operatorname{Lan}_u \mathcal{F})(V) = \varinjlim_{(V \downarrow u)} \mathcal{F}(U),$$
where the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:limit_and_colimit_of_a_diagram_in_a_category}{colimit} is taken over the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:comma_category_of_two_functors_to_a_category}{comma category $(V \downarrow u)$} of pairs $(U, V \to u(U))$ with $U \in \calC$. 

Now assuming that Let $(\calC,J)$ and $(\calD,K)$ are \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{sites}, that $u : \calC \to \calD$ is a cocontinuous functor of sites, and that the presheaf category $\operatorname{PreSh}(\calD,K;\mathcal{A})$ has \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheafification_functor_on_a_site}{sheafification}, this construction \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{theorem:additive_functor_between_abelian_categories_induces_an_exact_functor_between_homotopy_categories_as_triangulated_categories}{induces} a functor at the level of sheaves --- for any \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_on_a_site}{sheaf} $\mathcal{G} \in \operatorname{Sh}(\calC, J; \mathcal{A})$, we define the \hldef{sheafified left Kan extension} as:
$$\hlin{u_! \mathcal{G} = a(\operatorname{Lan}_u \mathcal{G}),}$$
where $a$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheafification_functor_on_a_site}{sheafification functor} on $\operatorname{PreSh}(\calD, K; \mathcal{A})$. In particular, $u_!$ yields a functor $$u_!: \Sh(\calC, J; \calA) \to \Sh(\calD, K; \calA).$$
\end{definition}

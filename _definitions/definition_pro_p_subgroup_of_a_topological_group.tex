\begin{definition}[Pro-p Subgroup] \label{definition:pro_p_subgroup_of_a_topological_group}
Let $G$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:profinite_group}{profinite group}. A closed subgroup $H \subseteq G$ is called a \hldef{pro-$p$ subgroup of $G$} if every open \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:normal_subgroup_and_characteristic_subgroup_of_an_algebraic_group_over_a_scheme}{normal subgroup} $N \triangleleft G$ such that $N \subseteq H$ has the property that the finite quotient $H/(H \cap N)$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:p_group_of_finite_order}{$p$-group} (i.e., its order is a power of $p$).

Equivalently, $H$ is a pro-$p$ subgroup if it is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:projective_and_inductive_limits_in_categories}{inverse limit} of finite $p$-groups. In other words, $H$ is itself a profinite group in which every open normal subgroup has index a power of $p$.
\end{definition}

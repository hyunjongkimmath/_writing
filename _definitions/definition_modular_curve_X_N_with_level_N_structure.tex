\begin{definition} \label{definition:modular_curve_X_N_with_level_N_structure}
Let $N$ be a positive integer. The \hldef{classical modular curve} \hl{$X(N)$} is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:compact_topological_space}{compact} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:riemann_surface}{Riemann surface} obtained as the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:quotient_of_a_topological_space_under_group_action}{quotient}
% $X(N) := \mathbb{H} \cuppoint \Gamma(N)\backslash\mathbb{H}$
$X(N) := \Gamma(N)\backslash\overline{\mathbb{H}}$
where $\mathbb{H} = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : \operatorname{Im}(z) > 0\}$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:upper_half_plane_in_the_complex_plane}{upper half-plane} and $\Gamma(N) \subset \operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:principal_congruence_subgroup_Gamma_N_of_SL_2_Z}{principal congruence subgroup} of level $N$.

The curve $X(N)$ classifies complex elliptic curves $E/\mathbb{C}$ equipped with a full level $N$ structure, i.e., an ordered basis of the $N$-torsion points $E[N] \cong (\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^2$.
The open modular curve $Y(N) = \Gamma(N)\backslash\mathbb{H}$ is the non-compact Riemann surface before compactification.
\end{definition}

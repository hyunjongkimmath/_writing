\begin{definition}[Gauss sum of a Dirichlet character] \label{definition:gauss_sum_of_a_dirichlet_character}

Let $k \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 1}$, and let $\chi$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dirichlet_character_modulo_a_nonnegative_integer}{Dirichlet character modulo $k$}. The \hldef{Gauss sum of $\chi$} is the complex number \hl{$\tau(\chi)$} defined by
$$\hlin{\tau(\chi) := \sum_{m=1}^k \chi(m) e^{2\pi i m / k}.}$$
\end{definition}

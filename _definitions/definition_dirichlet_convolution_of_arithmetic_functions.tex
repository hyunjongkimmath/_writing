\begin{definition} \label{definition:dirichlet_convolution_of_arithmetic_functions}
Let $f, g: \bbZ_{>0} \to R$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:arithmetic_function_on_positive_integers_with_values_in_a_ring}{arithmetic functions}. Their \hldef{Dirichlet convolution}, denoted by \hl{$(f * g)$}, is the arithmetic function defined by
    $$\hlin{(f * g)(n) = \sum_{d \mid n} f(d)\, g\left(\frac{n}{d}\right)}$$
    for each $n \in \bbZ_{>0}$. As a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:n_ary_operation_binary_operation_ternary_operation_on_a_set}{binary operation} on the set of arithmetic functions, Dirichlet convolution is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:commutative_binary_operation_on_a_set}{commutative} and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:associative_binary_operation_on_a_set}{associative}\CrefIfExists{proposition:algebraic_properties_of_dirichlet_convolution}, with \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:left_right_identity_element_of_a_binary_operation_on_a_set}{identity element} $\varepsilon$, where $\varepsilon(1) = 1$ and $\varepsilon(n) = 0$ for $n > 1$.
\end{definition}

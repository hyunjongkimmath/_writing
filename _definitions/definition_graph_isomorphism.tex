Let $G$ and $H$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:simple_graph_directed_graph_multigraph_weighted_graph}{simple graphs}. An \hldef{isomorphism from $G$ to $H$} is a bijection $\phi: V(G) \to V(H)$ such that for all $u, v \in V(G)$,
$$\{u,v\} \in E(G) \iff \{\phi(u), \phi(v)\} \in E(H).$$
If there exists an isomorphism from $G$ to $H$, then $G$ and $H$ are called \hldef{isomorphic}, denoted by \hl{$G \cong H$}.

An \hldef{automorphism of $G$} is an isomorphism from $G$ to itself.
\end{definition}

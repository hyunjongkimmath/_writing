\begin{definition}[$\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,S}$: Deligne--Mumford compactification of the moduli space of curves] \label{definition:deligne_mumford_compactification_of_moduli_space_of_curves}
    Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme} and let $g \geq 2$ be an integer. The \hldef{Deligne--Mumford compactification of the moduli space $\mathcal{M}_{g,S}$ of smooth curves of genus $g$ over $S$}, denoted by \hl{$\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,S}$}, is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:property_of_representable_1_morphism_of_categories_fibered_in_groupoids_over_asite_on_the_category_of_schemes_over_a_scheme}{proper} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:deligne_mumford_stack_over_a_scheme_for_a_grothendieck_topology_on_the_category_of_S_schemes}{Deligne--Mumford stack} over $S$ equipped with a closed immersion $\mathcal{M}_{g,S} \hookrightarrow \overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,S}$ that identifies $\mathcal{M}_{g,S}$ with a dense open substack. The complement $\partial\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,S} := \overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,S} \setminus \mathcal{M}_{g,S}$ is the boundary divisor, which parametrizes \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:stable_curve_over_a_scheme}{stable curves}.

    Concretely, for any $S$-scheme $T$, the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:groupoid}{groupoid} $\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,S}(T)$ is equivalent to the category of morphisms $C \to T$ that are \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:flat_morphism_of_schemes}{flat} and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:proper_morphism_of_schemes}{proper}, whose geometric fibers are connected, \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ordinary_double_point_of_a_finite_type_scheme_over_a_field}{nodal curves} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:arithmetic_genus_of_a_proper_scheme_over_a_field}{arithmetic genus} $g$, and whose \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dualizing_complex_of_proper_finite_type_scheme_over_locally_noetherian_scheme}{dualizing sheaf} $\omega_{C/T}$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:relative_ampleness_of_a_line_bundle_on_a_scheme}{relatively ample} over $T$. Such a morphism is called a family of stable curves of genus $g$ over $T$.

    When $S = \Spec \bbZ$, we write $\overline{\mathcal{M}}_g := \overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,\Spec \bbZ}$. The stack $\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,S}$ is a Deligne--Mumford stack that is smooth over $S$ of relative dimension $3g - 3$ for $g \geq 2$.
\end{definition}

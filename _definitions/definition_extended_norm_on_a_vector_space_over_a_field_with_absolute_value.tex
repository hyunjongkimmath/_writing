\begin{definition}[Extended norm on a vector space over a field with absolute value] \label{definition:extended_norm_on_a_vector_space_over_a_field_with_absolute_value}
    Let $T$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ordered_semiring}{ordered semiring}, let $F$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field} equipped with an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:absolute_value_on_a_ring_valued_in_an_ordered_semiring}{absolute value valued in $T$}:
    $$|\cdot| : F \to T,$$
    and let $V$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:vector_space_over_a_field}{vector space} over $F$. Let $T^\infty$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ordered_semiring_with_adjoined_infinity}{ordered semiring with adjoined infinity} obtained from $T$.

    An \hldef{extended norm on $V$ valued in $T^\infty$} is a function
    $$\|\cdot\| : V \to T^\infty$$
    satisfying for all $x,y \in V$ and all scalars $\alpha \in F$:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Positive definiteness: $\|x\| = 0_T$ if and only if $x = 0$.
        \item Homogeneity: $\|\alpha x\| = |\alpha| \cdot \|x\|$, where the multiplication on the right is in $T^\infty$.
        \item Triangle inequality: $\|x + y\| \leq \|x\| + \|y\|$, where addition and order are those of $T^\infty$.
    \end{enumerate}
    A vector space with an extended norm valued in $T^\infty$ over a field with absolute value is called an \hldef{extended normed (vector) space}.

    If the range of the extended norm is contained in $T$, then the extended norm is a \hldef{norm} in the usual sense and $V$ may be called a \hldef{normed vector space} or a \hldef{normed space}.

    In the case that $T = \bbR_{\geq 0}$\CrefIfExists{definition:field_of_real_numbers}\CrefIfExists{definition:positive_cone_in_an_ordered_monoid}\CrefIfExists{proposition:positive_cone_of_ordered_ring_is_ordered_semiring}, we recover the standard definition of an extended norm taking values in $\bbR_{\geq 0}$ or $[0,\infty]$.

    \TextIfExists{definition:extended_norm_on_a_module_over_a_commutative_ring_with_absolute_value}{
        An extended norm on $V$ valued in $T^\infty$ as above is a special case of an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:extended_norm_on_a_module_over_a_commutative_ring_with_absolute_value}{extended norm on a module over a commutative ring with absolute value}, in which the ring is the field $F$, the module is the vector space $V$.
    }
\end{definition}

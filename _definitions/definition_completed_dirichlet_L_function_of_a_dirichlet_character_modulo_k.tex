Let $k \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$ and let $\chi$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dirichlet_character_modulo_a_nonnegative_integer}{Dirichlet character modulo $k$}. The \hldef{completed (Dirichlet) $L$-function of $\chi$} is defined by

where $a(\chi) = 0$ if $\chi(-1) = 1$ (even character) and $a(\chi) = 1$ if $\chi(-1) = -1$ (odd character), and $L(s,\chi)$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dirichlet_L_function_of_a_dirichlet_character_modulo_a_nonnegative_integer}{Dirichlet $L$-function}.

\begin{definition} \label{definition:kappa_condensed_object_of_a_category_for_a_cardinal}
    Let $\calA$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category}{category}. Let $\kappa$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cardinal_number_and_cardinality_of_a_set}{cardinal}

    A \hldef{$\kappa$-condensed object of $\calA$} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_on_a_site}{sheaf} on  \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:kappa_condensed_object_of_a_category_for_a_cardinal}{$\operatorname{Prof}_{\kappa}$} valued in $\calA$.

    Names for objects of $\calA$ induce names for $\kappa$-condensed objects of $\calA$, e.g. a \hldef{$\kappa$-condensed group} is a $\kappa$-condensed object of the category of groups, etc.

    As per \Cref{proposition:category_of_sheaves_with_values_in_gamma_sets_is_equivalent_to_category_of_sheaves_of_gamma_objects_in_the_category_of_sheaves_of_sets}, for $\gamma$ a ``kind of algebraic structure defined by finite projective limits'', the category of $\kappa$-condensed objects of $\gamma-\mathbf{Sets}$ is equivalent to the category of $\gamma$-objects of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:kappa_condensed_set_for_a_cardinal}{$\operatorname{CondSet}_{\kappa}$}.
\end{definition}

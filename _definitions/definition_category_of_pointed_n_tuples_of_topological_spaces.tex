\begin{definition} \label{definition:category_of_pointed_n_tuples_of_topological_spaces}
    The \hldef{category of pointed $n$-tuples of topological spaces}, often denoted by notations such as \hl{$\mathbf{Top}^n_*$} or \hl{$\mathbf{Top}^n_\bullet$} (not to be confused with the $n$-time self \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:product_category_of_a_family_of_categories}{product} of the category \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:pointed_topological_space}{$\mathbf{Top}_*$} of topological spaces), is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category}{category} defined by:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Objects:} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:pointed_n_tuple_of_topological_spaces}{Pointed $n$-tuples} $(X_1, \dots, X_n, x)$ of topological spaces.
        \item \textbf{Morphisms:} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:morphism_of_pointed_n_tuples_of_topological_spaces}{Morphisms of pointed $n$-tuples}.
        \item \textbf{Composition:} The composition of morphisms is the usual composition of continuous maps.
        \item \textbf{Identity:} For each object $(X_1, \dots, X_n, x)$, the identity morphism is the identity map $\operatorname{id}_{X_1}$.
    \end{itemize}
    It is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_small_category}{locally small category}.
\end{definition}

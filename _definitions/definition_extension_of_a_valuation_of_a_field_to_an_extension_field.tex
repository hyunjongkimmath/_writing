\begin{definition} \label{definition:extension_of_a_valuation_of_a_field_to_an_extension_field}
    Let $L/K$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:extension_of_a_field}{extension} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:valuation_on_a_field}{valued fields} with valuations $v: K \to \Gamma_K \cup \{\infty\}$ and $w : L \to \Gamma_L \cup \{\infty\}$. 
    We say that $w$ is an \hldef{extension of $v$} if 
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item $\Gamma_K$ is an ordered subgroup of $\Gamma_L$
        \item For all $x \in K$, $w(x) = v(x)$. 
    \end{enumerate}
    In other words, $v = w|_K$. 

    It is appropriate to refer to $(L,w) / (K,v)$ as an \hldef{extension of valuation fields}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:ringed_topos}
    A \hldef{ringed topos} is a pair $(E, \mathcal{O}_E)$ consisting of a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topos}{topos} $E$ and a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring_object_in_a_category_with_a_terminal_object}{ring object} $\mathcal{O}_E$ in $E$ (often called the \hldef{structure sheaf}). 
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Riemann sphere] \label{definition:riemann_sphere}
    \TODO{one-point compactification}
    The \hldef{Riemann sphere}, denoted \hl{$\bbC_\infty$} or \hl{$\mathbb{P}^1(\bbC)$}, is the one-point compactification of the complex plane:
    $$\hlin{\bbC_\infty = \bbC \cup \{\infty\}}$$
    where $\infty$ is a point at infinity. Topologically, it is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:homeomorphism_of_topological_spaces}{homeomorphic} to the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:n_dimensional_unit_sphere}{$2$-sphere} $S^2$. 
    
    \TODO{holomorphic atlas}
    As a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complex_analytic_manifold}{complex manifold}, $\bbC_\infty$ carries a unique holomorphic atlas consisting of two charts: $(U_0, \phi_0)$ with $U_0 = \bbC$, $\phi_0(z) = z$, and $(U_\infty, \phi_\infty)$ with $U_\infty = \bbC_\infty \setminus \{0\}$, $\phi_\infty(w) = 1/w$ for $w \neq 0$. The transition map on the overlap is $z \mapsto 1/z$, which is holomorphic. Thus $\bbC_\infty$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:compact_topological_space}{compact} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:riemann_surface}{Riemann surface} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:genus_of_a_topological_space}{genus} $0$.
\end{definition}

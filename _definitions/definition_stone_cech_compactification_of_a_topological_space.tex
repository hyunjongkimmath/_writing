\begin{definition}[Stone-Čech compactification of a topological space] \label{definition:stone_cech_compactification_of_a_topological_space}

    Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_space}{topological space} that is both \hldef{Tychonoff (completely regular Hausdorff)} and \hldef{locally compact}. The \hldef{Stone-Čech compactification} of $X$, denoted by \hl{$\beta X$}, is a pair $(\beta X, \iota)$ consisting of a compact Hausdorff space $\beta X$ and a continuous map $\iota : X \to \beta X$ with dense image such that for any compact Hausdorff space $K$ and any continuous map $f : X \to K$, there exists a unique continuous map $\tilde{f} : \beta X \to K$ making the following diagram commute:
    $$\hlin{\begin{tikzcd}
        X \arrow[r, "\iota"] \arrow[dr, "f"'] & \beta X \arrow[d, "\exists!\,\tilde{f}"] \\
        & K
    \end{tikzcd}}$$

    Equivalently, $\beta X$ is the unique (up to homeomorphism) compact Hausdorff space containing $X$ as a dense subspace such that every bounded continuous real-valued function on $X$ extends uniquely to a continuous function on $\beta X$. That is, the restriction map
    $$\hlin{C(\beta X, \mathbb{R}) \xrightarrow{\,\sim\,} C_b(X, \mathbb{R})}$$
    is a bijection, where $C_b(X, \mathbb{R})$ denotes the space of all bounded continuous functions $X \to \mathbb{R}$.

    For a Tychonoff space $X$, $\beta X$ can be constructed as the closure of the image of $X$ under the evaluation map into the product of closed unit intervals indexed by $C_b(X, [0,1])$:
    $$\iota : X \hookrightarrow \prod_{f \in C_b(X,\,[0,1])} [0,1], \qquad x \mapsto (f(x))_{f}.$$
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:direct_sum_of_two_representations_of_a_group_over_a_field}

    Let $(G, \cdot)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group}, $k$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}, and $(\rho_1, V)$, $(\rho_2, W)$ two \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:representation_of_a_group_on_a_vector_space_over_a_field}{representations} of $G$ over $k$.

    The \hldef{direct sum of $\rho_1$ and $\rho_2$} is the representation
    $$\hlin{\rho_1 \oplus \rho_2 : G \to \mathrm{GL}(V \oplus W)}$$
    defined by $(\rho_1 \oplus \rho_2)(g) = \rho_1(g) \oplus \rho_2(g)$ for all $g \in G$, i.e.,
    $$\hlin{(\rho_1 \oplus \rho_2)(g)(v, w) = (\rho_1(g)v, \rho_2(g)w).}$$

    More generally, if $\{(\rho_i, V_i)\}_{i \in I}$ is a family of representations of $G$ over $k$, the \hldef{direct sum of the family} is the representation $\bigoplus_{i \in I} \rho_i$ on $\bigoplus_{i \in I} V_i$ acting componentwise.
\end{definition}

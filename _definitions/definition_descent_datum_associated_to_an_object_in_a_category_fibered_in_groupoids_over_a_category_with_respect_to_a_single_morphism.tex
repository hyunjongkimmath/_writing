\begin{definition} \label{definition:descent_datum_associated_to_an_object_in_a_category_fibered_in_groupoids_over_a_category_with_respect_to_a_single_morphism}
    Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and let $p: \mathcal{F} \to \mathcal{C}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category_fibered_in_groupoids_over_a_category}{category fibered in groupoids}. We fix a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cleavage_splitting_for_a_category_fibered_in_groupoids}{cleavage} for $p$, denoting the chosen pullback functor along a morphism $f: V \to U$ by $f^*: \mathcal{F}_U \to \mathcal{F}_V$, and the structural natural isomorphisms for composition by $\alpha_{g,h}: (g \circ h)^* \xrightarrow{\sim} h^* \circ g^*$ for morphisms $W \xrightarrow{h} V \xrightarrow{g} U$.

    Let $\pi: U' \to U$ be a morphism in $
    \calC$ such that $U' \times_U U'$ \CrefIfExists{definition:cartesian_product_of_two_objects_in_a_category_over_an_object} exists in $\calC$ and let $S_\pi$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sieve_on_an_object_in_a_category}{sieve on $U$} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sieve_on_an_object_of_a_category_generated_by_a_family_of_morphisms}{generated by} $\pi$. 

    Write $\pi_1,\pi_2: U' \times_U U' \rightrightarrows U'$ for the two canonical projections.

    Given an object $y \in \calF_U$, there is a canonical descent datum $(X',\phi)$ defined by:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item $X' = \pi^*(y)$  
        \item $\phi: \pi_1^*(X') \xrightarrow{\sim} \pi_2^*(X')$ is defined as the composition
        $$\pi_1^*(\pi^* y) \xrightarrow{\alpha_{\pi, \pi_1}}^{-1} (\pi \circ \pi_1)^* (y) \xrightarrow{\id} (\pi \circ \pi_2)^*(y) \xrightarrow{\alpha_{\pi, \pi_2}} \pi_2^*(p^* y)$$
    \end{enumerate}

    We note that the canonical descent datum of an object $y$ is always \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:effective_descent_datum_for_a_category_fibered_in_groupoids_over_a_category_relative_to_a_sieve_on_an_object_of_the_base_category}{effective}.

Let $(R, \lambda)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_ring_with_respect_to_an_ideal}{complete} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{DVR} such that $R/\lambda^n$ is finite for all $n \geq 1$. Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme}. Let $X$ and $Y$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{noetherian} $S$-schemes, and consider sheaves on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{(small) \'etale sites} of $X$ and $Y$. Let $f: X \to Y$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:compactification_of_a_morphism_of_schemes}{$S$-compactifiable morphism}. Fix a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:compactification_of_a_morphism_of_schemes}{compactification} of $f$:
$$
\begin{tikzcd}
X \ar[rr, "f"] \ar[dr, "j"'] & & Y \\
& \bar{X} \ar[ur, "\bar{f}"'] &
\end{tikzcd}
$$
so that $j: X \hookrightarrow \bar{X}$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:open_and_closed_immersions_of_schemes}{open immersion} and $\bar{f}: \bar{X} \to Y$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:proper_morphism_of_schemes}{proper morphism}.

Let $D_c^b(X, R)$ denote the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:derived_category_of_bounded_constructible_complexes_of_adic_sheaves_with_integral_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}{bounded derived category of constructible complexes of $\lambda$-adic sheaves with integral coefficients} on $X$. For each $n \geq 1$, let $\mathbf{R}\nu^{(n)}: Rj_! \Rightarrow Rj_*$ be the canonical morphism on $D_c^b(\cdot, R/\lambda^n)$ as in \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:canonical_forgets_support_map_from_derived_direct_image_with_compact_support_to_derived_direct_image_for_complexes_of_etale_sheaves_of_lambda_modules_on_compactifiable_morphisms}{the torsion coefficient case}.

The \hldef{canonical forgets support map from derived direct image with compact support to derived direct image for bounded constructible complexes with integral coefficients on compactifiable morphisms} is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:natural_transformation_between_functors_between_categories}{natural transformation}
$$\hlin{\alpha_f := (R\bar{f}_*(\mathbf{R}\nu^{(n)}))_{n \geq 1}: Rf_! \Rightarrow Rf_*}$$
where $Rf_!: D_c^b(X, R) \to D_c^b(Y, R)$ is the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:derived_direct_image_functor_with_compact_support_on_bounded_constructible_complexes_with_integral_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}{derived direct image functor with compact support} and $Rf_*: D_c^b(X, R) \to D_c^b(Y, R)$ is the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:derived_direct_image_functor_on_bounded_constructible_complexes_with_integral_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}{derived direct image functor}.

For each $\mathcal{F} = (\mathcal{F}_n)_{n \geq 1} \in D_c^b(X, R)$, the component $\alpha_f(\mathcal{F}): Rf_! \mathcal{F} \to Rf_* \mathcal{F}$ is given level-wise by
$$R\bar{f}_*(\mathbf{R}\nu^{(n)}_{\mathcal{F}_n}): R\bar{f}_* Rj_! \mathcal{F}_n \longrightarrow R\bar{f}_* Rj_* \mathcal{F}_n, \quad n \geq 1.$$

This natural transformation is independent of the choice of compactification up to canonical isomorphism.
\end{definition}

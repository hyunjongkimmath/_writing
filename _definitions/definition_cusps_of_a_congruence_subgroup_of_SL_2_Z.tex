\begin{definition}[Cusps of classical modular curves] \label{definition:cusps_of_a_congruence_subgroup_of_SL_2_Z}
Let $H$ be a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:congruence_subgroup_of_SL_2_Z}{congruence subgroup} of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$. The \hldef{cusps of the classical modular curve associated to $H$} are the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:orbit_of_a_group_action}{orbits} of $\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{Q})$ under the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:modular_action_of_a_subgroup_of_SL_2_Z_on_the_upper_half_plane}{modular action} of $H$, i.e. the elements of the quotient set
$$H \backslash \mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{Q}).$$
This set of cusps is regarded as a subset of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:extended_upper_half_plane}{extended upper half-plane} $\hat{\mathbb{H}} = \mathbb{H} \cup \mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{R})$.
The cusps are finite in number and correspond to the points adjoined to the affine curve $Y_H = H \backslash \bbH$ to form the compactified modular curve $X_H$.
\end{definition}

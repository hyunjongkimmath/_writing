\begin{definition} \label{definition:metric_induced_by_an_absolute_value_on_a_field}
    Let $K$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field} with an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:absolute_value_on_a_field}{absolute value} $|\cdot|$. 

    By the \hldef{metric induced by $|\cdot|$} we mean the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:extended_metric_induced_by_an_extended_norm_on_a_vector_space_over_a_field_with_absolute_value}{metric induced by $|\cdot|$} regarded as a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:extended_norm_on_a_vector_space_over_a_field_with_absolute_value}{norm} on the $1$-dimensional vector space over the  $(K, |\cdot|)$.

    More explicitly, the metric induced by $|\cdot|$ is the map 
    $$K \times K \to [0, \infty), \quad (a,b) \mapsto |a-b|.$$
\end{definition}

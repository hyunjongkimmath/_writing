\begin{definition} \label{definition:identity_matrix}
For any positive integer $n$ and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring} $R$, the \hldef{identity matrix} \hl{$I_n$} (or \hl{$I_n(R)$} when the ring must be specified) is the $n \times n$ diagonal matrix with entries
$(I_n)_{ij} = \delta_{ij} = \begin{cases} 1_R & \text{if } i = j \\ 0_R & \text{if } i \neq j \end{cases}$.

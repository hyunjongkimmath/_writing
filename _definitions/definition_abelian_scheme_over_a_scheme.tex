\begin{definition}[Abelian scheme] \label{definition:abelian_scheme_over_a_scheme}
Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme}. An \hldef{abelian scheme over $S$} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:proper_morphism_of_schemes}{proper} and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:smooth_morphism_of_schemes}{smooth} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebraic_group_scheme_over_a_scheme}{group scheme} 
$$\pi: A \to S$$ 
with \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:geometrically_connected_scheme_over_a_field}{geometrically connected} fibers. Each \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:geometric_fiber_of_a_morphism_of_schemes_at_a_geometric_point}{geometric fiber} $A_{\overline{s}}$ (over a geometric point $\overline{s} \to S$) is then an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_variety_over_a_field}{abelian variety}.

In particular, over a field, an abelian scheme is precisely an abelian variety.
\end{definition}

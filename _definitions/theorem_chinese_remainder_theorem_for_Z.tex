\begin{theorem} \label{theorem:chinese_remainder_theorem_for_Z}
Let $N$ be a positive integer with prime factorisation $N = p_1^{e_1} \cdots p_r^{e_r}$, where $p_1, \ldots, p_r$ are distinct primes and $e_1, \ldots, e_r \geq 1$.
The canonical \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring_homomorphism}{ring homomorphism}
$\hlin{\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z} \to \prod_{i=1}^r \mathbb{Z}/p_i^{e_i}\mathbb{Z}}$
induced by the projections $\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}/p_i^{e_i}\mathbb{Z}$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring_homomorphism}{ring isomorphism}.
In particular, for pairwise coprime positive integers $m_1, \ldots, m_r$ with $M = m_1 \cdots m_r$, the map
$\hlin{\mathbb{Z}/M\mathbb{Z} \to \prod_{i=1}^r \mathbb{Z}/m_i\mathbb{Z}}$
is a ring isomorphism.
On the level of unit groups, this yields an isomorphism of abelian groups:
$\hlin{(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^\times \cong \prod_{i=1}^r (\mathbb{Z}/p_i^{e_i}\mathbb{Z})^\times}$.
\end{theorem}

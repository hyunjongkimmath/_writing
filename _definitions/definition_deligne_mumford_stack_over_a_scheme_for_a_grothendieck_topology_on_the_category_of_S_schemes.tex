Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme}. Let $\tau$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{Grothendieck topology} on \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:representable_by_schemes_for_a_morphism_of_presheaves_on_Sch_S}{$\mathit{Sch}/S$} for which surjectivity and \'etaleness are preserved under base change and are \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:tau_local_property_of_a_morphism_of_schemes_over_a_scheme}{$\tau$-local on the base}. 
\TextIfExists{proposition:list_of_properties_of_morphisms_of_schemes_over_a_base_preserved_under_base_change_and_fpqc_local_on_the_base}{\footnote{For example, $\tau$ can be any Grothendieck topology coarser than the fpqc topology thanks to \CrefIfExists{proposition:list_of_properties_of_morphisms_of_schemes_over_a_base_preserved_under_base_change_and_fpqc_local_on_the_base}.}}

A \hldef{Deligne--Mumford (DM) stack over $S$ for $\tau$} or a \hldef{$\tau$-Deligne--Mumford (DM) stack} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:stack_in_groupoids_over_a_site}{stack} $\mathcal{X}$ over the 
site $(\mathit{Sch}/S, \tau)$ such that 
% \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:big_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{big \'etale site} $(\mathit{Sch}/S)_{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}$ such that
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:diagonal_morphism_of_an_object_over_an_object_in_a_category}{diagonal morphism} $\Delta : \mathcal{X} \to \mathcal{X} \times_S \mathcal{X}$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:representable_by_algebraic_spaces_for_a_1_morphism_of_stacks_in_groupoids_over_a_site_on_S_schemes}{representable by algebraic spaces}.
    \item There exists a scheme $U$ and a morphism $U \to \mathcal{X}$ that is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:representable_by_algebraic_spaces_for_a_1_morphism_of_stacks_in_groupoids_over_a_site_on_S_schemes}{representable by algebraic spaces}, \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:property_of_representable_1_morphism_of_categories_fibered_in_groupoids_over_asite_on_the_category_of_schemes_over_a_scheme}{surjective}, and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:property_of_representable_1_morphism_of_categories_fibered_in_groupoids_over_asite_on_the_category_of_schemes_over_a_scheme}{étale} (i.e., an étale atlas). 
\end{enumerate}

In other words, a DM stack is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:artin_stack_over_a_scheme_for_a_grothendieck_topology_on_the_category_of_S_schemes}{algebraic stack} with an \'etale atlas.

A \hldef{Deligne-Mumford stack} usually refers to one over the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:big_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{big \'etale topology} on $\mathit{Sch}/S$.

It should be noted that classical definitions of DM stacks further require the diagonal morphism $\Delta$ to be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:property_of_representable_1_morphism_of_categories_fibered_in_groupoids_over_asite_on_the_category_of_schemes_over_a_scheme}{quasi-compact, and separated}. 
\end{definition}

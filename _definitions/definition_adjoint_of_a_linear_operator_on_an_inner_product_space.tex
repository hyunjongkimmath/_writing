\begin{definition} \label{definition:adjoint_of_a_linear_operator_on_an_inner_product_space}
    Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional inner product space over $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, and let $T: V \to V$ be a linear endomorphism.

    The unique linear endomorphism $T^*: V \to V$ satisfying
    $$\hlin{\langle T(v), w \rangle = \langle v, T^*(w) \rangle \quad \text{for all } v, w \in V}$$
    is called the \hldef{adjoint of $T$}.

    If $\mathcal{B}$ is an orthonormal basis of $V$, then $[T^*]_{\mathcal{B}}^{\mathcal{B}} = [T]_{\mathcal{B}}^{\mathcal{B}\,*}$, where the right-hand side denotes the conjugate transpose of the matrix.

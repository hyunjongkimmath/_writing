\begin{definition} \label{definition:vector_space_of_modular_and_cusp_forms}

Let $k$ be a positive even integer and let $\Gamma \leq \mathrm{SL}_2(\bbZ)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:congruence_subgroup_of_SL_2_Z}{congruence subgroup}. The set of all \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:modular_form_and_cusp_form_of_weight_k_with_respect_to_a_congruence_subgroup_of_SL_2_Z}{modular forms of weight $k$ with respect to $\Gamma$} forms a complex \CrefAndHyperref{definition:vector_space_over_a_field}{vector space}, denoted by
$$\hlin{M_k(\Gamma).}$$

The subset of those modular forms that are also \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:modular_form_and_cusp_form_of_weight_k_with_respect_to_a_congruence_subgroup_of_SL_2_Z}{cusp forms} is a linear subspace, denoted by
$$\hlin{S_k(\Gamma) \subseteq M_k(\Gamma).}$$

The quotient vector space
$$\hlin{\mathcal{E}_k(\Gamma) = M_k(\Gamma) / S_k(\Gamma)}$$
is called the \hldef{space of Eisenstein series of weight $k$ for $\Gamma$}. (Not every element of $\mathcal{E}_k(\Gamma)$ is represented by an Eisenstein series in the classical sense, but the name reflects that the image of the standard Eisenstein series spans this quotient when it exists.)

For each cusp $s$ of $\Gamma$, there is a well-defined \hldef{constant term functional}
$$\operatorname{ct}_s : S_k(\Gamma)^\perp \longrightarrow \bbC,$$
where $S_k(\Gamma)^\perp \subseteq M_k(\Gamma)$ denotes the subspace of modular forms whose Fourier expansion at every cusp other than $s$ has zero constant term. The dimension of $\mathcal{E}_k(\Gamma)$ is bounded above by the number of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cusps_of_a_congruence_subgroup_of_SL_2_Z}{cusps} of $\Gamma$.

\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:continuous_functor_of_sites}
Let $(\calC,J)$ and $(\calD,K)$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{sites} (or \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_site_locally_small_site_U_site_U_small_site_U_topology}{$\mathscr{U}$-sites} if a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_universe}{Grothendieck universe} $\mathscr{U}$ is available). 

A functor $u: \calC \to \calD$ is said to be a \hldef{continuous functor of sites} if for every object $U$ of $\calC$ and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{covering sieve} $S \in J(U)$, the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sieve_on_an_object_in_a_category}{sieve} in $\calD$ \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_generated_by_a_pretopology}{generated by} the image $\{u(\phi) | \phi \in S\}$ is a covering sieve in $K(u(U))$. 

Note that this differs from the definition of continuous morphisms of sites given in {\cite[Expos\'e III, D\'efinition 1.1]{SGA4_I}}. See also \Cref{proposition:equivalent_conditions_for_continuous_functor_of_sites}. 


\end{definition}

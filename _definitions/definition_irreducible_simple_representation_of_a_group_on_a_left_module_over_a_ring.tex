Let $G$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group}, $R$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring with unity}, and $\rho : G \to \operatorname{Aut}_R(M)$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:representation_of_a_group_on_a_module_over_a_not_necessarily_commutative_ring}{representation of $G$ on a left $R$-module} $M$.

\begin{definition}[Endomorphism algebra up to isogeny of an abelian variety over a field] \label{definition:endomorphism_algebra_up_to_isogeny_of_an_abelian_variety_over_a_field}
    Let $k$ be a field and let $A$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_variety_over_a_field}{abelian variety over $k$}. The \hldef{endomorphism ring of $A$}, denoted by $\End(A)$, is the set of morphisms of abelian varieties over $k$ from $A$ to itself. With addition given by the group law on $A$ and multiplication given by composition, $\End(A)$ forms a ring. The \hldef{endomorphism algebra up to isogeny of $A$}, denoted by $\End^0(A)$, is defined by
    $$\hlin{\End^0(A) = \End(A) \otimes_{\Z} \Q}.$$
\end{definition}

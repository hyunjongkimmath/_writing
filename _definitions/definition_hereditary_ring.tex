\begin{definition}[Hereditary Ring] \label{definition:hereditary_ring}
A \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring} $R$ is called \hldef{right (resp. left) hereditary} if it satisfies any of the following equivalent conditions:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Every right (resp. left) \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ideal_of_a_ring}{ideal of $R$} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:projective_bimodule_over_rings}{projective $R$-module}.
    \item Every \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:submodule_of_a_module_over_a_ring}{submodule} of a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:projective_bimodule_over_rings}{projective} right (resp. left) \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:module_of_a_ring}{$R$-module} is projective.
    \item Every submodule of a free right (resp. left) $R$-module is projective.
    \item The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:left_right_global_dimension_of_a_ring}{right (resp. left) global dimension} of $R$ is at most $1$ ($\operatorname{gl\,dim} R \leq 1$).
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

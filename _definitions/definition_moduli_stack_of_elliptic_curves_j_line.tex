Let $\mathcal{M}_{1,1}$ denote the \hldef{moduli stack of elliptic curves}. For any base scheme $S$, the groupoid $\mathcal{M}_{1,1}(S)$ has:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textit{Objects:} Elliptic curves over $S$, i.e., proper flat morphisms $\pi: E \to S$ whose geometric fibers are smooth projective curves of genus~$1$, together with a section $0: S \to E$ serving as the identity.
    \item \textit{Morphisms:} Isomorphisms of elliptic curves over $S$ (i.e., $S$-scheme isomorphisms preserving the zero section).
\end{itemize}

The \hldef{$j$-invariant} defines a morphism of stacks
$$ j: \mathcal{M}_{1,1} \to \mathbb{A}^1_{\mathbb{Z}} $$
which is an isomorphism over $\operatorname{Spec}(\mathbb{Z}[1/6])$ away from the points with extra automorphisms (corresponding to $j=0$ and $j=1728$). The affine line $\mathbb{A}^1_{\mathbb{Z}}$, viewed as the coarse moduli space of $\mathcal{M}_{1,1}$, is often called the \hldef{$j$-line}.
\end{definition}

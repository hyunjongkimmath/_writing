\begin{definition} \label{definition:rost_nilpotence_for_a_chow_motive_over_a_field_with_coefficients_in_a_commutative_ring}
Let $k$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}, $R$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{commutative ring}, and $\mathfrak{Chow}(k, R)$ the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category_of_relative_chow_motives_over_a_noetherian_base_and_coefficients_in_a_commutative_ring}{category of Chow motives over $k$ with coefficients in $R$}. 
A motive $M \in \mathfrak{Chow}(k, R)$ satisfies \hldef{Rost nilpotence} if for every field extension $E/k$, the ideal $\mathcal{I}_{E/k}(M)$ (\Cref{definition:ideal_of_endomorphisms_for_a_chow_motive_over_a_field_with_coefficients_in_a_commutative_ring}) of endomoprhisms that vanish over $E$, i.e. the kernel of the restriction morphism 
$$\mathrm{res}_{E/k}: \mathrm{End}_k(M) \to \mathrm{End}_E(M_E)$$ 
is a nil ideal.

\end{definition}

    Let $(R, \lambda)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_ring_with_respect_to_an_ideal}{complete} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{DVR} such that $R/\lambda^n$ is finite for all $n \geq 1$. Consider sheaves on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{(small) \'etale site} of $X$.

    The category \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:derived_category_of_bounded_constructible_complexes_of_adic_sheaves_with_integral_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}{$\Dbc(X, R)$} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:triangulated_category}{triangulated category} (\Cref{proposition:bdd_constructible_derived_category_with_integral_adic_coefficients_is_triangulated}). Let $\mathbb{D}_X(-): \Dbc(X, R) \to \Dbc(X, R)$ denote the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:verdier_dual_of_an_object_in_the_bdd_constr_derived_category_of_adic_sheaves_with_integral_coefficients_on_a_finite_type_scheme_over_a_noetherian_scheme}{Verdier dual functor} on $X$ relative to $\Spec k$.

    The \hldef{self-dual (middle) perverse $t$-structure on $\Dbc(X, R)$} is the pair of strictly full subcategories $(\pDleqzero(X, R), \pDgeqzero(X, R))$ of $\Dbc(X, R)$ defined by

    \hlalign{
    \begin{align*}
        \pDleqzero(X, R) &:= \left\{ K \in \Dbc(X, R) : \dim \Supp\bigl(\mathscr{H}^{-i}(K)\bigr) \leq i \text{ for all } i \in \bbZ \right\}, \\
        \pDgeqzero(X, R) &:= \left\{ K \in \Dbc(X, R) : \dim \Supp\bigl(\mathscr{H}^{-i}(\mathbb{D}_X(K))\bigr) \leq i \text{ for all } i \in \bbZ \right\},
    \end{align*}
    }

    This is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:t_structure_on_a_triangulated_category}{$t$-structure} on $\Dbc(X, R)$\CrefIfExists{theorem:perverse_t_structure_on_bdd_constructible_derived_category_with_integral_coefficients_on_a_finite_type_scheme_over_a_separably_closed_or_finite_field_is_a_t_structure}. Its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:t_structure_on_a_triangulated_category}{core}, denoted by \hl{$\Perv(X, R)$}, is called the \hldef{category of perverse sheaves with integral adic coefficients on $X$}.

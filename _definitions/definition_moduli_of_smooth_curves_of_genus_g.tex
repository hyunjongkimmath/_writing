\begin{definition}[$\mathcal{M}_g$: moduli of smooth curves of genus $g$] \label{definition:moduli_of_smooth_curves_of_genus_g}
    Let $g$ be a non-negative integer. The \hldef{moduli of smooth curves of genus $g$} is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:moduli_problem_on_schemes}{moduli problem} \hl{$\calM_g$} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:smooth_morphism_of_schemes}{smooth}, \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:projective_variety_over_a_field}{projective}, \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:disconnected_and_connected_topological_spaces}{connected} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebraic_curve_over_a_field}{curves} of genus $g$. More precisely, for a given scheme $S$, it is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category_fibered_in_groupoids_over_a_category}{category fibered in groupoids}

    $$\calM_g \to (\operatorname{Sch}/S)_{\text{fppf}}$$

    In fact, it is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:artin_stack_over_a_scheme_for_a_grothendieck_topology_on_the_category_of_S_schemes}{Artin stack} (for the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:big_fppf_site_of_a_scheme}{fppf topology}) over $\operatorname{Spec} \bbZ$ and hence an Artin stack when base changed to any \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme} $S$. It is furthermore a DM stack for $g \geq 2$ over any scheme $S$. For $g = 1$, it is a DM stack over $\Spec \bbZ[1/6]$.

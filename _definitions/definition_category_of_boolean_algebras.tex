\begin{definition} \label{definition:category_of_boolean_algebras}
The \hldef{category of Boolean algebras} is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category}{category} whose objects are \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:boolean_algebra}{Boolean algebras} and the morphisms are \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:morphism_of_boolean_algebras}{Boolean algebra morphisms}. It is often denoted by \hl{$\mathsf{Bool}$}.
\end{definition}

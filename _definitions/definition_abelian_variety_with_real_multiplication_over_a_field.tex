\begin{definition}[Abelian variety with real multiplication over a field] \label{definition:abelian_variety_with_real_multiplication_over_a_field}
    Let $k$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field} and let $A$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_variety_over_a_field}{abelian variety over $k$} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dimension_of_a_scheme}{dimension} $g$. We say that $A$ has \hldef{real multiplication (RM)} if there exists a commutative semisimple $\Q$-subalgebra $F \subset \End^0(A)$ (\Cref{definition:endomorphism_algebra_up_to_isogeny_of_an_abelian_variety_over_a_field}) such that $[F:\Q] = g$, where $\End^0(A) = \End(A) \otimes_{\Z} \Q$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:endomorphism_algebra_up_to_isogeny_of_an_abelian_variety_over_a_field}{endomorphism algebra up to isogeny}.

    If $A$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:simple_abelian_variety_over_a_field}{simple}, then $F$ is a \hldef{totally real number field} (\CrefAndHyperref{definition:totally_real_and_totally_imaginary_number_fields}{totally real and totally imaginary number fields}) and it is the full endomorphism algebra, i.e. $\End^0(A) = F$. In general, $F$ decomposes as a product of totally real number fields corresponding to the simple factors of $A$ up to \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:isogeny_of_abelian_varieties_over_a_field}{isogeny}.
\end{definition}

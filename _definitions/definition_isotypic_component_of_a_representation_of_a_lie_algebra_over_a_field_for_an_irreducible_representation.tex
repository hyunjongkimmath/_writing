\begin{definition} \label{definition:isotypic_component_of_a_representation_of_a_lie_algebra_over_a_field_for_an_irreducible_representation}

    Let $k$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}, $\mathfrak{g}$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:lie_algebra_over_a_field}{Lie algebra over $k$}, and $(\rho, V)$ a finite-dimensional \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:representation_of_a_lie_algebra_on_a_vector_space_over_a_field}{representation of $\mathfrak{g}$}. Let $(\sigma, S)$ be an irreducible representation of $\mathfrak{g}$.

    The \hldef{$S$-isotypic component of $V$} is the subspace
    $$\hlin{V_{(S)} := \sum \{W \subseteq V \mid W \text{ is a subrepresentation and } W \cong S\}.}$$
    It is the largest subrepresentation of $V$ that is a direct sum of copies of $S$.

    If $(\rho, V)$ is semisimple, then for each isomorphism class $[S]$ of irreducible representations appearing in $V$, the isotypic component $V_{(S)}$ is a direct summand, and
    $$ V = \bigoplus_{[S]} V_{(S)},$$
    where the sum runs over a set of representatives of isomorphism classes of irreducible constituents. Each $V_{(S)} \cong S^{\oplus m_S}$ for some multiplicity $m_S \in \mathbb{N}$, called the \hldef{multiplicity of $S$ in $V$}.
\end{definition}

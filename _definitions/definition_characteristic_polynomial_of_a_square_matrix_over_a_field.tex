\begin{definition} \label{definition:characteristic_polynomial_of_a_square_matrix_over_a_field}
Let $F$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}, let $n$ be a positive integer, and let $A \in M_{n,n}(F)$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:matrix_over_a_ring}{$n \times n$ matrix over $F$}.

The \hldef{characteristic polynomial of $A$} is the monic polynomial
$$\hlin{p_A(t) = \det(tI_n - A)} \in F[t],$$
where $I_n$ is the $n \times n$ identity matrix.

This definition is compatible with definition of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:characteristic_polynomial_of_an_endomorphism_of_a_vector_space_over_a_field}{characteristic polynomial} of an endomorphism of an $n$-dimension vector space: if one identifies $A$ with the endomorphism of $F^n$ it defines via left multiplication, then $p_A(t)$ coincides with the characteristic polynomial of that endomorphism.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:compatible_system_of_representations_on_a_profinite_group_equipped_with_dense_frobenius_elements}
Let $G$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:profinite_group}{profinite group} endowed with a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dense_subset_of_a_topological_space}{dense subset} of "Frobenius" elements $\{F_\alpha\}_{\alpha \in A}$. Let $\{ \rho_\ell: G \to \mathrm{GL}_n(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell) \}_{\ell \in L}$ be a collection of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:continuous_representation_of_a_topological_group_on_a_topological_module_over_a_topological_ring}{continuous representations} indexed by a set of rational primes $L$. 

The system $\{\rho_\ell\}$ is called a \hldef{compatible system} if for every $\alpha \in A$:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:characteristic_polynomial_of_an_endomorphism_of_a_vector_space_over_a_field}{characteristic polynomial} of $\rho_\ell(F_\alpha)$ has coefficients in $\mathbb{Q}$.
    \item The characteristic polynomial does not depend on the prime $\ell$.
\end{enumerate}
In the specific case of the Galois group $G = G_K = \Gal(\overline{K}/K)$ for a number field $K$, the elements $F_\alpha$ are typically Frobenius representatives for the primes of $K$.

\end{definition}

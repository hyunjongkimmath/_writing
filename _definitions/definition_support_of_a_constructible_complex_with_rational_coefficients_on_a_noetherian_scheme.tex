\begin{definition}[Support of a constructible complex with rational coefficients on a noetherian scheme] \label{definition:support_of_a_constructible_complex_with_rational_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}

    Let $(R, \lambda)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_ring_with_respect_to_an_ideal}{complete} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{DVR} such that $R/\lambda^n$ is finite for all $n \geq 1$. Let $E$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_fractions_of_an_integral_domain}{fraction field} of $R$. Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{noetherian scheme}, and consider sheaves on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{(small) \'etale site} of $X$. Let $K \in \Dbc(X, E)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:derived_category_of_cohomologically_bounded_constructible_sheaves_with_rational_adic_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}{bounded constructible complex with rational coefficients}.

    By \Cref{definition:derived_category_of_cohomologically_bounded_constructible_sheaves_with_rational_adic_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}, $K$ has the same underlying objects as an object of $\Dbc(X, R)$. Let $\mathscr{H}^i(K)$ denote the $i$-th cohomology $\lambda$-adic sheaf of $K$. The \hldef{support of $K$} is
    $$\hlin{\Supp(K) := \overline{\bigcup_{i \in \bbZ} \Supp(\mathscr{H}^i(K))}}$$
    (\Cref{definition:support_of_a_lambda_adic_sheaf_with_integral_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}) Since $K$ has cohomologically bounded constructible cohomology sheaves, the union is finite and $\Supp(K)$ is a closed subset of $X$.
\end{definition}

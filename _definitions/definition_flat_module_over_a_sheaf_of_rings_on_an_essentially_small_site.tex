\begin{definition} \label{definition:flat_module_over_a_sheaf_of_rings_on_an_essentially_small_site}
    Let $(\calC, J, \calO)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ringed_site}{ringed site} whose underlying \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{site} is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:essentially_small_site}{essentially small}.

    A \CrefAndHyperref{definition:module_over_a_sheaf_of_rings_on_a_site}{left (resp. right) $\calO$-module} $\calF$ is called \hldef{flat} if the functor $- \otimes_{\calO} \calF: \Mod_{\calO} \to \Sh(\calC, J; \Ab)$ (resp. $\calF \otimes_{\calO} -: {}_{\calO} \Mod \to \Sh(\calC, J; \Ab)$) (\Cref{definition:sheafified_tensor_product_of_sheaves_of_modules_of_sheaves_of_rings_on_a_site_with_a_small_topologically_generating_family}) (\Cref{definition:sheaf_on_a_site}) is \CrefAndHyperref{definition:exact_functor_between_abelian_categories}{exact}. 

    Equivalently, $\calF$ is flat if and only if it is \CrefAndHyperref{definition:flat_object_in_an_abelian_category_with_respect_to_a_right_exact_monoidal_product_functor}{flat} with respect to $\otimes$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:punctured_disk_in_the_complex_plane}
Let $z_0 \in \bbC$ and $r > 0$.
The \hldef{punctured disk of radius $r$ centered at $z_0$} is the subset
$$\hlin{D^*(z_0, r) = \{z \in \bbC : 0 < |z - z_0| < r\}}$$
of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_complex_numbers}{field of complex numbers} $\bbC$, where $|\cdot|$ denotes the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complex_absolute_value}{complex absolute value}. Equivalently, $D^*(z_0, r) = D(z_0, r) \setminus \{z_0\}$\CrefIfExists{definition:complex_disk_open_and_closed_in_the_complex_plane}.
\end{definition}

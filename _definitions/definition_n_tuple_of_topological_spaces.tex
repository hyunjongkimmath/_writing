\begin{definition} \label{definition:n_tuple_of_topological_spaces}
    An \hldef{$n$-tuple of topological spaces} is a sequence $(X_1, X_2, \dots, X_n)$ of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_space}{topological spaces} such that $X_1 \supseteq X_2 \supseteq \dots \supseteq X_n$.
\end{definition}

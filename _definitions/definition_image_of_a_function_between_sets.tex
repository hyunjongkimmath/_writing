\begin{definition} \label{definition:image_of_a_function_between_sets}

Let $X$ and $Y$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:zermelo_fraenkel_set_theory}{sets} and let $f : X \to Y$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:function_of_sets}{function}.

The \hldef{image of $f$} (also called the \hldef{range of $f$}) is the subset of $Y$ defined by
$$\hlin{\operatorname{im}(f) := \{ f(x) : x \in X \} \subseteq Y.}$$
Equivalently, $\operatorname{im}(f) = \{ y \in Y : \exists x \in X \text{ such that } f(x) = y \}$.

If $A \subseteq X$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:subset_of_a_set}{subset}, then the \hldef{image of $A$ under $f$} is the subset of $Y$ defined by
$$\hlin{f(A) := \{ f(a) : a \in A \} \subseteq Y.}$$
In particular, $\operatorname{im}(f) = f(X)$.
\end{definition}

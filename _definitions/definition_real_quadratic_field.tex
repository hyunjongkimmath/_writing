\begin{definition}[Real Quadratic Field] \label{definition:real_quadratic_field}
A \hldef{real quadratic field} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:number_field}{number field} $K$ such that $[K : \mathbb{Q}] = 2$ and $K$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:totally_real_and_totally_imaginary_number_fields}{totally real}. Equivalently, $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{d})$ for some squarefree integer $d > 0$.

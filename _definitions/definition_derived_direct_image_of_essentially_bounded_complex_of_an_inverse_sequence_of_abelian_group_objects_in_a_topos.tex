\begin{definition}[{\cite[After Lemma 1.1.]{ekedahl_af}}] \label{definition:derived_direct_image_of_essentially_bounded_complex_of_an_inverse_sequence_of_abelian_group_objects_in_a_topos}

    Assume \Cref{context:ekedahl_af_ringed_topos_category_of_inverse_sequences}. let $M \in D^{e+}(\mathrm{Pro}_\bbN(S), \bbZ_\bullet)$ (\Cref{definition:essentially_zero_inverse_sequence_essentially_bounded_complex_of_sheaves_of_abelian_groups_on_a_site}).

    In either case, the pro-object $R\pi_* \tau(M)$ obtained by applying $R\pi_*$ (\Cref{context:ekedahl_af_topos_morphisms_to_and_from_Pro_N_S_to_S}) (\Cref{definition:total_right_derived_direct_image_functor_for_morphism_of_ringed_topoi}) to $\tau(M)$ (\Cref{notation:ekedahl_af_tau_M_either_pro_object_of_truncations_or_M_depending_on_whether_ekedahls_condition_A_holds}) termwise is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:essentially_constant_complex_of_abelian_group_objects_in_the_category_of_inverse_direct_sequences_of_objects_in_a_topos}{essentially constant} by \Cref{lemma:derived_direct_image_to_underlying_topos_of_cohomologically_bounded_below_complexes_of_ab_group_objects_in_inv_direct_sequence_cat_on_topos_vanishes}. By abuse of language let \hl{$R\pi_* M$} denote any limit of this \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:system_in_a_category_indexed_by_a_directed_poset}{pro-system}. The functor $R\pi_*$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:exact_functor_between_triangulated_categories}{triangulated functor} 
    $$D^{e+}(\mathrm{Pro}_{\bbN}(S), R_\bullet) \to D^+(S, R),$$
    where $D^+(S, R)$ is the derived category of modules of the ringed topos (S,R) as per \Cref{notation:derived_category_of_category_of_modules_of_a_ringed_site}. 
\end{definition}

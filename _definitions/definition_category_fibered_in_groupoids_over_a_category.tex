\begin{definition} \label{definition:category_fibered_in_groupoids_over_a_category}
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category}{category}.
A \hldef{category fibred in groupoids over $\mathcal{C}$} or a \hldef{CFG over $\calC$} for short, is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:fibered_category_over_a_category}{fibered category} $p: \calX \to \calC$ such that for every object $U$ of $\calC$, the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category_over_a_category_and_fiber_category_over_an_object}{fiber category} $\calX_U$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:groupoid}{groupoid}. 

A \hldef{morphism of categories fibred in groupoids over $\calC$} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:morphism_of_fibered_categories_over_a_category}{morphism as fibred categories} over $\calC$.

%     \item For any pair of morphisms $\phi : y \to x$ and $\psi : z \to x$ in $\mathcal{X}$, and any morphism $g : p(z) \to p(y)$ in $\mathcal{C}$ such that $p(\phi) \circ g = p(\psi)$, there exists a unique morphism $\chi : z \to y$ in $\mathcal{X}$ over $g$ (i.e., $p(\chi) = g$) such that $\phi \circ \chi = \psi$.
% \end{enumerate}

\end{definition}

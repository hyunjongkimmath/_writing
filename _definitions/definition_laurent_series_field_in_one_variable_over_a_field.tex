\begin{definition}[Laurent series field in one variable over a field] \label{definition:laurent_series_field_in_one_variable_over_a_field}
Let $k$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}. The \hldef{field of Laurent series in one variable over $k$}, denoted \hl{$k((X))$}, is the special case of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:hahn_series_over_a_commutative_ring_with_exponents_in_a_totally_ordered_abelian_group}{Hahn series field} where the exponent group is $\Gamma = (\mathbb{Z}, +, \leq)$ with its natural total order.

Elements of $k((X))$ are formal sums
$$\sum_{n=k}^{\infty} a_n X^n,$$
where $k \in \mathbb{Z}$ (the support is bounded below) and each $a_n \in k$. The field $k((X))$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_fractions_of_an_integral_domain}{fraction field} of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:formal_power_series_ring_in_one_variable_over_a_commutative_ring}{formal power series ring} $k \llbracket X \rrbracket$.
\end{definition}

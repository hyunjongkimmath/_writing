\begin{definition} \label{definition:category_of_stone_spaces}
The \hldef{category of Stone spaces} is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category}{category} denoted by \hl{$\mathsf{Stone}$} whose objects are \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:stone_space}{Stone spaces} (\CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:compact_topological_space}{compact}, \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:separation_axioms_of_topology}{Hausdorff}, and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:totally_disconnected_topological_space}{totally disconnected} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_space}{topological spaces}) and the morphisms are \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:continuous_map_of_topological_spaces}{continuous maps} between them.
\end{definition}

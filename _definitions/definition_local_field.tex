\begin{definition}[Local field] \label{definition:local_field}
A \hldef{local field} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field} $K$ with a nontrivial \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:absolute_value_on_a_ring_valued_in_an_ordered_semiring}{absolute value} $|\cdot|$ such that $K$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_compact_topological_space}{locally compact} under the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topology_induced_by_an_extended_norm_on_a_module_over_a_commutative_ring_with_absolute_value_valued_in_an_ordered_semiring}{topology induced by $|\cdot|$}; we almost always treat a local field as a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_field}{topological field} with this topology. 

The local field $K$ is called \hldef{archimedean} if its absolute value is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:archimedean_absolute_value_on_a_field}{archimedean} and is called \hldef{non-archimedean} if its absolute value is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:archimedean_absolute_value_on_a_field}{non-archimedean}.

\end{definition}

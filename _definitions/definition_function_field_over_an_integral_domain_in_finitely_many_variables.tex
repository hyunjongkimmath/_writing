\begin{definition} \label{definition:function_field_over_an_integral_domain_in_finitely_many_variables}
    Let $R$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:domain_integral_domain}{integral domain}. Let $n \geq 1$ be an integer.
    The \hldef{function field over $R$ in $n$ variables $x_1,\ldots,x_n$} is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field} \hl{$R(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$} defined as the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_fractions_of_an_integral_domain}{field of fractions} of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:polynomial_ring_over_a_commutative_ring}{polynomial ring} $R[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$, which is an integral domain \CrefIfExists{lemma:polynomial_ring_over_an_integral_domain_is_an_integral_domain}.

    $R(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ is also called the \hldef{field of rational numbers over $R$ in $n$ variables}. An element of $R(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ is called a \hldef{rational function over $R$ in $n$ variables}.
\end{definition}

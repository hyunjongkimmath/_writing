\begin{definition} \label{definition:wild_inertia_group_of_a_finite_galois_extension_of_valuation_fields}
    Assume \Cref{context:extension_of_valuation_fields} and further assume that $L/K$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_field_extension}{finite} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:galois_extension_of_fields}{Galois extension}. Let $p$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:characteristic_of_a_ring}{characteristic} of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:residue_field_of_a_ring_at_a_point}{residue field} $k_v$ (with the convention $p = 0$ if $k_v$ has characteristic zero).

    Let $I_w$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:inertia_group_of_a_finite_galois_extension_of_valuation_fields}{inertia group} of $L/K$.
    The \hldef{wild inertia group of $L/K$} \hl{$P_w$} is the unique smallest \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:normal_subgroup_of_a_group}{normal subgroup} of $I_w$ such that the quotient $I_w/P_w$ has order prime to $p$.
    Equivalently, it is the kernel of the natural surjection onto the maximal prime-to-$p$ quotient of $I_w$.
    $$P_w = \bigcap \{N \triangleleft I_w : [I_w:N] \text{ is prime to } p\}$$

    When $p = 0$, one has $P_w = \{1\}$. When $p > 0$, the quotient $I_w/P_w$ is a finite cyclic group of order prime to $p$, and $P_w$ is a Sylow $p$-subgroup of $I_w$.
\end{definition}

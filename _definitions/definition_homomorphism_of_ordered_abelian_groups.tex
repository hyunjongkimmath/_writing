    A \hldef{homomorphism of ordered abelian groups} from $(G,+,\leq_G)$ to $(H,+,\leq_H)$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group_homomorphism}{group homomorphism} $f : G \to H$ that preserves the partial order: for all $a,b \in G$, if $a \leq_G b$, then $f(a) \leq_H f(b)$.

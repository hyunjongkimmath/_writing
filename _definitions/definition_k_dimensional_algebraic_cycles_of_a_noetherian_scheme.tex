\begin{definition} \label{definition:k_dimensional_algebraic_cycles_of_a_noetherian_scheme}


Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{Noetherian scheme}. 
The \hldef{group of $k$-dimensional algebraic cycles}, denoted \hl{$Z_k(X)$}, is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:free_abelian_group_generated_by_a_set}{free abelian group generated by} the set of all \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dimension_of_a_topological_space}{$k$-dimensional} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:open_and_closed_subscheme_of_a_scheme}{closed} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:integral_scheme}{integral} subschemes $V \subseteq X$. In other words, an element $\alpha \in Z_k(X)$ is a formal sum $\sum n_i [V_i]$, where $n_i \in \mathbb{Z}$ and the $[V_i]$ is the \hldef{``prime cycle''} representing $k$-dimension closed integral subschemes of $X$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:kth_chow_group_chow_group_of_codimension_p_of_a_noetherian_scheme}
Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{Noetherian scheme}. 
The \hldef{$k$-th Chow group of $X$}, denoted \hl{$A_k(X)$}, is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:quotient_of_a_group_by_a_normal_subgroup}{quotient group} $Z_k(X) / \sim$ of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:k_dimensional_algebraic_cycles_of_a_noetherian_scheme}{$k$-dimensional cycles} modulo \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:rationally_equivalent_k_dimensional_algebraic_cycles_of_a_noetherian_scheme}{rational equivalence}. 
If $X$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:pure_dimensional_scheme}{equidimensional} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dimension_of_a_scheme}{dimension} $n$, we define the \hldef{Chow group of codimension $p$} to be \hl{$A^p(X) = A_{n-p}(X)$}.
\end{definition}

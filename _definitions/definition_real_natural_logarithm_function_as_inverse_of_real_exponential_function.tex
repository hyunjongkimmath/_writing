\begin{definition} \label{definition:real_natural_logarithm_function_as_inverse_of_real_exponential_function}
The \hldef{(real) natural logarithm function}, denoted by \hl{$\ln : (0, \infty) \to \mathbb{R}$}, is the inverse of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:real_exponential_function_with_eulers_number_as_base}{exponential function} on $\exp: \mathbb{R} \to (0,\infty)$. That is, for $y > 0$,
$$\ln(y) = x \quad \iff \quad y = \exp(x)$$

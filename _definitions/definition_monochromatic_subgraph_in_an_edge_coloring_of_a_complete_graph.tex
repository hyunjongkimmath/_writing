\begin{definition} \label{definition:monochromatic_subgraph_in_an_edge_coloring_of_a_complete_graph}

Let $N$ and $m$ be positive integers. An edge-coloring of the complete graph $K_N$ with $m$ colors is a function $c: E(K_N) \to \{1, 2, \dots, m\}$.

A subgraph $H \subseteq K_N$ is called a \hldef{monochromatic subgraph in color $i$} if all edges of $H$ receive color $i$, that is, $c(e) = i$ for all $e \in E(H)$. A \hldef{monochromatic clique of size $k$ in color $i$} is a monochromatic subgraph in color $i$ that is isomorphic to the complete graph $K_k$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:rationally_equivalent_k_dimensional_relative_algebraic_cycles_of_a_finite_type_scheme_over_a_noetherian_base}

Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{Noetherian base scheme} and let $X$ be a scheme of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_type}{finite type} over $S$. 
Two \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:k_dimensional_relative_algebraic_cycles_of_a_finite_type_scheme_over_a_noetherian_base}{relative $k$-dimensional cycles} $\alpha, \beta \in Z_k(X/S)$ are \hldef{rationally equivalent}, denoted \hl{$\alpha \sim \beta$}, if there exists a finite collection of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:integral_scheme}{integral} subschemes $W_j \subseteq X$ of relative dimension $k+1$ over $S$ and non-zero rational functions $\phi_j \in K(W_j)^*$ such that 
\[ \alpha - \beta = \sum_j [\mathrm{div}(\phi_j)], \]
where the \hldef{divisor cycle} is defined by $[\mathrm{div}(\phi_j)] = \sum_{V} \mathrm{ord}_V(\phi_j) [V]$, with the sum running over integral subschemes $V \subset W_j$ of relative dimension $k$ over $S$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition} \label{definition:acyclic_for_a_sheaf_of_abelian_groups_for_an_open_cover_of_a_topological_space}
    Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_space}{topological space}. Let $\calA$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_category}{abelian category} with \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:has_enough_injectives_or_projectives_for_an_abelian_category}{enough injectives} and such that a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheafification_of_a_presheaf_on_a_topological_space}{sheafification functor}
    $$a: \PreShv(X, \calA) \to \Shv(X, \calA) $$
    exists. In particular, the notion of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_cohomology_objects_of_a_sheaf_on_a_top_space_in_an_ab_catwith_enough_injs_and_sheafification}{sheaf cohomology objects} is well defined. Moreover, these conditions are satisfied for $\calA = \Ab$\CrefIfExists{theorem:examples_of_abelian_categories_with_enough_injectives_or_projectives}\CrefIfExists{theorem:sheafification_for_sheaves_of_sets_on_a_topological_space_exist}.  Let $\calF$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_on_a_topological_space_valued_in_a_category_with_a_terminal_object}{sheaf of} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{abelian groups} on $X$, and let $\calU = \{U_i\}_{i \in I}$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:open_covering_of_a_topological_space}{open cover} of $X$. 

    We say that the open cover $\calU$ is \hldef{acyclic for $\calF$} or a \hldef{Leray cover for $\calF$} if for every finite nonempty intersection $U_{i_0, ldots, i_n} = U_{i_0} \cap \cdots \cap U_{i_n}$ and all $q > 0$, the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_cohomology_objects_of_a_sheaf_on_a_top_space_in_an_ab_catwith_enough_injs_and_sheafification}{sheaf cohomology} $H^q(V,\calF)$ equals $0$.
\end{definition}

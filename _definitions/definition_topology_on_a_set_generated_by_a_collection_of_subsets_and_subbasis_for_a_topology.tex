\begin{definition} \label{definition:topology_on_a_set_generated_by_a_collection_of_subsets_and_subbasis_for_a_topology}
Let $X$ be a set and $\mathcal{S}$ a collection of subsets of $X$. The \hldef{topology on $X$ generated by $\mathcal{S}$}, often denoted \hl{$\tau(\mathcal{S})$}, is the intersection of all topologies on $X$ that contain $\mathcal{S}$. It is the unique \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:coarser_smaller_finer_larger_for_topologies_on_a_set}{coarsest topology} on $X$ in which every element of $\mathcal{S}$ is an open set.

The collection $\mathcal{S}$ is called a \hldef{subbasis for the topology $\tau(\mathcal{S})$}. Alternatively, one might generally call a family $\calS \subseteq \calP(X)$ \CrefIfExists{definition:power_set_of_a_set} a \hldef{subbasis on $X$} if $\bigcup_{S \in \calS} S  = X$ and we would consider $\calS$ the subbasis for the topology $\tau(\calS)$.
\end{definition}

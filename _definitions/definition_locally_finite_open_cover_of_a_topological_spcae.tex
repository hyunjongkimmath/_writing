\begin{definition} \label{definition:locally_finite_open_cover_of_a_topological_spcae}
    Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_space}{topological space} and let $\{U_i\}_{i \in I}$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:open_covering_of_a_topological_space}{open cover} of $X$.
    The open cover $\{U_i\}_{i \in I}$ is said to be \hldef{locally finite} if for every point $x \in X$, there exists an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:neighborhood_and_neighborhood_basis_of_a_point_in_a_topological_space}{open neighborhood} $W$ of $x$ such that $W \cap V_j \neq \emptyset$ for only finitely many indices $j \in J$.
\end{definition}

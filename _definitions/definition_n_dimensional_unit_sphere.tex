\begin{definition} \label{definition:n_dimensional_unit_sphere}
Let $n \ge 0$ be an integer.
The \hldef{$n$-dimensional (unit) sphere}, denoted by \hl{$S^n$}, is the subspace of $\mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ defined by:
$$S^n = \{x \in \mathbb{R}^{n+1} \mid \|x\| = 1\}$$
The sphere $S^n$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:interior_and_boundary_of_topological_space}{boundary} of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:n_dimensional_unit_ball_disk}{$(n+1)$-dimensional disk $D^{n+1}$}. Specifically, $S^0$ consists of the two discrete points $\{-1, 1\}$.

$S^1$ is called the \hldef{unit circle}.

One also often means by an \hldef{$n$-dimensional sphere} any space homeomorphic to $S^n$.
\end{definition}

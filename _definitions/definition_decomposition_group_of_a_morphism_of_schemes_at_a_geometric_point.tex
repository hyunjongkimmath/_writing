\begin{definition} \label{definition:decomposition_group_of_a_morphism_of_schemes_at_a_geometric_point}
    Let $Y \to X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:morphism_of_schemes}{morphism of schemes} where $X$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:disconnected_and_connected_topological_spaces}{connected}. Let $\overline{y}: \operatorname{Spec} \Omega \to Y$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:geometric_point_of_a_scheme}{geometric point} of $Y$ where $\Omega$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:separably_closed_field}{separably closed field}. 
    The \hldef{decomposition subgroup of $\operatorname{Aut}(Y/X)$ at $\overline{y}$}\CrefIfExists{definition:automorphism_group_of_a_scheme_over_a_scheme} is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:stabilizer_of_an_element_of_a_set_under_a_group_action}{stabilizer subgroup} 
    $$D_{\overline{y}} := \{ \sigma \in \operatorname{Aut}(Y/X): \sigma( \overline{y}) = \overline{y} \}$$
    where $\sigma( \overline{y})$ is the geometric point of $Y$ given by $\overline{y}: \Spec \Omega \to Y \xrightarrow{\sigma} Y$. 

    One usually speaks of this notion when $Y \to X$ is a finite \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:galois_morphism_of_schemes}{Galois cover}.
\end{definition}

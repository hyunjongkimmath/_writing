\begin{definition}[Degree and leading coefficient of a polynomial] \label{definition:degree_and_leading_coefficient_of_a_polynomial}
Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:commutative_ring}{commutative ring} and let $f \in R[x]$ be a nonzero polynomial, so $f = a_0 + a_1 x + \cdots + a_n x^n$ with $a_i \in R$ and $a_n \neq 0$. The \hldef{degree} of $f$, denoted $\deg(f)$, is the largest integer $n \geq 0$ such that $a_n \neq 0$. The \hldef{leading coefficient} of $f$, denoted \hl{$\operatorname{lc}(f)$} (or sometimes \hl{$\operatorname{lead}(f)$}), is the coefficient $a_n$.

If $f = 0$ is the zero polynomial, we define $\deg(0) = -\infty$ and the leading coefficient is undefined.
\end{definition}

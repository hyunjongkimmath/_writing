\begin{definition} \label{definition:n_dimensional_torus}
Let $n \ge 1$ be an integer. 
The \hldef{$n$-dimensional torus}, denoted by \hl{$T^n$}, is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_space}{topological space} defined as the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:product_of_sets}{$n$-fold Cartesian product} of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:n_dimensional_unit_sphere}{circle}:
$$T^n = \underbrace{S^1 \times S^1 \times \dots \times S^1}_{n \text{ times}}$$

\end{definition}

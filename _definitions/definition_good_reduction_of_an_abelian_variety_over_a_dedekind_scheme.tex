\begin{definition}[Good reduction of an abelian variety over a Dedekind scheme] \label{definition:good_reduction_of_an_abelian_variety_over_a_dedekind_scheme}
    Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dedekind_scheme}{Dedekind scheme}, let $\eta$ be its generic point, and let $K = \mathcal{O}_{S, \eta}$ be the function field of $S$. Let $A_K$ be an \CrefAndHyperref{definition:abelian_variety_over_a_field}{abelian variety over $K$}, and let $s \in S$ be a closed point.

    We say that $A_K$ has \hldef{good reduction at $s$} if there exists a smooth and proper scheme $\mathcal{A}$ over $S$ whose generic fiber $\mathcal{A}_\eta$ is isomorphic to $A_K$. In this case, the special fiber $\mathcal{A}_s = \mathcal{A} \times_S \Spec k(s)$ (where $k(s) = \mathcal{O}_{S, s} / \mathfrak{m}_s$ is the residue field at $s$) is an abelian variety over $k(s)$, and we say that $\mathcal{A}_s$ is the \hldef{good reduction of $A_K$ at $s$}.

    Equivalently, $A_K$ has \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:good_reduction_of_an_abelian_variety_over_a_dedekind_scheme}{good reduction at $s$} if and only if there is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:neron_model_of_an_abelian_variety_over_the_function_field_of_an_integral_scheme.tex}{N\'eron model} $\calA$ of $A$ over the local ring $\calO_{S,s}$ (or its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:completion_of_an_extended_metric_space}{completion} $\widehat{\calO}_{S,s}$  is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:proper_morphism_of_schemes}{proper} (or equivalently an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_scheme_over_a_scheme}{abelian scheme}).
\end{definition}

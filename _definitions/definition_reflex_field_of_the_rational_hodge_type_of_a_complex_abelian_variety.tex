\begin{definition}[Reflex Field of the Rational Hodge Type of a Complex Abelian Variety] \label{definition:reflex_field_of_the_rational_hodge_type_of_a_complex_abelian_variety}

    Let $A$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_variety_over_a_field}{abelian variety} over $\mathbb{C}$ of dimension $g$, with \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:endomorphism_algebra_up_to_isogeny_of_an_abelian_variety_over_a_field}{rational endomorphism algebra} $D = \End^0(A)$. Let $\xi: \mathbb{G}_{m,\mathbb{R}} \to D_\mathbb{R}$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:rational_hodge_type_of_a_complex_abelian_variety}{rational Hodge type} of $A$, a $D(\mathbb{Q})$-conjugacy class of cocharacters.

    The \hldef{reflex field of the rational Hodge type $\xi$}, denoted by \hl{$E^*(\xi)$}, is the smallest number field over which the conjugacy class of $\xi$ becomes rational when viewed as a cocharacter of the algebraic group $D_\mathbb{R}$. Equivalently, it is the field of moduli of the Hodge structure on $H_1(A(\mathbb{C}), \mathbb{Q})$ equipped with the action of $D$.

    When $D = E$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cm_field}{CM field} and $\xi$ corresponds to a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cm_type_and_reflex_field_of_a_cm_type}{CM type} $(E, \Phi)$, the reflex field $E^*(\xi)$ coincides with the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cm_type_and_reflex_field_of_a_cm_type}{reflex field of the CM type $(E, \Phi)$}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Based loop space] \label{definition:based_loop_space_of_a_pointed_topological_space}
    Let $(Y, y_0)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:pointed_topological_space}{pointed topological space}. 
    The \hldef{based loop space of $Y$}, denoted by \hl{$\Omega Y$}, is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:compact_open_topology_on_the_set_of_continuous_maps_between_topological_spaces_and_function_space}{based mapping space} $\operatorname{Map}_*(S^1, Y)$.
    
    Explicitly, $\Omega Y$ is the subspace of $\operatorname{Map}(I, Y)$ consisting of paths $\gamma : [0,1] \to Y$ such that $\gamma(0) = \gamma(1) = y_0$. The base point of $\Omega Y$ is the constant loop at $y_0$.

    In particular, $\Omega$ is a functor $\Top_* \to \Top_*$. 

    The $n$-fold loop space of $X$ is denoted by $\Omega^n X$.
\end{definition}

    Let $X$ be a scheme of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_type_morphism_of_schemes}{finite type} over a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field} $k$ that is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:separably_closed_field}{separably closed} or \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_field}{finite}. Let $A$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:noetherian_ring}{noetherian} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:torsion_ring}{torsion ring}. Consider sheaves of $A$-modules on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{(small) \'etale site} of $X$.

    The category $D_c^b(X_{\et}, A)$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:triangulated_category}{triangulated category} (\Cref{proposition:bdd_constructible_derived_category_with_torsion_coefficients_is_triangulated}). Let $\mathbb{D}_X(-): D_c^b(X_{\et}, A) \to D_c^b(X_{\et}, A)$ denote the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:verdier_dual_of_an_object_in_the_bdd_constr_derived_category_of_adic_sheaves_with_integral_coefficients_on_a_finite_type_scheme_over_a_noetherian_scheme}{Verdier dual functor} on $X$ relative to $\Spec k$.

    The \hldef{self-dual (middle) perverse $t$-structure on $D_c^b(X_{\et}, A)$} is the pair of strictly full subcategories $(\pDleqzero(X, A), \pDgeqzero(X, A))$ of $D_c^b(X_{\et}, A)$ defined by

    \hlalign{
    \begin{align*}
        \pDleqzero(X, A) &:= \left\{ K \in D_c^b(X_{\et}, A) : \dim \Supp\bigl(\mathscr{H}^{-i}(K)\bigr) \leq i \text{ for all } i \in \bbZ \right\}, \\
        \pDgeqzero(X, A) &:= \left\{ K \in D_c^b(X_{\et}, A) : \dim \Supp\bigl(\mathscr{H}^{-i}(\mathbb{D}_X(K))\bigr) \leq i \text{ for all } i \in \bbZ \right\},
    \end{align*}
    }

    This is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:t_structure_on_a_triangulated_category}{$t$-structure} on $D_c^b(X_{\et}, A)$. Its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:t_structure_on_a_triangulated_category}{core}, denoted by \hl{$\Perv(X, A)$}, is called the \hldef{category of perverse sheaves with torsion coefficients on $X$}.
\end{definition}

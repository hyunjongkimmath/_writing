\begin{definition} \label{definition:box_topology_on_a_cartesian_product_of_topological_spaces}
Let $I$ be an index set and $\{X_i\}_{i \in I}$ be a collection of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_spaces}{topological spaces}. Let $X = \prod_{i \in I} X_i$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:product_of_sets}{set theoretic Cartesian product} of these spaces. 

    The \hldef{box topology on $X$} is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:basis_for_a_topology}{topology generated by} the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:basis_for_a_topology}{base} consisting of all sets of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:double_complex_associated_to_biadditive_functor_and_chain_complexes}{form} $\prod_{i \in I} U_i$, where each $U_i$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_space}{open subset} of $X_i$.
\end{definition}

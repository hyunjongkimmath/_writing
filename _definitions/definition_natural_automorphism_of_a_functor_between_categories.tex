\begin{definition} \label{definition:natural_automorphism_of_a_functor_between_categories}
    Let $\calC, \calD$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category}{categories} and let $F: \calC \to \calD$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:functor_between_categories}{functor}. 
    \TODO{idetntity natural transofmration}
    A \hldef{natural automorphism of $F$} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:natural_transformation_between_functors_between_categories}{natural transformation} $\eta: F \to F$ such that there exists a natural transformation $\xi: F \to F$ such that $\xi \circ \eta, \eta \circ \xi = \id_F$.  

    Equivalently, a natural automorphism of $F$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:automorphism_of_an_object_of_a_category_automorphism_group_of_an_object_of_a_locally_small_category}{automorphism} of $F$ as an object in the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:diagram_in_a_category_indexed_by_a_category}{functor category} $\Fun(\calC, \calD)$.
\end{definition}

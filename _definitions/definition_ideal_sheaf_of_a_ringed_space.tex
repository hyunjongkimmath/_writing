\begin{definition} \label{definition:ideal_sheaf_of_a_ringed_space}
    Let $(X, \mathcal{O}_X)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ringed_space}{ringed space}. A \hldef{(left/right/two-sided) ideal sheaf} $\mathcal{I}$ of $\mathcal{O}_X$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:subsheaf_of_a_sheaf_on_a_topological_space}{subsheaf of $\mathcal{O}_X$} in the category of (left/right/two-sided) $\mathcal{O}_X$-\CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:module_over_a_sheaf_of_rings_on_a_ringed_space}{modules}, i.e., a sheaf of (left/right/two-sided) $\mathcal{O}_X$-modules $\mathcal{I}$ together with a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:monomorphism_and_epimorphism_in_categories}{monomorphism} of sheaves of $\mathcal{O}_X$-modules $\iota: \mathcal{I} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{O}_X$ such that for every open subset $U \subseteq X$, the morphism $\iota(U): \mathcal{I}(U) \to \mathcal{O}_X(U)$ identifies $\mathcal{I}(U)$ as an (left/right/two-sided) ideal of the ring $\mathcal{O}_X(U)$.

    Equivalently, a sheaf of ideals $\mathcal{I}$ is a subsheaf of $\mathcal{O}_X$ as a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:module_over_a_sheaf_of_rings_on_a_ringed_space}{sheaf of modules} of $\mathcal{O}_X$.
\end{definition}

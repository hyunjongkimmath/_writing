The exact values of the diagonal Ramsey numbers $R(k,k)$ are known only for small $k$:
$$R(2,2) = 2, \quad R(3,3) = 6, \quad R(4,4) = 18.$$
The value $R(5,5)$ remains unknown; the best current bounds are $43 \leq R(5,5) \leq 48$. The recursive bound from \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{lemma:recursive_upper_bound_for_two_color_ramsey_numbers}{the lemma} gives the explicit estimate
$$R(k,k) \leq \binom{2k-2}{k-1},$$
which grows exponentially in $k$. Determining tighter bounds for diagonal Ramsey numbers is an active area of research.
\end{remark}

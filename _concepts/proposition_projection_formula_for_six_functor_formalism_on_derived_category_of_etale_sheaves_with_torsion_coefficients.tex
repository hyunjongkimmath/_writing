Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme}. Let $f: X \to Y$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:compactification_of_a_morphism_of_schemes}{compactifiable $S$-morphism} between $S$-schemes where $Y$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:quasi_compact_morphism_of_schemes}{quasi-compact} and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:quasi_separated_morphism_of_schemes}{quasi-separated} over $S$. Let $A$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:noetherian_ring}{noetherian} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:torsion_ring}{torsion ring}.

    \item For $K \in D^-(X_{\et}, A)$\CrefIfExists{notation:derived_category_of_category_of_modules_of_a_ringed_site} and $L \in D^-(Y_{\et}, A)$, there is a canonical isomorphism in $D^-(Y_{\et}, A)$:
    $$Rf_!(K \otimes_A^L Lf^* L) \cong Rf_! K \otimes_A^L L.$$
    \CrefIfExists{definition:derived_direct_image_functor_with_proper_or_compact_support_for_compactifiable_morphisms_of_schemes_over_a_scheme}\CrefIfExists{definition:derived_tensor_product_of_complexes_of_modules_on_an_essentially_small_ringed_site_with_a_pretopology}\CrefIfExists{definition:total_left_derived_inverse_image_functor_for_morphism_of_ringed_sites}

    In particular, if $f$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:proper_morphism_of_schemes}{proper}, then $Rf_! = Rf_*$ and hence the projection formula takes the form
    $$Rf_*(K \otimes_A^L Lf^* L) \cong Rf_* K \otimes_A^L L.$$

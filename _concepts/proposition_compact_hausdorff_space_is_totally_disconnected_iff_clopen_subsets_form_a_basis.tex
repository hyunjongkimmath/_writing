\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:compact_hausdorff_space_is_totally_disconnected_iff_clopen_subsets_form_a_basis}
Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:compact_topological_space}{compact} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:separation_axioms_of_topology}{Hausdorff space}.
$X$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:totally_disconnected_topological_space}{totally disconnected} if and only if the set of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:clopen_subset_of_a_topological_space}{clopen subsets} of $X$ forms a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:basis_for_a_topology}{basis for the topology} of $X$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Minkowski's inequality] \label{proposition:minkowski_inequality_for_L_p_norms}
Let $(X, \mathcal{M}, \mu)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:measure_on_a_measurable_space_and_measure_space}{measure space} and let $p \geq 1$. For any measurable functions $f, g : X \to \mathbb{C}$ such that $f, g \in L^p(\mu)$,
$$\|f + g\|_p \leq \|f\|_p + \|g\|_p,$$
where $\|h\|_p = \left(\int_X |h|^p \, d\mu\right)^{1/p}$ for $h \in L^p(\mu)$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:L_p_norm_on_a_measure_space}{$L^p$-norm}. Equality holds if and only if $f$ and $g$ are positively linearly dependent almost everywhere, i.e., there exist constants $a, b \geq 0$, not both zero, such that $af(x) = bg(x)$ for \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:property_holds_almost_everywhere_for_a_measure_space}{$\mu$-almost every} $x \in X$.
\end{proposition}

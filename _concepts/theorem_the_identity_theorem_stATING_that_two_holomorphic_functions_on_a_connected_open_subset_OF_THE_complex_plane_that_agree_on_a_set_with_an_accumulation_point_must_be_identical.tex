\begin{theorem} \label{theorem:the_identity_theorem_stating_that_two_holomorphic_functions_on_a_connected_open_subset_OF_THE_complex_plane_that_agree_on_a_set_with_an_accumulation_point_must_be_identical}
Let $U \subset \mathbb{C}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:disconnected_and_connected_topological_spaces}{connected} open subset (a domain) and let $f, g : U \to \mathbb{C}$ be two \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:holomorphic_function_from_a_subset_of_Cn_to_Cm_and_derivative_of_a_holomorphic_function_at_a_point}{holomorphic functions} on $U$. If the set
$\{z \in U : f(z) = g(z)\}$
has an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:accumulation_point_in_an_extended_metric_space_valued_in_ordered_commutative_monoid_with_adjoined_infinity}{accumulation point} — that is, there exists $z_0 \in U$ such that every neighborhood of $z_0$ contains infinitely many points $z \neq z_0$ with $f(z) = g(z)$ — then
$f(z) = g(z)$
for all $z \in U$. In other words, $f \equiv g$ on $U$.
\end{theorem}

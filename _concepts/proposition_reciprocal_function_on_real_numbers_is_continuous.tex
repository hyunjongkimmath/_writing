\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:reciprocal_function_on_real_numbers_is_continuous}
    Let $\bbR$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_real_numbers}{field of real numbers} equipped with its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:standard_absolute_value_on_real_numbers}{standard topology} and let $\bbR^\times = \bbR \setminus \{0\}$ be equipped with the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:subspace_of_a_topological_space}{subspace topology}. The reciprocal function
    $$\operatorname{rec} : \bbR^\times \to \bbR^\times, \quad \operatorname{rec}(x) = \frac{1}{x}$$
    is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:continuous_map_of_topological_spaces}{continuous}.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
    We show that $\operatorname{rec}$ is continuous at every point $a \in \bbR^\times$. Fix $a \neq 0$ and $\varepsilon > 0$. We need $\delta > 0$ such that $|x - a| < \delta$ implies $|1/x - 1/a| < \varepsilon$, for $x \in \bbR^\times$.

    Observe that
    $$\left|\frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{a}\right| = \frac{|x - a|}{|a| \cdot |x|}.$$
    Choose $\delta_1 = |a|/2$. Since $(\bbR, |\cdot|)$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:extended_metric_on_a_set_valued_in_an_ordered_semiring_with_adjoined_infinity}{extended metric space} via the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:standard_absolute_value_on_real_numbers}{standard absolute value}, the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{proposition:reverse_triangle_inequality_in_extended_metric_space}{reverse triangle inequality} gives $|x| = |a - (a - x)| \geq |a| - |a - x|$. Thus $|x - a| < \delta_1$ implies $|x| > |a|/2$. Then
    $$\frac{|x - a|}{|a| \cdot |x|} < \frac{|x - a|}{|a| \cdot (|a|/2)} = \frac{2|x - a|}{|a|^2}.$$
    Choose $\delta_2 = \varepsilon |a|^2 / 2$ and set $\delta = \min(\delta_1, \delta_2)$. For $|x - a| < \delta$, we have
    $$\left|\frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{a}\right| =  \frac{|x - a|}{|a| \cdot |x|} < \frac{2|x-a|}{|a|^2} < \frac{2\delta}{|a|^2} \leq \frac{2 \delta_2}{|a|^2} \leq \varepsilon.$$
    Hence $\operatorname{rec}$ is continuous at $a$. Since $a$ was arbitrary, $\operatorname{rec}$ is continuous \CrefIfExists{lemma:function_between_topological_spaces_is_continuous_iff_continuous_at_all_points}.
\end{proof}

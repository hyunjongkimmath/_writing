\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:finite_degree_field_extension_is_separable_iff_degree_many_K_embeddings_into_algebraic_closure}
Let $L/K$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_field_extension}{finite extension} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{fields} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:degree_of_a_field_extension}{degree} $n$, and let $\overline{K}$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebraic_closure_of_a_field}{algebraic closure} of $K$. Then $L/K$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:separable_algebraic_field_extension}{separable extension} if and only if there are exactly $n$ distinct \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_homomorphism}{$K$-embeddings} of $L$ into $\overline{K}$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:hecke_operators_commute_and_satisfy_recurrence_relations}

Let $k \in \bbZ$ and let $\Gamma = \mathrm{SL}_2(\bbZ)$. The Hecke operators acting on the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:vector_space_of_modular_and_cusp_forms}{space of modular forms} $M_k(\Gamma)$ satisfy the following relations for all positive integers $m$, $n$:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \hldef{Multiplicativity for coprime indices:} If $(m, n) = 1$, then
    $$\hlin{T_m T_n = T_{mn}.}$$

    \item \hldef{Prime power recurrence:} For any prime $p$ and any integer $r \geq 1$,
    $$\hlin{T_p^r T_p = T_p^{r+1} + p^{k-1} T_p^{r-1}.}$$

    \item \hldef{Commutativity:} For all $m, n \geq 1$, the operators commute:
    $$\hlin{T_m T_n = T_n T_m.}$$
\end{enumerate}

As a consequence of (1) and (2), the Hecke algebra generated by $\{T_n : n \geq 1\}$ is commutative, and every $T_n$ can be expressed as a polynomial in the prime power operators $\{T_{p^r}\}$ using the relations above.

\CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:hecke_operator_on_modular_forms_for_SL_2_Z}{Hecke operator}
\end{proposition}

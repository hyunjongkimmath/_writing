\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:equivalent_conditions_for_continuous_functor_of_sites}
Let $(\calC,J)$ and $(\calD,K)$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{sites} (or \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_site_locally_small_site_U_site_U_small_site_U_topology}{$\mathscr{U}$-sites} if a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_universe}{Grothendieck universe} $\mathscr{U}$ is available). Let $u : \calC \to \calD$ be a functor. Consider the following conditions:
\begin{enumerate}

    \item \label{item:continuous_functor_of_sites_pushforward_of_sieves_definition} $u$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:continuous_functor_of_sites}{continuous}, i.e. for every object $U$ of $\calC$ and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{covering sieve} $S \in J(U)$, the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sieve_on_an_object_in_a_category}{sieve} in $\calD$ \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_generated_by_a_pretopology}{generated by} the image $\{u(\phi) | \phi \in S\}$ is a covering sieve in $K(u(U))$.



    \item \label{item:continuous_functor_of_sites_sheaf_valued_in_general_category_definition} For any (large) category $\calA$ and every \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_on_a_site}{sheaf} $F$ on $\calD$ valued in $\calA$, the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:presheaf_on_a_category}{presheaf} $u^s \calF: \calC^{\op} \to \calA, X \mapsto F(u(X))$ is a sheaf on $\calD$. 

    \item \label{item:continuous_functor_of_sites_sheaf_of_sets_definition} For every \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_on_a_site}{sheaf} of sets $F$ on $\calD$, the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:direct_image_of_a_sheaf_on_a_site_under_a_continuous_functor_of_sites_or_a_site_morphism}{pushforward/direct image} $u^s \calF: \calC^{\op} \to \Sets, X \mapsto F(u(X))$, which is a priori a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:presheaf_on_a_category}{presheaf}, is in fact a sheaf is a sheaf on $\calD$. Note that this is the definition of continuous functor of sites given in {\cite[Expos\'e III, D\'efinition 1.1]{SGA4_I}}.
\end{enumerate}
Condition \eqref{item:continuous_functor_of_sites_pushforward_of_sieves_definition} implies Condition \eqref{item:continuous_functor_of_sites_sheaf_valued_in_general_category_definition}, which in turn implies Condition \eqref{item:continuous_functor_of_sites_sheaf_of_sets_definition}. If $\calC$ and $\calD$ are \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{lemma:category_of_presheaves_on_a_small_category_of_locally_small_value_is_locally_small}{locally small} and a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheafification_of_a_presheaf_on_a_topological_space}{sheafification functor}
\begin{equation} \label{eq:equivalent_conditions_for_continuous_functor_of_sites_proof_sheafification_functor}
a: \PreShv(\calD, \Sets) \to \Shv(\calD, \Sets)
\end{equation}
exists (e.g. $\calD$ has a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{topologically generating family} or is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:essentially_small_category}{essentially small}, see \Cref{theorem:sheafification_of_a_presheaf_of_sets_on_a_small_enough_site}), then Condition \eqref{item:continuous_functor_of_sites_sheaf_of_sets_definition} implies \eqref{item:continuous_functor_of_sites_pushforward_of_sieves_definition}
\TODO{might need to impose these conditions for other things that might use this statement}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
    Clearly, \eqref{item:continuous_functor_of_sites_sheaf_valued_in_general_category_definition} implies \eqref{item:continuous_functor_of_sites_sheaf_of_sets_definition}. We show that \eqref{item:continuous_functor_of_sites_pushforward_of_sieves_definition} implies \eqref{item:continuous_functor_of_sites_sheaf_valued_in_general_category_definition}.
    Suppose that $U$ is an object of $\calC$ and that $S \in J(U)$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{covering sieve}. Since $u$ is continuous, the sieve $u(S)^+$ on $u(U)$ \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sieve_on_an_object_of_a_category_generated_by_a_family_of_morphisms}{generated by} the image $u(S)$ of $S$ under $u$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{covering sieve} of $u(U)$ in $K$. Write $\calD_S \hookrightarrow \calC/U$\CrefIfExists{definition:category_of_objects_over_under_a_fixed_object_in_a_category} for the full \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:downward_upward_closed_subcategory_of_a_category}{downward-closed subcategory} such that $\operatorname{Ob}(\calD_S) = \{(f: V \to U): f \in S(V)\}$. Write $\calD_{u(S)^+} \hookrightarrow \calD/u(U)$ for the analogous subcategory. The functor $u$ induces a functor $u_U: \calD_S \to \calD_{u(S)^+}, \quad (V \to U) \mapsto (u(V) \to u(U))$. Due to the continuity of $u$, this functor is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cofinal_and_final_functors}{cofinal}\TODO{prove that $u_U$ is cofinal: for every $(g : W \to u(U)) \in \calD_{u(S)^+}$, the comma category $(g \downarrow u_U)$ is non-empty and connected — follows because $g$ factors through some $u(f)$ with $f \in S$, so non-emptiness is clear; connectedness uses that $S$ is a sieve}

    We want to show that the limit
    \begin{align} \label{eq:projective_limit_of_sections_for_direct_image_sheaf_under_a_continuous_functor_of_sites}
    \varprojlim_{(V \to U) \in (\calD_S)^{\op}} (u^s \calF)|_{\calD_S}(V)
    \end{align}
    exists and the canonical natural morphism
    $$u^s \calF(U) \to \varprojlim_{(V \to U) \in (\calD_S)^{\op}} (u^s \calF)|_{\calD_S}(V)$$
    is an isomorphism. \eqref{eq:projective_limit_of_sections_for_direct_image_sheaf_under_a_continuous_functor_of_sites} equals
    $$ \varprojlim_{(V \to U) \in (\calD_S)^{\op}} \calF|_{\calD_S}(u(V)).$$
    % Because $u$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:continuous_functor_of_sites}{continuous functor}, the image $u(S)$ of the sieve $S$ under $u$ \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sieve_on_an_object_of_a_category_generated_by_a_family_of_morphisms}{generates} a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{covering sieve} $u(S)^+ \in K(u(U))$ \TODO{Why does the image of a sieve under a continuous functor generate a covering sieve?}.

    Moreover, $u^s \calF(U) = \calF(u(U))$. Since $\calF$ is a sheaf on $(\calD, K)$, there is a canonical isomorphism
    $$\calF(u(U)) \cong \varprojlim_{W \to u(U)) \in (\calD_{u(S)^+})^{\op}} \calF(W).$$
    Because $u_U$ is cofinal, the limit above, which is assumed to exist is isomorphic to
    $$\varprojlim_{(V \to U) \in \calD_S^{\op}} \calF(u(V))$$
    \Cref{theorem:cofinal_functors_reduce_colimits_final_functors_reduce_limits} as needed.

    Now assume that $\calC$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_small_category}{locally small} and that a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheafification_of_a_presheaf_on_a_topological_space}{sheafification functor} \eqref{eq:equivalent_conditions_for_continuous_functor_of_sites_proof_sheafification_functor} exists, we show that \eqref{item:continuous_functor_of_sites_sheaf_of_sets_definition} implies \eqref{item:continuous_functor_of_sites_pushforward_of_sieves_definition}. Let $U$ be an object of $\calC$ and let $S \in J(U)$ be any covering sieve. Let $\widetilde{h}_{u(U)}$ be the sheafification of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:representable_functor_on_a_locally_small_category}{representable functor} $h_{u(U)}: \calD \to \Sets$. Condition \eqref{item:continuous_functor_of_sites_sheaf_of_sets_definition} implies that $u^s \widetilde{h}_{u(U)} = \widetilde{h}_{u(U)} \circ u$ is a sheaf on $\calC$. In particular, we have an isomorphism 
    $$(u^s \widetilde{h}_{u(U)})(U) \cong \varprojlim_{(f: V \to U) \in S^{\op}} (u^s \widetilde{h}_{u(U)})(V).$$
    The left-hand side is $\widetilde{h}_{u(U)} (u(U))$. Note that there is a canonical map $h_{u(U)} \to \widetilde{h}_{u(U)}$ of presheaves and a canonical section $\id_{u(U)} \in h_{u(U)}(u(U))$. Let us still call by $\id_{u(U)}$ the image of this canonical section under the canonical function $h_{u(U)}(u(U)) \to \widetilde{h}_{u(U)}(u(U))$. The isomorphism above then identifies this section $\id_{u(U)} \in \widetilde{h}_{u(U)}(u(U))$ as an element of $\varprojlim_{(f: V \to U) \in S^{\op}} \widetilde{h}_{u(U)}(u(V))$ --- this element is given by the compatible family $\calG \coloneq \{\eta_{u(V)}(u(f))\}_{f \in S}$ of elements of $\widetilde{h}_{u(U)}(u(V))$ where $\eta: h_{u(U)} \to \widetilde{h}_{u(U)}$ is the canonical morphism. To show that the sieve $R \coloneq S(u(S))$ generated by $u(S)$ is a covering sieve of $u(S)$, it is equivalent to show 
    \TODO{somehow, the following should be true: a sieve $S \in K(X)$ is a covering if and only if the image of the identity $\id_X$ under the canonical map $h_X(X) \to \widetilde{h}_X(X)$ is in the image of hte map $\varinjlim_{f: V \to X \in S} \widetilde{h}_X(V) \to \widetilde{h}_X(X)$}
    that the section $\id_{u(U)} \in \widetilde{h}_{u(U)}(u(U))$ lies in the image of the canonical map
    \begin{equation} \label{eq:inverse_limits_restriction_map}
    \varprojlim_{(g: W \to u(U)) \in R^{\op}} \widetilde{h}_{u(U)}(W) \to \widetilde{h}_{u(U)}(u(U)).
    \end{equation}
    Since $u(S)$ is by construction a subcollection of $R$, we have a restriction map
    $$\rho: \varprojlim_{(g: W \to u(U)) \in R^{\op}} \widetilde{h}_{u(U)}(W) \to \varprojlim_{(g: V \to U) \in S^{\op}} \widetilde{h}_{u(U)}(u(V)).$$
    Given any memeber $g: W \to u(U)$ of the sieve $R$, we show that the family $\{\eta_{W}(g)\}$ is well-defined in the inverse limit in \eqref{eq:inverse_limits_restriction_map} over $R$, i.e. that these $\eta_W(g)$ are compatible in the following sense: for any $g: W \to u(U)$ in $R$ and any moprhism $k: W' \to W$ in $\calD$, the restriction of $\eta(g)$ to $W'$ along $k$ equals $\eta(g \circ k)$. Since $g \in R$, we have that $g = u(f) \circ h$ for some $h: W \to u(V)$. Thus, $g \circ k = u(f) \circ (h \circ k)$. The section $\eta(g)$ is the image of $g$ via $\eta_W: h_{u(U)}(W) \to \widetilde{h}_{u(U)}(W)$. Restricting the image of $g$ to $W'$ along $k$ is exactly the image of $g \circ k$ under $\eta_{W'}: h_{u(U)}(W') \to \widetilde{h}_{u(U)}(W')$. Thus, the desired compatibility holds. The canonical map \eqref{eq:inverse_limits_restriction_map} is the composition
    $$\varprojlim_{(g: W \to u(U)) \in R^{\op}} \widetilde{h}_{u(U)}(W) \xrightarrow{\rho} \varprojlim_{(g: V \to U) \in S^{\op}} \widetilde{h}_{u(U)}(u(V)) \cong \widetilde{h}_{u(U)}(u(U))$$
    and hence the image of this map contains the section $\id_{u(U)} \in \widetilde{h}_{u(U)}(u(U))$ from earlier.
\end{proof}

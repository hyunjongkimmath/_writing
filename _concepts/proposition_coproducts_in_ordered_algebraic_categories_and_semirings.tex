\begin{proposition}[Coproducts in ordered algebraic categories and semirings] \label{proposition:coproducts_in_ordered_algebraic_categories_and_semirings}

For the categories discussed in \Cref{definition:category_of_semigroups_monoids_ordered_variants}, \Cref{definition:category_of_lattice_ordered_groups}, and \Cref{definition:category_of_semirings_and_ordered_semirings}, coproducts are constructed as follows.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item [\textbf{(OSemi, OMon)}] In $\mathbf{OSemi}$ (resp.\ $\mathbf{OMon}$), the coproduct of two objects $((A, \cdot_A), \leq_A)$ and $((B, \cdot_B), \leq_B)$ is obtained by first forming the algebraic free product $A *_{\mathbf{Semi}} B$ (resp.\ $A *_{\mathbf{Mon}} B$) as in \Cref{proposition:coproducts_of_semigroups_monoids_groups_as_free_products}, and then quotienting by the smallest order-compatible congruence. Concretely, define a preorder $\preceq$ on $A * B$ generated by the relations
    $$\iota_A(a) \preceq \iota_A(a') \text{ whenever } a \leq_A a',$$
    $$\iota_B(b) \preceq \iota_B(b') \text{ whenever } b \leq_B b',$$
    together with the compatibility conditions: $x \preceq y \implies xz \preceq yz$ and $zx \preceq zy$. The coproduct is the quotient of $A * B$ by the associated congruence, equipped with the induced partial order.

    \item [\textbf{(lGrp)}] In $\mathbf{lGrp}$, the coproduct of two lattice-ordered groups $(G, +_G, \leq_G)$ and $(H, +_H, \leq_H)$ is called the r-product (for "radical product") or the ordered free product. It is constructed by taking the group-theoretic free product $G *_{\mathbf{Grp}} H$ and quotienting by the smallest convex normal subgroup that makes the quotient into a lattice-ordered group in which the images of $G$ and $H$ embed as lattice subgroups. The resulting object satisfies the universal property: for any lattice-ordered group $K$ and morphisms $f : G \to K$, $g : H \to K$ in $\mathbf{lGrp}$, there exists a unique morphism $[f, g] : G *_{\mathbf{lGrp}} H \to K$ such that $[f, g] \circ \iota_G = f$ and $[f, g] \circ \iota_H = g$.

    \item [\textbf{(OSRing)}] In $\mathbf{OSRing}$, the coproduct of two ordered semirings $(R, \leq_R)$ and $(S, \leq_S)$ is constructed analogously: take a free-product-style algebraic construction on the underlying sets, impose bilinearity relations for both addition and multiplication, then quotient by the smallest order-compatible congruence extending $\{(r, r') : r \leq_R r'\}$ and $\{(s, s') : s \leq_S s'\}$.

    \item [\textbf{(cSRing)}] In the category of commutative semirings $\mathbf{cSRing}$, the coproduct simplifies dramatically: for two commutative semirings $R$ and $S$, the coproduct is the tensor product over the initial semiring $\mathbb{N}_0$:
    $$R \coprod_{\mathbf{cSRing}} S = R \otimes_{\mathbb{N}_0} S.$$
    This is the free commutative semiring generated by $R$ and $S$ subject to bilinearity of addition and multiplication. For general (non-commutative) $\mathbf{SRing}$, the coproduct does not coincide with a tensor product; it requires a free-product-like construction with additional relations encoding the interaction between the two semiring structures.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

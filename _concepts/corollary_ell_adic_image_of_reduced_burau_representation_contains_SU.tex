    Let $d \geq 2$ be an integer, and write $\bbZ[\zeta_d] = \bbZ[\zeta_d]_{\text{all}}$. Equip $\bbZ[\zeta_d]^{\oplus (n-1)}$ with the $\varepsilon_{\bbZ[\zeta_d]}$-Hermitian form $\tilde{h}_n^r$ (from \Cref{proposition:hermitian_form_preserved_by_reduced_burau_representation} and \Cref{proposition:skew_symmetric_matrix_for_reduced_burau_at_minus_one}). In particular, $\psi_n^r|_{t=\zeta_d}: B_n \to \GL_{n-1}(\bbZ[\zeta_d])$ maps into the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:unitary_group_of_a_matrix_form_over_a_ring_with_involution}{unitary group} $U(\bbZ[\zeta_d]^{\oplus(n-1)})$. If $n \geq 2d+1$, then the closure of the image of the composition
    $$B_n \xrightarrow{\psi_n^r|_{t=\zeta_d}} \mathrm{U}(\bbZ[\zeta_d]^{\oplus(n-1)}) \to U(\bbZ_\ell[\zeta_d]^{\oplus (n-1)})$$
    contains the special unitary group $\mathrm{SU}(\bbZ_\ell[\zeta_d]^{\oplus (n-1)})$ for all but finitely many prime numbers $\ell$.

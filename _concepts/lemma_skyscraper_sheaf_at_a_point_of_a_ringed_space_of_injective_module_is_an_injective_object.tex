\begin{lemma} \label{lemma:skyscraper_sheaf_at_a_point_of_a_ringed_space_of_injective_module_is_an_injective_object}
    Let $(X,\calO_X)$ be a ringed space. Let $i_x: \{x\} \hookrightarrow I$ be the embedding of a point $x$ of $X$. Let $I_x$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:injective_bimodule_over_rings}{injective} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:stalk_of_a_presheaf_on_a_topological_space_at_a_point}{$\calO_{X,x}$}-module. 

    The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:skyscraper_sheaf_of_a_topological_space_valued_in_a_category_with_a_terminal_object}{skyscraper sheaf} $(i_{x})_* \underline{I_x}$\CrefIfExists{definition:direct_image_of_a_sheaf_on_a_topological_space} of the (\CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:constant_sheaf_on_a_topological_space_valued_in_category_admitting_sheafification}{constant sheaf} whose value is the) \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:stalk_of_a_presheaf_on_a_topological_space_at_a_point}{stalk} is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:injective_and_projective_objects_in_a_category}{injective object} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:module_over_a_sheaf_of_rings_on_a_ringed_space}{$\Mod(\calO_X)$}.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
    By \Cref{proposition:equivalent_conditions_projective_injective_objects_in_abelian_categories}, it is tantamount to show that the Hom-functor $\Hom_{\Mod(\calO_X)} (-, (i_x)_* I_x): \Mod(\calO_X) \to \Ab$\CrefIfExists{definition:category_of_groups_of_abelian_groups} is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:exact_functor_between_abelian_categories}{exact}. By abuse of notation, write $\calO_{X,x}$ for inverse image $i_x^*(\calO_X)$, which is a sheaf of rings on $\{x\}$; by \Cref{lemma:restriction_of_presheaf_on_a_topological_space_to_a_point_is_the_presheaf_whose_global_sections_is_the_stalk}, it can be \CrefAndHyperref{lemma:categories_of_sheaves_presheaves_on_a_singleton_set_are_equivalent_to_value_category}{identified with} the stalk $\calO_{X,x}$. 
    
    For all $\calA \in \Mod(\calO_X)$, we have
    $$\Hom_{\Mod(\calO_X)}(\calA, (i_x)_* \underline{I_x}) \cong \Hom_{\Mod(\calO_{X,x})}(i_x^* \calA, \underline{I_x}) \cong \Hom_{\calO_{X,x}}(\calA_x, I_x) $$
    where the rightmost Hom is that of modules over the stalk $\calO_{X,x}$. The stalk functor $\calA \mapsto \calA_x$ is exact (\Cref{proposition:stalk_functor_for_presheaves_and_sheaves_on_a_topological_space_valued_in_an_AB5_category_is_exact}) and $\Hom_{\calO_{X,x}}(-, I_x)$ is also exact (\Cref{proposition:equivalent_conditions_projective_injective_objects_in_abelian_categories}), so 
    $$\calA \mapsto \Hom_{\calO_{X,x}}(\calA_x, I_x) \cong \Hom_{\Mod(\calO_X)}(\calA, (i_x)_* \underline{I_x})$$
    is exact as desired.
\end{proof}

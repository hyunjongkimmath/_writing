Let $G$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:simple_graph_directed_graph_multigraph_weighted_graph}{simple graph}. Then the following inequalities hold:
$$\omega(G) \leq \chi(G) \leq \Delta(G) + 1.$$

The lower bound follows because every vertex in a clique must receive a distinct color. The upper bound follows from the greedy coloring algorithm, which colors the vertices one by one, assigning to each vertex the smallest available color not used by its already-colored neighbors.

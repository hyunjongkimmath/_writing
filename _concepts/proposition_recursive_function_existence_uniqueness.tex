\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:recursive_function_existence_uniqueness}
Let $(\bbN, 1, s)$ be the set of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:natural_numbers}{natural numbers}. Let $A$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:zermelo_fraenkel_set_theory}{set}, let $a \in A$, and let $g: A \to A$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:function_of_sets}{function}. There exists a unique \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:recursive_function_on_naturals}{recursive function} on $(a, g)$.
\end{proposition}

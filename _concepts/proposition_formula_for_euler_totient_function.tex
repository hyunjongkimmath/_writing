\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:formula_for_euler_totient_function}

    Let $n \in \bbZ_{>0}$ with prime factorization $n = p_1^{e_1} \cdots p_k^{e_k}$, where $p_1, \dots, p_k$ are distinct \CrefAndHyperref{definition:prime_number}{prime numbers} and $e_i \geq 1$. Then
    $$\varphi(n) = n \prod_{i=1}^k \left(1 - \frac{1}{p_i}\right).$$
    In particular, if $p$ is prime and $e \geq 1$, then $\varphi(p^e) = p^e - p^{e-1}$.

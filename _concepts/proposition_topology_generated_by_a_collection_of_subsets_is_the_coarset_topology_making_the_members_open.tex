\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:topology_generated_by_a_collection_of_subsets_is_the_coarset_topology_making_the_members_open}
Let $X$ be a set and $\mathcal{S}$ a collection of subsets of $X$.
The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_space}{topology} $\tau(\mathcal{S})$ \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topology_on_a_set_generated_by_a_collection_of_subsets_and_subbasis_for_a_topology}{generated by} $\mathcal{S}$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:coarser_smaller_finer_larger_for_topologies_on_a_set}{coarsest topology} on $X$ such that every element of $\mathcal{S}$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_space}{open} set.
\end{proposition}

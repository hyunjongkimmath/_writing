Let $G$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group}. Let $H$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:subgroup_of_a_group}{subgroup} of $G$, and let $N$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:normal_subgroup_of_a_group}{normal subgroup} of $G$.

Then $HN = \{hn \mid h \in H, n \in N\}$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:subgroup_of_a_group}{subgroup} of $G$, $H \cap N$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:normal_subgroup_of_a_group}{normal subgroup} of $H$, and there is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group_homomorphism}{group isomorphism}
$$ \hlin{\psi: H/(H \cap N) \xrightarrow{\,\sim\,} HN/N} $$
given by $\psi(h(H \cap N)) = hN$ for all $h \in H$.

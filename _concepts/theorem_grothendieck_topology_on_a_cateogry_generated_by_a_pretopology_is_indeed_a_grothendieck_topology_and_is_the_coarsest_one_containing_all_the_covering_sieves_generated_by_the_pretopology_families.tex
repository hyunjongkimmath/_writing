\begin{theorem} \label{theorem:grothendieck_topology_on_a_cateogry_generated_by_a_pretopology_is_indeed_a_grothendieck_topology_and_is_the_coarsest_one_containing_all_the_covering_sieves_generated_by_the_pretopology_families}
    Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category equipped with a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:basis_and_grothendieck_pretopology_for_a_grothendieck_topology_on_a_category}{Grothendieck pretopology} $K$.
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{Grothendieck topology} $J_K$ \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_generated_by_a_pretopology}{generated by} $K$ is indeed a Grothendieck topology.
        \item $J_K$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:coarser_and_finer_grothendieck_topologies}{coarsest} Grothendieck topology such that every member of $K(U)$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{covering family} for $J_K$, i.e. \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sieve_on_an_object_of_a_category_generated_by_a_family_of_morphisms}{generates} a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{covering sieve}.
    \end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item We first show that $J$ is stable under ``base change''. Let $S \in J_K(U)$. By the definition of $J_K$, there exists some covering family $\calF = \{\alpha_i: U_i \to U\}_{i \in I} \in K(U)$ such that the sieve $S(\calF)$ generated by $\calF$ is a sub-sieve of $S$, i.e. that $S$ contains 
        $$S(\calF) =  \{h: W \to U \text{ in } \calC: \exists i \in I \; \exists \alpha: W \to U_i \text{ such that } h = \alpha_i \circ \alpha\},$$
        and let $f: V \to U$ be a morphism in $\calC$. We need to show that the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:pullback_sieve_of_an_object_in_a_category_via_a_morphism_to_the_object}{pullback sieve} 
        $$f^* S = \{g: X \to V \text{ in } \calC: f \circ g \in S\}$$
        contains 
        $$S((f^* \alpha_i)_{i \in I}) = \{g: X \to V \text{ in } \calC: \exists i \in I \; \exists \tilde{\alpha} : X \to U_i \times_U V \text{ such that } g = f^* \alpha_i \circ \tilde{\alpha} \}.$$
        Since $S(\calF) \subseteq S$, we have that $f^*(S(\calF)) \subseteq f^* S$. 

        Say that $g: X \to V$ is a member of $S((f^* \alpha_i)_{i \in I})$, so there exists $i \in I$ and $\widetilde{\alpha}: X \to U_i \times_U V$ such that $g = f^* \alpha_i \circ \tilde{\alpha}$. In other words, we have a diagram 
        \begin{equation} 
        \begin{tikzcd}
            X \ar[rrd, bend left, "g"] \ar[ddr, bend right, "\alpha"] \ar[dr, dotted, "\tilde{\alpha}"] & & \\
            & U_i \times_U V \ar[r, "f^* \alpha_i"] \ar[d] & V \ar[d, "f"] \\
            & U_i \ar[r, "\alpha_i"] & V
        \end{tikzcd}
        \end{equation}
        The composition $f \circ g$ then coincides with the composition $\alpha_i \circ \alpha$, which is a member of $S$. Thus, $g$ is a member of $f^* S$, so the desired inclusion $S((f^* \alpha_i)_{i \in I}) \subseteq f^* S$ holds. Therefore, $J$ is indeed stable under base change.

        We show that $J$ satisfies the ``local character condition''. Let $U$ be an object of $\calC$, let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sieve_on_an_object_in_a_category}{sieve} on $U$, let $R$ be a sieve that is a member of $J(U)$ such that for all $f: V \to U$ in $R$ the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:pullback_sieve_of_an_object_in_a_category_via_a_morphism_to_the_object}{pullback sieve} $f^* S$ is a member of $J(V)$. We show that $S$ is a member of $J(U)$. Say that $R$ is generated by the member $\{\alpha_i: U_i \to U\}_{i \in I}$ of $K(U)$. By hypothesis, the pullback sieve $\alpha_i^* S$ is a member of $J(U_i)$. Thus, there exists a family $\calF_i = \{\beta_{ij} : V_{ij} \to U_i\}_{j \in J_i} \in K(U_i)$ that \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sieve_on_an_object_of_a_category_generated_by_a_family_of_morphisms}{generates} $\alpha_i^* S$. The composition 
        $$\calF' = \{\alpha_i \circ \beta_{ij} : V_{ij} \to U\}_{i \in I, j \in J_i}$$
        of the families $\{\alpha_i\}$ and $\calF_i$ is a family in $K(U)$ by the composition axiom of Grothendieck pretopologies. To show that $S \in J(U)$, it now suffices to show that $S(\calF') \subseteq S$\CrefIfExists{definition:sieve_on_an_object_of_a_category_generated_by_a_family_of_morphisms}. Let $g: W \to U$ be any morphism in the sieve $S(\calF')$. By the definition of $S(\calF')$, there exists some $i \in I$, $j \in J_i$, and a morphism $h: W \to V_{ij}$ such that 
        $$g = (\alpha_i \circ \beta_{ij}) \circ h = \alpha_i \circ (\beta_{ij} \circ h)$$
        Since $\calF_i$ generates a sub-sieve of $\alpha_i^* S$, and since $\beta_{ij} \in \calF_i$, we have $\beta_{ij} \in \alpha_i^* S$. In other words, $\alpha_i \circ \beta_{ij} \in S$. Since $S$ is a sieve and hence is closed under precomposition, we have that $g \in S$.We have thus shown that $S(\calF') \subseteq S$ as desired. 

        We show that the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:maximal_sieve_on_an_object_in_a_category}{maximal sieve} is a covering sieve of $J_K$. By the axioms of a Grothendieck pretopology, the singleton family $\calF = \{\id_U: U \to U\}$ is a member of $K(U)$. The sieve $S(\calF)$ \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sieve_on_an_object_of_a_category_generated_by_a_family_of_morphisms}{generated by} this family consists of all the morphisms $g: V \to U $ in $\calC$ such that there exists a morphism $h: V \to U$ such that $g = \id_U \circ h$, i.e. $g = h$. Thus, the sieve $S(\calF)$ is in fact the maximal sieve.  Thus, the maximal sieve is a covering sieve of $J_K$.

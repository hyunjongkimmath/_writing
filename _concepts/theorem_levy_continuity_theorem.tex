\begin{theorem}[Continuity theorem (Lévy)] \label{theorem:levy_continuity_theorem}
Let $\{\mu_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ be a sequence of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:distribution_of_a_random_variable_on_a_probability_measure_space}{distributions} on $\mathbb{R}$, and let $\varphi_n(t) = \int_{\mathbb{R}} e^{itx}\, d\mu_n(x)$ be the corresponding sequence of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:characteristic_function_of_a_random_variable}{characteristic functions}. Let $\mu$ be a distribution on $\mathbb{R}$ with characteristic function $\varphi$.

If $\varphi_n(t) \to \varphi(t)$ pointwise for all $t \in \mathbb{R}$, and if $\varphi$ is continuous at $0$, then $\mu_n \Rightarrow \mu$ (weak convergence). Conversely, if $\mu_n \Rightarrow \mu$, then $\varphi_n(t) \to \varphi(t)$ pointwise for all $t \in \mathbb{R}$.
\end{theorem}

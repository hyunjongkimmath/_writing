\begin{proposition}[Reflex of the Reflex: Shimura--Tate Duality for CM Types] \label{proposition:reflex_of_reflex_shimura_tate_duality_for_cm_types}
    Let $(E, \Phi)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cm_type_and_reflex_field_of_a_cm_type}{CM type}, and let $E^*(\Phi)$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cm_type_and_reflex_field_of_a_cm_type}{reflex field}.

    There exists a CM type $\Phi^*$ for $E^*(\Phi)$ such that $(E^*(\Phi))^*(\Phi^*)$ is canonically identified with $E$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, and the reflex of $(E^*(\Phi), \Phi^*)$ recovers $(E, \Phi)$. In particular, the reflex operation is an involution on the set of isomorphism classes of CM types.
\end{proposition}

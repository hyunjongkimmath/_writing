\begin{lemma} \label{lemma:maximum_minimum_element_of_a_subset_of_a_poset_is_unique_if_exists}
Let $(X, \leq)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:partially_ordered_set}{partially ordered set} and let $A \subseteq X$ be a subset. 
\begin{enumerate}
    \item If $A$ has a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:least_element_minimum_element_in_a_subset_of_a_partially_ordered_set}{least element}, then it is unique.
    \item If $A$ has a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:greatest_element_maximum_element_in_a_subset_of_a_partially_ordered_set}{greatest element}, then it is unique.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
    We show that least elements are unique. The argument for greatest elements is similar. Say that $a,b \in A$ are least elements of $A$. In other words, $a \leq b$ and $b \leq a$. by the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:reflexive_symmetric_antisymmetric_transitive_total_binary_relations_on_a_set}{antisymmetricity} of $\leq$, we have that $a = b$.
\end{proof}

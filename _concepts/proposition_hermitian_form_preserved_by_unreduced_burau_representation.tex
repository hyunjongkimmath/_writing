    Equip $\bbZ[t,t^{-1}]$ with the involution determined by $t \mapsto t^{-1}$. Let $(R,\sigma)$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:homomorphism_of_involution_rings}{involution algebra} over $\bbZ[t,t^{-1}]$. For integers $n \geq 2$, let $H_n \in M_n(R)$ be the matrix with entries
    \begin{equation} \label{eq:hermitian_matrix_H_n}
        (H_n)_{ij} = \begin{cases}
            a & \text{if } i = j, \\
            b t^{j-i-1} & \text{if } j > i, \\
            \overline{b} t^{1-i+j} & \text{if } i > j,
        \end{cases}
    \end{equation}
    where $b \in R$ and $a = \frac{b - t\overline{b}}{t-1} \in R^{\sigma}$ (the fixed subring of $R$ under the involution). Explicitly,
    $$H_n = \begin{pmatrix}
        a & b & bt & bt^2 & \cdots \\
        \overline{b} & a & b & bt & \cdots \\
        \overline{b}t^{-1} & \overline{b} & a & b & \cdots \\
        \overline{b}t^{-2} & \overline{b}t^{-1} & \overline{b} & a & \cdots \\
        \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots
    \end{pmatrix}.$$
    Then $H_n^* = H_n$, so $H_n$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:epsilon_hermitian_matrix_over_a_ring_with_involution}{Hermitian matrix} (i.e., $1$-Hermitian matrix) over $(R,\sigma)$, and the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebraic_unreduced_Burau_representation_of_artin_braid_group_on_a_module_over_a_ring}{unreduced Burau representation} $\psi_n: B_n \to \GL_n(R)$\CrefIfExists{definition:artin_braid_group} satisfies

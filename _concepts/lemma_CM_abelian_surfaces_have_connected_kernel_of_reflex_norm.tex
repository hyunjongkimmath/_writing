\begin{lemma}[See {\cite[6.4.]{lombardo_graavCMt}}] \label{lemma:CM_abelian_surfaces_have_connected_kernel_of_reflex_norm}
Let $A/K$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:absolutely_simple_abelian_variety_over_a_field}{absolutely simple abelian variety} over a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:number_field}{number field} with \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complex_multiplication_of_an_abelian_variety_over_a_field}{complex multiplication} by a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cm_field}{CM field} $E$. 
    
The kernel of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:reflex_norm_for_a_cm_field}{reflex norm} associated to a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cm_type_and_reflex_field_of_a_cm_type}{CM type} $(E, \Phi)$ with \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cm_type_and_reflex_field_of_a_cm_type}{reflex field} $E^*$ is connected.
\end{lemma}

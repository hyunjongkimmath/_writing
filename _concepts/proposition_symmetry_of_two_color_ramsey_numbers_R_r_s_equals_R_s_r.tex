For all positive integers $r$ and $s$, the two-color Ramsey numbers are symmetric:
$$R(r,s) = R(s,r).$$

This follows from the fact that a clique of size $r$ in a graph $G$ is an independent set of size $r$ in its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{proposition:complement_of_a_graph_clique_and_independent_set_relationship}{complement $\overline{G}$}, and vice versa. Equivalently, swapping the two colors in any edge-coloring exchanges the roles of $r$ and $s$.
\end{proposition}

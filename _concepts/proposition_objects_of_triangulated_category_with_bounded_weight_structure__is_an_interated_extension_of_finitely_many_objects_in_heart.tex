\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:objects_of_triangulated_category_with_bounded_weight_structure__is_an_interated_extension_of_finitely_many_objects_in_heart}
Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:triangulated_category}{triangulated category} equipped with a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:bounded_weight_structure_on_a_triangulated_category}{bounded} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:weight_structure_on_a_triangulated_category}{weight structure} $w$. 
Every object $X \in \mathcal{D}$ is an iterated extension of a finite number of objects in the heart $\mathcal{H}_w$; that is, there exists a finite sequence of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:distinguished_triangle}{distinguished triangles} 
\[
X_{i-1} \to X_i \to H_i[n_i] \to X_{i-1}[1]
\]
for $i=1, \dots, m$, where $X_0 = 0$, $X_m \cong X$, and each $H_i \in \mathcal{H}_w$ with $n_i \in \mathbb{Z}$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[Jordan Canonical Form] \label{theorem:existence_and_uniqueness_of_jordan_canonical_form_over_an_algebraically_closed_field}
    Let $F$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebraically_closed_field}{algebraically closed field}, and let $V$ be a finite-dimensional nonzero \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:vector_space_over_a_field}{$F$-vector space}. Let $T: V \to V$ be a linear endomorphism.

Moreover, the Jordan blocks appearing in this matrix are uniquely determined by $T$ up to their ordering.

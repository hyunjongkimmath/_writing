\begin{proof}
    A constant polynomial defines a constant function, which is continuous by \Cref{proposition:constant_function_between_topological_spaces_is_continuous}. The identity function $\operatorname{id}_F : F \to F$ is continuous by \Cref{proposition:identity_function_between_topological_spaces_is_continuous}. By repeatedly applying \Cref{proposition:pointwise_product_of_continuous_functions_on_topological_spaces_valued_in_topological_ring_is_continuous} and \Cref{proposition:pointwise_sum_of_continuous_functions_on_topological_spaces_valued_in_topological_ring_is_continuous}, continuity is preserved under multiplication and addition of continuous functions. Since every polynomial is a finite sum of products of constants and the identity function, it defines a continuous function.
\end{proof}

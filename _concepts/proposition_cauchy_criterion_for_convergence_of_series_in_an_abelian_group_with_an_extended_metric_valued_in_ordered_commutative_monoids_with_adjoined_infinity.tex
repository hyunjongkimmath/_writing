    Let $T$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ordered_commutative_monoid}{ordered commutative monoid}, let $T^\infty$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ordered_monoid_with_adjoined_infinity}{ordered monoid with adjoined infinity} obtained from $T$, and let $A$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_group}{abelian group} equipped with a translation-invariant \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:extended_metric_on_a_set_valued_in_an_ordered_commutative_monoid_with_adjoined_infinity}{extended metric valued in} $T^\infty$, denoted by $d(\cdot, \cdot)$. Let $(a_n)_{n=1}^\infty$ be a sequence in $A$.

    The series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n$ \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:infinite_series_and_convergence_in_a_topological_abelian_group}{converges} if and only if the sequence of partial sums $(\sum_{n=1}^N a_n)_{N=1}^\infty$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cauchy_sequence_in_an_extended_metric_space_valued_in_ordered_commutative_monoid_with_adjoined_infinity}{Cauchy sequence} in $A$. Equivalently, the series converges if and only if for every $\varepsilon \in T_{>0}$ there exists $N \in \mathbb{N}$ such that for all $M \geq N$,
    $$d\left(0_A, \sum_{n=N+1}^M a_n\right) < \varepsilon.$$

    In particular, if $(V, \|\cdot\|)$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:extended_norm_on_a_module_over_a_commutative_ring_with_absolute_value}{extended normed module} over a commutative ring with \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:absolute_value_on_a_ring_valued_in_an_ordered_semiring}{absolute value} valued in $T^\infty$, and $d(x,y) = \|x-y\|$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:extended_metric_induced_by_an_extended_norm_on_a_module_over_a_commutative_ring_with_absolute_value}{induced metric}, then this criterion applies because that metric is translation-invariant.
\end{proposition}

    $$d(S_M, S_K) = d(0_A, S_M - S_K) = d\left(0_A, \sum_{n=K+1}^M a_n\right).$$

\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:subset_of_a_topology_generated_by_a_collection_of_subsets_is_open_if_and_only_if_union_of_finite_intersections}
Let $X$ be a set and $\mathcal{S}$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topology_on_a_set_generated_by_a_collection_of_subsets_and_subbasis_for_a_topology}{subbasis} for a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_space}{topology} $\tau$.
A \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:subset_of_a_set}{subset} $U \subseteq X$ belongs to $\tau$ if and only if $U$ is the union of finite intersections of elements of $\mathcal{S}$. That is, the collection of all finite intersections of elements of $\mathcal{S}$ forms a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:basis_for_a_topology}{basis} for $\tau$.
\end{proposition}

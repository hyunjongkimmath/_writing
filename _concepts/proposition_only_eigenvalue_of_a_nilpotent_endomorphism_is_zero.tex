\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:only_eigenvalue_of_a_nilpotent_endomorphism_is_zero}
    Let $F$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}, and let $V$ be a nonzero finite-dimensional \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:vector_space_over_a_field}{$F$-vector space}.

    If $T: V \to V$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:nilpotent_endomorphism_and_nilpotent_matrix}{nilpotent endomorphism}, then the only \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:eigenvalue_and_eigenvector_of_an_endomorphism_of_a_vector_space_over_a_field}{eigenvalue} of $T$ is $0$. Consequently, its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:characteristic_polynomial_of_an_endomorphism_of_a_vector_space_over_a_field}{characteristic polynomial} is $\chi_T(t) = t^{\dim_F(V)}$, and its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:determinant_of_an_endomorphism_of_a_vector_space_over_a_field}{determinant} and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:trace_of_an_endomorphism_of_a_vector_space_over_a_field}{trace} are both $0$.

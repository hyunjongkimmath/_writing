Let $f: C_\bullet \to D_\bullet$ be a morphism of chain complexes in an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_category}{abelian category}. The \CrefAndHyperref{definition:mapping_cone_of_a_map_of_chain_cochain_complexes_in_an_additive_category}{mapping cone} $\operatorname{Cone}(f)$ is the quotient complex of the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:mapping_cylinder_of_a_map_of_chain_cochain_complexes}{mapping cylinder} by the subcomplex $C_\bullet \oplus 0 \oplus 0 \cong C_\bullet$. 

Specifically, there exists a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:short_exact_sequence_of_chain_complexes}{short exact sequence} of chain complexes:
$$ 0 \to C_\bullet \xrightarrow{\alpha} \operatorname{Cyl}(f) \xrightarrow{q} \operatorname{Cone}(f) \to 0 $$
where $q$ is the natural quotient map. This sequence expresses the mapping cone as the homotopy cokernel of $f$.
\end{lemma}

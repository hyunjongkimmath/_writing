\begin{theorem}[See {\cite[Section III.3]{kiehl_weissauer_wcps}}] \label{theorem:perverse_t_structure_on_bdd_constructible_derived_category_with_algebraic_coefficients_on_a_finite_type_scheme_over_a_separably_closed_or_finite_field_is_a_t_structure}
    Let $(R, \lambda)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_ring_with_respect_to_an_ideal}{complete} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{DVR} such that $R/\lambda^n$ is finite for all $n \geq 1$. Let $E$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_fractions_of_an_integral_domain}{fraction field} of $R$, and let $K/E$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebraic_element_minimal_polynomial_of_an_algebraic_element_algebraic_field_extension_transcendental_element_field_extension}{algebraic extension}. Let $X$ be a scheme of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_type_morphism_of_schemes}{finite type} over a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field} $k$ that is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:separably_closed_field}{separably closed} or \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_field}{finite}. Consider sheaves on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{(small) \'etale site} of $X$.

    The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:self_dual_middle_perverse_t_structure_on_bdd_constructible_derived_category_with_algebraic_coefficients}{perverse $t$-structure} on $\Dbc(X, K)$ is indeed a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:t_structure_on_a_triangulated_category}{$t$-structure}.
\end{theorem}

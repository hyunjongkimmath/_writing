\begin{proposition}[Equivalent Conditions for a Finite Galois Extension] \label{proposition:equivalent_conditions_for_a_finite_degree_field_extension_to_be_galois}
Let $L/K$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_field_extension}{finite extension}. The following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item $L/K$ is both a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:splitting_field_of_polynomials_over_a_field_normal_field_extension}{normal extension} and a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:separable_algebraic_field_extension}{separable extension} (i.e.,\ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:galois_extension_of_fields}{Galois extension}).
    \item $|\operatorname{Aut}(L/K)| = [L:K]$.
    \item There exist $\alpha \in L$ and $\beta \in K$ such that $L = K(\alpha)$ and $\beta$ is the product of the distinct \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_homomorphism}{$K$-embeddings} of $\alpha$ into an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebraic_closure_of_a_field}{algebraic closure} of $K$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

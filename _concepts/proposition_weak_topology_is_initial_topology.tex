\begin{proposition}[Weak topology is the initial topology with respect to all continuous linear functionals] \label{proposition:weak_topology_is_initial_topology}

    Let $V$ be a topological vector space over $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, and let $V'$ denote its continuous dual space. The weak topology on $V$ (induced by the pairing $\langle V, V' \rangle$) coincides with the initial topology on $V$ with respect to the family of all linear functionals $\{f : V \to k\}_{f \in V'}$, where each copy of $k$ carries its standard Euclidean topology.

    In particular, a net $(x_\alpha)_{\alpha \in D}$ in $V$ converges weakly to $x \in V$ if and only if $f(x_\alpha) \to f(x)$ for every $f \in V'$.
\end{proposition}

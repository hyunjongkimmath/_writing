\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:vector_space_decomposes_as_direct_sum_along_idempotent}
    Let $F$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}, and let $V$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:vector_space_over_a_field}{$F$-vector space}.

    If $P: V \to V$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:idempotent_linear_map_and_projection_operator}{idempotent linear map}, then
    $$\hlin{V = \ker(P) \oplus \operatorname{im}(P)}.$$
    Moreover, $P$ acts as the identity on $\operatorname{im}(P)$ and as zero on $\ker(P)$. Conversely, if $V = U \oplus W$, there exists a unique idempotent linear map whose image is $U$ and kernel is $W$.
\TextIfExists{definition:idempotent_element_of_a_ring}{This decomposition is the vector-space special case of the direct-sum decomposition induced by an \Cref{definition:idempotent_element_of_a_ring} in a ring: $R = R(1-e) \oplus Re$ for $e^2 = e$.}

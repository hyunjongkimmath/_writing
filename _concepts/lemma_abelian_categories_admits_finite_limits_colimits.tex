\begin{lemma} \label{lemma:abelian_categories_admits_finite_limits_colimits}
    Any \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_category}{abelian category} admits all \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_and_finite_limits_and_colimits_in_a_category}{finite} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:limit_and_colimit_of_a_diagram_in_a_category}{limits} and all finite colimits.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
    By definition, any morphism in an abelian category has a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:kernel_and_cokernel_of_a_morphism_in_a_pointed_category}{kernel and a cokernel}, which are \CrefAndHyperref{lemma:kernels_and_cokernels_are_tantamount_to_equalizers_and_coequalizers_in_abelian_categories}{tantamount to} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:equalizer_and_coequalizer_of_morphisms_in_a_category}{equalizers and coequalizers}. Moreover, any \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:additive_category_preadditive_category}{additive category} admits finite products by definition, which coincide with finite coproducts by \Cref{lemma:finite_products_and_finite_coproducts_coincide_in_preadditive_categories}. Therefore, any abelian category admits finite limits and finite colimits by \Cref{theorem:limits_colimits_are_equalizers_coequaulizers_of_products_coproducts}.
\end{proof}

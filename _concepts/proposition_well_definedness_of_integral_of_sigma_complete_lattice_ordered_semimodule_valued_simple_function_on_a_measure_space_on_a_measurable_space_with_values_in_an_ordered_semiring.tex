\begin{proposition}[Well-definedness of the integral of module-valued simple functions] \label{proposition:well_definedness_of_integral_of_sigma_complete_lattice_ordered_semimodule_valued_simple_function_on_a_measure_space_on_a_measurable_space_with_values_in_an_ordered_semiring}

Let $K$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ordered_semiring}{ordered semiring}, $(X,\mathcal{A},\mu)$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:measure_on_a_measurable_space_valued_in_an_ordered_semiring_and_measure_space_with_such_a_measure}{$K$-valued measure space} with $\mu : \mathcal{A} \to K$, and $M$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sigma_complete_lattice_ordered_semimodule_over_an_ordered_semiring}{$\sigma$-complete lattice-ordered semimodule} over $K$.

If an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sigma_complete_lattice_ordered_semimodule_valued_simple_function_on_a_measure_space_on_a_measurable_space_with_values_in_an_ordered_semiring}{$M$-valued simple function} $s : X \to M_{\geq 0}$ admits two representations
$$s = \sum_{k=1}^m a_k \mathbf{1}_{E_k} = \sum_{j=1}^n b_j \mathbf{1}_{F_j},$$
with $a_k, b_j \in M_{\geq 0}$ and $\{E_k\}$, $\{F_j\}$ pairwise disjoint measurable sets, then the integral is independent of the representation:
$$\sum_{k=1}^m \mu(E_k) \cdot a_k = \sum_{j=1}^n \mu(F_j) \cdot b_j.$$

The proof reduces to refining both partitions to their common refinement $G_{kj} = E_k \cap F_j$ and using the additivity of $\mu$ together with the distributivity of the semimodule action.
\end{proposition}

\begin{corollary}[Values of the Riemann zeta function at even positive integers] \label{corollary:values_of_riemann_zeta_at_even_positive_integers}

For every $n \in \bbN$, the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:riemann_zeta_function}{Riemann zeta function} takes the value
$$
\boxed{ \zeta(2n) = \frac{(-1)^{n+1} (2\pi)^{2n}}{2 (2n)!} B_{2n}, }
$$
where $B_{2n}$ are the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:bernoulli_numbers}{Bernoulli numbers} (with the convention $B_1 = -1/2$, $B_{2n+1} = 0$ for $n \ge 1$).

In particular, $\zeta(2) = \pi^2/6$, $\zeta(4) = \pi^4/90$, $\zeta(6) = \pi^6/945$, etc.

We prove the formula using the infinite product expansion of the sine function and the generating function for Bernoulli numbers.

First, we use the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{proposition:weierstrass_factorization_sine_function}{Weierstrass factorization of the sine function}:
\begin{equation} \label{eq:sine_product}
\sin(\pi z) = \pi z \prod_{n=1}^\infty \left( 1 - \frac{z^2}{n^2} \right), \qquad z \in \bbC.
\end{equation}

Next, we take the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:logarithmic_derivative_of_a_holomorphic_function}{logarithmic derivative} of both sides of \eqref{eq:sine_product}:
\begin{align*}
\pi \cot(\pi z) &= \frac{d}{dz} \log \sin(\pi z) \\
&= \frac{1}{z} + \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{d}{dz} \log \left( 1 - \frac{z^2}{n^2} \right) \\
&= \frac{1}{z} + \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{-2z/n^2}{1 - z^2/n^2} \\
&= \frac{1}{z} - 2z \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{n^2 - z^2}
\end{align*}

For $|z| < 1$, we expand each term as a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:geometric_progression_and_geometric_series_over_a_commutative_semiring}{geometric series}:
\begin{align*}
\pi \cot(\pi z) &= \frac{1}{z} - 2z \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{n^2} \frac{1}{1 - z^2/n^2} \\
&= \frac{1}{z} - 2z \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{n^2} \sum_{k=0}^\infty \left( \frac{z^2}{n^2} \right)^k \\
&= \frac{1}{z} - 2 \sum_{k=0}^\infty \left( \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{n^{2k+2}} \right) z^{2k+1}.
\end{align*}

By the definition of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:riemann_zeta_function}{Riemann zeta function}, the inner sum is $\zeta(2k+2)$. Thus
\begin{equation} \label{eq:cotangent_expansion}
\pi \cot(\pi z) = \frac{1}{z} - 2 \sum_{m=1}^\infty \zeta(2m) z^{2m-1}, \qquad |z| < 1.
\end{equation}

Now we use the Bernoulli number generating function. The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:bernoulli_numbers}{Bernoulli numbers} are defined by
$$
\frac{w}{e^w - 1} = \sum_{n=0}^\infty B_n \frac{w^n}{n!}, \qquad |w| < 2\pi.
$$
Setting $w = 2\pi i z$ and \TODO{this should be written as a lemma and referenced}\TODO{also, Euler's identity should be written as a proposition}using $e^{2\pi i z} - 1 = 2i e^{\pi i z} \sin(\pi z)$, we obtain
\begin{align*}
\frac{2\pi i z}{e^{2\pi i z} - 1} &= \frac{\pi z}{i \sin(\pi z)} e^{-\pi i z} \\
&= \pi z \frac{\cos(\pi z) - i \sin(\pi z)}{i \sin(\pi z)} \\
&= \pi z \left( -i \cot(\pi z) - 1 \right).
\end{align*}
Equating the two expressions gives
$$
\pi z \left( -i \cot(\pi z) - 1 \right) = \sum_{n=0}^\infty B_n \frac{(2\pi i z)^n}{n!}.
$$
Solving for $\pi \cot(\pi z)$ and using that $B_{2k+1} = 0$ for $k \ge 1$ and $B_1 = -1/2$, we find
\begin{align*}
\pi \cot(\pi z) &= -\frac{1}{z} - \frac{1}{\pi i z} \sum_{n=0}^\infty B_n \frac{(2\pi i z)^n}{n!} \\
&= \frac{1}{z} - 2 \sum_{m=1}^\infty \frac{(-1)^{m+1} (2\pi)^{2m} B_{2m}}{(2m)!} z^{2m-1}.
\end{align*}

Comparing the expansions in \eqref{eq:cotangent_expansion} and above, we equate coefficients of $z^{2m-1}$:
$$
-2 \zeta(2m) = -2 \frac{(-1)^{m+1} (2\pi)^{2m} B_{2m}}{(2m)!}.
$$
Simplifying gives the desired formula
$$
\zeta(2m) = \frac{(-1)^{m+1} (2\pi)^{2m}}{2 (2m)!} B_{2m}.
$$

\zeta(6) &= \frac{(-1)^{4} (2\pi)^6}{2 \cdot 6!} \cdot \frac{1}{42} = \frac{\pi^6}{945},

and so on.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary} \label{corollary:tor_objects_of_modules_over_rings_are_well_defined}
    Let $R,S,T$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{(not necessarily commutative) rings}, let $M$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:bimodule_over_rings_module_over_a_ring}{$R$-$S$ bimodule}, and let $N$ be an $R$-$T$ bimodule. Let $n \geq 0$ be an integer.

    The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:tor_functors_of_a_right_exact_monoidal_functor_on_an_abelian_category}{Tor objects} $\Tor_n(M,N)$ are well defined up to \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:natural_transformation_between_functors_between_categories}{natural isomorphism}, independent of 
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Whether a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:left_right_resolution_of_an_object_by_a_class_of_objects_in_an_abelian_category}{projective resolution} of $M$, a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:flat_left_right_module_over_a_ring}{flat} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:flat_resolution_of_an_object_in_an_abelian_category_with_respect_to_a_right_exact_monoidal_product_functor}{resolution} of $M$, an projective resolution of $N$, or a flat resolution of $N$ is used to compute the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:generalized_derived_functors_for_biadditive_functors_of_abelian_categories_via_resolutions_by_flat_objects}{derived functor}, and 
        \item the choice of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:injective_and_projective_objects_in_a_category}{projective} or flat resolution of $M$ or $N$.
    \end{itemize}
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
    Note that a category of bimodules \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:flat_resolution_of_an_object_in_an_abelian_category_with_respect_to_a_right_exact_monoidal_product_functor}{has enough} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:free_bimodule_over_rings}{free modules} on the left. Moreover, all free modules are projective (\Cref{proposition:module_over_ring_is_projective_if_and_only_if_direct_summand_of_a_free_module}) and thus flat (\Cref{proposition:projective_modules_are_flat}), so the categories of bimodules have enough projectives and have enough flats. Moreover, categories of bimodules are \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendiecks_additional_axioms_for_abelian_categories}{Grothendieck} (\Cref{theorem:the_category_of_R_S_bimodules_is_a_grothendieck_abelian_category_and_AB4_star}) and hence \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendiecks_additional_axioms_for_abelian_categories}{Ab5}, so the desired result follows from \Cref{corollary:right_exact_bifunctor_on_abelian_categories_with_enough_projectives_and_flats_yield_unambiguous_tors}.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[See {\cite[02WH]{stacks-project}}] \label{proposition:list_of_properties_of_morphisms_of_schemes_over_a_base_preserved_under_base_change_and_fpqc_local_on_the_base}
    Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme}. The following are properties for morphisms of $S$-schemes that are preserved under \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:extension_of_scalars_base_change_induction_for_modules_along_ring_homomorphisms}{base change} and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:tau_local_property_of_a_morphism_of_schemes_over_a_scheme}{fpqc-local on the base}:

    \begin{itemize}
        \item \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:open_and_closed_immersions_of_schemes}{closed immersions}
        \item \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:open_and_closed_immersions_of_schemes}{open immersions}
        \item quasi-compact immersions
        \item \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:quasi_compact_morphism_of_schemes}{quasi-compact}
        \item \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:universally_closed_morphism_of_schemes}{universally closed}
        \item \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:quasi_separated_morphism_of_schemes}{quasi-separated}
        \item \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:quasi_separated_morphism_of_schemes}{separated}
        \item \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:monomorphism_and_epimorphism_in_categories}{monomorphism}
        \item \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:injective_surjective_bijective_map_of_sets}{surjective}
        \item universally injective
        \item \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:affine_morphism_of_schemes}{affine}
        \item quasi-affine
        \item locally of finite type
        \item finite type
        \item locally quasi-finite
        \item \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:quasifinite_morphism_of_schemes}{quasi-finite}
        \item \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_of_finite_presentation_finite_presentation_morphism_of_schemes}{locally of finite presentation}
        \item \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_of_finite_presentation_finite_presentation_morphism_of_schemes}{finite presentation}
        \item locally of finite type of relative dimension $d$
        \item universally open
        \item \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:flat_morphism_of_schemes}{flat}
        \item syntomic
        \item \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:smooth_morphism_of_schemes}{smooth}
        \item \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:unramified_morphism_of_schemes}{unramified}
        \item $G$-unramified
        \item \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:etale_morphism_of_schemes}{\'etale}
        \item \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:proper_morphism_of_schemes}{proper}
        \item \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_morphism_of_schemes}{finite}
        \item integral
        \item finite locally free
        \item universally submersive
        \item universal homeomorphism
    \end{itemize}

    In particular, these properties must be local on the base for the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:big_fppf_site_of_a_scheme}{fppf}, syntomic, smooth, \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:big_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{\'etale}, and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:big_zariski_site_of_a_scheme}{Zariski} topologies as well as per Remark \ref{remark:a_property_that_is_local_on_the_base_for_a_fine_topology_must_be_local_on_the_base_for_a_coarse_topology} because they are all \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:coarser_and_finer_grothendieck_topologies}{coarser} than the fpqc topology.

    As per \Cref{definition:property_for_a_representable_morphism_of_sheaves_coming_from_a_property_of_morphisms_of_schemes_preserved_by_base_change_and_base_local}, the above properties of morphisms of schemes induce properties of morphisms of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebraic_space_on_a_site_on_Sch_S_that_preserves_under_base_change_and_has_tau_local_surjectivity_and_etaleness}{algebraic spaces}.
% Here is a list of common properties of morphisms of schemes over a base that are preserved under base change and fpqc-local on the base --- 
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:structure_of_PGL_2_of_C_as_mobius_transformations}
The complex \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:projective_general_linear_group_PGL_n_over_a_field}{projective general linear group} $\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb{C})$ is isomorphic to the group of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:mobius_transformation_linear_fractional_transformation}{Möbius transformations} acting on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:riemann_sphere}{Riemann sphere} $\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C}) = \mathbb{C} \cup \{\infty\}$. The isomorphism identifies the class of a matrix $\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \in \operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb{C})$ with the Möbius transformation $z \mapsto \frac{az + b}{cz + d}$.
\end{proposition}

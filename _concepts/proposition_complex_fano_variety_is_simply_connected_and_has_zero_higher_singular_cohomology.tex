\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:complex_fano_variety_is_simply_connected_and_has_zero_higher_singular_cohomology}
Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:smooth_morphism_of_schemes}{smooth} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:fano_variety_over_a_field}{Fano variety} over the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_complex_numbers}{complex numbers} $\mathbb{C}$. 
Then $X(\bbC)$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:simply_connected_space}{simply connected} and the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_cohomology_objects_of_a_sheaf_on_a_top_space_in_an_ab_catwith_enough_injs_and_sheafification}{sheaf cohomology} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{groups} satisfy $H^i(X, \mathcal{O}_X) = 0$ for all $i > 0$.
\end{proposition}

    Let $X$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:integral_scheme}{integral} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:normal_and_factorial_scheme}{normal scheme}. Let $U \subseteq X$ be a non-empty open subscheme. Let $x \in X \setminus U$ be a point. Let $\calF$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_constant_sheaf_on_a_site_with_sheafification}{locally constant sheaf} on $U$ corresponding to (\Cref{theorem:locally_constant_sheaves_on_X_etale_are_equivalent_to_representations_of_the_etale_fundamental_group}) a (discrete) set with a continuous $\pioneet(U, \overline{x})$-action. The sheaf $\calF$ is \CrefAndHyperref{definition:unramified_at_a_point_outside_a_dense_open_subscheme_of_an_integral_normal_scheme_for_a_locally_constant_sheaf_on_the_dense_open}{unramified at} $x$ if and only if the action is \CrefAndHyperref{definition:unramified_at_a_valuation_for_a_galois_representation_or_galois_module_of_fields}{unramified at} any (equivalently all) valuations of the function field of $X$ centered at $x$.

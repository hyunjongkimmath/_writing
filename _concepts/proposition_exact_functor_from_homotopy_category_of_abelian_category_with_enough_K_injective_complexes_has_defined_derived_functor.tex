\begin{proposition}[see {\cite[Tag 070K]{stacks-project}}] \label{proposition:exact_functor_from_homotopy_category_of_abelian_category_with_enough_K_injective_complexes_has_defined_derived_functor}
    Let $\calA$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_category}{abelian category}. Assume that every \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:chain_complex_of_objects_in_a_preadditive_category}{complex} of objects in $\calA$ has a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:left_right_resolution_of_a_chain_complex_in_an_abelian_category}{right resolution} by a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:K_injective_projective_complex_in_an_abelian_category}{$K$-injective} complex. 
    For any \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:exact_functor_between_triangulated_categories}{exact functor} $F: K(\calA) \to \calD'$(\Cref{definition:homotopy_category_of_chain_complexes_of_an_additive_category}) of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:triangulated_category}{triangulated categories}, the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:right_left_derived_multifunctor_of_an_additive_multifunctor_between_abelian_categories}{right derived functor} 
    $$RF: D(\calA) \to \calD'$$
    is \CrefAndHyperref{definition:defined_at_objects_derivable_and_adapted_objects_for_the_derived_functors_of_an_exact_multifunctor_between_triangulated_categories}{defined} and $RF(I^\bullet) = F(I^\bullet)$ for $K$-injective complexes $I^\bullet$. 
\end{proposition}

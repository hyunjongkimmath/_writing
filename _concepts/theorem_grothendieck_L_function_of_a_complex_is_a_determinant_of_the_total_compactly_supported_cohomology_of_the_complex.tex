\begin{theorem}[Grothendieck, see {\cite[Rapport 3 Th\'eor\`eme 3.1]{SGA4_5}}; see also {\cite[Theorem 10.5.1]{fu_ect}} for a statement and cf. {\cite[Theorem I.1.1]{kiehl_weissauer_wcps}}] \label{theorem:grothendieck_L_function_of_a_complex_is_a_determinant_of_the_total_compactly_supported_cohomology_of_the_complex} 
Let \(X_0\) be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:quasi_separated_morphism_of_schemes_and_separated_morphism_of_schemes}{separated} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_type_morphism_of_schemes}{finite type} over the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_field}{finite field} \(\mathbb{F}_q\), and let \(K_0 \in D^b_c(X_0, \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)\) (\Cref{definition:lambda_adic_cohomology_of_a_derived_etale_adic_sheaf_with_rational_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}). Write \(X = X_0 \times_{\mathbb{F}_q} \overline{\mathbb{F}}_q\) for the base change, and \(K\) for the the pullback of \(K_0\) to \(X\).

We have 
\[
L(X_0, K_0, t) = \prod_{i \in \mathbb{Z}} \det\left( 1 - t \cdot \mathrm{Frob}_q \mid H_c^i(X, K) \right)^{(-1)^{i+1}}.
\]
(\Cref{definition:L_function_of_a_constructible_complex_on_a_scheme_of_finite_type_over_a_finite_field}) where $\mathrm{Frob}_q$ is the geometric Frobenius endomorphism
\end{theorem}

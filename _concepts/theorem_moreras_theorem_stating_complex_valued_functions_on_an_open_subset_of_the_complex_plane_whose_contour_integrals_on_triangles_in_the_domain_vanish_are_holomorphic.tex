\begin{theorem}[Morera's theorem] \label{theorem:moreras_theorem_stating_complex_valued_functions_on_an_open_subset_of_the_complex_plane_whose_contour_integrals_on_triangles_in_the_domain_vanish_are_holomorphic}

Let $U \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be an open set and let $f : U \to \mathbb{C}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:continuous_map_of_topological_spaces}{continuous} function. Suppose that for every closed \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:piecewise_continuously_differentiable_path_from_a_closed_real_interval_to_the_complex_plane}{piecewise continuously differentiable path} $\gamma$ in $U$ whose image is the boundary of a triangle contained entirely in $U$, we have
$$\hlin{\oint_{\gamma} f(z)\,dz = 0.}$$
Then $f$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:holomorphic_function_from_a_subset_of_Cn_to_Cm_and_derivative_of_a_holomorphic_function_at_a_point}{holomorphic} on $U$. In particular, $f$ admits a holomorphic primitive $F : U \to \mathbb{C}$ such that $F'(z) = f(z)$ for all $z \in U$.
\end{theorem}

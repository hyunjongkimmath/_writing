\begin{proof}
    We show the first adjunction --- The second adjunction can be shown similarly. The adjunction holds for $n = 1$ by Claim \ref{claim:adjunction_of_truncation_functors}. The desired adjunction is a natural isomorphism 
    $$\Hom_{D}(A,B) \cong \Hom_{\Dleqn}(A, \tauleqn B)$$
    for all $A \in \Dleqn$ and $B \in D$. This is equivalent to the isomorphism
    $$\Hom_{D}(A,B) \cong \Hom_{\Dleqn}(A, \tauleqzero(B[n])[-n]).$$
    The right hand side Hom-group is equivalent to the Hom-group
    $$\Hom_{\Dleqzero}(A[n], \tauleqzero(B[n])) \cong \Hom_{D}(A[n], B[n]) \cong \Hom_{D}(A,B)$$
    and hence the desired isomorphism holds. 
\end{proof}

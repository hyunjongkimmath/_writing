\begin{proposition}[Nakayama's surjection criterion]  \label{proposition:nakayamas_surjection_criterion_for_homomorphisms_to_finitely_generated_modules_over_a_ring}

	Let $A$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring}, $\mathfrak{a}$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ideal_of_a_ring}{two-sided ideal} of $A$ with $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq \CrefAndHyperref{definition:jacobson_radical_of_a_ring}{J(A)}$. Let $M$ and $N$ be left $A$-modules, with $N$ \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finitely_generated_modules_over_rings}{finitely generated}, and let $u \colon M \to N$ be an $A$-linear homomorphism.

	If the induced homomorphism $\bar{u} \colon M/\mathfrak{a}M \to N/\mathfrak{a}N$ is surjective, then $u$ is surjective. (cf.\ \cite[Chapter 2, Exercise 10]{atiyah_macdonald})
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}

	Suppose that $\bar{u} \colon M/\mathfrak{a}M \to N/\mathfrak{a}N$ is surjective. Let $K = \operatorname{im}(u)$ be the image of $u$ in $N$. We wish to show that $K = N$.

	Consider the quotient module $N/K$. For any $\bar{n} \in N/\mathfrak{a}N$, by surjectivity of $\bar{u}$ there exists some $\bar{m} \in M/\mathfrak{a}M$ such that $\bar{u}(\bar{m}) = \bar{n}$. This means that for any representative $n \in N$ of $\bar{n}$, there exist $m \in M$ and $x \in \mathfrak{a}N$ such that $n - u(m) = x \in \mathfrak{a}N$. In other words, every element of $N$ lies in $K + \mathfrak{a}N$.

	Therefore $N = K + \mathfrak{a}N$, which implies $(K + \mathfrak{a}N)/K = N/K$, i.e., $\mathfrak{a}(N/K) = N/K$. Since $N$ is finitely generated and $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq J(A)$, apply Nakayama's lemma\CrefIfExists{lemma:nakayamas_lemma_for_finitely_generated_modules_over_a_ring_with_ideal_in_jacobson_radical} to the finitely generated module $N/K$ with ideal $\mathfrak{a}$ to conclude that $N/K = 0$. Therefore, $K = N$, which means $u$ is surjective.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem} \label{theorem:homotopy_equivalent_pairs_of_topological_spaces_have_homotopy_equivalent_chain_complexes}
Let $(X, A)$ and $(Y, B)$ be pairs of topological spaces and $G$ an abelian group. If $(X, A)$ and $(Y, B)$ are \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:homotopy_equivalence_between_topological_spaces}{homotopy equivalent} as pairs, then their corresponding \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:singular_chain_complex_of_a_topological_space_with_coefficients_in_an_abelian_group}{singular chain complexes} $C_{\bullet}(X, A; G)$ and $C_{\bullet}(Y, B; G)$ are \CrefAndHyperref{definition:chain_homotopy_between_chain_maps_between_complexes}{chain homotopy equivalent}. 

Consequently, there are isomorphisms of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:singular_homology_and_relative_singular_homology_of_topological_space_with_coefficients_in_an_abelian_group}{homology} and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:singular_cohomology_and_relative_singular_homology_of_topological_space_with_coefficients_in_an_abelian_group}{cohomology} groups:
$$ H_n(X, A; G) \cong H_n(Y, B; G) \quad \text{and} \quad H^n(X, A; G) \cong H^n(Y, B; G) $$
for all $n \ge 0$.
\end{theorem}

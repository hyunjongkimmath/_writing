\begin{proposition}[cf. {\cite[XVII Th\'eor\`eme 1.1]{SGA4_5}}] \label{proposition:bdd_constructible_derived_category_with_torsion_coefficients_is_triangulated}
    Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_type_morphism_of_schemes}{finite type} over a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field} $k$. Let $A$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{noetherian} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:torsion_ring}{torsion ring}. Consider \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_on_a_site}{sheaves} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:bimodule_over_rings_module_over_a_ring}{$A$-modules} on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{(small) \'etale site} of $X$.

    The category of \CrefAndHyperref{definition:constructible_sheaf_on_a_small_site_on_a_scheme_or_a_topological_space}{constructible sheaves} of $A$-modules on $X_{\et}$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_category}{abelian subcategory} of the category of all sheaves of $A$-modules on $X_{\et}$. Consequently, the full subcategory
    $$D_c^b(X_{\et}, A) \subseteq D(X_{\et}, A)$$
    \CrefIfExists{definition:derived_category_of_cohomologically_constructible_complexes_of_sheaves_of_modules_of_a_sheaf_of_rings_on_a_topological_space_or_scheme}\CrefIfExists{notation:derived_category_of_sheaves_of_abelian_groups_on_small_site_of_a_scehem}
    consisting of objects whose cohomology sheaves are constructible and vanish in all but finitely many degrees is a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:thick_subcategory_of_a_triangulated_category}{thick} \CrefAndHyperref{definition:triangulated_category}{triangulated subcategory} of $D(X_{\et}, A)$. In particular, $D_c^b(X_{\et}, A)$ is itself a triangulated category.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:adjunction_of_extension_by_zero_and_inverse_image_via_open_immersion_for_etale_sheaves_with_algebraic_adic_coefficients_on_noetherian_scheme}
    Let $(R, \lambda)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_ring_with_respect_to_an_ideal}{complete} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{DVR} such that $R/\lambda^n$ is finite for all $n \geq 1$. Let $E$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_fractions_of_an_integral_domain}{fraction field} of $R$, and let $K/E$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebraic_element_minimal_polynomial_of_an_algebraic_element_algebraic_field_extension_transcendental_element_field_extension}{algebraic extension}. Let $j: U \hookrightarrow X$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:open_and_closed_immersions_of_schemes}{open immersion} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{noetherian schemes}.

    The functors $j_! : \Sh(U, K) \to \Sh(X, K)$ (\Cref{definition:direct_image_functor_with_compact_support_on_etale_sheaves_with_algebraic_adic_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}) and $j^* : \Sh(X, K) \to \Sh(U, K)$ (\Cref{definition:inverse_image_functor_on_etale_sheaves_with_algebraic_adic_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}) form an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:adjoint_functors_between_categories_unit_counit_of_adjoint_functors}{adjunction} $j_! \dashv j^*$ between the categories of \'etale sheaves with algebraic adic coefficients on $U$ and $X$.

    This follows because the adjunction for \'etale sheaves with rational adic coefficients (\Cref{proposition:adjunction_of_extension_by_zero_and_inverse_image_via_open_immersion_for_etale_sheaves_with_rational_adic_coefficients_on_noetherian_scheme}) is preserved by filtered colimits over finite extensions of $E$ inside $K$, as both $j_!$ and $j^*$ commute with these colimits.
\end{proposition}

\begin{lemma}[Determinant lemma for modules]  \label{lemma:determinant_lemma_for_modules_over_a_ring}

Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring}, $M$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finitely_generated_modules_over_rings}{finitely generated} right $R$-module, $I$ a left ideal of $R$, and $\varphi \colon M \to M$ an $R$-linear endomorphism such that $\varphi(M) \subseteq MI$.

Then there exist elements $a_1, \ldots, a_{n-1} \in I$ and $a_n \in R$ such that
$$\varphi^n + a_1 \varphi^{n-1} + a_2 \varphi^{n-2} + \cdots + a_{n-1} \varphi + (a_n - 1) \operatorname{id}_M = 0$$
as an endomorphism of $M$.

In particular, if $I \subseteq \CrefAndHyperref{definition:jacobson_radical_of_a_ring}{J(R)}$, then $\varphi$ is invertible.
\end{lemma}

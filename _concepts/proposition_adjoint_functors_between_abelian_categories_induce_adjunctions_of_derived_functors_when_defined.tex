\begin{proposition}[{\cite[Tag 0DVC]{stacks-project}}] \label{proposition:adjoint_functors_between_abelian_categories_induce_adjunctions_of_derived_functors_when_defined}
Let $F : \mathcal{A} \to \mathcal{B}$ and $G : \mathcal{B} \to \mathcal{A}$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:functor_between_categories}{functors} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_category}{abelian categories} such that $F$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:adjoint_functors_between_categories_unit_counit_of_adjoint_functors}{right adjoint} to $G$; in particular, $F$ and $G$ are \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:additive_functor_between_additive_categories}{additive} (\Cref{lemma:left_right_exact_functor_between_abelian_categories_is_additive}). Let $K^\bullet$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:chain_complex_of_objects_in_a_preadditive_category}{complex} of $\mathcal{A}$ and let $M^\bullet$ be a complex of $\mathcal{B}$. If \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:right_left_derived_multifunctor_of_an_additive_multifunctor_between_abelian_categories}{$RF$} is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:defined_at_objects_derivable_and_adapted_objects_for_the_derived_functors_of_an_exact_multifunctor_between_triangulated_categories}{defined} at $K^\bullet$ and $LG$ is defined at $M^\bullet$, then there is a canonical isomorphism
\[ \text{Hom}_{D(\mathcal{B})}(M^\bullet, RF(K^\bullet)) = \text{Hom}_{D(\mathcal{A})}(LG(M^\bullet), K^\bullet) \]
This isomorphism is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:functor_between_categories}{functorial} in both variables on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:triangulated_category}{triangulated subcategories} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:derived_category_of_an_abelian_category}{$D(\mathcal{A})$} and $D(\mathcal{B})$ where $RF$ and $LG$ are defined.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Expansion of $\pi z \cot(\pi z)$ in terms of Bernoulli numbers] \label{proposition:cotangent_expansion_bernoulli_numbers}
\TODO{AI generated, verify}
For $|z| < 1$, the function $\pi z \cot(\pi z)$ has the power series expansion
$$
\pi z \cot(\pi z) = 1 - 2 \sum_{m=1}^\infty \frac{(-1)^{m+1} (2\pi)^{2m} B_{2m}}{(2m)!} z^{2m},
$$
where $B_{2m}$ are the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:bernoulli_numbers}{Bernoulli numbers} (with $B_1 = -1/2$, $B_{2m+1} = 0$ for $m \ge 1$).

Equivalently,
$$
\pi \cot(\pi z) = \frac{1}{z} - 2 \sum_{m=1}^\infty \frac{(-1)^{m+1} (2\pi)^{2m} B_{2m}}{(2m)!} z^{2m-1}, \qquad 0 < |z| < 1.
$$

This follows from the generating function of the Bernoulli numbers:
$$
\frac{w}{e^w - 1} = \sum_{n=0}^\infty B_n \frac{w^n}{n!}, \qquad |w| < 2\pi,
$$
by setting $w = 2\pi i z$ and using $e^{2\pi i z} - 1 = 2i e^{\pi i z} \sin(\pi z)$, together with the definition of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:real_sine_and_cosine_functions_on_real_line_via_power_series}{sine and cosine functions}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}

    We start from the generating function for the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:bernoulli_numbers}{Bernoulli numbers}:
    $$\frac{w}{e^w - 1} = \sum_{n=0}^\infty B_n \frac{w^n}{n!}, \qquad |w| < 2\pi.$$

    Setting $w = 2\pi i z$ with $|z| < 1$, we obtain
    $$\frac{2\pi i z}{e^{2\pi i z} - 1} = \sum_{n=0}^\infty B_n \frac{(2\pi i z)^n}{n!}.$$

    Using $e^{2\pi i z} - 1 = e^{\pi i z}(e^{\pi i z} - e^{-\pi i z}) = e^{\pi i z} \cdot 2i \sin(\pi z)$, we can rewrite the left-hand side as
    $$\frac{2\pi i z}{e^{2\pi i z} - 1} = \frac{\pi z}{e^{\pi i z} \sin(\pi z)} = \frac{\pi z (\cos(\pi z) - i \sin(\pi z))}{i \sin(\pi z)} = \pi z (-i \cot(\pi z) + 1).$$

    Equating the two expressions:
    $$\pi z (1 - i \cot(\pi z)) = \sum_{n=0}^\infty B_n \frac{(2\pi i z)^n}{n!}.$$

    Solving for $\pi z \cot(\pi z)$:
    $$\pi z \cot(\pi z) = i \pi z - \frac{i}{z} \sum_{n=0}^\infty B_n \frac{(2\pi i z)^n}{n!}.$$

    Separating the $n=0$ term ($B_0 = 1$):
    $$\pi z \cot(\pi z) = i \pi z - i(2\pi) - \frac{i}{z} \sum_{n=1}^\infty B_n \frac{(2\pi i z)^n}{n!}.$$

    Since $B_1 = -1/2$, the $n=1$ term is $B_1 (2\pi i z) = -\pi i z$. The remaining terms for $n \geq 2$ use $B_{2m+1} = 0$ for $m \geq 1$:
    $$\pi z \cot(\pi z) = i \pi z - 2\pi i + \pi i z - \frac{i}{z} \sum_{m=1}^\infty B_{2m} \frac{(2\pi i z)^{2m}}{(2m)!}.$$

    Simplifying the constant terms: $i\pi z - 2\pi i + \pi i z = 2\pi i z - 2\pi i$. We verify that this equals $1$ by checking at $z=0$: $\lim_{z \to 0} \pi z \cot(\pi z) = 1$. The series term:
    $$-\frac{i}{z} \sum_{m=1}^\infty B_{2m} \frac{(2\pi i)^{2m} z^{2m}}{(2m)!} = -i \sum_{m=1}^\infty B_{2m} \frac{(2\pi i)^{2m} z^{2m-1}}{(2m)!}.$$

    Since $(2\pi i)^{2m} = (2\pi)^{2m} (-1)^m$, we have
    $$-i B_{2m} \frac{(2\pi)^{2m}(-1)^m z^{2m-1}}{(2m)!} = -2 \cdot \frac{(-1)^{m+1}(2\pi)^{2m} B_{2m}}{(2m)!} z^{2m},$$
    where we used $i(-1)^m = (-1)^{m+1} i$ and simplified the factor of $2$. This gives the claimed expansion.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:adjunction_of_extension_by_zero_and_inverse_image_via_open_immersion_for_lambda_adic_sheaves_with_integral_coefficients_on_noetherian_scheme}
    Let $(R, \lambda)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_ring_with_respect_to_an_ideal}{complete} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{DVR} such that $R/\lambda^n$ is finite for all $n \geq 1$. Let $j: U \hookrightarrow X$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:open_and_closed_immersions_of_schemes}{open immersion} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{noetherian schemes}.

    The functors $j_! : \Sh(U, R) \to \Sh(X, R)$ and $j^* : \Sh(X, R) \to \Sh(U, R)$ (\Cref{definition:direct_image_functor_with_compact_support_on_lambda_adic_sheaves_with_integral_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}, \Cref{definition:inverse_image_functor_on_lambda_adic_sheaves_with_integral_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}) form an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:adjoint_functors_between_categories_unit_counit_of_adjoint_functors}{adjunction} $j_! \dashv j^*$ between the categories of $\lambda$-adic sheaves on $U$ and $X$.

    This follows because, for each $n \geq 1$, the functors $j_!^{(n)} : \Sh(U_{\et}, R/\lambda^n) \to \Sh(X_{\et}, R/\lambda^n)$ and $j^* : \Sh(X_{\et}, R/\lambda^n) \to \Sh(U_{\et}, R/\lambda^n)$ form an adjunction (\Cref{definition:direct_image_functor_with_compact_support_on_etale_sheaves_of_lambda_modules_for_a_compactifiable_morphism}, \Cref{proposition:adjunction_of_extension_by_zero_and_inverse_image_via_open_immersion_for_etale_sheaves_of_abelian_groups_on_scheme}), and the $\lambda$-adic functors are defined level-wise from these.
\end{proposition}

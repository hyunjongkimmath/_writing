Therefore, any complex in $\calA$ has a left-resolution by a $K$-projective object by \Cref{theorem:AB5_AB5_star_categories_with_left_right_resolutions_for_bounded_complexes_by_a_class_has_resolutions_for_all_complexes_by_the_class}

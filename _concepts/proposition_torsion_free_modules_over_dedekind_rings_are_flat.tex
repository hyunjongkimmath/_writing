\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:torsion_free_modules_over_dedekind_rings_are_flat}
Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dedekind_domain}{Dedekind ring}. 
A \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:torsion_free_one_sided_module_over_a_ring}{torsion free module} over $R$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:flat_left_right_module_over_a_ring}{flat}.
\end{proposition}

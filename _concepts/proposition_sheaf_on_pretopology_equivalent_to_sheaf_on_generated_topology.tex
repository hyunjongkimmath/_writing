\begin{proposition}[Sheaves on a pretopology vs. sheaves on the generated topology] \label{proposition:sheaf_on_pretopology_equivalent_to_sheaf_on_generated_topology}

    Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category}{category} equipped with a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:basis_and_grothendieck_pretopology_for_a_grothendieck_topology_on_a_category}{Grothendieck pretopology} $K$.
    Let $J_K$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_generated_by_a_pretopology}{Grothendieck topology generated by $K$}.
    Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category}{category}.

    A \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:presheaf_on_a_category}{presheaf} $F: \mathcal{C}^{\op} \to \mathcal{A}$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_on_a_grothendieck_pretopology_valued_in_a_category}{sheaf on $(\mathcal{C}, K)$ valued in $\mathcal{A}$} if and only if $F$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_on_a_site}{sheaf on the site $(\mathcal{C}, J_K)$ valued in $\mathcal{A}$}.

    In other words, the full subcategory of $\PreShv(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{A})$ consisting of sheaves with respect to $K$ coincides with the full subcategory consisting of sheaves with respect to $J_K$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}

    $(\Rightarrow)$ Suppose $F$ is a sheaf on $(\mathcal{C}, K)$. Let $U$ be an object of $\mathcal{C}$ and let $S \in J_K(U)$ be a covering sieve. By the definition of $J_K$\CrefIfExists{definition:grothendieck_topology_generated_by_a_pretopology}, there exists a covering family $\{U_i \to U\}_{i \in I} \in K(U)$ whose generated sieve is contained in $S$.

    The sheaf condition for the pretopology asserts that the canonical morphism
    $$F(U) \longrightarrow \varprojlim_{[n] \in \Delta} F(\check{C}(\{U_i \to U\})_n)$$
    is an isomorphism, where $\check{C}(\{U_i \to U\})$ is the Čech nerve of the covering family.

    The sieve $S$ contains the sieve generated by $\{U_i \to U\}_{i \in I}$, so there is a canonical functor from the index category of the limit over $S$ to the index category of the Čech nerve (via the inclusion of the sub-diagram). This functor is cofinal: every object $(V \to U)$ in the sieve $S$ admits a map from some object in the Čech nerve, since by definition of $J_K$, any morphism in $S$ factors through some $U_i$. By the cofinality of limits (a limit over a diagram indexed by $\Delta$ can be computed as a limit over any cofinal sub-diagram), it follows that
    $$\varprojlim_{(V \to U) \in (\mathcal{D}_S)^{\op}} F(V) \cong \varprojlim_{[n] \in \Delta} F(\check{C}(\{U_i \to U\})_n),$$
    so $F(U) \cong \varprojlim_{(V \to U) \in (\mathcal{D}_S)^{\op}} F(V)$, meaning $F$ satisfies the sheaf condition for $S$.

    $(\Leftarrow)$ Suppose $F$ is a sheaf on $(\mathcal{C}, J_K)$. Let $\{U_i \to U\}_{i \in I}$ be a covering family in $K(U)$. The sieve $S$ generated by this family belongs to $J_K(U)$, so the sheaf condition for $F$ with respect to $S$ gives an isomorphism
    $$F(U) \cong \varprojlim_{(V \to U) \in (\mathcal{D}_S)^{\op}} F(V).$$

    The Čech nerve of $\{U_i \to U\}_{i \in I}$ embeds as a cofinal sub-diagram into the diagram indexed by $(\mathcal{D}_S)^{\op}$. (Every morphism $V \to U$ in $S$ factors through some $U_i$, and the simplicial structure of the Čech nerve captures all higher-order matching conditions.) Therefore,
    $$F(U) \cong \varprojlim_{[n] \in \Delta} F(\check{C}(\{U_i \to U\})_n),$$
    which is precisely the sheaf condition for the pretopology $K$.

    Therefore, the two notions of sheaf coincide.
\end{proof}

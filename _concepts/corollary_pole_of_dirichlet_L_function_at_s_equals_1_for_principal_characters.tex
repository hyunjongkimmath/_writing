Let $\chi_0$ be the principal \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dirichlet_character_modulo_a_nonnegative_integer}{Dirichlet character modulo $k$}. The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:dirichlet_L_function_of_a_dirichlet_character_modulo_a_nonnegative_integer}{Dirichlet $L$-function} $L(s, \chi_0)$ has a simple pole at $s = 1$ with residue
$$
\operatorname{Res}_{s=1} L(s, \chi_0) = \frac{\varphi(k)}{k} = \prod_{p \mid k} \left( 1 - \frac{1}{p} \right),
$$
where $\varphi$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:euler_totient_function}{Euler totient function}.

In particular, for the trivial character $\mathbf{1}_1$ modulo $1$, $L(s, \mathbf{1}_1) = \zeta(s)$ has a simple pole at $s=1$ with residue $1$.

\begin{proposition}[Weak* topology is the coarsest making evaluations continuous] \label{proposition:weak_star_topology_is_coarsest_making_evaluations_continuous}
    Let $V$ be a normed vector space over $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, and let $V'$ denote its continuous dual. The weak* topology on $V'$ is the coarsest (i.e., smallest) topology such that every evaluation map $\mathrm{ev}_v : V' \to k$ defined by $\mathrm{ev}_v(f) = f(v)$ for $v \in V$ is continuous, where each copy of $k$ carries its standard Euclidean topology.

    Equivalently, the weak* topology on $V'$ coincides with the initial topology with respect to the family of evaluation maps $\{\mathrm{ev}_v : V' \to k\}_{v \in V}$. In particular, a net $(f_\alpha)_{\alpha \in D}$ in $V'$ converges weak* to $f \in V'$ if and only if $f_\alpha(v) \to f(v)$ for every $v \in V$.
\end{proposition}

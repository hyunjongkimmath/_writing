\begin{theorem}[Relative Riemann--Roch for a family of curves] \label{theorem:relative_riemann_roch_for_a_family_of_curves}

    Let $k$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}. Let $f: X \to S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:smooth_morphism_of_schemes}{smooth}, \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:proper_morphism_of_schemes}{proper morphism} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:relative_dimension_for_a_morphism_of_schemes}{relative dimension} $1$ between \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:projective_variety_over_a_field}{projective varieties} over $k$, such that each geometric fiber $C_s = f^{-1}(s)$ is a connected curve of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:geometric_genus_of_a_smooth_projective_variety_over_a_field}{genus} $g$.

    Let $\mathcal{L}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:free_and_locally_free_modules_on_a_ringed_site}{line bundle} on $X$. For each closed point $s \in S$, let $\mathcal{L}_s = \mathcal{L}|_{C_s}$ be the restriction to the fiber. The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:euler_characteristic_of_a_coherent_sheaf_on_a_proper_scheme_over_a_field}{Euler characteristic} $\chi(\mathcal{L}_s) = \dim_k H^0(C_s, \mathcal{L}_s) - \dim_k H^1(C_s, \mathcal{L}_s)$ is independent of $s$, and
    $$\chi(\mathcal{L}_s) = \deg(\mathcal{L}_s) + 1 - g.$$

    Moreover, the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:inverse_image_and_direct_image_of_sheaves_on_schemes_for_various_topologies}{direct image} $f_*\mathcal{L}$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebraic_vector_bundle_over_a_scheme}{vector bundle} on $S$, and by Grauert's theorem, $R^1 f_* \mathcal{L}$ is also a vector bundle. The rank of $f_*\mathcal{L}$ equals $\dim_k H^0(C_s, \mathcal{L}_s)$ for all $s \in S$.
\end{theorem}

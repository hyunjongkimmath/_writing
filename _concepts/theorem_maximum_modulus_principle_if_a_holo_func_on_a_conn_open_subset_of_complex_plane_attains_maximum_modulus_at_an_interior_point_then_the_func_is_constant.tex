\begin{theorem} \label{theorem:maximum_modulus_principle_if_a_holo_func_on_a_conn_open_subset_of_complex_plane_attains_maximum_modulus_at_an_interior_point_then_the_func_is_constant}
Let $U \subset \mathbb{C}$ be a connected open subset (a domain) and let $f : U \to \mathbb{C}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:holomorphic_function_from_a_subset_of_Cn_to_Cm}{holomorphic function} on $U$. 
If there exists a point $z_0 \in U$ such that
$\hlin{|f(z_0)| \geq |f(z)|}$
for all $z \in U$, then $f$ is constant on $U$. Equivalently, a non-constant holomorphic function on a connected open subset of $\mathbb{C}$ cannot attain its maximum modulus at an interior point.
\end{theorem}

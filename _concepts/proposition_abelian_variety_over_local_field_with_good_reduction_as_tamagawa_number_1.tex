\begin{proposition}
    Let $A/K$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_variety_over_a_field}{abelian variety} over a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:local_field}{local field}. Write $v$ for the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:prime_and_maximal_ideal_of_a_ring}{maximal ideal} of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring_of_integers_of_a_global_or_local_ring}{ring of integers} $\calO_K$ or equivalently the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:closed_point_of_a_topological_space}{closed point} of $\Spec \calO_K$.

    If $A/K$ has \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:good_reduction_of_an_abelian_variety_over_a_dedekind_scheme}{good reduction}, then the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:local_tamagawa_numbers_of_abelian_varieties_over_local_fields}{local Tamagawa number} $c_v$ of $A$ is $1$. 
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
    Write $\calA$ for the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:neron_model_of_an_abelian_variety_over_the_function_field_of_an_integral_scheme.tex}{N\'eron model} of $A$.  Since $A$ has good reduction, the special \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:fiber_of_a_map_of_topological_spaces_over_a_point}{fiber} $\calA_v$ of the N\'eron model is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_variety_over_a_field}{abelian variety}. In particular, $\calA_v$ is connected, so $\calA_v^0 = \calA_v$. Thus, 
    $$c_v \coloneq |(\calA_v/\calA_v^0)(\kappa_v)| = 1$$
    as desired.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Lying Over for an Integral Extension of Commutative Rings] \label{theorem:lying_over_for_integral_extension_of_commutative_rings}

Let $f : A \to B$ be a homomorphism of commutative rings with unity such that $B$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:integral_element_over_a_ring}{integral over the image of $A}$ (i.e., every element of $B$ satisfies a monic polynomial with coefficients in $f(A)$). Then the induced map on spectra
$$\hlin{f^* : \operatorname{Spec}(B) \to \operatorname{Spec}(A), \quad \mathfrak{q} \mapsto f^{-1}(\mathfrak{q})}$$
is surjective. Equivalently, for every prime ideal $\mathfrak{p} \subset A$, there exists a prime ideal $\mathfrak{q} \subset B$ such that $f^{-1}(\mathfrak{q}) = \mathfrak{p}$ (we say $\mathfrak{q}$ lies over $\mathfrak{p}$).
\end{theorem}

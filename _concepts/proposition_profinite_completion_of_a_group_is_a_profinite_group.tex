\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:profinite_completion_of_a_group_is_a_profinite_group}
Let $G$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group} and $\widehat{G}$ its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:profinite_completion_of_a_group}{profinite completion}. 
The group $\widehat{G}$, equipped with the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:profinite_set}{profinite topology} (where each $G/N$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_group}{discrete}), is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:profinite_group}{profinite group}. 
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[Essential smallness of common small sites] \label{theorem:common_small_sites_of_a_scheme_are_essentially_small}
Let $X$ be a scheme. The underlying categories of the following sites are \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:essentially_small_category}{essentially small}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_zariski_site_of_a_schem}{small Zariski site $X_{\mathrm{Zar}}$} (\CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{lemma:small_zariski_site_of_a_scheme_is_essentially_small}{lemma:small\_zariski\_site\_of\_a\_scheme\_is\_essentially\_small}).
    \item The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_nisnevich_site_of_a_schem}{small Nisnevich site $X_{\mathrm{Nis}}$} (\CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{lemma:small_nisnevich_site_of_a_scheme_is_essentially_small}{lemma:small\_nisnevich\_site\_of\_a\_scheme\_is\_essentially\_small}).
    \item The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{small étale site $X_{\et}$} (\CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{lemma:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme_is_essentially_small}{lemma:small\_etale\_site\_of\_a\_scheme\_is\_essentially\_small}).
    \item The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_fppf_site_of_a_scheme}{small fppf site $X_{\mathrm{fppf}}$} (\CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{lemma:small_fppf_site_of_a_scheme_is_essentially_small}{lemma:small\_fppf\_site\_of\_a\_scheme\_is\_essentially\_small}).
\end{itemize}
The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_fpqc_site_of_a_schem}{small fpqc site $X_{\mathrm{fpqc}}$} is not essentially small in general, as faithfully flat quasi-compact morphisms lack the finiteness constraints that bound the number of isomorphism classes.
\end{theorem}

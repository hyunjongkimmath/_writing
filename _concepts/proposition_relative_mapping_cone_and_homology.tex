Let $f: (X, A) \to (Y, B)$ be a continuous map of pairs. Let $G$ be an abelian group. 
The mapping cone of the pair $(C_f, C_{f|_A})$ relates to the mapping cylinder pair $(M_f, M_{f|_A})$ via a natural isomorphism:
$$ H_n(C_f, C_{f|_A} \cup \{pt\}; G) \cong H_n(M_f, M_{f|_A} \cup X; G) $$

Furthermore, if $f$ is an inclusion of CW pairs $(X, A) \hookrightarrow (Y, B)$, then the relative mapping cone $(C_f, C_{f|_A})$ is homotopy equivalent to the pair of quotients:
$$ (C_f, C_{f|_A}) \simeq (Y/X, B/A) $$
under the condition that the inclusions are good pairs.

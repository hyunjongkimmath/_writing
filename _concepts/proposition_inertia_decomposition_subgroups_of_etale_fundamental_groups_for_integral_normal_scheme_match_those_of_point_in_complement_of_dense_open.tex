\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:inertia_decomposition_subgroups_of_etale_fundamental_groups_for_integral_normal_scheme_match_those_of_point_in_complement_of_dense_open}
    Let $X$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:integral_scheme}{integral} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:normal_and_factorial_scheme}{normal scheme} with \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:generic_point_of_a_topological_space}{generic point} $\eta$ and \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:function_field_over_a_field}{function field} $K$. Let $\overline{\eta}$ be a geometric point of $X$ whose image is $\eta$. Let $U \subseteq X$ be a non-empty \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:open_and_closed_subscheme_of_a_scheme}{open subscheme}. Let $x \in X \setminus U$ be a point and let $\overline{x}$ be a geometric point of with image $x$.
    
    Recall that (\Cref{proposition:map_of_etale_fundamental_group_for_open_in_irreducible_geometrically_unibranch_scheme_is_surjective}) the canonical map
    $$\pioneet(U, \overline{\eta}) \to \pioneet(X, \overline{\eta})$$
    is surjective. Recall also that $\pioneet(X, \overline{\eta})$ is isomorphic to $\pioneet(X, \overline{x})$ \TODO{}. 

    The images of the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:decomposition_and_inertia_subgroups_of_etale_fundamental_group_of_dense_open_of_integral_normal_scheme_at_point_in_complement}{decomposition and inertia subgroups} of $\pioneet(U, \overline{\eta})$ under the surjection
    $$\pioneet(U, \overline{\eta}) \twoheadrightarrow \pioneet(X, \overline{\eta}) \cong \pioneet(X, \overline{x})$$
    coincide with the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:decomposition_group_of_the_etale_fundamental_group_of_a_connected_scheme_at_a_geometric_point}{decomposition} and \CrefAndHyperref{definition:inertia_group_of_the_etale_fundamental_group_of_a_connected_scheme_at_a_geometric_point}{inertia subgroups} of $\pioneet(X, \overline{x})$
\end{proposition}

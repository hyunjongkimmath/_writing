\begin{proposition}[Local Verdier duality pairing, cf. {\cite[Theorem 8.4.4]{fu_ect}}] \label{proposition:local_verdier_duality_pairing_for_compactifiable_morphisms_with_torsion_coefficients}
    Let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:locally_noetherian_and_noetherian_scheme}{noetherian scheme}. Let $A$ be a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:noetherian_ring}{noetherian} \CrefAndHyperref{definition:torsion_ring}{torsion ring}. Consider sheaves of $A$-modules on the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{(small) \'etale sites} of schemes over $S$.

    Let $g: X \to Y$ be an $S$-\CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:compactification_of_a_morphism_of_schemes}{compactifiable morphism} between $S$-schemes of \CrefAndHyperref{definition:finite_type_morphism_of_schemes}{finite type}. For any $K \in D^+(X_{\et}, A)$ with constructible cohomology sheaves and any $L \in D^+(Y_{\et}, A)$ with constructible cohomology sheaves, the adjunction $(Rg_!, Rg^!)$ from \Cref{definition:six_functor_formalism_on_derived_category_of_etale_sheaves_with_torsion_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme} yields a natural isomorphism
    $$\Hom_{D^+(X_{\et}, A)}(K, Rg^! L) \cong \Hom_{D^+(Y_{\et}, A)}(Rg_! K, L)$$

    In particular, taking $Y = S$ and $L = \mathbb{D}_S^\bullet = A_S$, one obtains $\mathbb{D}_{X/S}^\bullet = Rf^! \mathbb{D}_S^\bullet$ where $f: X \to S$ is the structure morphism.
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem} \label{theorem:descent_datum_for_a_category_fibered_in_groupoids_over_a_category_with_respect_to_a_single_morphism_is_eq_cat_of_sieve_gen_by_morphism}
    Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and let $p: \mathcal{F} \to \mathcal{C}$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category_fibered_in_groupoids_over_a_category}{category fibered in groupoids}. We fix a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cleavage_splitting_for_a_category_fibered_in_groupoids}{cleavage} for $p$, denoting the chosen pullback functor along a morphism $f: V \to U$ by $f^*: \mathcal{F}_U \to \mathcal{F}_V$, and the structural natural isomorphisms for composition by $\alpha_{g,h}: (g \circ h)^* \xrightarrow{\sim} h^* \circ g^*$ for morphisms $W \xrightarrow{h} V \xrightarrow{g} U$.

    Let $\pi: U' \to U$ be a morphism in $
    \calC$ such that $U' \times_U U'$ \CrefIfExists{definition:cartesian_product_of_two_objects_in_a_category_over_an_object} exists in $\calC$. Let $S_\pi$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sieve_on_an_object_in_a_category}{sieve on $U$} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sieve_on_an_object_of_a_category_generated_by_a_family_of_morphisms}{generated by} $\pi$.

    The \CrefAndHyperref{definition:descent_datum_for_a_category_fibered_in_groupoids_over_a_category_relative_to_a_sieve_on_an_object_of_the_base_category}{category of descent data for $\calF$ with respect to $S_\pi$} is equivalent to the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:descent_datum_for_a_category_fibered_in_groupoids_over_a_category_relative_to_a_single_morphism}{category of descent data for $\calF$ with respect to $\pi$}.

    Under this equivalence, the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:effective_descent_datum_for_a_category_fibered_in_groupoids_over_a_category_relative_to_a_sieve_on_an_object_of_the_base_category}{effective descent data} correspond to the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:effective_descent_datum_for_a_category_fibered_in_groupoids_over_a_category_relative_to_a_single_morphism_in_the_base_category}{effective descent data}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proposition}[Archimedean property of the real numbers] \label{proposition:archimedean_property_of_real_numbers}

    Let $\bbR$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_real_numbers}{field of real numbers}.

    \begin{enumerate}
        \item For every $x \in \bbR$, there exists a natural number $n \in \bbN$ such that $n > x$.

        \item For every $\varepsilon \in \bbR$ with $\varepsilon > 0$, there exists a natural number $N \in \bbN$ such that $\frac{1}{N} < \varepsilon$.

\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:there_are_enough_K_flat_resolutions_for_modules_on_an_essentially_small_ringed_site}
    Let $(\calC, J, \calO)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ringed_site}{ringed site} whose underlying \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{site} is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:essentially_small_site}{essentially small}.

    There are enough $K$-flat resolutions to the left of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:chain_complex_of_objects_in_an_additive_category}{chain complexes} in  of objects in $\Mod_{\calO}$ and ${}_{\calO} \Mod$. More precisely, for any \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:chain_complex_of_objects_in_an_additive_category}{chain complex} $A_\bullet \in \mathbf{Ch}(\Mod_{\calO})$ (resp. $\mathbf{Ch}({}_{\calO} \Mod$)), there is a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:K_flat_complex_in_an_abelian_category_with_respect_to_a_biadditive_functor}{$K$-flat} (with respect to $\otimes: \Mod_{\calO} \times {}_{\calO} \Mod \to \Sh(C,J;\Ab)$) chain complex $F_\bullet \in \Mod_{\calO}$ and a \CrefAndHyperref{definition:left_right_resolution_of_a_chain_complex_in_an_abelian_category}{left resolution} $F_\bullet \to A_\bullet$. 
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
    Since $\Mod_{\calO}$ and ${}_{\calO} \Mod$ have enough flats on the left by \Cref{proposition:category_of_sheaves_of_modules_over_a_ringed_site_on_an_essentially_small_site_has_enough_flats}, every (homologically) bounded below complex in $\Mod_{\calO}$ and ${}_{\calO} \Mod$ has a left resolution by a (homologically) bounded below complex of flat objects; a (homologically) bounded below complex of flats is $K$-flat with respect to $\otimes$ by \Cref{lemma:bounded_above_complex_of_flat_objects_are_K_flat_with_respect_to_bi_daditive_functor_on_abelian_categories_where_target_is_AB5}. To see that \Cref{lemma:bounded_above_complex_of_flat_objects_are_K_flat_with_respect_to_bi_daditive_functor_on_abelian_categories_where_target_is_AB5} applies, note that $\otimes$ is a left adjoint by \Cref{theorem:tensor_hom_adjucntion_for_sheaves_of_modules_over_sheaves_of_rings_on_a_site_with_a_small_topologically_generating_family} and hence commutes with colimits by \Cref{theorem:right_left_adjoints_commute_with_limits_colimits}, and note that $\Sh(C,J;\Ab)$ is AB5. \Cref{theorem:AB5_AB5_star_categories_with_left_right_resolutions_for_bounded_complexes_by_a_class_has_resolutions_for_all_complexes_by_the_class} then applies to conclude that chain complex of objects in $\Mod_{\calO}$ and ${}_{\calO} \Mod$ has a left $K$-flat resolution.
\end{proof}

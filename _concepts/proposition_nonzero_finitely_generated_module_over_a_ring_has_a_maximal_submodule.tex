\begin{proposition}[Nonzero finitely generated module has a maximal submodule]  \label{proposition:nonzero_finitely_generated_module_over_a_ring_has_a_maximal_submodule}

	Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring} and let $M$ be a nonzero \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finitely_generated_modules_over_rings}{finitely generated} right $R$-module. Then there exists a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:submodule_of_a_module_over_a_ring}{proper submodule} $L \subsetneq M$ that is maximal among proper submodules, i.e., no proper submodule of $M$ strictly contains $L$.

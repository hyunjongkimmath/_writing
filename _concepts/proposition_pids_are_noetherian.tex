\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:pids_are_noetherian}
    Every \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:principal_ideal_ring}{PID} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:noetherian_ring}{Noetherian ring}.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
    By definition, every ideal of a PID is finitely generated. In general, a commutative ring is Noetherian if and only if all of its ideals are finitely generated\CrefIfExists{proposition:commutative_ring_is_noetherian_iff_every_ideal_is_finitely_generated}. Therefore, every PID is Noetherian.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary} \label{corollary:left_right_artinian_rings_are_left_right_perfect}
Every \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:left_right_artinian_ring}{left Artinian} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring} is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:perfect_ring}{left perfect}. However, a left perfect ring need not be left Artinian, as the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ascending_descending_chain_condition_on_a_partially_ordered_set}{DCC} is only required on \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ideal_generated_by_a_subset_of_a_ring}{principal} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ideal_of_a_ring}{right ideals}.
\end{corollary}

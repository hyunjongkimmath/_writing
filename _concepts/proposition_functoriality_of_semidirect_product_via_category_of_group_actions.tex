\begin{proposition}[Functoriality of the semidirect product] \label{proposition:functoriality_of_semidirect_product_via_category_of_group_actions}
    The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:semidirect_product_of_groups_external_and_internal}{external semidirect product} defines a functor
    $$\rtimes : \mathbf{ActGrp} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Grp}$$
    from the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category_of_group_actions}{category of group actions} to the category of groups.

    On objects, $\rtimes(N, H, \phi) = N \rtimes_\phi H$.
    On morphisms, given $(f, g): (N, H, \phi) \to (M, K, \psi)$, the induced homomorphism
    $$(f \rtimes g) : N \rtimes_\phi H \longrightarrow M \rtimes_\psi K$$
    is defined by $(f \rtimes g)(n, h) = (f(n), g(h))$.
\end{proposition}

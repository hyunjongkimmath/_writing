\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:category_of_abelian_groups_is_equivalent_to_categories_of_Z_modules}

    The following categories are pairwise \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:equivalence_of_categories}{equivalent}:
    the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category_of_groups_of_abelian_groups}{category of abelian groups}, the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category_of_modules_and_bimodules_over_rings}{category of left $\bbZ$-modules}, the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category_of_modules_and_bimodules_over_rings}{category of right $\bbZ$-modules} $\mathsf{Mod}_\bbZ$, and the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category_of_modules_and_bimodules_over_rings}{category of two-sided $\bbZ$-modules} ${}_\bbZ\mathsf{Mod}_\bbZ$. More precisely, the equivalences can be constructed as follows:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item
        Equivalence with left $\bbZ$-modules.
        Let $F_\ell \colon \mathbf{Ab} \to {}_\bbZ\mathsf{Mod}$ be the functor that equips an abelian group $(A,+)$ with the canonical left $\bbZ$-action
        $$n \cdot m = \underbrace{m + \cdots + m}_{n \text{ times}} \quad (n > 0), \qquad 0 \cdot m = 0_A, \qquad (-n) \cdot m = -(n \cdot m) \quad (n > 0),$$
        and acts as the identity on morphisms. Let $G_\ell \colon {}_\bbZ\mathsf{Mod} \to \mathbf{Ab}$ be the forgetful functor that sends a left $\bbZ$-module to its underlying abelian group. Then:

        \begin{enumerate}
            \item $F_\ell \circ G_\ell$ is naturally isomorphic to $\operatorname{id}_{{}_\bbZ\mathsf{Mod}}$, because for any left $\bbZ$-module $M$ the scalar multiplication by integers is uniquely determined by the additive structure of $M$: namely, $n \cdot m$ must equal $m + \cdots + m$ ($n$ times) for $n > 0$, with $0 \cdot m = 0_M$ and $(-n) \cdot m = -(n \cdot m)$.

            \item $G_\ell \circ F_\ell = \operatorname{id}_{\mathbf{Ab}}$, because the canonical left $\bbZ$-action on an abelian group uses only the addition and inverse operations already present.
        \end{enumerate}

        Any group homomorphism between left $\bbZ$-modules is automatically $\bbZ$-linear because $f(n \cdot m) = n \cdot f(m)$ for all $n \in \bbZ$, by additivity of $f$. Hence, $F_\ell$ is fully faithful and essentially surjective, so it is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:equivalence_of_categories}{equivalence of categories}.

        \item Equivalence with right $\bbZ$-modules.
        Similarly, let $F_r \colon \mathbf{Ab} \to \mathsf{Mod}_\bbZ$ equip an abelian group $(A,+)$ with the canonical right $\bbZ$-action
        $$m \cdot n = \underbrace{m + \cdots + m}_{n \text{ times}} \quad (n > 0), \qquad m \cdot 0 = 0_A, \qquad m \cdot (-n) = -(m \cdot n) \quad (n > 0),$$
        and let $G_r \colon \mathsf{Mod}_\bbZ \to \mathbf{Ab}$ be the forgetful functor. The same argument shows that $F_r$ is an equivalence of categories with inverse $G_r$.

        \item Equivalence with two-sided $\bbZ$-modules.
        Let $F_{\ell r} \colon \mathbf{Ab} \to {}_\bbZ\mathsf{Mod}_\bbZ$ equip an abelian group $(A,+)$ with the left and right $\bbZ$-actions defined above (which coincide since $\bbZ$ is commutative), making $n \cdot m = m \cdot n$. Let $G_{\ell r} \colon {}_\bbZ\mathsf{Mod}_\bbZ \to \mathbf{Ab}$ be the forgetful functor. Then:
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item $F_{\ell r} \circ G_{\ell r}$ is naturally isomorphic to $\operatorname{id}_{{}_\bbZ\mathsf{Mod}_\bbZ}$, because any two-sided $\bbZ$-module has scalar multiplication determined by the additive structure.
            \item $G_{\ell r} \circ F_{\ell r} = \operatorname{id}_{\mathbf{Ab}}$.
        \end{enumerate}
        Any group homomorphism between two-sided $\bbZ$-modules is automatically a bimodule map, so $F_{\ell r}$ is fully faithful and essentially surjective.

    \end{enumerate}

\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem} \label{theorem:constant_sheaves_are_acyclic_on_contractible_and_loc_contr_spaces}
    Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:topological_space}{topological space}. Let $\calA$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_category}{abelian category} with \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:has_enough_injectives_or_projectives_for_an_abelian_category}{enough injectives} and such that a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheafification_of_a_presheaf_on_a_topological_space}{sheafification functor}
    $$a: \PreShv(X, \calA) \to \Shv(X, \calA) $$
    exists. In particular, the notion of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_cohomology_objects_of_a_sheaf_on_a_top_space_in_an_ab_catwith_enough_injs_and_sheafification}{sheaf cohomology objects} is well defined. Moreover, these conditions are satisfied for $\calA = \Ab$\CrefIfExists{theorem:examples_of_abelian_categories_with_enough_injectives_or_projectives}\CrefIfExists{theorem:sheafification_for_sheaves_of_sets_on_a_topological_space_exist}.  Let $\calF$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_on_a_topological_space_valued_in_a_category_with_a_terminal_object}{sheaf of} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{abelian groups} on $X$.
    
    Assuming that $X$ is a contractible and locally contractible topological space and $\calF$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:constant_sheaf_on_a_topological_space_valued_in_category_admitting_sheafification}{constant sheaf} on $X$ valued in $\calA$, the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_cohomology_objects_of_a_sheaf_on_a_top_space_in_an_ab_catwith_enough_injs_and_sheafification}{sheaf cohomology objects $H^p(X, \calF)$} all vanish for $p > 0$. 
\end{theorem}

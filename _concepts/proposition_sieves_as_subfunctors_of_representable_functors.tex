\begin{proposition}[{see \cite[Expos\'e I, Proposition 4.2.]{SGA4_I}}] \label{proposition:sieves_as_subfunctors_of_representable_functors}
Let $\calC$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_small_category}{locally small category} (or a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:locally_small_category}{$\mathscr{U}$-locally small category} if a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_universe}{Grothendieck universe} $\mathscr{U}$ is available), and let $X$ be an object of $\calC$. Denote by $h_X = \operatorname{Hom}_{\calC}(-, X): \calC^{\operatorname{op}} \to \mathbf{Set}$ the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:representable_functor_on_a_locally_small_category}{representable functor} associated to $X$.

There exists a bijective correspondence between:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sieve_on_an_object_in_a_category}{sieves} on $X$, and
    \item \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:subfunctor_of_a_presheaf_of_sets_on_a_category}{subfunctors} of $h_X$ (i.e., subobjects of $h_X$ in the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:diagram_in_a_category_indexed_by_a_category}{functor category} $\operatorname{PSh}(\calC) = [\calC^{\operatorname{op}}, \mathbf{Set}]$\CrefIfExists{definition:presheaf_on_a_category}).
\end{enumerate}

Explicitly, given a sieve $S$ on $X$, one defines a subfunctor $F_S \subseteq h_X$ by setting
$$F_S(U) = \{f \in \operatorname{Hom}_{\calC}(U, X) \mid f \in S\}$$
for each object $U$ of $\calC$, with the restriction maps inherited from $h_X$. Conversely, given a subfunctor $F \subseteq h_X$, one recovers a sieve by taking
$$S_F = \bigcup_{U \in \operatorname{Ob}(\calC)} F(U) \subseteq \bigcup_{U \in \operatorname{Ob}(\calC)} \operatorname{Hom}_{\calC}(U, X),$$
which is a sieve because the naturality condition on $F$ ensures closure under precomposition.

These two constructions are mutually inverse, establishing a bijection. Moreover, this correspondence is natural in $X$: if $g: Y \to X$ is a morphism in $\calC$, then the pullback map $h_Y \to h_X$ induced by composition with $g$ sends a sieve $S$ on $X$ to the sieve $g^*(S)$ on $Y$ generated by precomposing elements of $S$ with $g$.
\end{proposition}

Let $G$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group}{group}. Let $N$ and $M$ be \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:normal_subgroup_of_a_group}{normal subgroups} of $G$ with $M \subseteq N$.

Then $M$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:normal_subgroup_of_a_group}{normal subgroup} of the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:quotient_of_a_group_by_a_normal_subgroup}{quotient group} $G/M$, and there is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:group_homomorphism}{group isomorphism}
$$ \hlin{\Phi: (G/M)/(N/M) \xrightarrow{\,\sim\,} G/N} $$
given by $\Phi((gM)(N/M)) = gN$ for all $g \in G$.

\begin{theorem}[Cayley--Hamilton Theorem] \label{theorem:cayley_hamilton_for_linear_operators_on_finite_dimensional_vector_spaces}
    Let $F$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}, and let $V$ be a finite-dimensional \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:vector_space_over_a_field}{$F$-vector space}. Let $T: V \to V$ be a linear endomorphism, and let $\chi_T(t) \in F[t]$ be its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:characteristic_polynomial_of_an_endomorphism_of_a_vector_space_over_a_field}{characteristic polynomial}.

    Then $\chi_T(T) = 0$ as an endomorphism of $V$.

Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, (\mathcal{F}_t)_{0 \leq t \leq T}, P)$ be a filtered probability space supporting a standard Brownian motion $\{B_t\}_{t \geq 0}$. Let $X = \{X_s\}_{s \in [t, T]}$ satisfy the SDE
$$dX_s = \mu(s, X_s) \, ds + \sigma(s, X_s) \, dB_s, \quad X_t = x.$$

Let $f: [0, T] \times \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $g: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be bounded measurable functions. Consider the parabolic PDE
$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} + \mu(t, x) \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} + \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2(t, x) \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} - f(t, x)u + g(t, x) = 0$$
with terminal condition $u(T, x) = 0$.

Then the unique bounded solution is given by
$$u(t, x) = \mathbb{E}\left[\int_t^T e^{-\int_t^s f(\tau, X_\tau) \, d\tau} g(s, X_s) \, ds \;\bigg|\; X_t = x\right].$$

In the special case $f \equiv 0$ and terminal condition $u(T, x) = \Phi(x)$,
$$u(t, x) = \mathbb{E}\left[\Phi(X_T) \;\big|\; X_t = x\right]$$
solves $\tfrac{\partial u}{\partial t} + \mu \tfrac{\partial u}{\partial x} + \tfrac{1}{2}\sigma^2 \tfrac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} = 0$.
\end{theorem}

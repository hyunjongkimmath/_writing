	Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring}, $M$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finitely_generated_modules_over_rings}{finitely generated} right $R$-module, and let $m_1, \ldots, m_n \in M$.

Then $m_1, \ldots, m_n$ generate $M$ as an $R$-module.
\end{proposition}

	Suppose that the images $\bar{m}_1, \ldots, \bar{m}_n$ of $m_1, \ldots, m_n$ in $M/J(R)M$ generate this quotient as an $R/J(R)$-module. Let $N$ be the submodule of $M$ generated by $m_1, \ldots, m_n$.

	$$x - \sum_{i=1}^n m_i r_i \in J(R)M.$$
	Hence $x \in N + J(R)M$. Therefore, $N + J(R)M = M$.

\begin{proposition}[Nakayama's generator criterion]  \label{proposition:nakayamas_generator_criterion_for_finitely_generated_modules_over_a_ring}

Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring}, $M$ a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finitely_generated_modules_over_rings}{finitely generated} right $R$-module, and let $m_1, \ldots, m_n \in M$.

Suppose the images of $m_1, \ldots, m_n$ in the quotient module $M/\CrefAndHyperref{definition:jacobson_radical_of_a_ring}{J(R)}M$ generate this quotient as a module over the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:quotient_ring_of_a_ring_by_a_two_sided_ideal}{quotient ring} $R/J(R)$. Then $m_1, \ldots, m_n$ generate $M$ as an $R$-module.
\end{proposition}

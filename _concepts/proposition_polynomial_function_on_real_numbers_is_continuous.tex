\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:polynomial_function_on_real_numbers_is_continuous}
    Let $\bbR$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field_of_real_numbers}{field of real numbers} equipped with its \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:standard_absolute_value_on_real_numbers}{standard topology}. Every polynomial $p \in \bbR[x]$\CrefIfExists{definition:polynomial_ring_over_a_commutative_ring} defines a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:continuous_map_of_topological_spaces}{continuous} function $p : \bbR \to \bbR$ via evaluation.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
    A constant polynomial is continuous by \Cref{proposition:constant_function_is_continuous_between_topological_spaces}. The identity function is continuous by \Cref{proposition:identity_function_is_continuous_between_topological_spaces}. By repeatedly applying \Cref{proposition:continuity_of_pointwise_product_of_continuous_real_valued_functions} and \Cref{proposition:continuity_of_pointwise_sum_of_continuous_real_valued_functions}, continuity is preserved under multiplication and addition of continuous functions. Since every polynomial is a finite sum of products of constants and the identity function, it is continuous.
\end{proof}

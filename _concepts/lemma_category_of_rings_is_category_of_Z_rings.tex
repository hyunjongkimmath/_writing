\begin{lemma}  \label{lemma:category_of_rings_is_category_of_Z_rings}
    The \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category_of_rings}{category of rings} is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:equivalence_of_categories}{equivalent} to the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:category_of_rings_over_a_ring}{category of $\bbZ$-rings}.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
    By \Cref{theorem:integers_universal_property_ring_of_integers_is_the_initial_object_in_the_category_of_rings}, for every ring $R$ there exists a unique ring homomorphism $\phi_R: \bbZ \to R$. This makes $\bbZ$ an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:initial_final_zero_objects_of_a_category}{initial object} of $\mathbf{Ring}$.

    We construct an equivalence of categories $F: \mathbf{Ring} \to \mathbf{Ring}_\bbZ$. On objects, send each ring $R$ to the pair $(R, \phi_R)$, where $\phi_R: \bbZ \to R$ is the unique ring homomorphism. On morphisms, send each ring homomorphism $f: R \to S$ to itself, regarded as a morphism in $\mathbf{Ring}_\bbZ$. To see that this is well-defined, note that for any ring homomorphism $f: R \to S$, the triangle
    \begin{center}
        \begin{tikzcd}
            & \bbZ \arrow[ld, "\phi_R"'] \arrow[rd, "\phi_S"] & \\
            R \arrow[rr, "f"'] & & S
        \end{tikzcd}
    \end{center}
    commutes because both $f \circ \phi_R$ and $\phi_S$ are ring homomorphisms from $\bbZ$ to $S$, and by uniqueness in \Cref{theorem:integers_universal_property_ring_of_integers_is_the_initial_object_in_the_category_of_rings} they must coincide.

    The functor $F$ is clearly \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:full_and_faithful_functor_between_locally_small_categories}{fully faithful}, since it acts as the identity on morphism sets. It is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:essentially_surjective_functor_between_categories}{essentially surjective} because every object of $\mathbf{Ring}_\bbZ$ has the form $(R, \psi)$ for some ring homomorphism $\psi: \bbZ \to R$, and by uniqueness $\psi = \phi_R$, so $(R, \psi) = F(R)$.

    Therefore, $F$ is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:equivalence_of_categories}{equivalence of categories}.
\end{proof}

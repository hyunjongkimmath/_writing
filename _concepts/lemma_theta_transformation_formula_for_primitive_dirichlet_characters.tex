Let $k \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 1}$, and let $\chi$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:primitive_dirichlet_character}{primitive Dirichlet character modulo $k$}. Let $\theta_\chi(u)$ denote the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:theta_function_associated_to_a_dirichlet_character}{theta function associated to $\chi$}, and let $\varepsilon(\chi)$ be the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:root_number_of_a_dirichlet_character}{root number of $\chi$}. Then for all $u > 0$,
$$\theta_\chi\!\left( \frac{1}{u} \right) = \varepsilon(\chi) \, \left( \frac{u}{k} \right)^{\!1/2} \theta_{\overline{\chi}}(u),$$
where $\overline{\chi}$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complex_conjugate_of_a_dirichlet_character}{complex conjugate character}.
\end{lemma}

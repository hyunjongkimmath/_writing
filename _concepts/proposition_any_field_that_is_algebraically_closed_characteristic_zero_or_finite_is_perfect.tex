\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:any_field_that_is_algebraically_closed_characteristic_zero_or_finite_is_perfect}
Let $k$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field}. 
If $k$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebraically_closed_field}{algebraically closed}, of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:characteristic_of_a_ring}{characteristic} $0$, or a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_field}{finite field}, then $k$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:perfect_field}{perfect field}.
\end{proposition}

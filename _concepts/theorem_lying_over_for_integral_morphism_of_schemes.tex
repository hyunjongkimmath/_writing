\begin{theorem}[Lying Over for an Integral Morphism of Schemes] \label{theorem:lying_over_for_integral_morphism_of_schemes}

Let $f : X \to Y$ be an integral morphism of schemes (i.e., an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:affine_morphism_of_schemes}{affine morphism} such that for every affine open $\operatorname{Spec}(B) \subseteq Y$, the ring $\mathcal{O}_X(f^{-1}(\operatorname{Spec}(B)))$ is integral over $B$). Then $f$ is surjective and a closed map.

Equivalently, for every point $y \in Y$, there exists $x \in X$ such that $f(x) = y$. In terms of residue fields, this means for each $y \in Y$ there is an $x \in f^{-1}(y)$ such that the induced extension $\kappa(y) \to \kappa(x)$ is an integral extension of fields (hence an algebraic field extension).
\end{theorem}

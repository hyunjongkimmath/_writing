\begin{theorem}[Characteristic function determines distribution] \label{theorem:characteristic_function_determines_distribution}
Let $X$ and $Y$ be two \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:random_variable_on_a_probability_measure_space}{random variables} defined on possibly different \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:probability_measure_space_for_a_measure_on_a_measurable_space}{probability measure spaces}, and suppose their \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:distribution_of_a_random_variable_on_a_probability_measure_space}{distributions} $\mu_X$ and $\mu_Y$ have characteristic functions $\varphi_X$ and $\varphi_Y$, respectively.

If $\varphi_X(t) = \varphi_Y(t)$ for all $t \in \mathbb{R}$, then $\mu_X = \mu_Y$.
\end{theorem}

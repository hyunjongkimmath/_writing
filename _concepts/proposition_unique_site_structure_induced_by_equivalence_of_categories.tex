\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:unique_site_structure_induced_by_equivalence_of_categories}

Let $F: \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:equivalence_of_categories}{equivalence of categories} with \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:equivalence_of_categories}{quasi-inverse} $G: \mathcal{D} \to \mathcal{C}$. Let $(\mathcal{C}, J)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{site}. Then there exists a unique \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:grothendieck_topology_on_a_category_site_covering_sieve_topologically_generating_family}{Grothendieck topology} $K$ on $\mathcal{D}$ such that $F: (\mathcal{C}, J) \to (\mathcal{D}, K)$ and $G: (\mathcal{D}, K) \to (\mathcal{C}, J)$ are both \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:continuous_functor_of_sites}{continuous functors of sites} (i.e., $F$ and $G$ form an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:equivalence_of_sites}{equivalence of sites}).

Explicitly, $K$ is the topology on $\mathcal{D}$ defined by: for $U \in \mathcal{D}$, a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sieve_on_an_object_in_a_category}{sieve} $S$ on $U$ is $K$-covering if and only if the sieve on $G(U)$ generated by $\{G(\varphi) \mid \varphi \in S\}$ is $J$-covering. Equivalently, $K$ is the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:induced_topology_on_a_category_by_a_functor_to_a_site}{induced topology} $G^*J$ along $G$, or equivalently the topology for which $F$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cocontinuous_functor_of_sites}{cocontinuous} and $G$ is continuous.

\begin{proof}
Existence: Define $K$ as above. Since $G$ is a functor, $K$ is a Grothendieck topology on $\mathcal{D}$ (it is the pullback of $J$ along $G$). By construction, $G: (\mathcal{D}, K) \to (\mathcal{C}, J)$ is continuous. To see that $F: (\mathcal{C}, J) \to (\mathcal{D}, K)$ is continuous, let $S$ be a $J$-covering sieve on $V \in \mathcal{C}$. The sieve generated by $\{F(\psi) \mid \psi \in S\}$ on $F(V)$ is $K$-covering because its pullback along $G$ is the sieve generated by $\{GF(\psi) \mid \psi \in S\}$, which contains the $J$-covering sieve $S$ (since $\eta: \mathrm{Id}_{\mathcal{C}} \to GF$ is a natural isomorphism, the map $S \to GF(S)$ is an isomorphism of sieves). Hence $F$ is continuous.

Uniqueness: Suppose $K'$ is another topology on $\mathcal{D}$ making $F$ and $G$ continuous. Then $\mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{D}}: (\mathcal{D}, K) \to (\mathcal{D}, K')$ is continuous (since $G$ is continuous for both, and $F$ is a quasi-inverse), and similarly $\mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{D}}: (\mathcal{D}, K') \to (\mathcal{D}, K)$ is continuous. Hence $K = K'$.
\end{proof}
\end{proposition}

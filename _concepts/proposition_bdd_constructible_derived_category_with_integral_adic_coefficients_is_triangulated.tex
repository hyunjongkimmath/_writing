\begin{proposition}[{\cite[Corollary 10.1.21]{fu_ect}}] \label{proposition:bdd_constructible_derived_category_with_integral_adic_coefficients_is_triangulated}
    Let $(R, \lambda)$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:complete_ring_with_respect_to_an_ideal}{complete} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:discrete_valuation_ring}{DVR} such that $R/\lambda^n$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:cardinal_number_and_cardinality_of_a_set}{finite} for all $n \geq 1$. Let $X$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme}{scheme} of \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:scheme_of_finite_type}{finite type} over a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:finite_field}{finite} or \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebraically_closed_field_or_separably_closed_field}{separably closed} \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:field}{field} $k$. Consider \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:sheaf_on_a_site}{sheaves} on the \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:small_etale_site_of_a_scheme}{(small) \'etale site} of $X$.

    The category \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:derived_category_of_bounded_constructible_complexes_of_adic_sheaves_with_integral_coefficients_on_a_noetherian_scheme}{$\Dbc(X, R)$} is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:triangulated_category}{triangulated category}.

    \TODO{This is probably still true over general fields, see Bhatt and Scholze's profinite topology Proposition 6.8.14}

    % Classically, this was first established when $k$ is \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:algebraically_closed_field_or_separably_closed_field}{separably closed} or finite (\cite[Corollary 10.1.21]{fu_ect}). More generally, \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{theorem:six_functor_formalism_of_ekedahls_adic_categories_on_schemes}{Ekedahl's reformulation} of adic sheaves yields the triangulated structure for $X$ finite type over an arbitrary field.
\end{proposition}

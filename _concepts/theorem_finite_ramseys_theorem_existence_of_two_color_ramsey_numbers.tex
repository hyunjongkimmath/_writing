For all positive integers $r$ and $s$, the two-color Ramsey number $R(r,s)$ exists and is finite.

This is proved by double induction on $r+s$. The base cases $r=1$ or $s=1$ are trivial since $R(1,k) = 1$ and $R(k,1) = 1$. For the inductive step, \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{lemma:recursive_upper_bound_for_two_color_ramsey_numbers}{the recursive bound} gives
$$R(r,s) \leq R(r-1,s) + R(r,s-1),$$
where both terms on the right are finite by the induction hypothesis.

An explicit upper bound follows from this recursion:
$$R(r,s) \leq \binom{r+s-2}{r-1}.$$

\begin{proposition} \label{proposition:hecke_operators_are_self_adjoint_wrt_petersson_inner_product}
\TODO{AI generated, verify}
Let $k$ be a positive even integer and let $\Gamma = \mathrm{SL}_2(\bbZ)$. For each prime $p$, the Hecke operator $T_p$ is self-adjoint with respect to the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:petersson_inner_product_on_cusp_forms_of_a_congruence_subgroup}{Petersson inner product} on the \CrefAndHyperref{definition:vector_space_of_modular_and_cusp_forms}{space of cusp forms} $S_k(\Gamma)$: for all $f, g$ in this space,
$$\hlin{\langle T_p f, g \rangle = \langle f, T_p g \rangle.}$$

As a consequence, the operators $\{T_p : p \text{ prime}\}$ are simultaneously diagonalizable on $S_k(\Gamma)$ with respect to an orthonormal basis\CrefIfExists{definition:orthogonal_and_orthonormal_sets_in_an_inner_product_space} of eigenforms.
\end{proposition}

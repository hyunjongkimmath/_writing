Let $G$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:simple_graph_directed_graph_multigraph_weighted_graph}{simple graph}. The \hldef{complement of $G$}, denoted by \hl{$\overline{G}$}, is the simple graph with $V(\overline{G}) = V(G)$ and
$$E(\overline{G}) = \{\{u,v\} : u,v \in V(G), u \neq v, \{u,v\} \notin E(G)\}.$$

A subset $S \subseteq V(G)$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:clique_independent_set_of_a_graph}{clique in $G$} if and only if it is an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:clique_independent_set_of_a_graph}{independent set in $\overline{G}$}. In particular,
$$\omega(G) = \alpha(\overline{G}), \quad \alpha(G) = \omega(\overline{G}).$$

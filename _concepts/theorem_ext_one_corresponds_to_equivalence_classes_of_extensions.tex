\begin{theorem}[cf. {\cite[Theorem 3.4.3]{weibel}} for a discussion for modules] \label{theorem:ext_one_corresponds_to_equivalence_classes_of_extensions}
    Let $\calA$ be an \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:abelian_category}{abelian category} with \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:has_enough_injectives_or_projectives_for_an_abelian_category}{enough injectives} or with \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:has_enough_injectives_or_projectives_for_an_abelian_category}{enough projectives}. Let $A,B \in \calA$.

    The assignment $\Theta$ establishes a bijective correspondence
    $$\{\text{ equivalence classes of extensions of } A \text{ by } B \} \leftrightarrow{\cong} \Ext^1(A,B)$$
    in which the equivalence cclass of the split extension corresponds to $0 \in \Ext^1(A,B)$.
\end{theorem}

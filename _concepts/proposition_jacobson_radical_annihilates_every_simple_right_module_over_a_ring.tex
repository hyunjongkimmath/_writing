\begin{proposition}[Jacobson radical annihilates every simple right module]  \label{proposition:jacobson_radical_annihilates_every_simple_right_module_over_a_ring}

	Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring}, not assumed commutative, and let $S$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:simple_module_of_a_ring}{simple} right $R$-module.

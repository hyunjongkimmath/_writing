\begin{conjecture}[Köthe Conjecture, 1930] \label{conjecture:kothe_conjecture}
Let $R$ be a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:ring}{ring}. If $R$ contains a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:left_ideal}{left ideal} $L$ such that every element of $L$ is nilpotent (i.e., $L$ is a \CrefAndHyperrefIfExist{definition:nil_ideal_of_a_ring}{nil left ideal}), then $L$ is contained in a two-sided nil ideal of $R$.
\end{conjecture}
